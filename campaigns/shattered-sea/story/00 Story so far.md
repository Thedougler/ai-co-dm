# Story so far
## Cold open: Aruhe
On [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]], a purple Grung and wolfrabbits made the next chapter immediate: strange local life, contested ground, and a survey assignment that is more than cartography.
## Season 1 — closed
- The crew came together in [[Calven and Calveno]], crossing sewer routes and colonial obligations while the [[Passage]] moved them through danger.
- [[Delmar Fisk]]’s stolen [[Pearl of Souls]] made the crew a target for [[Umberlee]] and connected personal losses to the [[Drowned Maw]].
- A raid and the [[Mercatura]] crisis exposed Grung operations, missing people, and the cost of power hidden beneath a functioning city.
- [[Solange Barret]]’s ritual culminated in [[Otar the Foul]], a twelve-foot regenerating horror under Mercatura. The crew stopped it; Otar is dead and the Mercatura bombs are a closed Season 1 thread.
- [[Nona Black-Jaw]]’s Passage network, [[Fieschi]]’s credit, [[Shepherd Grigori]]’s unexplained healing, and the Sentinels’ secrets survived the finale.
## Season 2 — open state
The crew is aboard [[Uncertainty]] in a Midchain sandbox. The route points to [[Sparhold]], then [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]], while [[Nona Black-Jaw]] expects Perrin to survey Aruhe as a [[Tangle]] anchor. The 314+ taken by the Grung fleet, the Pearl at the bottom of the Maw, Simone’s hunters, the Crown hunt for Grigori, and the Red Wake remain in motion.
