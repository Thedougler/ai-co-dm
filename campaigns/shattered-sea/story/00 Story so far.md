# Story so far

## Season 1 — closed
- The crew came together in [[Calven and Calveno]], crossing sewer routes and colonial obligations while the [[Passage]] moved them through danger.
- [[Delmar Fisk]]'s stolen [[Pearl of Souls]] made the crew a target for [[Umberlee]] and connected personal losses to the [[Drowned Maw]].
- A raid and the [[Mercatura]] crisis exposed Grung operations, missing people, and the cost of power hidden beneath a functioning city.
- [[Solange Barret]]'s ritual culminated in [[Otar the Foul]], a twelve-foot regenerating horror under Mercatura. The crew stopped it; Otar died and the Mercatura bombs became a closed Season 1 thread.
- [[Nona Black-Jaw]]'s Passage network, [[Fieschi]]'s credit, [[Shepherd Grigori]]'s unexplained healing, and the Sentinels' secrets survived the finale.

## Season 2 — open state
- The crew reached [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]], where a purple Grung and wolfrabbits made the next chapter immediate: strange local life, contested ground, and a survey assignment that was more than cartography.
- The crew then travelled aboard [[Uncertainty]] through the Midchain with [[Sparhold]] and Aruhe as open route handles.
- [[Nona Black-Jaw]] expected Perrin to survey Aruhe as a [[Tangle]] anchor; the 314+ taken by the Grung fleet, the Pearl at the bottom of the Maw, Simone's hunters, the Crown hunt for Grigori, and the Red Wake remained in motion.

## Open handle
The next choice remained the route aboard [[Uncertainty]]—[[Sparhold]] or [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]]—while the taken people, the Pearl, the Maw seal, Simone, Grigori, and the Red Wake remained unresolved.
