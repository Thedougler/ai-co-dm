# Letters of Marque

> [!narration] Narration
> Weathered Crown papers sit under a seal that still looks official at a glance. The parchment is soft at the folds, the edges salt-worn, and the wording vague enough to make a careful clerk uneasy.

## Look
Weathered parchment bearing the Dravosi Crown's seal; worn folds and salt-stained edges.

## What it does (table)
**Social/legal credential only.** These are Crown privateer papers authorizing [[Barnaby Rook]] to work under the Crown. They are nonmagical and carry no combat or spell effect.

The papers were found in [[Barnaby Rook]]'s cabin aboard the *[[Uncertainty]]* (the *Surety* in the legacy wording). Their paper value is approximately **30,000 gp**, but converting that value requires a buyer willing to honor or exploit the commission.

## Charges / limits
- No charges; no attunement.
- A clerk, officer, or rival may treat the commission as live, stale, forged, or politically inconvenient.

## Story hooks
The papers can open a port, buy a hearing, or put a price on the bearer's head. Their connection to Crown bureaucracy makes [[Aldric Drave]]'s shadow relevant even when Rook is not present.

A sailor or Crown clerk can recognize the seal and format. Whether the commission remains live is a separate legal question.
