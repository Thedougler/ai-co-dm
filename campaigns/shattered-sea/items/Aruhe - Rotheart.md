# Rotheart

> [!narration] Narration
> Black-hearted fruit hangs on the sickest trees of the Marshes, warm and faintly pulsing in the hand. The pulp is the colour of old blood. Heat and a faint pulse sit under the dark skin.

Consumable, Very Rare

As a Bonus Action, eat this fruit. You remove all levels of **Exhaustion**, neutralize poison, and cure any disease affecting you. For **24 hours**, each Hit Die you spend restores its maximum number of hit points.
