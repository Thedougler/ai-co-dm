# Forged Rig Trial Certificate

> [!narration] Narration
> An official-looking certificate claims a successful deep-pressure trial for a Sunkline rig. The ink and seal look ready for investors and recruits. The paper is clean where a working log would show revisions.


## Look
Official-looking forged certificate; claims a successful deep-pressure trial for a Sunkline rig.

## What it does (table)
No magic or mechanical effect. It is the public face of the fraud, used to court investors and recruit divers.

## Charges / limits
- None; document only.
- Its leverage depends on exposing the mismatch between the paper and the trial record.

## Story hooks
The certificate and [[Last Berth False Log]] are two halves of the same fraud: one persuades the public, the other hides the false result.

## Provenance
Legacy source: `/Users/nick/shattered-sea/wiki/shattered-sea/pearl-and-the-maw/item.forged-rig-trial-certificate.md`.
Associated with [[Sunkline]]'s operation in [[Fathomrush]] and hidden at the Last Berth.

## Identification
A review against public trial records shows that no matching mid-shelf trial was passed.

## Design notes (DM)
- Document, Common, no magic. Use as evidence/leverage only.
- If exposed, the forgery threatens Sunkline's funding and recruitment; do not add a separate mechanical effect.
