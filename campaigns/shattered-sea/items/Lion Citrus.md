# Lion Citrus

> [!narration] Narration
> A fist-sized citrus wears a lion-pelt rind, warm oil shining under the thumb.
> Its sweet-electric flesh wakes the mouth before the courage reaches the heart.


## Look
Fist-sized citrus; lion-pelt rind; warm oily peel; sweet-electric flesh.

## What it does (table)
**Fruit (consumable).** Bonus Action to eat. Gain **10 temporary hit points** and the benefits of *bless* for **1 hour**, with no Concentration required.

Genuinely beneficial — no secret poison. Plucking living fruit from Aruhe draws notice — local life may converge on the taker (DM adjudicates; not a hive mind, not a timed island OS). Passage, watching, and spring water stay quiet.

## Charges / limits
- One fruit, one use.
- Abundant in the Old Gardens, especially on the first terraces.

## Story hooks
The courage fruit on the first terraces. Its value makes an easy export temptation; the garden notices who strips a living branch.

## Provenance
Old Gardens terrace fruit. Stimulant, sugar, and terpene chemistry pushed to magical extremes by the Blight, not a new species.

## Identification
DC 13 Nature/Medicine (1 min): broad fortifying function. DC 15 or *Identify*: exact effect.

## Design notes (DM)
- **Chassis:** Potion of Heroism, reskinned as fruit ([D&D Beyond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/4709-potion-of-heroism)); 2024 potions use a Bonus Action, an intentional 2014 Action → 2024 Bonus Action recalibration ([2024 Basic Rules — Potions](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/magic-items#Potions)).
- **Trade:** Rare ceiling effect traded against one use, abundant supply, and soft garden notice; no attunement or concentration.
- **Knob:** Make the supply uncommon rather than abundant if the party is banking too many one-hour courage effects.
