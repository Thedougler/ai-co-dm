# Karath Gold-Seal Toxin

> [!narration] Narration
> A narrow sealed vial holds a cultivated toxin dose, cool glass under the thumb. The stopper keeps it as a reagent, not a charm. One dose sits ready to pour or coat a blade.


## Look
One dose in a sealed vial; a nonmagical biological and chemical reagent.

## What it does (table)
- **Gold-seal reagent.** One dose is the Ossketh reagent for the intended **gold** color. With other-cast [[Ossketh]], a second practitioner using **Complete the Seal**, and this toxin, the target becomes **Sealed gold**. Without this toxin, gold cannot finish as Sealed gold.
- **Injury poison.** As a Bonus Action, apply the dose to one weapon or up to three pieces of ammunition. The next piercing or slashing hit within 1 minute forces the target to make a **DC 15 Constitution save**. On a failure, it takes **4d6 poison damage** and is **Poisoned** until the end of its next turn. On a success, it takes half damage and suffers no Poisoned condition.
- **Contact dose (optional).** A small handling dose can be used as a contact hazard: **DC 14 Constitution save** or **Poisoned** for 1 minute, with a save at the end of each turn to end the condition. This is a farm-handling hazard, not the main combat dial.
- **Not magical.** *Detect Magic* finds nothing. Nature or Medicine can identify the biology/chemistry.

## Charges / limits
- One dose per vial; the dose is consumed by a seal, weapon/ammunition application, or contact use.
- No attunement. Its price is scarcity and farm secrecy, not magic-item access.
- The Gold reagent farms are hidden in the interior of [[Karath]], even from lower castes. Simone never received this toxin.

## Story hooks
- A vial is a dangerous lead toward Karath's hidden Gold farms, not proof that a buyer knows their location.
- [[Simone]]'s stalled partial gold is evidence of substitute stock, not access to this reagent.
- A Crown buyer may mistake any gold-staining toxin for the thing that can finish an Ossketh seal.

## Provenance
Vault sources: [[Karath]], [[Simone]], [[Grung Color and the Sealing Rite]], and [[Ossketh]]. Karath's note establishes the secret interior Gold reagent farms and Simone's lack of Karath toxin; the sealing notes establish the gold boundary and the second pair of hands. Legacy context cited by [[Karath]] remains read-only at `/Users/nick/shattered-sea/wiki/shattered-sea/aruhe-hungry-isle/`.

## Identification
- **DC 13 Nature or Medicine:** Grung farm toxin / caste reagent.
- **DC 15, or Identify-equivalent expertise:** the true gold-seal reagent; it cannot be faked by Crown stock.

## Design notes (DM)
- **Chassis:** public poison types in [2024 Poisons](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg/hazards#Poisons) and [Roll20 poison rules](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Poison), compared against Basic Poison, Serpent Venom, and Wyvern Poison.
- **Dial:** the injury poison stays below Wyvern Poison while remaining stronger than the Crown substitute; the primary dial is its exclusive ability to finish gold.
- **Trade:** scarcity plus farm secrecy, not attunement.
- **Knob:** if this is too hot for the Outer Midchain, drop the injury poison to **3d6** or lower the save to **DC 14**.
