# Ghost Plum

> [!narration] Narration
> A milk-glass plum hangs under a thick bloom, pale enough to catch the dim light.
> Its outline fades when you look away, as if the fruit is already leaving.


## Look
Milk-glass pale plum; thick bloom; outline fades in dim light.

## What it does (table)
**Fruit (consumable).** Bonus Action to eat. You have the **Invisible** condition for **1 hour**. The effect ends early if you make an attack roll, deal damage, or cast a spell.

Genuinely beneficial — no secret poison. Plucking living fruit from Aruhe draws notice — local life may converge on the taker (DM adjudicates; not a hive mind, not a timed island OS). Passage, watching, and spring water stay quiet.

## Charges / limits
- One fruit, one use.
- Uncommon in the Quiet; an uncommon find, not a dependable orchard harvest.

## Story hooks
A stealth crossing becomes possible, but the fruit's pale outline and the garden's attention make concealment a choice rather than a guarantee.

## Provenance
Quiet fruit, grown where ambient sound and dim light swallow edges. Its thick bloom carries the Blight's light-bending chemistry.

## Identification
DC 13 Nature/Medicine (1 min): broad concealment function. DC 15 or *Identify*: Invisible for 1 hour with the stated early-ending triggers.

## Design notes (DM)
- **Chassis:** Potion of Invisibility, reskinned as fruit ([D&D Beyond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228935-potion-of-invisibility)); the current public page lists that chassis as Rare, so this legacy Rare rating is retained. Consumption intentionally follows the 2024 Bonus Action rather than the 2014 Action ([2024 Basic Rules — Potions](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/magic-items#Potions)).
- **Trade:** Rare is held by one use, the attack/damage/spell break, uncommon supply, and soft garden notice; no attunement.
- **Knob:** Bump to Very Rare if Ghost Plums become too common in play; do not hide a rarity change behind the fiction.
