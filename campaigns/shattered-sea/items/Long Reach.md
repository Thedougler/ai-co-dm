# Long Reach

> [!narration] Narration
> _Sentinel apprentices learn to strike past their wingspan — a breath, a stretch of will, and empty air answers like a limb. TotM / Visualizer welcome._

## Look
No object. A trained stance and breath-mark; some Sentinels bear a claw-sigil scar or ink as mnemonic, not the power’s source.

## What it does (table)
**Sentinel training boon** (counts as an uncommon wondrous item for attunement). While you have this technique bonded (attuned):

- Your unarmed strikes are magical, and you gain a **+1 bonus** to attack and damage rolls with unarmed strikes.
- **Extend the Reach (Bonus Action; 1/Dawn).** For 1 minute, your melee attacks with weapons or unarmed strikes can reach a target up to **15 feet** away. Those attacks deal an extra **1d6 force** damage on a hit.

## Story hooks
Apprentice milestone of the [[Sentinels of the Eyrie]]. Expected on trained Sentinels; rare outside the order. [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] may already have it or be mid-mastery — confirm in play / with Courtney.

## Provenance
Order training, not a found treasure. Losing attunement means the breath-mark won’t answer until retrained or re-bonded.

## Design notes (DM)
- **Chassis:** Reskin of public Eldritch Claw Tattoo loop (uncommon, attune, +1 unarmed magical, BA 1 min / 15 ft reach / +1d6, 1/dawn). Fiction is technique, not needle-tattoo item.
- **Pitch:** Lets a melee Sentinel (esp. monk) choose when to own space by spending a bonus action and a daily use.
- **Peers:** Eldritch Claw Tattoo (chassis); boundary = permanent reach weapons / higher-tier force riders.
- **Knobs:** Cut the +1 if she already has magical strikes from class; swap force → thunder for sky-flavor; deny Extend if exhausted.
