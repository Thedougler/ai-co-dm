# Long Reach

> [!narration] Narration
> A Sentinel strike that lands past ordinary wingspan. Breath and stance lock to the peak of a gust on a wind-open terrace. The timing is exact; the blow answers that measure.



## Fiction
Every Sentinel apprentice earns Long Reach, a martial technique learned through exact movement, breath, and stance in peak wind. It answers the order’s training rather than a found object. Fiction only: a trained Sentinel can strike beyond ordinary wingspan.

## Training
Apprentices learn on cantilevered terraces open on three sides. Fair is not the standard; exact is.
