# Whisper Fig

> [!narration] Narration
> The small green fig splits to show pale nerve-veins in its flesh.
> As it opens, the air goes quiet around the thoughts it is about to hear.


## Look
Small green fig; pale nerve-veins; quiet air around the split flesh.

## What it does (table)
**Fruit (consumable).** Bonus Action to eat. Gain the effects of *detect thoughts* for **10 minutes** (save DC **13**), with no Concentration required.

Genuinely beneficial — no secret poison. Plucking living fruit from Aruhe draws notice — local life may converge on the taker (DM adjudicates; not a hive mind, not a timed island OS). Passage, watching, and spring water stay quiet.

## Charges / limits
- One fruit, one use.
- Uncommon in the Quiet and along the Rot edge, where quiet minutes gather.

## Story hooks
Mind-signal fruit for a silent negotiation, a hidden route, or a conversation the party was not meant to hear. The quiet around the eater is its tell.

## Provenance
Quiet/Rot-edge fig. Pale vein-like flesh carries a short-lived mind-signal, a familiar plant network pushed into telepathy by the Blight.

## Identification
DC 13 Nature/Medicine (1 min): broad mental-sensing function. DC 15 or *Identify*: *detect thoughts* effects for 10 minutes, save DC 13, no Concentration.

## Design notes (DM)
- **Chassis:** *Detect thoughts* as a Rare consumable effect, with a fixed save DC 13 and no Concentration to make the short signal runnable; current public magic-item rules explain potion activation ([2024 Basic Rules — Potions](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/magic-items#Potions)). The 2014 Action → 2024 Bonus Action change is intentional.
- **Trade:** Rare social/information power traded against one use, ten minutes, a fixed DC, uncommon placement, and a conspicuous quiet tell.
- **Knob:** If the fig replaces too much investigation, reduce the duration to 1 minute or make the save DC 12; retain one-use scarcity before changing both.
