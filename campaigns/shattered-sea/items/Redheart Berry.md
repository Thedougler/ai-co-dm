# Redheart Berry

> [!narration] Narration
> A child-fist berry hangs deep red to near-black, staining the fingers before it breaks.
> Its wet flesh looks like a fresh wound and a ripe berry at once.


## Look
Child-fist berry; bloodlike juice; wet reactive red flesh.

## What it does (table)
**Fruit (consumable).** Bonus Action to eat. Regain **8d4 + 8** hit points.

Genuinely beneficial — no secret poison. Plucking living fruit from Aruhe draws notice — local life may converge on the taker (DM adjudicates; not a hive mind, not a timed island OS). Passage, watching, and spring water stay quiet.

## Charges / limits
- One berry, one use.
- Abundant on Old Gardens terraces (dozens in sight) — temptation is the point.

## Story hooks
Appears when someone is badly hurt. Export bait. Grung treat taking fruit as a death sentence.

## Provenance
Wiki `item.redheart-berry` (provisional). Blight-pushed plant chemistry, not a new species.

## Identification
DC 13 Nature/Medicine (1 min): restores. DC 15 or *Identify*: exact hit points.

## Design notes (DM)
- **Chassis:** Potion of Superior Healing numbers on a fruit ([D&D Beyond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/5134-potion-of-healing-superior)); 8d4 + 8 is the Rare ceiling benchmark. The 2014 Action → 2024 Bonus Action potion change is intentional ([2024 Basic Rules — Potions](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/magic-items#Potions)).
- **Trade:** Abundance + soft garden notice if claimed living, not attunement; the strong healing is still one use.
- **Knob:** 4d4 + 4 if too strong for Outer Aruhe; keep fiction either way.
