# Crown Substitute Toxin

> [!narration] Narration
> A privateer-ready vial of substitute stock can be carried beside a smaller canister prepared to break into gas.

**Also called:** Simone's stock; privateer vials.

## Look
A vial of nonmagical export toxin. A concentrated gas canister is the rarer prep form, recognized as Simone's method.

## What it does (table)
- **Seal interaction.** As an Ossketh gold-reagent substitute, it can contribute to an **Incomplete Seal** or stalled partial gold—bands and dots—on a self-cast. It **never produces Sealed gold**. Only the true Karath reagent can finish that result.
- **Injury poison (vial).** As a Bonus Action, coat one weapon or up to three pieces of ammunition. For 1 minute, the next hit forces a **DC 13 Constitution save**. On a failure, the target takes **2d6 poison damage** and is **Poisoned** until the end of its next turn. On a success, it takes half damage and suffers no Poisoned condition.
- **Inhaled gas (canister or alchemist throw).** Fill a **5-foot Cube**. Each creature in it makes a **DC 13 Constitution save**. On a failure, it takes **2d6 poison damage** and is **Poisoned** for 1 minute, repeating the save at the end of each turn. On a success, it takes half damage only.
- **Not magical.** *Detect Magic* finds nothing.

## Charges / limits
- One vial is one injury dose. A gas canister is a separate concentrated form, not an extra charge on a vial.
- **Uncommon** reflects export volume. Treat a concentrated gas canister as **Rare** if that delivery form is unusually scarce in the campaign.
- No attunement. Jean-Claude's twenty-vial cargo is story inventory, not a magic-item stack bonus.

## Story hooks
- [[Simone]] supplies the [[Dravosi Crown]] with substitute stock, **not Karath product**; the Crown cannot claim the farms by buying this.
- The toxin is already in privateer kit and [[Calven and Calveno]]'s sewers. [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] has twenty vials aboard [[Uncertainty]].
- The gas form is recognized as Simone's prep method, even when the label or carrier is missing.
- A stalled partial gold on a Grung is a warning that a substitute was used, not a failed Sealed gold rite.

## Identification
- **DC 13 Nature or Medicine:** Grung toxin / export stock.
- **DC 15:** Crown substitute; it fails as a gold-seal reagent. [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] or an expert Grung can also tell from the preparation method.
