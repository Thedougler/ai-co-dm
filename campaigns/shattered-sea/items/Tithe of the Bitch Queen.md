# Tithe of the Bitch Queen

> [!narration] Narration
> A palm-sized hoard piece sits cold in the hand: an unclouded jewel, untarnished gold, or a salt-fresh statue. Salt and silt have left no mark on it. Cool metal or stone feels newly claimed, as if it never spent years on the seafloor.


## Look
A generic piece from the Sunken Crown Tithe hoard: an unclouded jewel, untarnished gold, or salt-fresh statue. Divine preservation makes it look newly claimed, whatever its age.

## What it does (table)
| Dial | Rule |
|---|---|
| Tithe piece | Any piece taken from the Sunken Crown Tithe hoard is a Tithe piece. |
| Curse (object-bound) | While a Tithe piece is aboard a vessel or carried out of saltwater, unnatural **localized squalls** follow that vessel or carrier. Handing the piece to another creature transfers the weather with it. |
| Squalls | DM weather pressure: travel setbacks, checks to keep course or rigging, and boarding or visibility risk. They are not automatic ship destruction and are not a *Control Weather* spell for the party to Counterspell. |
| Not a spell | The curse cannot be Counterspelled at theft. *Remove Curse* fails. *Dispel Magic* suppresses the following weather for **1 minute**, then attention reasserts. |
| Release | The piece touches saltwater again. This ends direct weather-following; it does not settle ownership or what is owed. |
| Preservation | Jewels stay unclouded, gold untarnished, and statues as-if-new. There is no secret poison; the cost is attention. |
| Attunement | None. The curse rides the object, unlike the Shield of Missile Attraction chassis, which requires attunement. |

## Charges / limits
- No charges and no attunement; the curse is always present while the piece is out of saltwater.
- The default release condition is contact with open seawater. A submerged-but-dry hold does not count as touched saltwater.
- Squall severity is DM pressure, not a fixed damage effect or automatic wrecking rule.

## Story hooks
A salvage crew must choose between selling an immaculate relic and sailing with squalls in its wake. A buyer hands it onward to an innocent carrier. Waveservants demand its return but refuse to settle whether another treasure, including the Pearl, is also Tithe.

## Provenance
Dials owner: this item note. Fiction and the unresolved Pearl question remain in `campaigns/shattered-sea/lore/Tithe of the Bitch Queen.md`. The Sunken Crown hoard is Umberlee's older seafloor tithe; taking a piece draws attention, while returning it to saltwater ends only the direct weather-following.

## Identification
- **Detect Magic (paraphrase):** divine necromancy; an old, deliberate weight clings to the object.
- **Identify / lore:** the mark belongs to the *act of taking* and transfers with the object. It is not a normal removable spell effect.
- **DC 13 Religion or History:** sailors' warning—storm-taken treasure draws her weather.
- **DC 15 Religion or History, or Identify:** the exact release condition is contact with saltwater.

## Design notes (DM)
- **Sample pieces:** (1) an unclouded fist-sized jewel, a Rare temptation; (2) an untarnished gold bar or coin-hoard piece, also Rare; (3) a salt-fresh statue, heavier and Very Rare, built to tempt a crew into taking the worst cargo aboard.
- **Chassis / comparator:** The 2024 Shield of Missile Attraction is a Rare cursed item that requires attunement; its curse targets nearby ranged attacks and can be ended by *Remove Curse* ([D&D Beyond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/4754-shield-of-missile-attraction)). The Tithe is stronger socially: no attunement gate, and the weather follows the hull or carrier. See the [2024 Free Rules cursed items](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/magic-items).
- **Weather pressure:** Squalls are an adventure clock and seamanship problem—setbacks, course or rigging checks, visibility, and boarding risk—not a spell slot or a combat spell that the party can simply Counterspell.
- **Trade:** Treasure value is weighed against sailing the piece. The Taking Rule analogue is Umberlee's attention, not a poison rider.
- **Knobs:** Adjust squall severity and the one-minute *Dispel Magic* suppression window. By default, a submerged-but-dry hold does not count as saltwater contact; only open seawater releases the direct weather-following.
