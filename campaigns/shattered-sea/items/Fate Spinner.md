# Fate Spinner

> [!narration] Narration


## Fiction
A thumb-sized quartz four-sided top with worn faces, always cool. [[Master Kyzil]] gave it to [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] before her pilgrimage as a “meditation focus.” It is actually older than the Sentinels’ non-intervention oath. It watches and acts.

## What it does in play
The Spinner provides partial [[Long Sight]]: remote sight and communication with people the holder knows, visions of those people, advantage on rolls, and a spiritual connection across distance. At the table it is used like a phone. These are fiction-facing notes only.

## Reactions and provenance
- It reacts to the hidden ley line under [[Yssenmoor]].
- [[Sir Quackers the Fowl]] does not understand the dreidel reaction.
- [[Talon Vantyrus]] recognizes the Soul Incarnate signature; if he reads the imprint, it may crack the seal’s access to the transformation technique.
- Kyzil arranged a partial Long Sight leak because of the danger and never explained it.
