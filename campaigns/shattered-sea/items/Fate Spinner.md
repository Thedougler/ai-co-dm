# Fate Spinner

> [!narration] Narration
> _A thumb-sized four-sided quartz top, faces worn smooth, always cool. Spun in sunlight it throws coloured light-coins across the deck. It watches. It acts. TotM / Visualizer welcome._

## Look
Quartz four-sided top (dreidel-like), thumb-sized, cool, worn faces; spins a long time. Sunlight → coloured light coins.

## What it does (table)
**Wondrous item (relic), rare (requires attunement).** Older than the Sentinels’ non-intervention oath. Grants **partial [[Long Sight]]** — not the full order discipline.

### Spin (Action)
You spin the Fate Spinner. It remains spinning for **1 minute** or until you pocket it, it is knocked over (DC 10 Strength or a shove), or you drop concentration on the spin (this concentration is only for keeping it upright/spinning; it does **not** replace spell concentration rules for other effects unless noted).

While it spins, choose **one** creature you **personally know** on the same plane of existence (friend, enemy, or rival you have met and would recognize). That creature feels a familiar tug and knows you are reaching; it can refuse the link (no action). If it accepts:

**Call (at will while spinning).** You and the linked creature can speak and hear each other as if you stood 5 feet apart (languages still apply). This is the “phone.” Only one link at a time.

### Far Glance (1/Dawn; while linked)
You briefly share the linked creature’s sight: for up to **1 minute**, you see through their eyes (or a tight vision of their immediate surroundings if they are unconscious). You are blinded to your own surroundings while Far Glance lasts unless you end it early (no action). This is **partial Long Sight**.

### Fate’s Lean (3 charges; while linked)
When the linked creature makes an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can spend **1 charge** (reaction) to grant that roll **advantage**. The creature feels the lean. The Fate Spinner regains **1d3** expended charges daily at dawn.

### Costs and weirdness (always on while attuned)
- **Watched.** Each time you use Far Glance or Fate’s Lean, the DM advances or marks a quiet pressure (Sentinel notice, Maw attention, Countless interest, or the Spinner’s own agenda — pick what the scene needs). Telegraph with a tell: a light-coin colour that doesn’t match the sun, a second tug from an unknown direction, etc.
- **Shared sting.** When you spend Fate’s Lean and the roll still fails, you take **1d6 psychic** damage (the Spinner exacts a toll).
- **Severance.** If the linked creature dies while the Call is open, the Spinner goes inert until the next dawn, and you take **2d6 psychic** damage.
- **Refusal & politics.** A creature that refuses becomes harder to reach for 24 hours (Call fails automatically). Sentinel observers may recognize light-coin flares as illicit Long Sight.

## Story hooks
Gift of [[Master Kyzil]] to [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. Meaning sits between Sentinel teaching, pilgrimage, and the Maw. The Spinner **acts** — it is not a neutral tool.

## Provenance
Older than the order’s non-intervention oath (brief-canon). Kyzil’s gift; exact origin UNKNOWN.

## Design notes (DM)
- **Pitch:** A rare “phone” for a bonded cartographer-monk that buys remote help and glimpses by paying psychic sting, daily limits, and faction heat.
- **Chassis:** Between Sending Stones (uncommon, 1/day message pair) and Crystal Ball (very rare, full Scrying). Narrower than Scrying (known people only; consent); stronger than Sending (sustained call + advantage + glance).
- **Peers:** Sending Stones; Crystal Ball (SRD 5.2); Help action / limited remote Guidance-analogue for Fate’s Lean.
- **Envelope:** Exploration/social strong; combat support is 3 reactions/day with fail-pain — not a damage item.
- **Loop:** Tell (spin + light-coins) → Choice (call / glance / lean) → Cost (action to spin, charges, psychic, watched) → Payoff (info, ally success) → Counterplay (knock the top, refuse link, jam with silence wards, track the light).
- **Knobs:** 2 charges; Far Glance requires a Wis save DC 15 to maintain if the target is in the Maw; remove at-will Call and make Call 1/dawn if phone is too strong; make Watched a visible 4-step clock toward Kyzil or Talon.
- **Assumption:** Courtney/Crissdalynn is the attuner; party level mid-tier (Season 2).
