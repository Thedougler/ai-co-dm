# Terrace Citrus

> [!narration] Narration
> A fist-sized ordinary citrus hangs bright among the broken terrace stones.
> Its clean peel snaps with juice and a small, welcome spark of sourness.


## Look
Fist-sized ordinary citrus; bright peel; clean juice and sour flesh.

## What it does (table)
**Fruit (consumable).** Bonus Action to eat. Regain **1d4 Hit Points**.

Genuinely beneficial — no secret poison. Plucking living produce from Aruhe draws notice — local life may converge on the taker (DM adjudicates; not a hive mind, not a timed island OS). For this ordinary staple, the notice is a lighter garden complication rather than an automatic crisis.

## Charges / limits
- One citrus, one use.
- Ordinary terrace staple; common on the Old Gardens steps.

## Story hooks
The first harmless-looking harvest temptation. It teaches the party that even a small restorative has a relationship with the living garden.

## Provenance
Old Gardens terrace staple. Ordinary citrus chemistry, enlarged and sweetened by Aruhe's growth pressure but not a miracle fruit.

## Identification
DC 13 Nature/Medicine (1 min): broad restorative function. DC 15 or *Identify*: regains 1d4 Hit Points.

## Design notes (DM)
- **Chassis:** Potion of Healing baseline, reduced to a light 1d4 effect for a mundane-feeling common staple ([D&D Beyond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/8960641-potion-of-healing)). The 2014 Action → 2024 Bonus Action change is intentional for this potion-like consumable ([2024 Basic Rules — Potions](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/magic-items#Potions)).
- **Trade:** Common, no attunement, and weak healing keep it below miracle-fruit ceilings; the only pressure is one use and soft garden notice.
- **Knob:** If common healing is over-supplied, limit harvest by visible terrace clusters rather than reducing the 1d4 below a useful baseline.
