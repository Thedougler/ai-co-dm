# Grung Authority Seal

![[attachments/shattered-sea/items/grung-authority-seal.jpg]]

> [!narration] Narration
> A palm-sized scrap of handmade rainforest papyrus sits with ragged fibrous edges and pale pith strips still clinging to the weave. Gold ink is printed across the brown-green sheet in even rows of frog, tadpole, leaf, and water glyphs, the metal catching light on the warped paper. The sheet feels damp and plant-rough in the hand, and it smells faintly of wet fiber.

*Wondrous item, consumable, very rare*

When placed on a grung of the red caste or lower, the inscribed command is imprinted into the target's memory as their own belief and goal. The effect lasts indefinitely, or until ended by *greater restoration*, *wish*, or comparable magic.
