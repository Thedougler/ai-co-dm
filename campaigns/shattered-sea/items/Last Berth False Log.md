# Last Berth False Log

> [!narration] Narration
> A forged trial result lies beneath a loose floorboard at the Last Berth in [[Fathomrush]]. The paper is one discovery away from daylight.

## Look
A hidden, forged mid-shelf trial log; salt-worn paper tucked under a loose floorboard at Petera Ngata's boardinghouse.

## What it does (table)
No magic or mechanical effect. It is evidence and leverage: the log records a mid-shelf trial that [[Sunkline]] never passed.

## Charges / limits
- None; document only.
- Its value depends on who can authenticate it and who fears its release.

## Story hooks
The false log backs [[Sunkline]]'s recruiting and investor story while the company rebuilds the same failing valve. If exposed, crews and recruits may move to [[Voyvode]].
