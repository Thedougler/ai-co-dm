# Breathmelon

> [!narration] Narration
> A pale green melon floats at the river margin, its rind dimpled like a pair of lungs.
> Its air-cell flesh trembles when the current turns.


## Look
Pale green floating melon; lung-dimpled rind; air-cell lattice flesh.

## What it does (table)
**Fruit (consumable).** Bonus Action to eat. For **1 hour**, you can breathe air and water, and your swim speed equals your walking speed.

Genuinely beneficial — no secret poison. Plucking living fruit from Aruhe draws notice — local life may converge on the taker (DM adjudicates; not a hive mind, not a timed island OS). Passage, watching, and spring water stay quiet.

## Charges / limits
- One fruit, one use.
- Abundant along river margins and wetland edges.

## Story hooks
A Breathmelon releases trapped gas underwater when squeezed. It turns a dangerous river crossing into a tempting shortcut, not a quiet one.

## Provenance
River-margin fruit, abundant near wetlands. The air-cell lattice and buoyant rind are natural flotation adaptations pushed into magical water-breathing by the Blight.

## Identification
DC 13 Nature/Medicine (1 min): broad water-breathing function. DC 15 or *Identify*: air and water breathing plus walking-speed swim for 1 hour.

## Design notes (DM)
- **Chassis:** Potion of Water Breathing plus swim-speed parity ([D&D Beyond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228940-potion-of-water-breathing)); the added movement utility supports a Rare rather than the public potion's lower rarity. Consumption intentionally changed from the 2014 Action to the 2024 Bonus Action ([2024 Basic Rules — Potions](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/magic-items#Potions)).
- **Trade:** Rare exploration utility traded against one hour, one use, abundant-but-exposed placement, and soft garden notice; no combat damage or attunement.
- **Knob:** If the river ceases to be a meaningful obstacle, keep breathing but drop swim parity, or make the fruit uncommon; do not stack both reductions at once without a playtest.
