# A Sliver of the Unstable Form

> [!narration] Narration
> A fist-sized fragment of warm red hide rests in the hand. One edge still shows a wound that never quite settles. The hide keeps its shape and faint warmth.


## Look
Fist-sized red hide, faintly warm, with a wound-edge that never quite settles.

## What it does (table)
*Magic item, Very Rare (requires attunement).* As a **Bonus Action**, activate the sliver **once per Long Rest**. For 1 minute, at the start of each of your turns, regain **2d8 HP** unless you took fire or acid damage since the end of your previous turn.

Suppression is checked separately each turn. If you are stable at 0 HP, the regeneration still works. Fire or acid damage from your own spells does not suppress the sliver. You may dismiss the effect with no action.

## Charges / limits
- One activation per Long Rest; duration 1 minute.
- If you drop to 0 HP and fail a death save while the effect is suppressed, the effect ends.

## Story hooks
The sliver tore loose as [[Otar the Foul]] fell in [[Solange Barret]]'s Primary Chamber. Its survival may be as intentional as the summoning circle—or as accidental as the crew's escape.

A specialist can identify a regenerative magic woven through unstable living material. The exact trigger and suppression rule require attunement or testing.
