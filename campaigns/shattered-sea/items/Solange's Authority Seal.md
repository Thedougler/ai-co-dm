# Solange's Authority Seal

> [!narration] Narration
> The signet is heavier than gold. Its face is worn smooth except for one deep-cut symbol that refuses to dull.

## Look
Heavy signet; worn face; one deep-cut, unfading authority mark.

## What it does (table)
*Magic item, Rare (requires attunement).* As an **Action**, once per Long Rest, choose one Grung you can see within 60 feet. The target makes a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw**. On a failure, the seal imposes a one-word *command*-like order on the target's next turn.

The target is not Charmed or Frightened and knows what happened. The seal fails if another creature already controls the target. Gold-caste Grung do not answer. Non-Grung hear nothing that binds.

## Charges / limits
- One use per Long Rest.
- One visible Grung target within 60 feet; DC 15 Wisdom.
- No effect on Gold caste or a target already controlled by another creature.

## Story hooks
The seal makes red-caste authority portable. It was recovered from the Primary Chamber rubble after the Detonation and may connect [[Solange Barret]] to the officer who carried it before her.

## Identification
The deep-cut mark identifies a Grung authority seal. [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] can read its markings on sight.

## Design notes (DM)
- **Chassis:** limited *command* spell effect, narrowed to visible non-Gold Grung and one use per Long Rest; Rare rarity.
- This is a command-like effect, not the Charmed or Frightened condition.
