## Look
Unknown for the full order form. Partial access manifests through the [[Fate Spinner]], not a separate taught stance.

## What it does (table)
**Full Long Sight (order version):** UNKNOWN — do not invent. Stub only.

**Partial access (playable now):** What [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] has via the [[Fate Spinner]] — remote link, limited sight of people she personally knows, and the ability to lean fate toward them (advantage). See that item’s mechanics. This is a leak / workaround, not mastery.
