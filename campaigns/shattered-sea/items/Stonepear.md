# Stonepear

> [!narration] Narration
> The grey-green pear is hard as pumice and roughens the fingertips.
> Grit gives way to cold, sweet mineral flesh that tastes like deep water.


## Look
Pumice-hard grey-green pear; grit on the skin; sweet cold mineral flesh.

## What it does (table)
**Fruit (consumable).** Bonus Action to eat. Gain **Resistance to all damage** for **1 minute**.

Genuinely beneficial — no secret poison. Plucking living fruit from Aruhe draws notice — local life may converge on the taker (DM adjudicates; not a hive mind, not a timed island OS). Passage, watching, and spring water stay quiet.

## Charges / limits
- One fruit, one use.
- Common in the Quiet, often placed before Terror-Bird territory.

## Story hooks
Animals eat a stonepear before fighting. The tell gives the party a choice: take the obvious defensive fruit, or trust speed and silence instead.

## Provenance
Quiet fruit placed before Terror-Bird territory. Silicate grit, mineral salts, and dense cooling flesh are the plant's defensive chemistry pushed past nature by the Blight.

## Identification
DC 13 Nature/Medicine (1 min): broad protective function. DC 15 or *Identify*: Resistance to all damage for 1 minute.

## Design notes (DM)
- **Chassis:** Potion of Invulnerability, reskinned as fruit ([D&D Beyond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/5358-potion-of-invulnerability)); its one-minute all-damage Resistance remains the benchmark. The 2014 Action → 2024 Bonus Action change is intentional for all potions ([2024 Basic Rules — Potions](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/magic-items#Potions)).
- **Trade:** Rare defensive spike traded against one use, a short duration, no attunement, and visible placement in dangerous territory.
- **Knob:** If Resistance trivializes the Terror-Bird set piece, make the fruit uncommon or reduce the available count, not the advertised benefit.
