# Giant's Guava

> [!narration] Narration
> A skull-sized guava hangs pink-gold and heavy enough to need both hands. Soft flesh gives under the thumb. The smell is fruit mixed with something glandular and animal.



## Look
Skull-sized guava; pink-gold skin; glandular animal-under-fruit smell.

## What it does (table)
**Fruit (consumable).** Bonus Action to eat. Your **Strength score becomes 25** for **1 hour**.

Genuinely beneficial — no secret poison. Plucking living fruit from Aruhe draws notice — local life may converge on the taker (DM adjudicates; not a hive mind, not a timed island OS). Passage, watching, and spring water stay quiet.

## Charges / limits
- One fruit, one use.
- Common in the Old Gardens and the Quiet, often near Bear-Elk crossing evidence.

## Story hooks
A choice before a haul, climb, grapple, or Bear-Elk crossing. The fruit promises the body needed for the next obstacle, not a safe route through it.

## Provenance
Common Gardens/Quiet fruit, placed near signs of Bear-Elk passage. Glandular compounds and dense sugars are the natural chassis, enlarged by the Blight.

## Identification
DC 13 Nature/Medicine (1 min): broad physical-enhancement function. DC 15 or *Identify*: exact Strength effect.

## Design notes (DM)
- **Chassis:** Fire Giant Strength belt effect compressed into a one-hour consumable; a permanent belt is a higher-rarity item, so this fruit pays for the effect with disposability and the Taking Rule ([2024 Basic Rules — Magic Items](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/magic-items)). Drinking is intentionally a 2024 Bonus Action rather than the 2014 Action.
- **Trade:** Rare for a one-hour Strength 25 spike because it is one use, no attunement, and common only in dangerous living gardens.
- **Knob:** If the party uses it to erase too many obstacles, make it uncommon rather than common in the Old Gardens/Quiet; do not raise the permanent-belt comparison.
