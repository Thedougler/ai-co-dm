# Sending Stone (Nona's)

> [!narration] Narration
> A smooth grey river stone is warm to the touch and heavier than it looks. One face has been polished nearly flat by a thumb. Somewhere across the water, its twin rests with [[Nona Black-Jaw]].

## Look
Matched smooth stone; warm, thumb-polished face; twin held by [[Nona Black-Jaw]].

## What it does (table)
*Magic item, Common; no attunement.* This stone is paired with [[Nona Black-Jaw]]'s twin. While touching it, the bearer can use a **Bonus Action** to cast *sending* to the bearer of the other stone. If no creature bears the twin, the user knows that the attempt cannot go through.

The message is private to the twin's bearer. It is a connection, not a general communication channel.

## Charges / limits
- Once *sending* is cast through either stone, the pair cannot be used again until the next dawn.
- The stone has no independent target beyond its paired twin.

## Story hooks
[[Perrin Black-Jaw]] holds the stone; [[Nona Black-Jaw]] holds the twin. The stone is how she collects the favor debt that bought Perrin's help, but the terms remain unheard.

## Provenance
Legacy source: `/Users/nick/shattered-sea/wiki/shattered-sea/calveno-raid/item.sending-stone-nona.md`.
[[Nona Black-Jaw]] gave it to [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] in exchange for a favor.

## Identification
A caster familiar with paired communication items recognizes a standard sending-stone pair. The matching stone's bearer can be identified when a message is attempted.

## Design notes (DM)
- Public reference: [Sending Stones](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9180147-sending-stones). The 2024 listing confirms the paired bearer and recharge at next dawn.
- The public listing uses an Action; this campaign note uses the requested Bonus Action timing.
- Campaign rarity is Common here, despite the public listing's usual Uncommon label.
