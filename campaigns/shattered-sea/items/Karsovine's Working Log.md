# Karsovine's Working Log

> [!narration] Narration
> These are the honest, incomplete trial notes of [[Branimir Karsovine]], [[Voyvode]]'s chief engineer at [[Fathomrush]]. The pages are dense with pressure readings, revisions, and the cost of attempts that did not work.

## Look
Working engineering notebook; incomplete deep-pressure trial data; practical notes rather than polished claims.

## What it does (table)
No magic or mechanical effect. The log contains real engineering data from [[Voyvode]]'s trials—valuable leverage and a possible route to understanding the rig's failures.

## Charges / limits
- None; document only.
- Incomplete by design: the rig has not worked yet.

## Story hooks
[[Branimir Karsovine]] runs trials on his own crews and records the results honestly. [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] could use the data for her non-magical flying machine, but access requires earning Karsovine's trust.

## Identification
An engineer or experienced diver can distinguish measured trial notes from [[Sunkline]]'s sales copy. The incomplete data is part of its credibility.

## Design notes (DM)
- Document, Uncommon. Leverage and engineering data only.
- Do not turn incomplete notes into a finished design; the open problem is the hook.
