# Quiet verge soft landing

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: pending._
>

## Setup
The party reaches [[Aruhe Quiet Green Verge]] after the Gardens spine. The verge is a soft landing and a reading of silence, with a handoff to [[Aruhe Silence Threshold]] (Q1), not a Grove finish line.

## Forces
- [[Aruhe Quiet Green Verge]] — eaten-out life, quiet edges, and survivor-trail convergence.
- [[Aruhe Silence Threshold]] — possible handoff into [[The Quiet]].
- Optional [[Deer-Stalker]] or [[Strangler Fig]] — only if the party pinches the trail or needs pressure.
- [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] — cartography under pressure and silence reading spotlight.

## Objectives
Read the quiet, make a map decision, and secure a defensible rest or onward route. Reach Q1 with agency; avoid turning optional pressure into a mandatory fight.

## Twists / dials
- **Trigger:** The party follows the strongest inland signs or camps directly on a narrowed trail.
- **Fuse:** The silence breaks at the dim edge, or a survivor signal appears just beyond the safe verge.
- **Responses:** Observe and map from cover; withdraw to a broader verge for camp; or accept an optional [[Deer-Stalker]] / [[Strangler Fig]] pinch and disengage toward Q1.

## Treasure / fallout
A clearer survivor-trail state, a safe camp position, and a map toward [[The Quiet]]. A pinched trail costs time or gear but may reveal a torn cloth, ash, or urgency mark.
