# Taking-places Hunger temptation

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: pending._
>

## Setup
At [[Aruhe Taking-Places Plaza]], traces of dead claim, offered food, and survivor movement make the [[Aruhe Hunger Rule]] legible. Present the temptation, explain enough to support an informed choice, and track what the party does without forcing a claim.

## Forces
- [[Aruhe Hunger Rule]] — taking without receiving converges life.
- [[Aruhe Taking-Places Plaza]] — claim traces, offerings, and survivor signs.
- [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] and [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] — salvage, survey, and Hunger-rule spotlights.

## Objectives
Investigate, accept only what is received, leave safe nonliving material alone, and choose whether to continue toward the terraces or divert. The encounter succeeds when the table understands the cost and agency remains intact.

## Twists / dials
- **Trigger:** Someone reaches for fruit on the vine, a marked claim, or an offered item without clarifying receipt.
- **Fuse:** The object is handled, cut, or carried; the rule's convergence becomes visible rather than theoretical.
- **Responses:** Pause and return it; accept an explicitly received offering; or abandon the tempting route and record the claim as a future complication.

## Treasure / fallout
Received food can sustain the party without creating a claim. Loose stone, shed shell, and free-flowing water remain safe; an unreceived claim becomes Hunger fallout and a sharper survivor-trail signal.
