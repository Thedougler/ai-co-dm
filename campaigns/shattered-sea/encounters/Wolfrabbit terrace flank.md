# Wolfrabbit terrace flank

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: pending._
>

## Setup
During Gardens transit through [[Aruhe Snapped Canopy Gallery]], [[Aruhe Fruit Stair Terraces]], or [[Aruhe Irrigation Ditch Maze]], [[Wolfrabbit]] packs test the party's route. The pressure is a flank and separation problem, not a demand to clear the Gardens.

## Forces
- [[Wolfrabbit]] packs — dawn/dusk pressure, coordinated flanks, and pursuit limits.
- [[Old Gardens]] terraces, canopy, and ditches — broken sightlines and multiple exits.
- [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] and the traveling party — route and ambush survival spotlight.

## Objectives
Keep a viable transit line toward [[Aruhe Quiet Green Verge]], preserve the party's supplies, and disengage without turning the route into a prolonged fight.

## Twists / dials
- **Trigger:** A pack gets an angle across a stair, ditch, or exposed fruit terrace.
- **Fuse:** One member is isolated or the party doubles back into the same choke.
- **Responses:** Close ranks through canopy; use a ditch or terrace break to change elevation; or retreat to a known cover point and let the packs lose interest.

## Treasure / fallout
Dropped gear, disturbed fruit, and fresh prints can alter the survivor-trail read. A clean withdrawal preserves time for the Quiet verge or the [[Western Landing]] boat path.
