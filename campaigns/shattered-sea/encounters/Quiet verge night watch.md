# Quiet verge night watch

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: pending._
>

## Setup
At I, the party can take a quiet camp before the [[Aruhe Silence Threshold]]. The watch is primarily a silence and evidence read; danger appears only if the party pinches a trail or chooses an exposed edge.

## Forces
- [[Aruhe Quiet Green Verge]] and [[Aruhe Silence Threshold]] — quiet, eaten-out life, and the Q1 handoff.
- [[Deer-Stalker]] — optional dim-edge pressure.
- [[Strangler Fig]] — optional pinch if the camp blocks or follows a trail.
- [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] — cartography and silence-reading spotlight.

## Objectives
Finish watches, preserve a defensible camp, and decide whether to enter Q1, retrace the inland signs, or take the boat fallback. No encounter is required if the party keeps the verge broad and quiet.

## Twists / dials
- **Trigger:** A watch follows a narrow survivor mark into the dim edge, or someone disturbs the quiet boundary.
- **Fuse:** The camp's escape lane closes, or the distant human call / urgency mark is mistaken for a nearby threat.
- **Responses:** Keep watch from the broad verge; withdraw to Q1 without pursuing; or accept a brief [[Deer-Stalker]] / [[Strangler Fig]] pinch and disengage.

## Treasure / fallout
A full watch yields a stronger survivor-trail state and a safe Q1 transition. A pinch costs time or leaves a camp trace but need not become a fight.
