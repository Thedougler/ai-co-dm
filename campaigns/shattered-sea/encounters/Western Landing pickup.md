# Western Landing pickup

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: pending._
>

## Setup
If the party bails from the Gardens, move from K [[Aruhe Beach Stair]] through L1–L6 toward [[Uncertainty]]. The landing is a timing and route-choice pickup, with wreck shade and tide gaps creating the pressure.

## Forces
- [[Reef Skull]] — tide-meat threat around the landing.
- [[Small Blood Hawk]] — opportunistic air pressure at the strand and wreck shade.
- [[Uncertainty]] — mobile boat pickup; the valve and timing matter.
- [[Aruhe Tideline Moss Edge]], [[Aruhe Shingle Strand]], [[Aruhe Wreck Shade Hull]], [[Aruhe Reef Gap Approach]], [[Aruhe Low-Tide Teeth]], [[Aruhe Deep-Water Pickup]] — L1–L6 path.
- [[Delmar Fisk]] — orders under scare-and-drop and boat-valve spotlight.

## Objectives
Reach the pickup, keep the crew and boat access intact, and choose whether to follow, abandon, or sever the inland survivor trail. The [[Aruhe Cliff Cove Dead-End]] is a false exit, not a hidden route.

## Twists / dials
- **Trigger:** A signal from [[Uncertainty]] meets a tide gap, reef movement, or a blocked L-step.
- **Fuse:** The boat's valve or pickup window narrows while a [[Reef Skull]] or [[Small Blood Hawk]] claims the exposed route.
- **Responses:** Use wreck shade and wait for a gap; cross the low-tide teeth quickly; or call the boat off and return to the Gardens / Quiet route.

## Treasure / fallout
Boat escape, salvaged wreck material, and a changed ship position. Taking the boat can abandon the inland trail; delaying preserves clues but risks the pickup window.
