# Bloodhawk scare-and-drop

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: pending._
>

## Setup
A [[Bloodhawk]] owns the open thermal above [[Aruhe Drop Rim Court]] and is hunting a [[Great Crown Squid]] at the tree line. The party is collateral in the pressure and must choose ground, cover, or a boatward break.

## Forces
- [[Bloodhawk]] — open-thermal hunter; it will not stoop into closed canopy.
- [[Great Crown Squid]] — tree-line pressure; it stops at the grass edge.
- [[Aruhe Drop Rim Court]] — rim, canopy, and open thermal as the field.

## Objectives
Reach a closed canopy or open grass landing, keep the party together, and preserve a route toward [[Old Gardens]] or [[Western Landing]]. Winning does not require killing either creature.

## Twists / dials
- **Trigger:** A visible body, sudden movement, or exposed flight path draws the hawk's attention.
- **Fuse:** The hawk commits to a stoop while the squid presses the tree line; a second pass raises the drop risk.
- **Responses:** Break into closed canopy (the hawk will not stoop); run for open grass (the squid stops at the tree line); or use rim cover and redirect the pass toward the other predator.

## Treasure / fallout
A safe landing, a changed map position, and evidence of the squid's range. A messy drop can cost gear or leave scuffs that strengthen the inland survivor trail.
