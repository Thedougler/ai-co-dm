# Gardens night watch

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: pending._
>

## Setup
A field Long Rest in the Gardens lee or among the fruit stairs creates a night-watch card. The party may camp, but the watch reads the local ecology and any overnight claim rather than forcing a combat.

## Forces
- [[Grinning Ape]] — stones first, then a drop from cover.
- [[Vine Lash]] — route and camp-edge pressure.
- [[Grubnade]] — blooms that punish careless shelter or movement.
- [[Aruhe Hunger Rule]] — convergence if anyone makes an overnight claim at Taking-places.

## Objectives
Complete watches, protect food and gear, and choose a camp position that keeps the next route open. Ask whether the party ate that day; warn that no food means 1 exhaustion next, distinct from the Hunger Rule.

## Twists / dials
- **Trigger:** A stone lands outside camp, a vine tests the lee, or a bloom opens beside a sleeping position.
- **Fuse:** A watcher leaves the protected line, or a claim made at C is carried into camp.
- **Responses:** Hold a quiet perimeter; relocate along the fruit stairs; or break camp before dawn and use the disturbance to cover movement.

## Treasure / fallout
A secure watch preserves supplies and route time. Disturbed stones, broken fruit, and bloom traces can make the next transit safer or expose the camp's direction.
