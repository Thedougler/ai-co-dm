# Warren Mouths bait

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: pending._
>

## Setup
G [[Aruhe Warren Mouths]] and H [[Aruhe Old Mouth Threshold]] offer a short Blackrail foreshadow while the party crosses the Gardens. This is bait only: the party may look, mark the route, and leave without a fight.

## Forces
- [[Aruhe Warren Mouths]] — multiple mouths and misleading route signals.
- [[Aruhe Old Mouth Threshold]] — older threshold marks and Blackrail pressure.
- [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] — route-survival and Grung-instinct spotlight.

## Objectives
Notice the foreshadow, preserve a route back to E/F or I, and choose whether to spend time investigating. Do not require entry, pursuit, or combat.

## Twists / dials
- **Trigger:** A fresh scratch, cold draft, or bait item appears to invite a closer look.
- **Fuse:** The party commits beyond the mouth or loses the daylight route to the terraces.
- **Responses:** Mark and leave; send one observer while the group holds the retreat; or defer the threshold and continue toward I / the boat path.

## Treasure / fallout
A marked future route, a Blackrail clue, or a small survivor-trail signal. Spending time here costs Gardens transit time; leaving it alone preserves the Session 11 soft landing.
