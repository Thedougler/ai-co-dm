# Jean-Claude Tabarnack

> [!narration] Narration
> Jean-Claude Tabarnack is a three-foot Grung in electric-blue skin, scarred and hard to mistake. His red beret and false moustache—his “Corto di Velo” disguise—are not only costume: kin can read the hat as a Twiceborn censure sign and walk past him.

## Player-facing summary
Three-foot electric-blue Grung Gloom Stalker Ranger, scarred, in a red beret with false moustache “Corto di Velo.”

## Bonds / ideals that matter
- Botukuri blue scout: Jean-Claude's former clan identity and route knowledge are bound to Sorn's farm heart.
- Pell, a gnome captive: a living reminder of the captive pipeline he wants to end.
- His sister [[Simone]] sold the household and reported him; she hunts him as a leak.
- A red beret lets kin read the Twiceborn/censure sign and walk past rather than claim him.
- Wants exploration, freedom, and a better way for the Grung. Bird phobia; Crissdalynn tests it daily.

## Spotlight wants
Exploration, ambushes, survival, and a freer Grung future. The [[Grung clans]], [[Grung clans#Clan note: Botukuri|Botukuri]], [[Karath]] pipeline, [[Sparhold]], and the 314+ taken people keep those ideals under pressure.
