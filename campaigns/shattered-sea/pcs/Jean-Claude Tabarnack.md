# Jean-Claude Tabarnack

> [!narration] Narration
> A three-foot electric-blue Grung, scarred and hard to mistake, in a red beret and false moustache he calls Corto di Velo. Kin can read the hat as a Twiceborn censure sign and walk past rather than claim him.


## Player-facing summary
Three-foot electric-blue Grung Gloom Stalker Ranger, scarred, wearing a red beret and the false moustache called Corto di Velo.

## Bonds / ideals that matter
- His sister [[Simone]] sold the household and reported him; she hunts him as a leak.
- Pell, his gnome friend and captive, was killed by slavers. Jean-Claude cannot repair that debt.
- Former Botukuri scout and former slaver: he once identified settlements, took people, and wrote ambush doctrine. The crew does not know.
- He wants exploration, freedom, and a better future for the Grung. Slavers and abuse end the conversation.

## Spotlight wants
Exploration, ambushes, survival, route knowledge, and the collision between freedom and the past he refuses to narrate. Simone's hunters and the captive pipeline keep those choices moving.

## Lightning rods
- [[Simone]], her hunters, and the [[Grung clans]]
- [[Pell]] and the dead captive pipeline
- [[Sorn]], [[Botukuri]], and the [[Uncertainty]]
- [[Truth Stone]], Crissdalynn's bird-shape, and the hidden toxin

## Gear that matters
- [[Truth Stone]] — a tool that has already forced truth into the open.
- Twenty hidden vials of Simone toxin aboard [[Uncertainty]] — a liability, not a trusted tool.
- Red beret — disguise and readable Twiceborn/censure sign.

## Session deltas
He cannot lie or mask intent; he is painfully honest with one exception of omission. He cannot comfortably tell the crew everything. Touch reads as mating too often, birds trigger fear, and Simone's toxin remains hidden cargo.
