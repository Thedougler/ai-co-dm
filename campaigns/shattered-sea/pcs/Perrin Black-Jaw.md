# Perrin Black-Jaw
## Player-facing summary
Rattkin fancy-rat with a black head, white body, long pink tail; College of Lore Bard with a Pact of the Blade Warlock dip. Reads people, exits, and edges before he commits.
## Bonds / ideals that matter
Grandfather Vincenzo built and captained family brig [[Vestra]]. Something massive rose and took it; Perrin carries guilt for the crew he believes died. [[Nona Black-Jaw]] tasked him with surveying [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]] as a [[Tangle]] anchor.
## Spotlight wants
People-reading, leadership, social leverage, and discovering what happened to Vestra.
## Lightning rods
- [[Nona Black-Jaw]] and [[Rattkin Runs and Black-Jaw Run]]
- [[Auralis]] and [[Pearl of Souls]]
- [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]]
## Gear that matters
Manta cloak lost in the wreck; Perrin credits it for the pact without knowing the true source.
## Session deltas
Mortis: disadvantage on Strength; bonus to Charisma. Warlock pact forged in the wreck with [[Auralis]].
