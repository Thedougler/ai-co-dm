# Hazard — Snakewood

This hazard describes an active [[Snakewood]] colony section attacking from the canopy. It is the environmental ambush layer for the existing adult and young Snakewood creature notes, not a replacement for either statblock.

## Trigger

A **Small or larger creature moves beneath an active colony**. Snakewood reacts instinctively to movement and attacks from above.

## Notice

- **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 14`** or **Wisdom (Survival) — `DC 14`** → Spot stripped carcasses hanging in the canopy, unnatural coils among the branches, and vines slowly repositioning overhead.

## Ambush

The target makes a **Dexterity save — `DC 14`**.

- **Success** → The vine lashes past and recoils into the canopy.
- **Failure** → The creature takes `1d6` bludgeoning damage, is **Grappled**, and is pulled up to `15 feet` toward the canopy.

## Consumption

At the start of each **Grappled** creature's turn, feeding roots spread across exposed flesh. The creature takes `2d6` acid damage.

Each consecutive round it remains **Grappled** increases this damage by `1d6`, to a maximum of `4d6`.

## Escape

As an Action, make a **Strength (Athletics) — `DC 14`** or **Dexterity (Acrobatics) — `DC 14`** check to tear or slip free.

## Sever the vine

The grasping section has `AC 13` and `15 HP`, and is immune to psychic damage. Destroying it immediately releases the creature.

If suspended above the ground, the released creature falls normally.

## Movement instinct

Snakewood attacks **movement, not creatures specifically**. A thrown object, swinging pack, moving animal, *mage hand*, or similar distraction can trigger the ambush instead.

## Colony

A Snakewood colony may stretch hundreds of meters through connected treetops, but every section remains anchored to living trees. Destroying one attacking vine does not destroy the colony.

## Counterplay

- Move beneath it while another object is kept moving elsewhere.
- Bait the strike before passing.
- Cut a captured creature free.
- Stay beneath solid overhead cover.
- Avoid the connected canopy entirely.

## Placement

- Adult colonies dominate sections of [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest|the Quiet]] and [[Aruhe - The Marshes|the Marshes]], especially above game trails and waterways.
- Younger Snakewood appears in [[Aruhe - Old Gardens|the Old Gardens]].
- Dense colonies also hunt directly above [[Aruhe - The Mangroves|Mangrove channels]].

## Open rules check

The supplied hazard gives the severable grasping section `AC 13` and `15 HP`. The adult [[Snakewood]] creature note currently gives its grasping bundle `AC 15` and `15 HP`. This hazard preserves the supplied value as a provisional colony-hazard rule; confirm with Co-DM/Nick whether the two values represent different modes or should be reconciled before use together.
