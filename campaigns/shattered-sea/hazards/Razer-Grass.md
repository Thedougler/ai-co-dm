# Razer-Grass

> [!narration] Narration
> Waist-high Razer-Grass stands in discrete colonies, stalks armored in opaline glass with hard cut-ready edges. Fixed sparkles catch along the blades. A glassy tinkle carries in the wind.


## At the table
Grass armored in opaline glass. Contact cuts. Breaking is worse. Fire is the wrong answer.

## Trigger
Contact with intact stands. Shatter conditions below.

## Effect
**Razor Field.** Difficult terrain. **1d4** slashing per **5 feet** moved through intact Razer-grass (no save).

**Careful Passage.** Action: until start of next turn, move up to **5 feet** through it without movement damage (no roll).

**Shatter.** A 5-foot section shatters if within it a creature falls Prone, Dashes, is forcibly moved ≥10 feet, or the section takes thunder / significant bludgeoning / deliberate smash. Creatures in or within 5 feet: DC 14 Dexterity save, **7 (2d6)** slashing on failure, half on success.

**Glass Bloom.** For 1 round after Shatter, glittering haze in a **10-foot radius**. Breathing creature: DC 14 Constitution save or become **Glass-Choked** until a Short or Long Rest (speed −10 ft.; disadvantage on Con saves from environmental hazards).

**Fire.** Burning plant tissue leaves glass shells that snap — burning a section **automatically** triggers Shatter + Glass Bloom there.

## Detection
From >30 ft looks like frosted grass. Nearby DC 14 Perception/Survival: no trails through it, fixed sparkles, geometric fractures, old blood on lower blades, glassy tinkle in wind. Close examine of one blade: **automatic** recognition.

## Counterplay
Go around; Careful Passage; soak with water (Glass Bloom suppressed when thoroughly wet; Shatter still hurts); never fireball it; use as barrier vs pursuers that won't enter.

## Placement
Discrete waist-high colonies in Grasslands valleys (crossings, wallows, flood clearings) — not a blanket. Fauna skirts it; Wolfrabbits leap narrow bands.

## Provenance
Wiki `hazard.razer-grass` (provisional). Tuned vs spike growth / razorvine patterns at Aruhe DC 14 cluster.
