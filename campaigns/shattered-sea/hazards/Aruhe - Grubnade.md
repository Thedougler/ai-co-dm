> A fat cream-green caterpillar longer than a thumb rests on a flower. It chews steadily. Soft segments bunch and ease as it feeds.

### Hazard — Grubnade

- **Trigger:** A creature touches or picks up a mature [[Aruhe - Grubnade|Grubnade]], damages it, deliberately shakes or crushes the plant supporting it, or subjects its space to significant fire, thunder, or bludgeoning damage. Normal footsteps nearby do not trigger it.
- **Notice:** **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 12`** to spot a mature Grubnade in dense foliage. Learned predators step around the flower or abort a pounce before reaching it.
- **Nature:** **Intelligence (Nature) — `DC 13`** → Recognize the swelling grub as a volatile living trap. **Intelligence (Nature) — `DC 15`** → Understand that nearby brood chambers and hot blooms may hold several mature Grubnades.
- **Thermal Runaway:** When disturbed, the Grubnade visibly swells and hisses. It detonates at the end of the current turn.
- **Chemical Burst:** Each creature within `5 feet` of the Grubnade makes a **Dexterity save — `DC 12`**. On a failure, take `2d6` fire damage; on a success, take half damage. Unattended flammable objects in the area ignite.
- **Blast-Birth:** Immediately after the detonation, `1d4 + 1` juvenile Grubnades are hurled `10-30 feet` in different directions and land harmlessly in nearby vegetation. Juveniles are Tiny, translucent, and roughly finger-joint sized. They cannot detonate. After seven days of feeding, each becomes mature.
- **Hot Bloom:** Three or more mature Grubnades on one flower can chain disturbance through the bloom. Predators walk around any bloom that ticks.
- **Counterplay:** Leave it alone, go around the flower, move the plant without shaking it, use reach or cover before disturbing it, or trigger it from outside the `5-foot` burst.

## At the table

Make it cute first. The delayed detonation is the encounter: once a Grubnade is disturbed, the party has until the end of the current turn to drop it, throw it, dive back, shield someone, or make a mess on purpose.

## Terrain / friends

Old Gardens primary; Quiet leaves with neat bites and five-foot scorch rings. Learned predators refuse it: [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbits]], [[Aruhe - Deerstalker|Deer-Stalkers]], [[Aruhe - River Otter|Otters]], and [[Aruhe - Terror-Bird|Terror-Birds]].

## Canon ecology - living stock ingest

- Thumb-long cream-green caterpillars work flower throats. Touch, shake the plant, or burn it and two dark lines race together under the skin; the grub swells, hisses like fat in a pan, and pops.
- The flash is small and hot. What crawls away afterward are its daughters, blown ten to thirty feet into the leaves; there is no moth, because the explosion is how this species breeds.
- Feeding marks remain on leaves because nothing eats the eater. [[Aruhe - River Otter|Otters]], [[Aruhe - Deerstalker|deer-stalkers]], and [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit|wolfrabbits]] give them room.

## Aruhe flora ecology

- A **hot bloom** is a terrace flower holding several mature Grubnades. Predators walk around any bloom that ticks; leave it rather than disturb the caterpillars or their flower.
- The flower is the habitat and the warning sign, while the Grubnade remains the living trap and reproduction system described above.
