> [!narration] Narration
> A pale flowering plant leans out of the green, its swollen pollen sacs tight as blisters. Old claw marks, weapon cuts, and scorch rings circle the stand, while the plant itself remains untouched.

### Hazard — Spiritpollen

- **Trigger:** The plant releases a pale pollen cloud in a `15-foot` radius when touched, struck, cut, shaken, burned, or otherwise disturbed.
- **Notice:** **Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Nature) — `DC 15`** to notice the swollen pollen sacs and the old claw marks, weapon cuts, and scorch marks around otherwise untouched plants.
- **Exposure:** Each breathing creature in the cloud makes a **Wisdom save — `DC 15`**. On a failure, it becomes **Spirit-Haunted**. Creatures that do not breathe are immune.
- **Spirit-Haunted:** A **Spirit-Haunted** creature hallucinates swarms of spectral insects and grasping spirits overwhelming its companions. On its turn, it must spend its Action using an attack, spell, or ability it can use and believes will destroy the greatest number of hallucinated threats. It treats affected companions as valid targets when necessary.
- **Repeat save:** At the end of each of its turns, a **Spirit-Haunted** creature repeats the **Wisdom save — `DC 15`**, ending the effect on a success.
- **Recovery:** A creature that succeeds on any Spiritpollen Wisdom save is immune to **Spiritpollen** for `1 hour`. Avoiding exposure by holding breath, covering the mouth and nose, staying out of the cloud, or not breathing does not grant this immunity.
- **Counterplay:** Hold breath or cover the mouth and nose before disturbing the plant, disturb it from beyond `15 feet`, use strong wind to disperse the pollen, destroy it from outside the cloud, or let the cloud settle before entering the area.
- **Placement:** Spiritpollen belongs in old Aruhe growth where clawed animals and previous travelers have learned to leave some pale plants untouched. It is strongest as a crossing or touch-choice hazard, not as hidden unavoidable punishment.
