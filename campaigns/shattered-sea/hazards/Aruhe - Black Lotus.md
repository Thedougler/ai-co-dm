> [!narration] Narration
> A velvet-black flower spreads wider than a crouched person, its petals heavy enough to hide the ground beneath them. Faint violet veins pulse through the dark bloom when magic moves nearby. Old bones lie under the petal edge, and smaller scorched flowers lean the same way as the large one.

### Hazard — Black Lotus

**Appearance:** An oversized flower of velvet-black petals, broad enough to hide a crouched person. Faint violet veins pulse across the petals whenever magic is used nearby.

**Behavior:** Black Lotuses feed on ambient magic as ordinary plants feed on sunlight. Strong magic draws their attention immediately.

**Trigger:** A creature enters within `10 feet`, touches the flower, or casts a spell within `30 feet`.

**Notice:** **Wisdom (Perception) or Intelligence (Nature) — `DC 15`** → Notice old bones beneath the petals, scorched vegetation around smaller blooms, and the flower turning subtly toward magical effects.

**Ambush:** The lotus lashes shut around one creature within `10 feet`.

**Dexterity save — `DC 15`**

- Failure → The creature is **Restrained** inside the flower.
- Success → The creature avoids being trapped.

**Magic Drain:** At the start of each of the trapped creature's turns, it loses one unexpended spell slot of the highest level it currently has available. The lotus visibly brightens as the magic disappears into its veins.

- A creature with no remaining spell slots instead takes `4d6` necrotic damage.
- A creature without spellcasting immediately suffers the necrotic damage.

**Life Drain:** Once the victim has no spell slots remaining, the damage increases to `6d6` necrotic at the start of each of its turns. The lotus regains hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt.

**Escape:** **Strength (Athletics) — `DC 16`** as an Action → Force the petals apart and escape.

A creature outside the flower can also deal `20` slashing or fire damage to the enclosing petals to tear them open.

**Magic Reaction:** Whenever a spell of 1st level or higher is cast within `30 feet`, the lotus has **Advantage** on its next Ambush attack or escape contest before the end of its next turn.

### Lesser Black Lotus — Spellburst Bloom

**Trigger:** A creature steps directly on or violently disturbs the flower.

**Notice:** **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 14`** → The petals are unnaturally warm and tiny sparks crawl through their black veins.

**Spellburst:** The flower releases the magic stored inside it, casting *Fireball* centered on itself.

**Dexterity save — `DC 15`**

- Failure → `8d6` fire damage.
- Success → Half damage.

The flower is destroyed by the blast.

**Counterplay:** Step around it, probe the ground ahead, trigger it from range, or carefully harvest it before disturbing the petals.

### Loot — Black Lotus Heart

A mature Black Lotus contains a dense knot of crystallized magical sap beneath its petals.

**Harvest:** **Intelligence (Arcana) or Wisdom (Survival) — `DC 16`**, requiring `10 minutes`.

- Success → Recover one **Black Lotus Heart**.
- Failure → The stored magic discharges and the harvester takes `4d6` force damage. The Heart is destroyed.

A living, active Black Lotus must first be killed, restrained, or otherwise rendered unable to close around the harvester.

**Black Lotus Heart — Consumable, Very Rare**

As a **Bonus Action**, consume the refined Heart to regain one expended spell slot of the highest level you are capable of casting.

If your highest-level spell slot is not currently expended, the Heart restores the highest-level expended slot instead.

A creature without spell slots gains no benefit.

**Lesser Harvest:** A successfully harvested Spellburst Bloom yields unstable magical material worth one-quarter of a Black Lotus Heart. Four such harvests can be refined into one Heart with **Intelligence (Arcana) — `DC 15`** during a Long Rest.
