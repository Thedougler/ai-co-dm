# Stillbloom

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Aruhe flora pending._

## At the table
A dull four-foot waxy stalk stands where green first goes black at the leaf edge. Pale hollow bracts end in dark needles; punctured bodies and exaggeratedly slow scavengers mark the shallow root mat.

## Ecology
- Stillbloom reads vibration, not malice. Rain, a falling leaf, a mouse, and a careful footstep mean nothing.
- A Small or larger creature that bolts, jumps, or falls triggers the needles. After firing, the air sacs visibly swell back over a short interval.
- Once in its life, after years underground, it raises one enormous cream-white flower with a faint green center, then dies.
- The plant does not eat what it drops. The soil does. Scavengers walk the mat slowly and feed on the aftermath.

## Trigger
A charged mature stalk fires when a **Small or larger grounded** creature within **20 feet**:
- moves more than **10 feet** on its turn,
- Dashes, jumps, or falls,
- is forcibly moved 10+ feet,
- or makes another violent motion (DM call).

**Safe:** ≤10 feet/turn; flyers that never touch the root mat.

## Effect
**Volley.** Each creature within **15 feet** of the plant makes a **DC 14 Dexterity** saving throw. Failure: **7 (2d6)** piercing damage, one spine embeds, and make the venom save below. Success: no damage.

**Reload.** Cannot fire again for **1 minute**. Pressure sacs visibly swell — solvable window.

**Cover.** Half/three-quarters cover help the Dex save; total cover blocks the volley.

### Venom — Exposure
Each embedded hit raises **Exposure** by 1. Constitution save DC = **11 + Exposure** (first hit DC 12, then 13…).

- **Success:** local numbness only (struck arm: no that-hand/somatic/two-handed use; struck leg: −10 ft speed, no Dash; both legs: speed 5 ft). Lasts 10 minutes after last exposure.
- **Failure:** advance one step on the Paralytic Spread track:

| Spread | Effect |
|---|---|
| 0 Local | Limb only |
| 1 Systemic | Poisoned |
| 2 Motor Failure | Poisoned and Restrained |
| 3 Paralysis | Paralyzed |

**Recovery.** End of turn at Spread 2–3: repeat Con save; success reduces Spread by 1. After 1 minute without another hit, Spread drops 1 more. After 10 minutes, Exposure 0 and local numbness ends. Poison-clearing effects clear the venom.

Poison immunity blocks venom, not piercing.

## Detection
- **DC 14 Perception:** launch-spines, punctured carcasses, odd scavenger paths.
- **DC 14 Nature/Survival:** root vibration; slow movement is safe. Watching scavengers for 1 minute teaches this automatically.

## Counterplay
Move ≤10 ft/turn; provoke reload with a thrown object / *mage hand*; fly over; spread out; neutralize poison; weaponize the patch against pursuers. **Do not** sprint-rescue through a charged field.

## Placement
Quiet-to-Rot transition trails and sunbreaks, not the open valleys.
