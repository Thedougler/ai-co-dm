# session-10

## SUMMARY
The session advanced the voyage toward Aruhe and focused on three major events: a late-night warning, a naval ambush, and the first Aruhe landing. Shepherd Gregori appeared, warned that Talon Vantyrus had sent a ship to rob the party for the Fate Spinner/dreidel, and revealed Delmar’s Umberlee curse. The party disabled the pursuing ship with cannon fire and fire, then escaped without boarding. At Aruhe, two survivors were recovered and told the party that the island was extremely lethal, that taking anything from it caused deadly responses, and that other survivors might remain inland. A landing party entered the terraces, identified multiple lethal hazards, and fled after attracting a CR17 Crown Squid. The Aruhe survivor rescue remains unresolved, and the session ended after the retreat.

## KEY DISCUSSION POINTS
- **Night voyage and crew preparation** – Delmar used the time to train Jeffrey in finesse, sparring, and confidence, including encouraging him to practice openly. This supported crew readiness but had no immediate strategic effect.
- **Shepherd Gregori’s warning** – Gregori appeared below deck, thanked the party for the Calveno warning, and said a ship sent by Talon Vantyrus was one to two hours behind and intended to rob the party for the carried artifact. This directly led to the decision to prepare an ambush.
- **Fate Spinner and Sentinel Schism lore** – Gregori connected the party’s dreidel/Fate Spinner to the Sentinels’ schism over manipulating fate. He also requested help: injure, but do not kill or mentally break, Corvin Knighton, with a reward offered; no firm party commitment was finalized.
- **Naval engagement** – The party slowed the attacking ship with gust, delivered 55 bludgeoning damage via broadside, and ignited the sails. The enemy vessel was left listing and unable to pursue; the party chose to flee rather than board or finish the crew.
- **Aruhe survivor debrief** – Two rescued survivors reported originally about 100–150 people shipwrecked there from the raid, with many dead. They described the island as cursed: taking fruit or island resources caused deadly responses, ocean fishing appeared safer, and Grung seemed specifically targeted.
- **Terraces hazard exploration** – The landing party found wolf rabbits, black lotus, spore plants, hazardous caterpillars, fruit traps with blood/skulls, and tracks leading toward the terraces. At least six deaths were identified near fruit sites.
- **Crown Squid contact** – After Jean-Claude killed a spore plant, an invisible Crown Squid attacked from below with 80-ft tentacle reach. It hit Delmar for 18 slashing damage, reduced to 9 by Uncanny Dodge, and attempted DC 19 Strength saves for grapples. The party used Silent Image and flight to escape; the creature stopped at the tree line.
- **Risk evaluation and session close** – The DM indicated the Crown Squid was CR17 and a possible TPK-level threat. The party chose escape over combat and ended the session after returning over the terraces.

## DECISIONS MADE
- Use a stealthy broadside ambush against the pursuing ship rather than waiting to be boarded.
- Disable and flee from the attacking ship rather than boarding or eliminating its crew.
- Send a landing party to search for survivors on Aruhe despite the island’s known danger.
- Keep the ship mobile instead of anchoring close to shore; maintain lookouts for skull crabs and other threats.
- Escape the Crown Squid using illusion, disengage, and flight rather than fighting it.
- End the session early due to player fatigue.
