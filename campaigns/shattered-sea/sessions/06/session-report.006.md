# Session 06 Report

## Summary

Festival morning: [[npc.master-kyzil|Kyzil]] refused [[location.le-paludi|Le Paludi]] pranks; [[npc.thunk|Thunk]] signed 1,000 gp of cannon on ship credit; [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] threw a tomato back. The Primary Chamber resumed. [[npc.vashu-the-weeping-veil|Vashu]] and [[npc.ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Ozzeth]] died covering [[npc.solange-barret|Solange]]. She spoke "Agni," dropped the ceiling, and stood up remade at twelve feet.

> [!narration] Player-facing — read or adapt at the table.
> [[npc.master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] led a rooftop chase across [[location.le-paludi|Le Paludi]] during [[culture.il-gioco-delle-beffe|Il Gioco delle Beffe]]. She countered a water balloon, ducked a flower-and-balloon volley, saw through a boy's fake-drowning trap, and refused to play along.
>
> [[npc.thunk|Thunk]], blackout drunk, ordered 1,000 gp of new cannons on ship credit. He signed twice — once upright, once upside down — before vomiting on the order. His old captain's cabin is now a crew break room.
>
> [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] appeared at the [[location.le-paludi|Lothaludi]] fish market with [[npc.ruk|Ruk]] and [[npc.enzo|Enzo]]. [[npc.ruk|Ruk]] pinned one prankster. [[npc.enzo|Enzo]] blocked a chalk-powder attack. When a tomato came flying, [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] caught it and threw it back.
>
> The sewer fight resumed with [[npc.solange-barret|Solange]] in the Main Chamber and [[npc.vashu-the-weeping-veil|Vashu]] and [[npc.ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Ozzeth]] guarding her. [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] cast [[spell.fire-bolt|Fire Bolt]] and a boosted [[spell.shocking-grasp|Shocking Grasp]]; Ragnetto fell. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] one-shot a [[creature.purple-caste-enforcer|Purple-Caste Enforcer]] with a pistol. [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] took a strike, called to his patron, heard "Not yet," and steadied while coughing seawater. [[npc.solange-barret|Solange]] said, "I will not have my princesses stopped." [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] took two Still-Water Strikes, dodged Tongue Lash twice, and closed: "Dead. Dead. I cracked the throat."
>
> [[npc.ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Ozzeth]] tried [[spell.dominate-person|Dominate Person]] on [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]]; the save held with [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s Bardic Inspiration. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] shot [[npc.ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Ozzeth]]'s arm off. [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] finished him. [[npc.ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Ozzeth]]'s last acts were for [[npc.solange-barret|Solange]]: [[spell.misty-step|Misty Step]], [[spell.greater-invisibility|Greater Invisibility]], and "do it now."
>
> Grappled and poisoned by [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]], [[npc.solange-barret|Solange]] teleported to [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] anyway, spoke "Agni," and detonated the chamber ceiling. [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]]'s grab came up short. Where [[npc.solange-barret|Solange]] had knelt, something else stood up: twelve feet tall, her body already remade.

## Key Events

1. **Festival bits** — [[npc.master-kyzil|Kyzil]] will not play. [[npc.thunk|Thunk]]'s cannon order. [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] at the market.
2. **[[npc.vashu-the-weeping-veil|Vashu]] down** — [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] at 1 HP, throat cracked.
3. **[[npc.ozzeth-the-twiceborn|Ozzeth]] down** — Failed dominate; arm off; last wards on [[npc.solange-barret|Solange]].
4. **Detonation** — "Agni." Ceiling opens. [[npc.solange-barret|Solange]] stands remade. Session ends mid-transformation.
