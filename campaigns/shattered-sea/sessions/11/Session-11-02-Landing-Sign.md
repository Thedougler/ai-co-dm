# Session 11 — Landing sign and smoke

## Scene ends when

The party commits to following the north prints and smoke off this bank, handing to [[Session-11-03-Wolfrabbits]].

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Skip the crate search and the fruit sorting. Show the north prints and the smoke, ask for the commit, and end on the upriver commit.

**If ahead:** Lingering to pick living fruit on this bank is a [[Taking on Aruhe]] claim, and the later hazards-table roll uses `2d8`, keeping the lower result. The trail and smoke remain.

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** The sign is fresh. Waiting lets the smoke thin and the trail go cold.
- **Goal / exit:** Commit north to the upriver prints for [[Session-11-03-Wolfrabbits]].
- **Danger:** Plucking living plants turns surrounding life.
- **Silence:** Present this bank, then wait. If the party freezes here, the smoke thins once at the end of the round.
- **Situation magnets:** Anyone lingering on this bank. Anyone plucking living stems.

## Now

The party is on [[Aruhe - River Landing Bank]], in eight-foot gold-green grass and moving water. The river is immediately east and wraps south around this bank. The prints are underfoot and run 60 feet north up this bank toward the smoke. The fire scrape, closed crate, and fallen orange fruit sit 15 feet west of the water, on the river side of those prints. Mossy terrace stone stands 20 feet west of the prints. Crash-landed party members start **Prone** in those terrace woods. A 30-foot move stays on this bank. A Dash of 60 feet follows the prints north off this bank. The smoke is a thin column on the northern horizon in this same valley. It is not reachable this slice.

### Action cards

**This bank.** Eight-foot gold-green grass is Difficult Terrain and heavily obscures beyond 10 feet. Movement leaves a crushed corridor.

Raid survivors already used this bank and walked north upriver toward the smoke. The smoke is that group, or the next camp they lit, still in this valley. How many people are left stays unknown. Whether the fruit already here was found loose or picked as a claim stays unknown. The closed crate's contents stay unknown.

**[[Taking on Aruhe]] claim.** Fruit skins and whole fruit already on the ground do not turn the island. Plucking living plants, fishing, trapping, or killing to carry flesh does. On a claim, do not roll the hazards table now. The later [[Session-11-00-Random-Tables]] roll is `2d8`, keeping the lower result. The trail and smoke remain.

**Fallen [[Aruhe - Redheart Berry]].** Living red berries still hang on short thick stems at the wet margin. Eating a fallen berry is a Bonus Action and regains `8d4 + 8` hit points. Orange whole fruit at the crate is already down and has no listed heal.

**Identify.** **Intelligence (Nature) — identify**
- `DC 10` → The red berries are edible, not poison.
- `DC 15` or *Identify* → Eating a fallen [[Aruhe - Redheart Berry]] is a Bonus Action and regains `8d4 + 8` hit points.

> You see a crushed corridor of gold-green stems around you, and you feel mud sucking at a double line of footprints that still hold water. Those prints run sixty feet north up this bank and keep going. East, clear river water pulls against this bank at once. You see pale rock under the current, and the flow folds the underwater grass against your calves.
>
> Fifteen feet toward that water, on the river side of the track, a scraped fire patch sits in a circle of stones with grey ash in the dirt. A shut wooden crate rests in the flattened grass with whole orange fruit, torn rinds, and two bowls still in the mud. You smell the burst skins where they have gone sharp and sweet. At the wet margin, glossy red fruit hangs on short thick stems, ridged bodies wet and tight under dark curled caps, drops standing on the skin.
>
> Twenty feet west of the prints, mossy garden walls stack out of the trees, wet steps holding split orange fruit and pink-gold globes. You hear the river working the stems. North, a thin column of smoke stands on the horizon in this same valley.
>
> What do you do?

## Procedure

**Clue.** This bank is a look-and-commit slice. There is no fight on this card. After everyone has acted, if anyone remains lingering on this bank, tick the threat clock once. Freeze still ticks once. Failures impose the Be ready for ruling only. Failures never advance the threat clock. When the party follows the north prints, the scene ends. Do not roll the hazards table here.
