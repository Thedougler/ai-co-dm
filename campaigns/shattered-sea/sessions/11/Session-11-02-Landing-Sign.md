# Session 11 — Landing sign and smoke

## Scene ends when

The party follows the fresh northbound prints toward the inland smoke, handing to [[Session-11-03-Wolfrabbits]].

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Skip the crate search and fruit sorting. Show the wet prints, show the smoke, ask for the northbound commit, and cut to the upriver handoff.

**If ahead:** Lingering to take living fruit on this bank counts as [[Taking on Aruhe]]. The later hazards-table roll uses `2d8`, keeping the lower result. The trail and smoke remain usable.

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** Fresh sign is cooling. Delay thins the smoke and softens the wet prints.
- **Goal / exit:** Commit north along the upriver prints for [[Session-11-03-Wolfrabbits]].
- **Danger:** Taking living plants makes nearby life hostile.
- **Silence:** Present the bank, ask what they do, and wait. If the party freezes here, the smoke thins once at the end of the round.
- **Situation magnets:** Anyone lingering on this bank. Anyone taking living stems.

## Now

The party stands on [[Aruhe - River Landing Bank]], in eight-foot gold-green grass with moving water on the east edge. The river curls around the south edge of this bank. Wet prints start underfoot and run 60 feet north toward the smoke. The fire scrape, closed crate, bowls, and fallen orange fruit sit 15 feet west of the water, on the river side of those prints. Mossy terrace stone stands 20 feet west of the prints. Crash-landed party members start **Prone** in those terrace woods. A 30-foot move stays on this bank. A 60-foot Dash follows the prints north off this bank. The smoke is a thin column on the northern horizon in this same valley. It is not reachable in this slice.

### Action cards

**This bank.** Eight-foot gold-green grass is Difficult Terrain and heavily obscures anything beyond 10 feet. Movement crushes a visible corridor through it.

The people who used this stop already walked north upriver toward the smoke. The smoke marks that group, or the next camp they lit, still somewhere in this valley. How many people are left stays unknown. Whether they received fallen fruit or took living fruit stays unknown. The closed crate's contents stay unknown.

**[[Taking on Aruhe]].** Fruit skins and whole fruit already on the ground are received, not taken. Plucking living plants, fishing, trapping, or killing to carry flesh makes nearby life hostile. Do not roll the hazards table here. On a take, the later [[Session-11-00-Random-Tables]] roll is `2d8`, keeping the lower result. The trail and smoke remain.

**Fallen [[Aruhe - Redheart Berry]].** Living red berries still hang from short thick stems at the wet margin. Eating a fallen berry is a Bonus Action and regains `8d4 + 8` hit points. Orange whole fruit at the crate is already down and has no listed heal.

**Identify.** **Intelligence (Nature) — identify**
- `DC 10` → The red berries are edible, not poison.
- `DC 15` or *Identify* → Eating a fallen [[Aruhe - Redheart Berry]] is a Bonus Action and regains `8d4 + 8` hit points.

> You are knee-deep in mud and eight-foot gold-green grass. Around you, wet footprints press into a double track of crushed stems, running sixty feet north along the bank toward a thin smoke column. East, clear river water pulls past pale stone and folds the submerged grass around your calves.
>
> Fifteen feet toward the water, on the river side of the track, grey ash sits inside a scraped ring of stones. Beside it, a closed wooden crate, two mud-streaked bowls, whole orange fruit, and torn rinds lie in flattened grass. The burst skins smell sharp and sweet. At the wet edge, glossy red berries hang heavy from short stems, round and deeply ridged, with water standing on the skin and leaves.
>
> West of the prints, mossy terrace walls climb out of the trees twenty feet away. Wet stone steps hold split orange fruit and pink-gold globes. The river keeps knocking at the grass. North, the smoke stays thin and upright in this same valley.
>
> What do you do?

## Procedure

**Clue.** This bank is a look-and-commit slice. There is no fight on this card. After everyone has acted, if anyone remains on this bank, tick the threat clock once. Freezing still ticks once. Failed checks impose only their Be ready for ruling. Failed checks never advance the threat clock. When the party follows the north prints, the scene ends. Do not roll the hazards table here.
