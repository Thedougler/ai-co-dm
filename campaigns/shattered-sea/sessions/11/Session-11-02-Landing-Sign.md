# Session 11 — Landing sign and smoke

## Scene ends when

The party commits to following the north prints and smoke off this bank, handing to [[Session-11-03-Wolfrabbits]], or the party breaks south for the ship, the beach, or the downstream terraces.

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Skip the crate search and the fruit sorting. Show the north prints and the smoke, ask for the commit, and end on the upriver commit or the south break.

**If ahead:** Lingering to pick living fruit on this bank is a [[Taking on Aruhe]] claim, and the later hazards-table roll uses `2d8`, keeping the lower result. The trail and smoke remain.

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** The sign is fresh. Waiting lets the smoke thin and the trail go cold.
- **Goal / exit:** Commit north to the upriver prints for [[Session-11-03-Wolfrabbits]], or break south for the ship.
- **Danger:** Plucking living plants turns surrounding life. Open valley air is hawk sky.
- **Silence:** Present this bank, then wait. If the party freezes here, the smoke thins once at the end of the round.
- **Situation magnets:** Anyone lingering on this bank. Anyone plucking living stems.

The party is on [[Aruhe - River Landing Bank]], in eight-foot gold-green grass and moving water. The river is immediately east and wraps south around this bank. The prints are underfoot and run 60 feet north up this bank toward the smoke. The fire scrape, closed crate, and fallen orange fruit sit 15 feet west of the water, on the river side of those prints. Mossy terrace stone stands 20 feet west of the prints. Crash-landed party members start **Prone** in those terrace woods. A 30-foot move stays on this bank. A Dash of 60 feet follows the prints north off this bank, or goes 40 feet south toward the sandy terrace return. The smoke is a thin column on the northern horizon in this same valley. It is not reachable this slice. South, the old terraces still run back toward the beach and [[Uncertainty]]. [[Talon Skarn]] is behind the party in the air. He leaves no tracks.

### Action cards

**This bank.** Eight-foot gold-green grass is Difficult Terrain and heavily obscures beyond 10 feet. Movement leaves a crushed corridor.

Raid survivors already used this bank and walked north upriver toward the smoke. The smoke is that group, or the next camp they lit, still in this valley. How many people are left stays unknown. Whether the fruit already here was found loose or picked as a claim stays unknown. The closed crate's contents stay unknown.

**[[Taking on Aruhe]] claim.** Fruit skins and whole fruit already on the ground do not turn the island. Plucking living plants, fishing, trapping, or killing to carry flesh does. On a claim, do not roll the hazards table now. The later [[Session-11-00-Random-Tables]] roll is `2d8`, keeping the lower result. The trail and smoke remain.

**Fallen [[Aruhe - Redheart Berry]].** Living red berries still hang on short thick stems at the wet margin. Eating a fallen berry is a Bonus Action and regains `8d4 + 8` hit points. Orange whole fruit at the crate is already down and has no listed heal.

**Identify.** **Intelligence (Nature) — `DC 10`** → The red berries are edible, not poison. **`DC 15`** or *Identify* → Eating a fallen [[Aruhe - Redheart Berry]] is a Bonus Action and regains `8d4 + 8` hit points.

Fifteen feet west of the water, on the river side of that track, a ring of stones holds grey ash in cleared dirt, and a closed wooden crate sits in the grass with orange whole fruit, split skins, and a couple of bowls still in the mud.
>
> East, clear river water pulls against this bank at once, pale stone showing under the current where submerged blades bend around your legs. At the wet margin, glossy red berries hang heavy on short thick stems, each round body pulled into deep ridges under a crown of curled leaves, water beading on the tight skin. You smell the burst skins where they have gone sharp and sweet. You hear water working the stems, and you feel the current at your legs and the mud sucking at the prints.
>
> Twenty feet west of the prints, mossy terrace walls and stacked garden stone rise out of the trees, with split orange fruit and pink-gold globes on the lower steps. Sugar-sour air sits on that wet stone. North, a thin column of smoke stands on the horizon in this same valley. South, forty feet toward the terrace return, a sandy lip and old garden stone run back toward the beach and [[Uncertainty]] on the water.
>
> What do you do?

## Procedure

**Clue.** This bank is a look-and-commit slice. There is no fight on this card. After everyone has acted, if anyone remains lingering on this bank, the smoke thins once. A second stall cools the trail. Failures impose the Be ready for ruling only. When the party follows the north prints or breaks south for the ship, the scene ends. If they race, still end on that commit or that break. Do not roll the hazards table here.
