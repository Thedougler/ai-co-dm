# Session 11 — Angry Birds

## Scene ends when

The crew drops into eight-foot grass and moving water, crash-lands in the terrace woods, or forces the beach. This is not a fight to the death with the adult [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk]] or the [[Aruhe - Crown Squid]].

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** Land in the grass, or take hits in this air.
- **Goal / exit:** A quiet drop into grass and moving water.
- **Danger:** An adult [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk]] is already coming down out of the sun. High, loud, or obvious costs hits.
- **Silence:** Present the dive, then wait. If the crew freezes, tick the clock once at the end of the round.
- **Situation magnets:** Anyone still in High air. Anyone who attacks, flares, or screams.

The crew is 60 feet up over the old terraces. The [[Aruhe - Crown Squid]] is 30 feet ahead at the same height, bracing through the last trees. An adult [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk]] is already dropping out of the sun onto that squid. Grass and river cover lie 60 feet straight down. A Dash of 60 feet from High air reaches that grass. A normal move of 30 feet does not. The beach and [[Uncertainty]] are 60 feet of open sky toward the ship. The adult Bloodhawk flies 120 feet.

### Action cards

**Adult [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk]].** AC 15. Loop: Canopy Dive → Talon Grab → Haul Aloft → Sickle Claw. Tell: the four wings fold and the crimson tips flash. Bloodied at **124 HP or fewer:** the adult Bloodhawk releases a grabbed crew member and climbs for the cloud line. The adult Bloodhawk will not stoop closed canopy or eight-foot grass. Easy dial: Multiattack only, no Canopy Dive recharge. Canopy Dive or Beak is +13 to hit. Beak deals 28 piercing. Talon Grab is +13 to hit, 23 slashing, **Grappled** (escape `DC 19`) and **Restrained**.

The [[Aruhe - Crown Squid]] is fleeing through the last trees. It is not a second boss unless someone stays glued to it.

> [!narration] Initial Narration
> You see sixty feet of open air under you, with stacked garden stone, mossy terrace walls, and split orange fruit far below. Thirty feet ahead, at your same height, a house-sized squid braces through the last trees. Its swollen mantle is clear as wet glass, veined like a leaf and lit pale violet from inside, while thick bark-dark arms take separate trunks and thinner hooked lines hang toward the forest floor like roots looking for ground.
>
> Out of the sun above that squid, an enormous charcoal hawk folds four wings into a narrow fall. Crimson flight feathers flash along the black undersides, a ragged red crest streams behind its head, and a pale hooked beak points straight at the clear mantle. Talons the size of a rowboat open beneath it. You feel the wind off those folding wings slam across your height hard enough to rattle the last leaves.
>
> Straight down, gold-green grass stands higher than a person along a clear river, seed heads leaning where the current presses through. You hear water pulling through the stems, and warm sugar-sour air rises off the fruiting terraces. The other way, sixty feet of empty sky runs toward the short shingle beach and [[Uncertainty]] on the water, hull and rigging sharp in the salt wind. The adult hawk's wings pin tight. The dive is still aimed at the squid.
>
> What do you do?

## Procedure

**Escape mode.** After everyone has acted, if anyone remains exposed (high, loud, or obvious), tick once. Freeze still ticks once. **Combat mode:** if the party commits to killing the adult [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk]], stop the clock and run the embedded Bloodhawk statblock.

**Bloodied 124 HP.** The adult [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk]] releases a grabbed crew member and climbs for the cloud line.

**Cover reached.** The adult [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk]] will not stoop closed canopy or eight-foot grass. The clock stops.

**Scene dials.** Easy: Multiattack only, no Canopy Dive recharge.

## Secondary objective

If the adult [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk]] is diverted for **two** threat beats, the [[Aruhe - Crown Squid]] reaches dense inland canopy and escapes. Later, a living Great Crown Squid still claims those crowns. Otherwise the adult Bloodhawk pins the Great Crown Squid at tick 3. Later, a pinned carcass lies in the last trees, and this animal gives no later help. Damaging the adult Bloodhawk counts as a diversion only if that damage actually pulls the adult Bloodhawk off the Great Crown Squid for that beat.

## Landing

The crew is in moving water and eight-foot grass, or crash-landed or scattered in Terrace woods. On this same bank the crew can already see tracks and, inland in this valley, a thin column of smoke. Beat 2 is [[Session-11-02-Landing-Sign]]. Do not run Survival checks or the hazards table here.

> [!narration] Landing
> You come down in moving water with gold-green grass standing over your head. The clear river pulls at the stems around you, and mud sucks at the bank where fresh prints have already cut through it. Inland, up the valley, a thin column of smoke rises above the grass.

**Scattered.** That crew member lands 30 feet from the others.

> [!narration] Scattered
> You come down thirty feet from the others with tall grass and moving water between you. The river fills the gap with noise, and only broken glimpses of bodies show through the stems.

**Crash-landed.** That crew member takes `2d6` bludgeoning and is **Prone** in Terrace woods.

> [!narration] Crash-landed
> You hit the terrace woods and go down hard among stacked garden stone, mangrove roots, and split orange fruit. Sugar-sour air presses close to the wet steps, and leaves keep shaking above you.
