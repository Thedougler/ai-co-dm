# Session 11 — Farthest camp

![[attachments/shattered-sea/places/aruhe-spoke-ring.png|Spoke Ring, radial fire hub under Quiet leaves]]

## Scene ends when

The split at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]] is on the table (ship versus garden) and the party has a live choice, then hand to [[Session-11-07-False-Help]], or the party breaks (forced march, attack, or leave without hearing the woman). This is the farthest inland Calveno camp. It is not night watch, not [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], and not [[Talon Skarn]] on the ground.

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Put Split-lip and the two stories in front of the party, then ask.

**If ahead:** Leave the argument unfinished. Hand to beat 7. [[Talon Skarn]] stays in the sun. The garden stays out of reach tonight.

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** Split-lip is here if they pulled him from the hole. This camp wants a garden. They do not all want [[Uncertainty]].
- **Goal / exit:** Get the ship-versus-garden split on the table, then hand to [[Session-11-07-False-Help]], or break.
- **Danger:** Pushing them to the ship tonight breaks this group. Playing along points the night north. Leaving before they hear the woman starves beat 7 of her lure.
- **Silence:** Present Split-lip and the two stories, then wait.
- **Situation magnets:** Split-lip while he sits in this light. Anyone who walks a north trail alone.

## Now

The party is on the packed dirt hub of [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]] at last sun. The smoke is this fire. Split-lip sits 5 feet from the coals if they pulled him onto the sand at the hole: split lip still wet, one boot missing, grey salt-stiff shirt still damp. If they left him in the hole, he is not here, and this fire is fewer than the tracks. Three or four other Calveno wreck people sit in this light. One looks at the north trails when the branches move. One argues that anyone who listens is mad. Fallen [[Aruhe - Stonepear]] sits by the coals. Living stonepears still hang. The south trail runs 20 feet to gold-green grass. The west trail runs 30 feet to the river through a root-cut. North trails run 20 feet into darker wood. A 30-foot move stays on this hub. A Dash of 60 feet follows a trail off this ring. A Dash does not reach the garden, the beach, or [[Uncertainty]]. Packed hub is ordinary ground. Wood beyond the trail mouths is Difficult Terrain and heavily obscures beyond 10 feet. [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]] listens in the north trails. It does not show.

### Action cards

**Split-lip.** Unnamed. Handle after the Face: split lip, one missing boot, grey salt-stiff shirt. Attitude Friendly to the people who pulled him. Immediate want: stay with living people. Leverage: he can say the others left this water and kept walking toward the smoke. Limit: he does not name the woman. Contradiction: he almost died alone, and this fire wants to walk farther into the wood. Visual: wet split lip. Behavior: sits close to whoever pulled him, not to the north trails. Voice: short, wet. Sample: "They kept walking. I went back for the pack." If he is **Unconscious**, the camp talk still runs. If he was left in the hole, cut him from this card.

**Garden mouths.** Unnamed. They listened. They are alive. They want to see the garden. Visual: they look into the north trails when a branch moves. Behavior: they repeat the invitation. Voice: half-voice, the same phrases. Sample: "She asked us to come admire her garden. This way. Leave the living plants."

**Ship mouths.** Unnamed. They call the listeners mad. Visual: they watch the south grass more than the north trails. Behavior: they talk over the invitation. Voice: harder, still low. Sample: "She is leading you into danger. Anyone who listens is mad."

**[[Aruhe - Hinewai]] is not standing at this fire.** The woman in their mouths is her. She is courteous to guests. She is drawing them inland toward [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], which is still far beyond tonight. They are not under a spell. They are caught on the invitation. Her name, the graves, and how far the garden is stay unknown.

**Last sun.** Clue 3. A man-sized flyer sits up-sun over this leaf roof, metallic or chained gear catching the gold, eyes on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. Then the sun goes. After this glimpse [[Talon Skarn]] is done with the sun. Optional **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 27`** only if they ask who that flyer is. The check is not prompted.

**[[Taking on Aruhe]].** Fallen [[Aruhe - Stonepear]] is receiving. Plucking a living plant is a claim. Surrounding life converges, hostile. This camp treats that taking as the wrong walk. Hazard-table rolls stop once the party is at this fire.

**Fallen [[Aruhe - Stonepear]].** Eating one is a Bonus Action and grants **Resistance to all damage** for 1 minute.

> [!narration] Initial Narration
> You see last sun on the leaf roof, gold across packed dirt around a ring of river-stone coals. Heat comes off those coals. Woodsmoke hangs under the leaves instead of rising. The grey-shirted person sits five feet from the fire, split lip still wet, one boot still missing, salt-stiff cloth still damp. He stays close to whoever pulled him. He does not look at the north trails.
>
> You hear three or four other wreck people already arguing in half-voices. One mouth says a woman in the woods asked them to come admire her garden, this way, and not to take the living plants. Another says she is leading them into danger, and anyone who listens is mad. Fallen stonepears sit by the coals, grey-green plates chipped pale. Living ones still hang from mossed branches over the ring.
>
> South, gold-green grass still shows between the last trunks. West, water talks through hanging roots, cool and close. North, packed trails go darker between buttress roots, and the garden talk points that way. A branch moves. The people who listened look into that dark while the talk thins.
>
> What do you do?

## Procedure

**Development.** This fire is a talk-and-commit slice. There is no fight on this card. When the ship-versus-garden split is on the table and the party stays, plays along, waits, or breaks, the scene ends. If they stall, keep Split-lip and the two stories visible, then ask. Leave the argument unfinished on purpose.

Failures impose the Be ready for ruling only. There is no threat clock on this card.

If they force a march to the ship tonight, some of these people balk or slip into the north trails. Beat 7 may still fire on whoever is isolated.

If they agree to just see the garden, they are committing past this valley. The garden stays out of reach tonight. Still go to beat 7.

If they leave without hearing the woman, beat 7 has no woman-lure. Drop beat 7 or recompute a weaker peel. The invitation is not faked.

If a party member takes open valley air, that party member leaves this hub for hawk sky. That is last sun over this roof, not the bloodhawk stoop.

**Combat mode:** if someone attacks this camp, stop talking and rebuild. Lose the prize. They still have a night.

## Landing

If the split is on the table and the party stays at this fire, dusk is gone. Hand to [[Session-11-07-False-Help]] on this hub. A whisper from the garden-dark uses the words they just heard.

If they play along toward the garden, they are on the first north trail, still in this valley. The garden stays out of reach tonight. Hand to beat 7.

If Persuasion peeled some mouths toward [[Uncertainty]] and the party waits, they are still at this fire. Night is coming. Hand to beat 7.

If they forced a march tonight, some of these people balked or slipped into the north trails, plus any RAW conditions already applied. Beat 7 may still fire on whoever is isolated.

If they left without hearing the woman, beat 7 has no woman-lure. Rebuild or drop beat 7.

If they attacked this camp, the prize is gone. They still have a night. Rebuild from that.

If Split-lip is with them, he is still on this dirt or on the north trail, split lip wet, one boot missing, unless he was left in the hole.

> [!narration] Landing — Argument unfinished
> Last sun is gone from this leaf roof. The grey-shirted person is still in the firelight if they pulled him. The two stories are still in the mouths around the coals. From the north dark, the same garden words come back in a whisper.

> [!narration] Landing — North trail
> You are already on the first packed trail north of this fire. The garden talk is still in your ears. The hub's coals sit behind you.

> [!narration] Landing — Forced march
> Some people balk at the edge of the fire, or slip into the north trails. Stems close after them. The packed hub sits emptier by the coals.

> [!narration] Landing — No invitation
> You are already leaving this fire without the woman's words. Gold-green grass shows between the last trunks south of the hub.

> [!narration] Landing — Attack
> The people at the fire scatter. Fallen stonepears and coals remain in the dirt. The trails stand empty.

> [!narration] Landing — Hawk sky
> You are already in open air over this valley. Last sun sits on the leaf roof, then goes. The fire and close trunks shrink below.
