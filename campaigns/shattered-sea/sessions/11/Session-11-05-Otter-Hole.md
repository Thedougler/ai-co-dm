# Session 11 — Rescue at the otter hole

![[attachments/shattered-sea/places/aruhe-river-slack-basin.png|Slack Basin, still claimed pool behind the rock lip]]

## Scene ends when

The party takes the bloody-bank detour off [[Aruhe - River Slack Basin]] toward [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]], with or without the isolated person, or the party fights or loots in this basin and then leaves, or the party breaks south off the trail. Someone can go down here. This is the claimed stretch. It is not a hazards-table result, not night watch, and not the deer-stalker.

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Skip extra looting. Show the slack, the isolated person, the prints, and the smoke. Resolve rescue or leave, then land.

**If ahead:** Still end on the detour or on Hunt resolved. Do not start night watch. Do not start the deer-stalker.

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** One inland survivor is already in this hole. Isolation is how a body becomes the meal. Someone can go down here.
- **Goal / exit:** Pull that person onto this bank and take the bloody-bank detour for [[Session-11-08-Farthest-Camp]], or leave them, or break south.
- **Danger:** Touch this water, the dead, or that person, and the [[Aruhe - River Otter]] Hunt. Watching from this bank does not save the person in the hole.
- **Silence:** Present this basin and the person in it, then wait. Freeze still ticks once because that person remains in the hole.
- **Situation magnets:** The isolated person while they remain in this hole. Anyone in this basin, fishing, pulling a body or a pack, pulling that person, or attacking from this bank.

The party stands on the wet sand of [[Aruhe - River Slack Basin]], facing east, just upvalley of [[Aruhe - River Line Bank|Line Bank]]. The slack pool is 5 feet east. A thin cane stands in the blood smear at the waterline. The isolated person clings to hanging roots 20 feet north, hips in the slack. The closed pack sits 15 feet out in gin-clear slack. The face-down body is 20 feet out. Maroon cloth is 25 feet toward the hanging roots. White water over the rock lip is 30 feet east across the pool. Living prints and the blood trail climb 20 feet north into the jungle wall. Gold-green grass stands 10 feet west of the path. A 30-foot move reaches the water, the pack, the isolated person, or the first trees. A Dash of 60 feet follows the bloody-bank detour north off this basin, or goes south toward Line Bank. The smoke is a thin column still ahead up-valley. A Dash does not reach that smoke, the beach, or the terraces.

### Action cards

**Loop.** Two [[Aruhe - River Otter]] already have an unnamed Calveno wreck straggler in this hole. They play until someone enters this hole, fishes it, pulls a body, a pack, or that person, or attacks from this bank. Then Hunt: Multiattack Bite plus Tail, then Dunk on a **Grappled** target. They can wait under a dragged body.

**Easy.** Two Hunt adults. A family of four to six is ecology, not this card's default. Do not spawn extra otters here.

**Isolated person.** Unnamed. Split lip, one missing boot, grey salt-stiff shirt. Clinging to hanging roots 20 feet north, hips in the slack. No statblock. They went back for the pack after the group climbed out. They want the bank. They do not name the woman. Once the table has seen the Face, Split-lip is the handle.

**Compact numbers.** AC 15. Hit points 76. Bite +6 to hit, `14` piercing, reach 5 feet, and if the target is Medium or smaller it has the **Grappled** condition (escape `DC 14`). Tail +6 to hit, `11` bludgeoning, reach 10 feet. Dunk (Recharge 5–6): one creature **Grappled** by that otter is pulled up to 20 feet and shoved underwater, or held under if already in this water; that target has the **Restrained** condition until the grapple ends. Watery Ambush: advantage on attack rolls against a creature in this water if the other otter is within 10 feet of that target. Speed 20 feet, swim 40 feet. Hold Breath 30 minutes on the owner. Play Tug Toy: contested Athletics or Sleight of Hand against a held or worn object within 5 feet; on a success the otter takes the object and swims 10 feet, no damage.

**Handles.** Once the table has seen the throat marks: Whorl-bib, Thin-streak. Do not invent extra named otters.

**Abort.** They do not chase far overland. They do not haul out onto [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]]. They hold this hole. A body on this sand is off the magnets.

**Breath.** A dunked party member holds breath per 5.5e 2024, then drowning. The isolated person has already spent theirs. If they are still under at tick 3, they drop to 0 hit points and are **Unconscious**. Stabilize is Help plus **Wisdom (Medicine) — `DC 10`**. Do not resolve a finished drowning in the Open.

**[[Taking on Aruhe]].** Fishing this hole is Hunt, and it is a possible claim when you next roll. Carrying flesh from this hole is a claim. Do not invent pack contents.

## Procedure

**Rescue mode.** After everyone has acted, if the isolated person remains in this hole or anyone remains on the magnets (in this basin, fishing this basin, pulling a body, a pack, or that person, or attacking from this bank), advance the threat clock once and resolve that tick. Failures impose the Be ready for ruling only. Failures never advance the clock. Watching from this bank does not start Hunt on the party. Freeze still ticks once at the end of the round because the isolated person remains in the hole.

If a party member takes open valley air, that party member leaves this hole for hawk sky. Point at a [[Aruhe - Young Bloodhawk]]. Do not restage beat 1 on this card.

**Combat mode:** if the party commits to killing the [[Aruhe - River Otter]], stop the clock and run the embedded statblocks.

**Bloodied.** Bloodied is 38 hit points on an [[Aruhe - River Otter]]. This card has no extra bloodied action. Keep Hunt.

**Cover reached.** Grass, jungle wall, or other overland: that [[Aruhe - River Otter]] breaks contact with party members who actually leave this hole. They do not chase far overland. The isolated person is off the magnets once they are on this sand.

**Minions / dials.** Easy: two Hunt adults. Do not spawn a family of four to six on this card.

Do not spawn wolfrabbits, a hazards-table result, night watch, or the deer-stalker on this clock. Taking the bloody-bank detour while the isolated person is already on this sand ends this scene with no further tick.

## Secondary objective

Get the isolated person onto this sand before tick 3. If the party ignores them, they become another body in this hole, and [[Session-11-08-Farthest-Camp]] is fewer than the tracks. If they are on this sand, they walk the detour with the party and can say the others left while they went back for the pack. They do not name the woman.

## Landing

If the party takes the bloody-bank detour with the isolated person on this sand, they are on [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Cutoff Lip]] toward the smoke, and that person walks with them. After this hole, the forest-edge walk toward [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]] may take one [[Session-11-00-Random-Tables]] roll if this is still one of the two allowed rolls before beat 6. Do not run that roll during this hole. Hand to [[Session-11-08-Farthest-Camp]]. Do not run [[Session-11-06-Night-Watch]]. Do not run help-calls on [[Aruhe - Grasslands - Print Braid]].

If the party takes the bloody-bank detour while the isolated person is still in this hole, they are on [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Cutoff Lip]] toward the smoke without that person. Hand to [[Session-11-08-Farthest-Camp]] the same way. That person is lost.

If the party fought or looted in this hole and then left, they are still toward the smoke on [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Cutoff Lip]], plus any RAW conditions already applied, with or without that person. Hand to [[Session-11-08-Farthest-Camp]].

If the party broke off the trail, they are heading south through the valley toward [[Aruhe - River Line Bank|Line Bank]], the terrace line, the beach, or [[Uncertainty]], or a flyer is in hawk sky, plus any RAW conditions already applied. Rebuild from that.

**Scattered.** That party member is still apart until they join up.

**Prone.** Anyone knocked down on this stretch is **Prone** until they stand.

**Grappled / Restrained.** Anyone still in this hole at a break is still in Hunt until they leave this water.

**Unconscious.** If the isolated person hit 0 hit points and was not stabilized, they remain **Unconscious** at 0 hit points if the party has them, or they remain in this hole.

Do not run the hazards table, night watch, or the deer-stalker here. After this hole is used, do not roll the hazards table on this stretch again.
