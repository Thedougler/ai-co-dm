# Session 11 — Rescue at the otter hole

![[aruhe-river-slack-basin.jpg|Slack Basin, still claimed pool behind the rock lip]]

## Scene ends when

The party leaves this hole on the bloody-bank prints toward [[Session-11-06-Farthest-Camp]], with or without the isolated person.

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Skip extra looting. Show the slack, the isolated person, the prints, and the smoke. Resolve rescue or leave, then hand off.

**If ahead:** Still end on the detour or on Hunt resolved. Do not start night watch. Do not start the deer-stalker.

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** One survivor from the inland group is already it in this hole. If no one pulls him onto this bank, the game can drown him. A party member can go down here.
- **Goal / exit:** Pull the man onto this bank, then take the bloody-bank detour toward [[Session-11-06-Farthest-Camp]]. The party can also leave him and follow the prints.
- **Danger:** These otters play tag at twelve feet. Entering the water, throwing a toy, or chasing joins the game. Attacking, fishing, or pulling a dead body or pack turns play into Hunt.
- **Silence:** Present this basin and the person in it, then wait. Freeze still ticks once because that person remains in the hole.
- **Situation magnets:** The isolated person while they remain in this hole. Anyone in this basin, fishing, pulling a body or a pack, pulling that person, attacking from this bank, or playing tag in this water.

## Now

The party stands on the wet sand of [[Aruhe - River Slack Basin]], facing east, just upvalley of [[Aruhe - River Line Bank|Line Bank]]. The slack pool begins 5 feet east. A thin cane stands in the blood smear at the waterline. The isolated person clings to hanging roots 20 feet north, with his hips in the slack. The closed pack sits 15 feet out in the clear water. A face-down body lies 20 feet out, with maroon cloth 25 feet farther toward the hanging roots. White water breaks over the rock lip 30 feet east across the pool. Living prints and the blood trail climb 20 feet north into the jungle wall. Gold-green grass stands 10 feet west of the path. A 30-foot move reaches the water, the pack, the man, or the first trees. A Dash of 60 feet follows the bloody-bank detour north into the trees.

### Action cards

**Loop.** Two [[Aruhe - River Otter]] have an unnamed Calveno wreck survivor in this hole. They are playing tag with him, and they have taken the game too far. He became it when he went back for the pack: he splashed, ran the waterline, or grabbed the chewed pole, and the otters gave chase. They dunk and chase, but they do not Hunt until someone attacks, fishes this hole, or pulls a dead body or pack. Entering the water, throwing a toy, or chasing joins the game. The otters drop him and play with the new person or object. A throw from this bank keeps the game in the hole. A party member who enters or chases becomes it. If the game continues, the otters tire or get hungry and leave the living people for the pack, a body, or rest.

**Easy.** Two play adults. A family of four to six is ecology, not this card's default. Do not spawn extra otters here.

**[[Matteo Scola]].** Until the party pulls him onto the bank, call him the man in the grey shirt. His lip is split, one boot is missing, and his shirt is grey and salt-stiff. He clings to hanging roots 20 feet north with his hips in the slack. He has no statblock. He deserted the inland group after rejecting the woman in the woods' garden advice, then went back for the pack after the group climbed out. That return started the game. He wants the bank and thinks the otters are killing him. He does not name the woman. Once he is on the bank, he gives his name as Matteo if he can speak. If he is **Unconscious**, he does not give his name until he wakes.

**Compact numbers.** Each otter has AC 15 and 76 hit points. In play, a successful grab deals no Bite damage and imposes **Grappled** (escape `DC 14`). **Dunk (Recharge 5–6):** One creature **Grappled** by that otter is pulled up to 20 feet and shoved underwater, or held under if already in this water; that creature has the **Restrained** condition until the grapple ends. **Tug Toy:** Contest Athletics or Sleight of Hand against a held or worn object within 5 feet. On a success, the otter takes the object and swims 10 feet without dealing damage. **Hunt Multiattack:** Bite +6 to hit, `14` piercing, reach 5 feet, and if the target is Medium or smaller, it has the **Grappled** condition (escape `DC 14`). Tail +6 to hit, `11` bludgeoning, reach 10 feet. **Watery Ambush** is Hunt only: the otter has advantage on attack rolls against a creature in this water if the other otter is within 10 feet of that target. Speed 20 feet, swim 40 feet. Hold Breath lasts 30 minutes on the owner.

**Handles.** Once the table has seen the throat marks: Whorl-bib, Thin-streak. Do not invent extra named otters.

**Abort.** The otters hold this hole. They do not chase far overland or haul out onto [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]]. A body on this sand is off the magnets. At tick 4 of play, they tire or get hungry and stop chasing living people.

**Breath.** A dunked party member holds breath per 5.5e 2024, then begins drowning. The isolated person has already spent his breath. If he is still under at tick 3, he drops to 0 hit points and is **Unconscious**. A party member who is it still has unused breath and does not drop to 0 hit points from play on tick 3. Stabilizing him requires Help plus **Wisdom (Medicine) — `DC 10`**. He keeps making death saves until stabilized.

**[[Taking on Aruhe]].** Fishing this hole is Hunt, and it is a possible claim when you next roll. Carrying flesh from this hole is a claim. Do not invent pack contents.

## Procedure

**Play mode.** After everyone has acted, advance the threat clock once if the isolated person remains in this hole or anyone remains on the magnets. The magnets are this basin, fishing this basin, pulling a body, pack, or person, attacking from this bank, and playing tag in this water. A failed Be ready for roll imposes that row's failure only; it never advances the clock. Watching from this bank does not start Hunt on the party. Freeze still ticks once at the end of the round because the isolated person remains in the hole.

If any party member joins the game this round by entering the water, throwing a toy, or chasing, the otters drop the isolated person and play with that person or object. Joining does not start Hunt. The otters do not come onto this sand for a thrown toy. A party member who enters or chases becomes it, and the clock still ticks. If a later round has no one joining, throwing, or chasing, the otters return to the isolated person if he is still in this hole. At tick 4 of play, they tire or get hungry, stop chasing living people, and go to the pack, a body, or rest. Avoid combat unless the party forces it.

**Combat mode:** If the party commits to killing the [[Aruhe - River Otter]], or Hunt starts from an attack, fishing, or pulling a dead body or pack, stop the clock and run the embedded statblocks.

If a party member takes open valley air, that party member leaves this hole for hawk sky. Point at a [[Aruhe - Young Bloodhawk]].

## Threat clock

The play or Hunt turn happens after everyone has acted, and only if the isolated person remains in this hole or anyone remains on the magnets. Table failures do not also tick.

If Hunt has started, Whorl-bib uses Multiattack against the party member who fished this hole, pulled a body or pack, or struck from this bank: Bite (+6 to hit, `14` piercing, reach 5 feet, **Grappled** escape `DC 14` if Medium or smaller) and Tail (+6 to hit, `11` bludgeoning, reach 10 feet). | ==_The whorl-marked otter yanks the clinging man off the hanging roots and toward the pack. **Bloodied.** Bloodied is 38 hit points on an [[Aruhe - River Otter]]. This card has no extra bloodied action. Keep Hunt.

**Cover reached.** Once a party member reaches the grass, jungle wall, or other overland cover, that [[Aruhe - River Otter]] breaks contact. The otters do not chase far overland. The isolated person is off the magnets once he reaches this sand.

**Scene dials.** Easy: two play adults. Do not spawn a family of four to six on this card.

Do not spawn wolfrabbits, a hazards-table result, night watch, or the deer-stalker on this clock. Taking the bloody-bank detour while the isolated person is already on this sand ends this scene with no further tick. Tick 4 of play ends the chase without a fight; the party can still pull the isolated person or take the detour.

## Secondary objective

Get the isolated person onto this sand before tick 3. Joining the game makes the otters drop him while they chase whoever or whatever is it, giving the party a chance to pull him in. If the party ignores him, he becomes another body in this hole, and [[Session-11-06-Farthest-Camp]] has one fewer survivor than the tracks suggest. If he reaches this sand and can speak, he walks the detour, gives his name as Matteo, and says the others left while he went back for the pack. He does not name the woman.

## How the Scene Resolves

The party takes the bloody-bank detour toward the smoke on [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Cutoff Lip]], with or without the isolated person. Hand to [[Session-11-06-Farthest-Camp]]. The forest-edge walk toward [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]] may take one [[Session-11-00-Random-Tables]] roll if this is still one of the two allowed rolls before beat 6. Do not run that roll during this hole. Apply any RAW conditions already applied.

**Grappled / Restrained.** Anyone still in this hole at a break remains in play or Hunt until they leave the water, unless tick 4 of play has already ended the chase.

**Unconscious.** If the isolated person hit 0 hit points and was not stabilized, he remains **Unconscious** at 0 hit points if the party has him, or remains in this hole.
