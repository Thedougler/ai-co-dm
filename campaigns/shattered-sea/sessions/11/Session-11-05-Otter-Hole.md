# Session 11 — Rescue at the otter hole

![[aruhe-river-slack-basin.jpg|Slack Basin, still claimed pool behind the rock lip]]

## Scene ends when

The party leaves this hole on the bloody-bank prints toward [[Session-11-06-Farthest-Camp]], with or without the isolated person.

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Skip extra looting. Show the slack, the isolated person, the prints, and the smoke. Resolve rescue or leave, then hand off.

**If ahead:** Still end on the detour or on Hunt resolved. Do not start night watch. Do not start the deer-stalker.

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** One inland survivor is already it in this hole. The game is drowning him. Someone can go down here.
- **Goal / exit:** Pull that person onto this bank and take the bloody-bank detour for [[Session-11-06-Farthest-Camp]], or leave them.
- **Danger:** These otters play tag at twelve feet. Entering this water, throwing a toy, or chasing joins the game. Surviving that game is hard. Attack, fish, or pull a dead body or pack, and they Hunt.
- **Silence:** Present this basin and the person in it, then wait. Freeze still ticks once because that person remains in the hole.
- **Situation magnets:** The isolated person while they remain in this hole. Anyone in this basin, fishing, pulling a body or a pack, pulling that person, attacking from this bank, or playing tag in this water.

## Now

The party stands on the wet sand of [[Aruhe - River Slack Basin]], facing east, just upvalley of [[Aruhe - River Line Bank|Line Bank]]. The slack pool is 5 feet east. A thin cane stands in the blood smear at the waterline. The isolated person clings to hanging roots 20 feet north, hips in the slack. The closed pack sits 15 feet out in clear slack. The face-down body is 20 feet out. Maroon cloth is 25 feet toward the hanging roots. White water over the rock lip is 30 feet east across the pool. Living prints and the blood trail climb 20 feet north into the jungle wall. Gold-green grass stands 10 feet west of the path. A 30-foot move reaches the water, the pack, the isolated person, or the first trees. A Dash of 60 feet follows the bloody-bank detour north into the trees.

### Action cards

**Loop.** Two [[Aruhe - River Otter]] already have an unnamed Calveno wreck straggler in this hole. They are playing tag with him, and they have taken the game too far. He started it by going back for the pack: he splashed, ran this waterline, or grabbed the chewed pole, and they treated that as him being it. They dunk and chase. They do not Hunt until someone attacks, fishes this hole, or pulls a dead body or a pack. Entering this hole, throwing a toy, or chasing joins the game. They drop him and play with that person or object. A throw from this bank keeps the game in this hole. A party member who enters or chases is it. If the game keeps going, they tire or get hungry and leave the living people for the pack, a body, or rest.

**Easy.** Two play adults. A family of four to six is ecology, not this card's default. Do not spawn extra otters here.

**[[Matteo Scola]].** Until they pull him onto the bank, call him the man in the grey shirt. His lip is split, one boot is missing, and the shirt is grey and salt-stiff. He clings to hanging roots 20 feet north, hips in the slack. No statblock. He deserted the inland group after rejecting the woman in the woods' garden advice, then went back for the pack after the group climbed out. That return started the game. He wants the bank. He thinks they are killing him. He does not name the woman. After they pull him onto the bank, if he can speak, he gives his name: Matteo. If he is **Unconscious**, he does not give his name until he wakes.

**Compact numbers.** AC 15. Hit points 76. In play, a grab uses no Bite damage. **Grappled** (escape `DC 14`). Dunk (Recharge 5–6): one creature **Grappled** by that otter is pulled up to 20 feet and shoved underwater, or held under if already in this water; that target has the **Restrained** condition until the grapple ends. Play Tug Toy: contested Athletics or Sleight of Hand against a held or worn object within 5 feet; on a success the otter takes the object and swims 10 feet; no damage. Hunt Multiattack: Bite +6 to hit, `14` piercing, reach 5 feet, and if the target is Medium or smaller it has the **Grappled** condition (escape `DC 14`). Tail +6 to hit, `11` bludgeoning, reach 10 feet. Watery Ambush is Hunt only: advantage on attack rolls against a creature in this water if the other otter is within 10 feet of that target. Speed 20 feet, swim 40 feet. Hold Breath 30 minutes on the owner.

**Handles.** Once the table has seen the throat marks: Whorl-bib, Thin-streak. Do not invent extra named otters.

**Abort.** They do not chase far overland. They do not haul out onto [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]]. They hold this hole. A body on this sand is off the magnets. At tick 4 of play they tire or get hungry and stop chasing living people.

**Breath.** A dunked party member holds breath per 5.5e 2024, then drowning. The isolated person has already spent theirs. If they are still under at tick 3, they drop to 0 hit points and are **Unconscious**. A party member who is it still has unused breath, and does not drop to 0 hit points from play on tick 3. Stabilize is Help plus **Wisdom (Medicine) — `DC 10`**. They keep making death saves until stabilized.

**[[Taking on Aruhe]].** Fishing this hole is Hunt, and it is a possible claim when you next roll. Carrying flesh from this hole is a claim. Do not invent pack contents.

## Procedure

**Play mode.** After everyone has acted, if the isolated person remains in this hole or anyone remains on the magnets (in this basin, fishing this basin, pulling a body, a pack, or that person, attacking from this bank, or playing tag in this water), advance the threat clock once and resolve that tick. Failures impose the Be ready for ruling only. Failures never advance the clock. Watching from this bank does not start Hunt on the party. Freeze still ticks once at the end of the round because the isolated person remains in the hole.

If the party has joined the game this round by entering this water, throwing a toy, or chasing, the otters drop the isolated person and play with that person or object. Hunt does not start from joining. They do not come onto this sand for a thrown toy. A party member who enters or chases is it. The clock still ticks. If a later round has no join, throw, or chase, they return to the isolated person if he is still in this hole. At tick 4 of play they tire or get hungry: they stop chasing living people and go to the pack, a body, or rest. Combat is avoided.

**Combat mode:** if the party commits to killing the [[Aruhe - River Otter]], or Hunt has started from an attack, fishing, or pulling a dead body or pack, stop the clock and run the embedded statblocks.

If a party member takes open valley air, that party member leaves this hole for hawk sky. Point at a [[Aruhe - Young Bloodhawk]].

## Threat clock

The play or Hunt turn happens after everyone has acted, and only if the isolated person remains in this hole or anyone remains on the magnets. Table failures do not also tick.

**Bloodied.** Bloodied is 38 hit points on an [[Aruhe - River Otter]]. This card has no extra bloodied action. Keep Hunt.

**Cover reached.** Grass, jungle wall, or other overland: that [[Aruhe - River Otter]] breaks contact with party members who actually leave this hole. They do not chase far overland. The isolated person is off the magnets once they are on this sand.

**Scene dials.** Easy: two play adults. Do not spawn a family of four to six on this card.

Do not spawn wolfrabbits, a hazards-table result, night watch, or the deer-stalker on this clock. Taking the bloody-bank detour while the isolated person is already on this sand ends this scene with no further tick. Tick 4 of play ends the chase without a fight; the party can still pull the isolated person or take the detour.

## Secondary objective

Get the isolated person onto this sand before tick 3. Joining the game drops him so he can be pulled while they chase whoever or whatever is it. If the party ignores him, he becomes another body in this hole, and [[Session-11-06-Farthest-Camp]] is fewer than the tracks. If he is on this sand and can speak, he walks the detour, gives his name as Matteo, and can say the others left while he went back for the pack. He does not name the woman.

## How the Scene Resolves

The party takes the bloody-bank detour toward the smoke on [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Cutoff Lip]], with or without the isolated person. Hand to [[Session-11-06-Farthest-Camp]]. After this hole, the forest-edge walk toward [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]] may take one [[Session-11-00-Random-Tables]] roll if this is still one of the two allowed rolls before beat 6. Do not run that roll during this hole. Apply any RAW conditions already applied.

**Grappled / Restrained.** Anyone still in this hole at a break is still in play or Hunt until they leave this water, unless tick 4 of play has already ended the chase.

**Unconscious.** If the isolated person hit 0 hit points and was not stabilized, they remain **Unconscious** at 0 hit points if the party has them, or they remain in this hole.
