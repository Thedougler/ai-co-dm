# Session 11 — Theft on the watch

![[attachments/shattered-sea/places/aruhe-star-cut.jpg|Star Cut, night-watch aisle under a slit of stars]]

## Scene ends when

End the scene when the steal is disrupted or [[Talon Skarn]] has the [[Fate Spinner]] and the watch has answered. Hand to [[Session-11-10-Aftermath]].

This is the theft on the watch at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]], or on the column if they marched.

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Resolve one Hook and Drag on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], with the watch partner involved. [[Talon Skarn]] breaks contact down the star-cut, then hand to [[Session-11-10-Aftermath]].

**If ahead:** Still end here. Do not add [[Talon Vantyrus]], [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], or bloodhawks.

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** [[Talon Skarn]] came for the [[Fate Spinner]]. He creates the opening by isolating one body from the watch.
- **Goal / exit:** Disrupt the steal. [[Talon Skarn]] breaks contact with or without the [[Fate Spinner]], then the scene hands to [[Session-11-10-Aftermath]].
- **Danger:** While the [[Fate Spinner]] stays hidden, he works on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] and her gear. Once it shows, he can finish the theft with one Hook and Drag. A messy camp gives him cover.
- **Silence:** Present the north-mouth approach on this pair, then wait. If the party freezes, advance the clock once at the end of the round.
- **Situation magnets:** [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], her watch partner, anyone between [[Talon Skarn]] and her, anyone who shows the [[Fate Spinner]], anyone in the 15-foot chain lane, and anyone left alone.

## Now

The party is on [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]] unless beat 8 ended in a march or a knot. [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] and one other watcher stand at the east fire's north edge, facing the star-cut. If she is not on this watch, keep [[Talon Skarn]] focused on the carrier and place the nearest watcher with that carrier. If she is alone, the nearest watcher is already with her. The rest of the camp sleeps on rolls along both jungle walls.

[[Talon Skarn]] starts at the north mouth, 40 feet from the fire. He has a walk speed of 50 feet and no fly speed. A 30-foot move from the fire stays on the aisle and does not reach him; a 50-foot move from the north mouth reaches the coals. Chained Blade has 15-foot reach. Hook and Drag has 20-foot reach. A 50-foot Dash from the fire reaches the star-cut or the west river-talk, but not [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]], the beach, or [[Uncertainty]]. The packed aisle is ordinary ground. The jungle walls are Difficult Terrain and heavily obscure beyond 10 feet.

**Thin.** [[Talon Skarn]] starts 15 feet from [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], with the first chain already lifted. The party has **Disadvantage** on attack rolls and ability checks against him until the end of round 1.

**Paired.** Both sides see each other from 40 feet away. The opening is even.

**Fortified.** The party already holds posted positions. [[Talon Skarn]] cannot start unseen. He must enter through the north mouth in the open, or rattle a chain in the west wood as a visible decoy before crossing the 40-foot aisle. He still makes one genuine attempt.

**Column.** If they marched at beat 8, this aisle is the trail they are on. Do not move them to [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]]. The carrier remains the target. Reuse the 40-foot lane and the chain reaches.

**Knot.** If they sat awake at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]], start at that fire. A messy camp gives him cover.

If beats 7 and 8 left the fire messy, shouting, running survivors, and a kicked-over fire all give [[Talon Skarn]] cover.

### Action cards

**One [[Talon Skarn]] on this watch.** Run combat/theft mode by default. His win condition is to break contact down the star-cut.

**Committed attempt.** He came to make one real attempt at the [[Fate Spinner]] tonight. An excellent watch does not cancel the showdown; it forces him to expose himself more. A fortified watch can become a trap, a capture, or a kill.

**Loop.** Keep the action on this pair, not the camp at large. Work on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] until the [[Fate Spinner]] shows or an opening appears: a stun, a cut strap, a pinned wing, a shaken-out bag, or a Hook and Drag that pulls her away from the partner. If the watch partner hits him, spend the next beat on that person only long enough to reopen the grab. Once the [[Fate Spinner]] shows, stop fighting for anything else and take the object. Then break contact down the star-cut.

**What his actions reveal.** [[Talon Skarn]] ignores survivors, fruit, and anyone who is not between him and [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. He cuts gear before he cuts throats. He uses the chains to isolate one body on this aisle. He watches her hands and hips, not her face. His voice is unknown.

**Compact numbers.** AC 20; 171 hit points; walk 50 feet; no fly speed. Chained Blade is +10 to hit for `16` slashing plus necrotic damage, with 15-foot reach. Hook and Drag is +10 to hit for `14` slashing damage, with 20-foot reach. On a hit, the target makes a **Strength save — `DC 18`** or is pulled up to 15 feet toward him; a target pulled within 5 feet is also **Prone**. Bloodied is 85 hit points.

**If they break his read.** If they Grapple him, he still plays the steal, then breaks contact. If they throw a fake object, he checks it once and returns to [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. If they handed off the [[Fate Spinner]] before reaching this camp, he is wrong; that is the only reason he changes targets. He reads the pass and goes to the new carrier. If the pass happens during this fight, he reads it the same way.

**End the attempt.** Whether he gets the grab or not, he breaks contact down the star-cut after the attempt.

His eyes stay on Crissdalynn Khinriss's hands, hips, straps, and pouches. The chains begin to lift.

> What do you do?

## Procedure

**Combat / theft mode.** Run the embedded [[Talon Skarn]] statblock. His win condition is to break contact down the star-cut.

Use only the ruling in **Be ready for** when a check fails. A failed check does not advance the threat clock. The clock is [[Talon Skarn]]'s steal loop: after everyone has acted, if he is still on this watch and the [[Fate Spinner]] remains hidden or remains on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], advance it once. Resolve that tick as the action he spends on his combat turn. Do not give him a second Multiattack for the same tick. A freeze or "we watch" advances the clock once at the end of the round. Several failed checks in one round still produce one tick. If Hook and Drag already resolved on his combat turn, do not add a second grab for that action.

If they force-marched at beat 8, keep them on the column. If they sat awake in a knot, keep them at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]]. If the watch is fortified, [[Talon Skarn]] still comes, but he must cross the 40-foot aisle in the open.

**Bloodied 85 HP.** Unbroken Hatred fires. [[Talon Skarn]] still plays the steal, then breaks contact down the star-cut.

**Cover reached.** The star-cut is [[Talon Skarn]]'s exit. Jungle walls do not protect a party member who remains between him and [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. A messy camp gives him cover.

**Scene dials.** Easy: remove Legendary Resistance and start Chainstorm uncharged. Do not add a second attacker. Survivors who run are cover for [[Talon Skarn]], not extra enemies.

Freezing on this watch still advances the steal once.

## Secondary objective

Keep the [[Fate Spinner]] on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. Beats required: disrupt the steal with the watch partner involved. Ignore outcome: freezing still lets [[Talon Skarn]] make the attempt, and the watch still answers. Later consequence: if he leaves with the [[Fate Spinner]], [[Session-11-10-Aftermath]] treats the result as Stolen; if the steal is disrupted, that card treats it as Held.

## How the Scene Resolves

The watch has answered. The [[Fate Spinner]] is on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], on the packed dirt, or in a hooked blade. [[Talon Skarn]] is in the star-cut, still on this aisle, or already in the open with it. Carry forward any RAW damage and conditions already applied, then hand to [[Session-11-10-Aftermath]].

If the steal is disrupted, [[Talon Skarn]] is in the star-cut or still on this aisle without a clean steal. The [[Fate Spinner]] is still on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], or on the packed dirt if it was torn free and not recovered. If they Grapple or drop him, he remains on this aisle. He still played the steal.

If [[Talon Skarn]] leaves with the [[Fate Spinner]], it is in a hooked blade as he takes the north mouth. [[Session-11-10-Aftermath]] treats that result as Stolen.
