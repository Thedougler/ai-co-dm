# Session 11 — Theft on the watch

![[attachments/shattered-sea/places/aruhe-star-cut.png|Star Cut, night-watch aisle under a slit of stars]]

## Scene ends when

The steal attempt is disrupted and [[Talon Skarn]] breaks contact down the star-cut, or [[Talon Skarn]] has or does not have the [[Fate Spinner]] and the watch has answered. Hand to [[Session-11-10-Aftermath]]. Do not cut the night. This is the theft on the watch at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]], or on the column if they marched. It is not [[Talon Vantyrus]], not [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], and not a bloodhawk stoop.

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Resolve one Hook and Drag on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], with the watch partner in it. [[Talon Skarn]] breaks contact down the star-cut. Then hand to [[Session-11-10-Aftermath]].

**If ahead:** Still end here. Do not add [[Talon Vantyrus]], [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], or bloodhawks.

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** [[Talon Skarn]] tries to take the [[Fate Spinner]] off [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. Isolation is how he steals.
- **Goal / exit:** Disrupt the steal. [[Talon Skarn]] breaks contact with or without it. Hand to [[Session-11-10-Aftermath]].
- **Danger:** If the [[Fate Spinner]] stays hidden, he keeps [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] in the fight. If it comes into view, the theft can end in one Hook and Drag. A messy camp is cover for him.
- **Silence:** Present the north-mouth approach on this pair, then wait. If the party freezes, tick the clock once at the end of the round.
- **Situation magnets:** [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. The watch partner. Anyone between [[Talon Skarn]] and her. Anyone who shows the [[Fate Spinner]]. Anyone standing in the 15-foot chain lane. Anyone left alone.

## Now

The party is on [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]] unless beat 8 broke. [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] and one other stand the watch at the east fire's north edge, facing the star-cut. The rest of this camp sleeps on rolls along both jungle walls. [[Talon Skarn]] starts at the north mouth, 40 feet from that fire, on this pair, not the camp at large. Walk speed is 50 feet. A 30-foot move from the fire stays on the aisle and does not reach him. A 50-foot move from the north mouth reaches the coals. Chained Blade reach is 15 feet. Hook and Drag reach is 20 feet. A Dash of 50 feet from the fire can hit the star-cut or the west river-talk. A Dash does not reach [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring|Spoke Ring]], the beach, or [[Uncertainty]]. Packed aisle is ordinary ground. Wood off either wall is Difficult Terrain and heavily obscures beyond 10 feet.

**Thin.** [[Talon Skarn]] starts 15 feet from [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], with the first chain already lifted. The party has **Disadvantage** on attack rolls and ability checks against him until the end of the first round.

**Paired.** Both sides see each other at 40 feet. The opening is even.

**Fortified.** The party already holds posted positions. [[Talon Skarn]] cannot start unseen. He must step into the north mouth in the open, or rattle a chain in the west wood as a visible decoy and then cross the 40-foot aisle. He still makes one genuine attempt.

**Column.** If they marched at beat 8, this aisle is the trail they are on. Do not move them to [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]]. The carrier is still the target. Reuse the 40-foot lane and the chain reaches.

**Knot.** If they sat awake at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]], start at that fire. A messy camp is cover for him.

If [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] is alone, the nearest watcher is already on this watch. If the fire is messy from beats 7 and 8, shouting, running survivors, and a fire kicked over are cover for [[Talon Skarn]].

### Action cards

**One [[Talon Skarn]] on this watch.** Combat / theft mode is the default. Break-contact down the star-cut is his win condition. This is not a TPK. Do not spawn a second attacker. Do not play [[Talon Vantyrus]]. Do not spawn the [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]]. Do not restage beat 1.

**Committed attempt.** He came to make one real attempt at the [[Fate Spinner]] tonight. An excellent watch does not cancel the showdown. Better preparation forces him to expose himself more in order to try. The party can turn a fortified watch into a trap, a capture, or a kill.

**Loop.** Close on this pair, not the camp at large. Fight [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] until he sees the [[Fate Spinner]] or creates an opening: a stun, a cut strap, a pinned wing, a bag shaken out, or a Hook and Drag that pulls her off the partner. If the watch partner hits him, spend the next beat on that person only long enough to make the grab possible again. If the [[Fate Spinner]] comes into view, go for the object and stop fighting for anything else. Then break contact down the star-cut.

**Lore from what he does.** [[Talon Skarn]] ignores survivors, fruit, and everyone who is not between him and [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. He cuts gear before he cuts throats. He isolates one body the way the bloodhawk did, with chains on this aisle instead of a stoop. He looks at her hands and hips, not her face. Do not invent a line. Voice is unknown.

**Compact numbers.** AC 20. Hit points 171. Walk 50 feet. No fly speed. Multiattack is three Chained Blade attacks, +10 to hit, `16` slashing plus necrotic, reach 15 feet. He can replace one attack with Hook and Drag: +10 to hit, `14` slashing, reach 20 feet; **Strength save — `DC 18`** or the target is pulled up to 15 feet toward him, and if pulled within 5 feet the target is also **Prone**. Chainstorm (Recharge 5–6): each other creature of his choice within 15 feet makes a **Dexterity save — `DC 18`**; failure → `7d8` slashing and **Prone**; success → half damage, not **Prone**. Predator's Rush: Bonus Action, move up to 25 feet toward a hostile creature he can see, no opportunity attacks. Chain Snare: when a creature he can see within 20 feet moves willingly, one Chained Blade attack; on a hit, that creature's speed is 0 for the rest of the turn.

**If they break.** If they Grapple him, he still plays the steal, then breaks contact. If they throw a fake object, he checks it once and comes back at [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. If they already handed the [[Fate Spinner]] off before this camp, he is wrong, and that is the only time he changes targets: he reads the pass and goes to the new carrier. If the pass happens during this fight, he reads it the same way.

**Abort.** After the attempt, grab or no grab, he breaks contact down the star-cut. Do not linger into a speech. Do not add [[Talon Vantyrus]], [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], or bloodhawks.

## Procedure

**Combat / theft mode** is the default. Run the embedded [[Talon Skarn]] statblock. Break-contact down the star-cut is his win condition. This is not a TPK.

Failures impose the Be ready for ruling only. Failures never advance the threat clock. The threat clock is [[Talon Skarn]]'s steal loop. After everyone has acted, if he is still on this watch and the [[Fate Spinner]] is still hidden from him or still on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], advance the threat clock once. Resolve that tick as what he spends his combat turn on. Do not also give him a second Multiattack for the same tick. Freeze or "we watch" ticks once at the end of the round. Several failed checks in one round still produce one tick. A Hook and Drag already resolved on his combat turn does not also tick a second grab for that same action.

If they force-marched at beat 8, keep them on the column. If they sat awake in a knot, keep them at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]]. If the watch is fortified, he still comes; he has to cross the 40-foot aisle in the open.

**Scene dials.** Easy: remove Legendary Resistance and start Chainstorm uncharged. Do not invent a second attacker. Survivors who run are cover for [[Talon Skarn]], not extra enemies.

## Secondary objective

Keep the [[Fate Spinner]] on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. Beats required: disrupt the steal, with the watch partner in it. Ignore outcome: freeze still lets [[Talon Skarn]] get the attempt off, and the watch still answers. Later consequence: if he leaves with the [[Fate Spinner]], [[Session-11-10-Aftermath]] answers that take; if the steal is disrupted, he is still in the Quiet north of this aisle.

## How the Scene Resolves

The watch has answered. The [[Fate Spinner]] is on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], on the packed dirt, or in a hooked blade. [[Talon Skarn]] is in the star-cut, still on this aisle, or already in the open with it. Plus any RAW damage and conditions already applied. Hand to [[Session-11-10-Aftermath]]. Do not cut the night. Do not fight bloodhawks on this card.

If the steal is disrupted, [[Talon Skarn]] is in the star-cut, or still on this aisle, without a clean steal. The [[Fate Spinner]] is still on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], or on the packed dirt at this watch if it was torn and not recovered.
