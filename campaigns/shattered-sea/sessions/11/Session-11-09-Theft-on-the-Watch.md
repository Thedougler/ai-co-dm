# Session 11 — Theft on the watch

## Scene ends when

The steal attempt is disrupted and [[Talon Skarn]] breaks contact down the star-cut, or [[Talon Skarn]] has (or does not have) the [[Fate Spinner]] and the watch has answered, and the night cuts. Resolution is cut. The night ends on this beat. This is the theft on the watch at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]]. It is not [[Talon Vantyrus]], and it is not [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]].

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** One Hook and Drag on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], the watch partner in it, [[Talon Skarn]] breaks contact down the star-cut. Then cut.

**If ahead:** Still end here. Do not add [[Talon Vantyrus]] or [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]].

## L0 · Glance

- **Stakes:** [[Talon Skarn]] tries to take the [[Fate Spinner]] off [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. The night ends here.
- **Goal / exit:** Disrupt the steal. [[Talon Skarn]] breaks contact down the star-cut with or without it. Cut.
- **This aisle:** Packed dirt runs north-south at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]], with the fire offset east, sleeping rolls on both walls, fallen [[Aruhe - Giant's Guava]] on the west litter, and a star-cut of night sky at the north mouth.
- **Danger:** If the [[Fate Spinner]] stays hidden, he keeps her in the fight. If it comes out, the theft can end in one Hook and Drag.
- **Silence:** Present the north-mouth approach on this pair, then wait. If the crew freezes, tick the clock once at the end of the round.
- **Situation magnets:** [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. The watch partner. Anyone between [[Talon Skarn]] and her. Anyone who shows the [[Fate Spinner]]. Anyone standing in the 15-foot chain lane.

## Now

The crew is on [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]]. [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] and one other stand the watch at the east fire's north edge, facing the star-cut. The rest of this camp sleeps on rolls along both jungle walls. [[Talon Skarn]] starts at the north mouth, 40 feet from that fire, on this pair, not the camp at large. Walk 50 feet. A 30-foot move from the fire stays on the aisle. A 50-foot move from the north mouth reaches the coals. Chained Blade reach is 15 feet. Hook and Drag reach is 20 feet. A Dash of 50 feet from the fire can hit the star-cut or the west river-talk. A Dash does not reach [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring|Spoke Ring]], the beach, or [[Uncertainty]]. Packed aisle is ordinary ground. Wood off either wall is Difficult Terrain and heavily obscures beyond 10 feet.

## DM truth

This is a theft. [[Talon Skarn]] followed the ship from the disabled hunter. [[Talon Skarn]] picked this watch because [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] is the carrier. [[Talon Skarn]] knows the [[Fate Spinner]] is on her. [[Talon Skarn]] does not know which pouch, wrap, or hand. The chain kit from [[Talon Skarn]]'s fight sheet is how he takes it. [[Talon Skarn]] will almost surely get the attempt off. [[Talon Skarn]] will almost surely get disrupted before he leaves with it.

**Unrevealed:** Do not play [[Talon Skarn]]'s business with [[Talon Vantyrus]]. Do not name the [[Fate Spinner]] in the Open unless it is already in view. Lore comes from what [[Talon Skarn]] does, not from a speech. Voice is unknown. Do not invent a line.

### Action cards

**One [[Talon Skarn]] on this watch.** Combat / theft mode is the default. Break-contact down the star-cut is [[Talon Skarn]]'s win condition. This is not a TPK. Do not spawn a second attacker. Do not play [[Talon Vantyrus]].

**Loop.** Close on this pair, not the camp at large. Fight [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] until [[Talon Skarn]] sees the [[Fate Spinner]] or creates an opening: a stun, a cut strap, a pinned wing, a bag shaken out, or a Hook and Drag that pulls her off the partner. If the watch partner hits [[Talon Skarn]], spend the next beat on that person only long enough to make the grab possible again. If the [[Fate Spinner]] comes into view, go for the object and stop fighting for anything else. Then break contact down the star-cut.

**Lore from what he does.** [[Talon Skarn]] ignores survivors, fruit, and everyone who is not between him and [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. [[Talon Skarn]] cuts gear before he cuts throats. [[Talon Skarn]] isolates one body the way the bloodhawk did, with chains on this aisle instead of a stoop. [[Talon Skarn]] looks at her hands and hips, not her face.

**Compact numbers.** AC 20. Hit points 171. Walk 50 feet. Multiattack is three Chained Blade attacks, +10 to hit, 16 slashing plus necrotic, reach 15 feet. He can replace one attack with Hook and Drag: +10 to hit, 14 slashing, reach 20 feet; **Strength save — `DC 18`** or the target is pulled up to 15 feet toward him, and if pulled within 5 feet the target is also **Prone**. Chainstorm (Recharge 5–6): each other creature of his choice within 15 feet makes a **Dexterity save — `DC 18`**; failure → `7d8` slashing and **Prone**; success → half damage, not **Prone**. Predator's Rush: Bonus Action, move up to 25 feet toward a hostile creature he can see, no opportunity attacks. Chain Snare: when a creature he can see within 20 feet moves willingly, one Chained Blade; on a hit that creature's speed becomes 0 for the rest of the turn. Relentless Pursuer: Difficult Terrain costs him no extra movement; when a hostile creature he can see moves away, he can move 10 feet toward it with no opportunity attacks, once per turn. Unbroken Hatred at 85 hit points: he ends **charmed**, **frightened**, **grappled**, **prone**, and **restrained** on himself and moves up to 25 feet with no opportunity attacks. Legendary Resistance 3/Day. Three legendary actions: Stalking Advance, Chain Lash, or Scissor Chains for 2 actions.

**If they break.** If [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] is alone, the nearest watcher is already on this watch. If they Grapple [[Talon Skarn]], he still plays the steal, then breaks contact. If they throw a fake object, [[Talon Skarn]] checks it once and comes back at [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. If they already handed the [[Fate Spinner]] off before this camp, [[Talon Skarn]] is wrong, and that is the only time he changes targets.

**Abort.** After the attempt, grab or no grab, [[Talon Skarn]] breaks contact down the star-cut. Do not linger into a speech. Do not add [[Talon Vantyrus]] or [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]].

## Procedure

**Combat / theft mode** is the default. Run the embedded [[Talon Skarn]] statblock. Break-contact down the star-cut is [[Talon Skarn]]'s win condition. This is not a TPK.

Players declare and resolve turns, checks, and movement as normal. Failures impose the Be ready for ruling only. Failures never advance the threat clock. The threat clock is [[Talon Skarn]]'s steal loop. After everyone has acted, if [[Talon Skarn]] is still on this watch and the [[Fate Spinner]] is still hidden from him or still on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], advance the threat clock once. Resolve that tick as what [[Talon Skarn]] spends his combat turn on. Do not also give [[Talon Skarn]] a second Multiattack for the same tick. Freeze or "we watch" ticks once at the end of the round, not per failed check. Several failed checks in one round still produce one tick. A Hook and Drag already resolved on [[Talon Skarn]]'s combat turn does not also tick a second grab for that same action. If [[Talon Skarn]] uses Predator's Rush, Relentless Pursuer, or Stalking Advance to open the star-cut after the attempt, this scene can end. Grappling [[Talon Skarn]] does not end the steal. [[Talon Skarn]] still plays the steal, then breaks contact. If a crew member flies up the star-cut, that crew member is in star-cut sky. [[Talon Skarn]] does not follow into the air. Do not restage beat 1. Do not add [[Talon Vantyrus]] or [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]].

**Bloodied 85 HP.** Unbroken Hatred fires. [[Talon Skarn]] still plays the steal, then breaks contact down the star-cut. This is not a TPK.

**Cover reached.** The star-cut is [[Talon Skarn]]'s exit. Jungle walls are not cover from him if that crew member is between him and [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. A messy camp is cover for him.

**Minions / dials.** None. Do not invent a second attacker. Do not play [[Talon Vantyrus]]. Survivors who run are cover for [[Talon Skarn]], not extra enemies.

Do not spawn the [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]], otters, wolfrabbits, or [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]] on this clock. Do not restage beat 1. Freeze on this watch still ticks the steal once.

## Secondary objective

Keep the [[Fate Spinner]] on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. Beats required: disrupt the steal, with the watch partner in it. Ignore outcome: freeze still lets [[Talon Skarn]] get the attempt off, and the night still cuts. Later consequence: if [[Talon Skarn]] leaves with the [[Fate Spinner]], the crew does not have it; if the steal is disrupted, [[Talon Skarn]] is still in the Quiet north of this aisle. Do not start [[Talon Vantyrus]] or [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]] tonight.

## Landing

The night cuts. Resolution is cut. Do not start a next beat on this file.

If the steal is disrupted, [[Talon Skarn]] is in the star-cut, or still on this aisle, without a clean steal. The [[Fate Spinner]] is still on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], or on the packed dirt at this watch if it was torn and not recovered. The watch has answered.

If [[Talon Skarn]] leaves with the [[Fate Spinner]], the [[Fate Spinner]] is in a hooked blade as he takes the north mouth. The watch has answered.

If [[Talon Skarn]] is **Grappled** and the crew keeps hitting, still end here. [[Talon Skarn]] still played the steal. Do not add [[Talon Vantyrus]] or [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]].
