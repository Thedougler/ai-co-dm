# Session 11 — Torn Crossing ambush

## Scene ends when

The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] has a wolfrabbit and the remaining pack breaks off [[Aruhe - Torn Crossing]], and the crew can keep the upriver prints (hand to beat 4), or the crew breaks: back into the river and downstream (the hunt holds the bank), or a crew member takes open valley air (hawk sky). This is the fight. Someone can go down here. It is not a fight to the death with the channel cat, and it is not beat 4's fruit piles.

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Skip extra fruit-picking and extra rabbits. Show the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] already on a wolfrabbit, resolve the flushed jumps through the corridor, then either the hunt leaves this stretch or someone is in the river, in [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]], or being dragged, then land.

**If ahead:** Still end on hunt-leaving, break-to-river, or hawk sky. Do not start beat 4's clue bank or the hazards table. Inlined extra only if fully on this card: burning the grass on this stretch may scatter the pack and drive the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] off its kill, and it announces the crew down the valley. Attacking the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] makes the crew the meal. Picking living fruit on this stretch is an [[Taking on Aruhe]] claim (the later hazards-table roll uses `2d8`, keeping the lower result) and can pull more of the pack through the corridor.

## L0 · Glance

- **Stakes:** The crew has walked into a hunt already in motion. Someone can go down here.
- **Goal / exit:** Stay off the meal, let the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] keep its wolfrabbit, and keep the prints, or break to the river, or take hawk sky.
- **Danger:** Flushed [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit|wolfrabbits]] are already in the jump through this corridor. A miss or a knock-down puts someone in the current, into [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]], or in front of the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]].
- **Silence:** Present the hunt, then wait. If the crew freezes in the grass, tick the clock once at the end of the round.
- **Situation magnets:** Anyone isolated, bleeding, standing in the grass, loud, or striking the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]]. Flyers leave this hunt for hawk sky.

The crew is on [[Aruhe - Torn Crossing]], just off [[Aruhe - River Landing Bank]], standing on the flood-torn muddy corridor. The river is 10 feet at their backs. Three [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit]] are already in a 30-foot jump at mid-height through this corridor, flushed toward the crew. The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] is 20 feet off the path in the gold grass, already on a fourth striped body. A pale [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]] island stands 15 feet left in the scour. Two more pale stands sit 25 feet right in the grass. Bank-woods stand 40 feet inland. The upriver prints continue 30 feet beyond the leaping pack, then off this stretch toward the smoke. The smoke is a thin column on the horizon up-valley and does not get closer while this is happening. Wolfrabbit speed is 50 feet. Spiguar speed is 60 feet. A 30-foot move stays on this stretch: river, nearest razer island, or still in the grass. A Dash of 60 feet reaches the bank-woods or follows the prints off this stretch. A Dash does not reach the beach, the smoke, or the terraces. Crash-landed crew starts **Prone** until they stand.

## DM truth

The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] owns this channel and is hunting the [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit]] pack. The pack is already fleeing through the print corridor. The crew is not the chosen meal.

**Unrevealed:** The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] keeps a wolfrabbit kill unless a crew member is isolated, **Prone**, bleeding, loud, or hitting it. It will not chase far into deep water, onto [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]], or under open sky. The pack still hits whoever is in its escape lane. They will not chase far into deep water or the bank-woods. ### Action cards

**Loop.** The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] is already on a wolfrabbit. Flushed [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit|wolfrabbits]] use Pouncing Bound through the corridor. Tell: a thirty-foot silent launch from the grass, and a reed-matted clump that does not lean breaking open on a striped body.

**Pack of three [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit]] on Easy.** No Devour the Pack. Session stock allows three to five. Default Easy is three. Hard is five or six plus Devour the Pack and Frenzy Toward the Fallen when one drops (owner Hard is six). Handles once the table has seen the traits: Torn-ear, White-blaze, Kink-tail. Do not invent a fourth named rabbit. The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] already has one body; the three in the air are the rest of this Easy pack.

**Compact [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit]].** AC 15. Speed 50 feet. Bite +7 to hit, `14` piercing, reach 5 feet. Raking Claws +7 to hit, `12` slashing, reach 5 feet. Pouncing Bound: leap up to 30 feet, then one Raking Claws attack; if the leap began at least 20 feet away and hits, **Strength save — `DC 15`** or **Prone**; if the target remains standing, a 10-foot follow-up leap that does not provoke an opportunity attack from that target. Pack Rend: once per turn, Bite against a **Prone** creature deals extra `5 (1d10)` piercing if another [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit]] is within 5 feet of the target. Blood-Scented: advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to locate anyone below max HP.

**Compact [[Aruhe - Spiguar]].** AC 17. Speed 60 feet. Saber Bite +10 to hit, `19` piercing, reach 5 feet; Large or smaller is **Grappled** (escape `DC 18`). Claw +10 to hit, `17` slashing, reach 5 feet. Pounce: if it moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and hits with a Claw on the same turn, extra `10 (3d6)` slashing, then **Strength save — `DC 18`** or **Prone**; if **Prone**, one Saber Bite as a bonus action. Drag Through Grass: bonus action, move up to 30 feet dragging one **Grappled** creature, ignoring grass Difficult Terrain, no opportunity attacks from that creature. Grass Mantle: advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) in tall grass; Hide as a bonus action; while motionless, creatures more than 10 feet away have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to spot it. Reed-Silent Step: ignores grass Difficult Terrain; at half speed or slower it leaves no obvious crushed trail. It will not willingly rush [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]].

**Easy / Hard.** Easy: three [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit]] in the jump, no Devour the Pack, and the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] stays on its wolfrabbit unless a crew member is isolated, **Prone**, bleeding, loud, or hitting it. Hard: more rabbits, then Devour the Pack (`10` temporary hit points, Frenzied: speed +10 feet, Bite extra `3 (1d6)` piercing) and Frenzy Toward the Fallen (15-foot leap, no opportunity attacks) when one drops, and the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] treats the nearest isolated crew member as the next meal.

**Abort.** The pack aborts toward deep water and woods. They jump [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]] rather than land in it. The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] will not spend itself on long pursuit into deep water, bare stone, razer, or open sky. A knockdown can dump someone into the current or into a razer island.

**Compact [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]].** `1d4` slashing per 5 feet through an intact stand, Difficult Terrain. Careful Passage: one Action, 5 feet with no movement damage. Shatter: **Dexterity save — `DC 14`**, `2d6` slashing (half on success), if a creature falls **Prone** in it, Dashes through it, is forcibly moved at least 10 feet, or the section is smashed or burned. Mineral Dust: **Constitution save — `DC 14`** or **Glass-Choked** until a Short or Long Rest (speed −10 feet, disadvantage on Constitution saves against environmental hazards). Do not retype the whole hazard essay.

## Procedure

**Escape / survive-the-hunt** is the default. The crew can stay off the meal until the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] keeps its wolfrabbit and the pack leaves this stretch, or the crew can leave the grass. This is not a required TPK. Do not stand and kill the channel cat on Easy.

After everyone has acted, if anyone remains on the magnets (isolated, standing in the grass, bleeding, loud, or striking the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]]), advance the threat clock once and resolve that tick. Failures impose the Be ready for ruling only. Failures never advance the clock. Freeze or "we watch" still ticks once at the end of the round.

If a crew member takes open valley air, that crew member leaves this hunt for hawk sky. Point at a [[Aruhe - Young Bloodhawk]]. Do not restage beat 1 on this card.

**Combat mode:** if the party abandons escape and commits to killing the pack or the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]], stop the clock and run the embedded statblocks. Killing the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] is lethal for this band.

> [!narration] Spiguar
> A heavy spotted cat is already on a striped body 20 feet off the path, living grass and ferns piled over its back and neck. Pale saber teeth show against the dark muzzle, yellow eyes low, the whole shape flatter in the gold than a leopard should sit. The grass around it barely moves except where it has the kill.
