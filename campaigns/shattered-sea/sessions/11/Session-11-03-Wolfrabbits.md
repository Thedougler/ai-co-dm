# Session 11 — Torn Crossing hunt

![[aruhe-torn-crossing.jpg|Torn Crossing, flood-scoured river lip and pale razer-grass stands]]

## Scene ends when

The hunt leaves this stretch and the party can keep the upriver prints.

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Skip extra rabbits. Show the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] already on a wolfrabbit, resolve the flushed jumps, then either the hunt leaves or someone is in the river, in [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]], or being dragged.

**If ahead:** Burning the grass may scatter the pack and drive the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] off its kill, and it announces the party down the valley. Attacking the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] makes the party the meal. Picking living fruit on this stretch is a [[Taking on Aruhe]] claim and pulls another wolfrabbit.

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** The hunt is already in the air. Isolation is how a body becomes the meal. Someone can go down here.
- **Goal / exit:** Stay off the meal, let the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] keep its wolfrabbit, and keep the upriver prints for [[Session-11-04-What-They-Ate]].
- **Danger:** Three flushed [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit|wolfrabbits]] are already in a 30-foot jump through this corridor. A miss or a knock-down puts someone in the current, into [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]], or in front of the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]].
- **Silence:** Present the jump, then wait. If the party freezes in the grass, tick the clock once at the end of the round.
- **Situation magnets:** Anyone isolated, bleeding, standing in the grass, loud, or striking the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]].

## Now

The party stands on the flood-torn muddy corridor of [[Aruhe - Grasslands Torn Crossing]], facing north upriver. The river is 10 feet south at their backs and also runs along the west bank. Three [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit]] are already in a 30-foot jump at torso height through this corridor, flushed toward the party from the north. The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] is 20 feet east of the path in the gold grass, already on a fourth striped body. A pale [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]] island stands 15 feet west in the scour. Two more pale stands sit 25 feet east in the grass. Bank-woods stand 40 feet east. Living [[Aruhe - Redheart Berry|red berries]] hang on short thick stems at the wet margin, 10 feet south. The upriver prints bunch through this one corridor, bend around the white stands, and stay out of the cat's grass. Those prints continue north beyond the leaping bodies. A 30-foot move south reaches the river lip. The eight-foot grass is Difficult Terrain, so a 30-foot move through it covers 15 feet and does not reach the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] 20 feet east. A Dash of 60 feet through that grass covers 30 feet and does not reach the bank-woods 40 feet east. A Dash along the corridor still meets the leaping pack.

### Action cards

**Loop.** The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] is already on a wolfrabbit. Flushed [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit|wolfrabbits]] use Pouncing Bound through the corridor. Tell: a thirty-foot launch from the grass, and a reed-matted clump that does not lean breaking open on a striped body.

The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] owns this channel and is hunting the pack. The party is not the chosen meal. The flushed rabbits hit whoever is isolated, bleeding, or standing in their escape lane. The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] keeps its wolfrabbit unless a party member is isolated, **Prone**, bleeding, loud, or hitting it. Neither predator chases a body that reaches deep water south or west of this lip. The pack breaks contact among the first trunks 40 feet east. The pack will chase a runner who stays in the grass.

**Easy pack.** Three [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit]] in the jump. No Devour the Pack. Named bodies after the table sees the traits: Torn-ear, White-blaze, Kink-tail. Extra bodies stay unnamed. The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] already has one body.

**Hard pack.** Five or six [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit]], plus Devour the Pack and Frenzy Toward the Fallen when one drops. The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] treats the nearest isolated party member as the next meal.

**Compact [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit]].** AC 15. 68 hit points. Speed 50 feet. Pouncing Bound: leap up to 30 feet, then Raking Claws +7 to hit, `12` slashing; if the leap began at least 20 feet away and hits, **Strength save — `DC 15`** or **Prone**. Bite +7 to hit, `14` piercing. Pack Rend: extra `5 (1d10)` piercing on a **Prone** target if another [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit]] is within 5 feet.

**Compact [[Aruhe - Spiguar]].** AC 17. Speed 60 feet. Saber Bite +10 to hit, `19` piercing, **Grappled** (escape `DC 18`). Claw +10 to hit, `17` slashing. Pounce: 20-foot straight move then Claw hit, extra `10 (3d6)` slashing, **Strength save — `DC 18`** or **Prone**, then Saber Bite as a bonus action. Drag Through Grass: bonus action, 30 feet dragging a **Grappled** creature. Bloodied at 93 hit points: it Drags its current body into the bank-woods and breaks contact.

**Compact [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]].** `1d4` slashing per 5 feet through an intact stand, Difficult Terrain. Careful Passage: one Action, 5 feet, no movement damage. Shatter: **Dexterity save — `DC 14`**, `2d6` slashing. Mineral Dust: **Constitution save — `DC 14`** or **Glass-Choked**.

**Abort.** The pack jumps [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]] rather than land in it. The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] will not spend itself on long pursuit into deep water, onto [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]], or under open sky. A knockdown can dump someone into the current or into a razer island.

**Wet-margin berries.** Living stems at the south lip are [[Aruhe - Redheart Berry]]. Eating a fallen berry is a Bonus Action and regains `8d4 + 8` hit points. Plucking a living stem is a [[Taking on Aruhe]] claim and pulls an extra wolfrabbit.

## Procedure

**Escape mode.** After everyone has acted, if anyone remains on the magnets (isolated, standing in the grass, bleeding, loud, or striking the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]]), advance the threat clock once. Failures impose the Be ready for ruling only. Freeze still ticks once at the end of the round. Once the party leaves this hunting geometry, reaches deep water or the bank-woods, or stops presenting those magnets, the hunt breaks.

If a party member takes open valley air, that person leaves this hunt. Point at a [[Aruhe - Young Bloodhawk]].

**Combat mode:** if the party commits to killing the pack or the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]], stop the clock and run the embedded statblocks. Killing the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] is lethal for this band.

**Bloodied.** When one [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit]] dies, survivors frenzy on Hard (Devour the Pack and Frenzy Toward the Fallen). Easy skips Devour the Pack. The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] prefers a wolfrabbit corpse over a standing group. Spiguar bloodied is 93 hit points; if it takes that much, it Drags its current body into the bank-woods and breaks contact rather than standing in the open.

**Cover reached.** Deep water or the first trunks 40 feet east: the pack breaks contact with party members who actually reach those places. The pack still chases a runner who stays in the grass on this stretch. The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] breaks pursuit into deep water, onto [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]], or under open sky, and it drags a kill into the bank-woods to feed.

**Scene dials.** Easy and Hard are on the action cards.

## Secondary objective

The bunched prints are the survivor-safe lane: they skirt the white stands and stay out of the cat's grass. Reading that lane and staying in it keeps the trail without becoming the meal. Spreading into the gold grass or a white stand is how a body becomes isolated. If Be ready for is used to pull a dragged party member, that person is **Grappled** (escape `DC 18`) and 30 feet into the grass toward the bank-woods. Getting them free does not end the hunt.

## How the Scene Resolves

The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] keeps its wolfrabbit and the pack breaks off this stretch. The upriver prints still bunch through one corridor. Hand to [[Aruhe - River Line Bank]] and [[Session-11-04-What-They-Ate]]. Apply any [[Taking on Aruhe]] claim already made.

**Prone.** Anyone knocked down on this stretch is **Prone** until they stand.

**Grappled.** Anyone the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] dragged is **Grappled** (escape `DC 18`) until they break the hold.
