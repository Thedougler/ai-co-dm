# Session 11 — False help

![[attachments/shattered-sea/places/aruhe-spoke-ring.jpg|Spoke Ring, radial fire hub under Quiet leaves]]

## Scene ends when

The party stays grouped at the fire of [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]] and keeps anyone from walking a north trail alone, or the whole party answers the north-trail whisper until those voices die off, or one person walks that trail and that commit resolves, or the party breaks off this hub. This is the borrowed-voice ambush. It is not night watch, not [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], and not [[Talon Skarn]].

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Show this fire versus the north-trail whisper. Take one choice, then hand to [[Session-11-08-Night-Watch]].

**If ahead:** Still end on grouped-at-fire, voices-died, or the one-person commit. Do not run [[Session-11-08-Night-Watch]] on this card.

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** Ignoring the garden-voice means some people at this fire may walk a north trail alone. Isolation is how a body becomes prey. Mess here is cover for [[Talon Skarn]] on [[Session-11-09-Theft-on-the-Watch]].
- **Goal / exit:** Stay grouped at this fire and stop a peeler, answer as a group until the voices die, or break.
- **Danger:** One person on a north trail gets the face, then the hit. Two ready hostiles within 30 feet make the [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]] leave.
- **Silence:** Present this fire versus the north-trail whisper, then wait.
- **Situation magnets:** Anyone who walks a north trail alone. A survivor who believes her and steps off this hub.

## Now

The party is on the packed dirt hub of [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]]. Night has this fire. The stone ring sits at the center. Smoke hangs under the leaf roof. [[Matteo Scola]] sits 5 feet from the coals if they pulled him at the hole: his lip still split and wet, one boot missing, grey salt-stiff shirt still damp. If they left him in the hole, he is not here. Three or four other Calveno wreck people sit in this light. Garden mouths stand when the whisper comes. Fallen [[Aruhe - Stonepear]] sits by the coals. Living stonepears still hang. Two spears rest on the packed dirt south of the ring. Four woven mats lie 10 feet out. The south trail runs 20 feet to gold-green grass. The west trail runs 30 feet to the river through a root-cut. North trails run 20 feet into darker wood. The whisper comes from a north trail 20 feet off this hub. That trail holds no matching feet. Deeper wood is 50 feet north. [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]] is farther than a Dash. A 30-foot move stays on this hub or reaches the first north trail mouth. A Dash of 60 feet follows a trail off this ring. A Dash does not reach [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]], [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], the beach, or the terraces. The [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]] has speed 40 feet and climb 20 feet. Two ready hostiles within 30 feet scare it off.

### Action cards

**Cliffhanger.** This hub is a look-and-commit slice. The garden-voice is not a required fight. Do not attack at the start of this beat. Do not force a fight if the party stays grouped on this hub.

**One [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]].** It is not Famished on this card. Do not spawn a second. Do not put this creature on [[Session-11-09-Theft-on-the-Watch]].

**Compact numbers.** AC 15. Hit points 85. Claw +8 to hit, `12` slashing, reach 10 feet, and if the target is Medium or smaller the [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]] can Grapple it (escape `DC 16`) instead of dealing the slashing. Multiattack is two Claw attacks. The [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]] can Grapple only one creature at a time. Speed 40 feet, climb 20 feet.

**This Meal.** The quarry is whoever walks a north trail alone, or a survivor who believes her. The [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]] knows that quarry's direction within 1 mile. Once per turn, a hit on the quarry deals extra `7` (`2d6`) while fewer than two other conscious creatures are within 10 feet of that quarry.

**Voice.** The whisper comes from the [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]]'s space. A hearer knows the direction. The sound does not automatically reveal it if it is Hidden. This beat uses the garden words from the fire: come admire her garden, this way, leave the living plants. Loop a phrase, or stretch it. Nothing answers a shouted question with new information. The voice stays off the packed hub. After it uses a given sound without drawing a creature closer, it abandons that sound.

**Face.** One person in the north trail gets wrong movement, then "You're not welcome here," then the hit. That line is island law, not the woman. [[Aruhe - Hinewai]] is not in this trail tonight.

**Abort.** Two ready hostiles within 30 feet scare it off for now. The mark stays. It waits for a split. It will not walk [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]].

**Garden mouths.** Unnamed. They listened. They want the garden. They stand when the whisper comes. Sample: "She asked us to come admire her garden. This way. Leave the living plants."

**Ship mouths.** Unnamed. They call the listeners mad. They talk over a peel if someone holds this fire.

**[[Matteo Scola]].** If they pulled him at the hole and he could speak, the table has his name. If he is still **Unconscious**, call him the man until he wakes and says Matteo. He wants living people. He does not walk a north trail alone. If he was left in the hole, cut him from this card.

## Procedure

**Ambush.** After everyone has acted, if anyone remains isolated or a garden-mouth is still walking toward the voice unanswered, advance the threat clock once and resolve that tick. Failures impose the Be ready for ruling only. Failures never advance the clock. Freeze still ticks once at the end of the round because a garden-mouth remains standing or peeling.

When the party stays grouped at this fire and keeps anyone from walking a north trail alone, answers as a group, or breaks off this hub, the scene ends. If they stall, keep this fire versus the north-trail whisper visible, then ask.

One person walking a north trail resolves that commit in Be ready for. Do not also play that face as a later clock tick. Do not resolve Snatch from the Dim at the start of the walk.

If they already played along from [[Session-11-06-Farthest-Camp]], start from the North trail zone with this fire 20 feet south. A grouped answer from there still makes it break contact, lead them a little north, and die off.

If a forced march already put someone on a north trail, that person is already isolated. Use the one-person face row.

If beat 6 left this hub without the invitation, this card has no woman-lure. Do not fake the invitation.

If a party member takes open valley air, that party member leaves this hub for hawk sky. Do not restage beat 1 on this card.

**Combat mode:** if one person is in the north trail and the [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]] attacks, stop the clock and run the embedded statblock. If the whole party comes up ready, it leaves. Do not run [[Session-11-08-Night-Watch]]. Do not put this creature on [[Session-11-09-Theft-on-the-Watch]].

## Threat clock

The peel turn happens after everyone has acted, and only if anyone remains isolated or a garden-mouth is still walking toward the voice unanswered. Table failures do not also tick.

**Bloodied.** Bloodied is 42 hit points on the [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]]. This card has no extra bloodied action. Keep the isolated quarry.

**Cover reached.** Deeper wood, or two ready hostiles within 30 feet: the [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]] breaks contact. The mark stays. It waits for another split.

**Scene dials.** Easy: one [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]], not Famished. Do not spawn a second.

Do not spawn [[Talon Skarn]], night watch, or [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]] on this clock. Staying grouped while every garden-mouth remains on this dirt ends this scene with no further tick.

## Secondary objective

Stop a peeling survivor before tick 3. If the party ignores them, that person is lost into Quiet, and [[Session-11-08-Night-Watch]] is fewer than the mouths at this fire. Shouting, running survivors, or coals kicked over make this fire messy, and that mess is cover for [[Talon Skarn]] on [[Session-11-09-Theft-on-the-Watch]].

## How the Scene Resolves

They are back on this hub, or on the north trail short of [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], plus whoever is missing, marked, or convinced it was her, and a fire that may already be in disorder. They still need a watch. Hand to [[Session-11-08-Night-Watch]]. Do not run the watch posting here.

If the one-person commit resolved, apply any RAW damage and conditions already on that fight. Do not speak a finished Snatch or haul.

If the party broke off this hub, they are heading back toward the last grass, the river, the terrace line, the beach, or [[Uncertainty]], or a flyer is in hawk sky, plus any RAW conditions already applied. Rebuild from that. The mark can still sit on the locked quarry.

**Grappled.** Anyone still **Grappled** by the [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]] at a break is still **Grappled** (escape `DC 16`) until they escape or it drops them.

Do not run the hazards table, night watch, or [[Talon Skarn]] here. After this hub is used, do not roll the hazards table on this stretch again. This is the same [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]].
