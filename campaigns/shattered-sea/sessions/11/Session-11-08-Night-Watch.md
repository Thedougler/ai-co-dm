# Session 11 — Night watch

![[attachments/shattered-sea/places/aruhe-spoke-ring.jpg|Spoke Ring, radial fire hub under Quiet leaves]]

## Scene ends when

Watch terms are locked (fortified, paired, or thin; quiet or messy; [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] on the watch or not) and you can hand to [[Session-11-09-Theft-on-the-Watch]], or the party is already walking, or the party breaks. This is the watch-posting slice at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]]. It is not a fight, not [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], and not the theft.

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Ask who watches, where everyone stands, how [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] and the [[Fate Spinner]] are protected, and whether the fire is quiet or messy. Lock fortified, paired, or thin, then end.

**If ahead:** Still end on watch terms. [[Talon Skarn]] and the [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]] stay off this card. This valley does not reach [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]] tonight.

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** Isolation on this hub is how a body becomes prey. The watch they post is how [[Talon Skarn]] opens. A fortified watch still brings him. It changes who owns the opening.
- **Goal / exit:** Lock watch terms, then hand to [[Session-11-09-Theft-on-the-Watch]], or the party is already walking.
- **Danger:** A thin watch lets [[Talon Skarn]] start close, with the first chain already set. A messy fire is cover for him. Sending one person north is isolation.
- **Silence:** Present the fire, the garden talk, and the need for a watch, then wait.
- **Situation magnets:** Anyone left alone is a magnet. The marked person is a magnet if that person takes a north trail alone. [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] is a magnet if she stands a thin watch.

## Now

The party is on the packed dirt hub of [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]] unless beat 7 ended on a north trail, in which case they start 20 feet north of this fire. Night has this fire. The stone ring sits at the center. Four woven mats lie 10 feet out from those coals. [[Matteo Scola]] sits 5 feet from the coals if they pulled him: his lip still split and wet, one boot missing, grey salt-stiff shirt still damp. If they left him in the hole, he is not here. Unnamed wreck people sit in that light, fewer than the crushed prints that enter from the south, and fewer still if beat 7 lost a peeler. The fire is thinner or messier if beat 7 left it that way. Fallen [[Aruhe - Stonepear]] sits by the coals. Living stonepears still hang. Two spears rest on the packed dirt south of the ring. The south trail runs 20 feet to gold-green grass still showing between the last trunks. The west trail runs 30 feet to the river through a root-cut. North trails run 20 feet into darker wood. [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]] is the walk beyond that, farther than a Dash. A 30-foot move stays on this hub. A Dash of 60 feet follows a trail off this ring.

### Action cards

**Watch posting.** This is a Development. There is no fight on this card. Ask what they actually do, where everyone stands, how [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] and the [[Fate Spinner]] are protected, what is visible, and what deception or contingency they prepare. This beat does not collapse into one set-watch check.

**Watch states.** Fortified means they establish positions, lines of sight, and contingencies before [[Talon Skarn]] enters. He still comes. He must use a visible feint, a decoy approach, or an open entry, and he must expose himself enough to make one genuine attempt. Paired means opposed detection and a roughly even opening. Thin means [[Talon Skarn]] starts close, the first chain can already be set up, and the party reacts from disadvantage. Also record quiet or messy, and whether [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] is on the watch.

**[[Talon Skarn]] wants the carrier on a thin watch.** He does not appear on this card. The sun is gone. An excellent watch does not cancel [[Session-11-09-Theft-on-the-Watch]].

**[[Fate Spinner]].** A thumb-sized quartz top on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]. Ask which pouch, wrap, or hand holds it, and whether that hold is in view.

**[[Aruhe - Deerstalker]] waits for another split.** It does not appear on this card. It does not appear on [[Session-11-09-Theft-on-the-Watch]].

**[[Matteo Scola]].** If they pulled him at the hole and he could speak, the table has his name. If he is still **Unconscious**, call him the man until he wakes and says Matteo. He wants living people. He does not walk a north trail alone. If he was left in the hole, cut him from this card.

**Garden mouths.** They are unnamed. They listened. They want the garden. The talk is worse now, because they all heard the same invitation. Sample: "She asked us to come admire her garden. This way. Leave the living plants."

**Ship mouths.** They are unnamed. They call the listeners mad. They talk over a peel if someone holds this fire.

**[[Taking on Aruhe]].** Fallen [[Aruhe - Stonepear]] is receiving. Plucking a living plant is a claim. This camp notices, and Influence at this fire has Disadvantage. Surrounding life does not arrive on this card.

**Fallen [[Aruhe - Stonepear]].** **Intelligence (Nature) — identify.** `DC 10` → edible, not poison. `DC 15` or *Identify* → eating one is a Bonus Action and grants **Resistance to all damage** for 1 minute.

> [!narration] Initial Narration
> You see the same river-stone coals still burning on this packed dirt. Night has this fire. Smoke still pools into the leaf roof, and no stars show through those leaves. Heat comes off the stones. The people in this light keep the garden words going, worse now: admire her garden, this way, do not take the living plants. Some look north whenever a branch moves.
>
> Fallen stonepears still sit by the coals, grey-green plates chipped pale, and two spears still lie on the dirt south of the ring. Mats sit about ten feet out, some kicked crooked. You smell woodsmoke trapped under the leaves. You feel packed dirt hard underfoot.
>
> North, packed trails go darker between the big roots. A body that walks one of those trails alone leaves this fire behind.
>
> What do you do?

## Procedure

**Development.** This hub is a watch-posting slice. There is no fight on this card. When the party posts a fortified, paired, or thin watch, sits awake in a knot, or starts walking, the scene ends. If they stall, keep the need for a watch visible, then ask.

Failures impose the Be ready for ruling only. There is no threat clock on this card.

If beat 7 left them on a north trail, start 20 feet north of this fire. A grouped return still needs a watch on this hub.

If they force a march tonight, beat 9 is the column. Beat 9 does not start at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]].

If they all sit awake in a knot, [[Talon Skarn]] still comes, and a messy camp is his cover. Hand to beat 9 at this fire.

If they send the marked person to [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]] alone, the nearest watcher is already there, and isolation is worse. Hand to beat 9 as Thin.

If a party member takes open valley air, that party member leaves this hub for hawk sky. This is not the bloodhawk stoop.

**Combat mode:** if someone attacks this camp, stop looking and rebuild. Lose the prize. They still need a watch.
