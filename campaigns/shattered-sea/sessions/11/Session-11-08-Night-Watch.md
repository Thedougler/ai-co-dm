# Session 11 — Night watch

![[aruhe-spoke-ring.jpg|Spoke Ring, radial fire hub under Quiet leaves]]

## Scene ends when

Watch terms are locked (fortified, paired, or thin; quiet or messy; [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] on the watch or not) and you can hand to [[Session-11-09-Theft-on-the-Watch]], or the party is already walking, or the party breaks. This is the watch-posting slice at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]]. It is not a fight, not [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], and not the theft.

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Ask who watches, where everyone stands, how [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] and the [[Fate Spinner]] are protected, and whether the fire is quiet or messy. Lock fortified, paired, or thin, then end.

**If ahead:** Still end on watch terms. [[Talon Skarn]] and the [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]] stay off this card. This valley does not reach [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]] tonight.

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** Isolation on this hub is how a body becomes prey. The watch they post is how [[Talon Skarn]] opens.
- **Goal / exit:** Lock watch terms, then hand to [[Session-11-09-Theft-on-the-Watch]], or the party is already walking.
- **Danger:** A thin watch lets [[Talon Skarn]] start close, with the first chain already set. A messy fire is cover for him. Sending one person north is isolation.
- **Silence:** Present the fire, the garden talk, and the need for a watch, then wait.
- **Situation magnets:** Anyone left alone is a magnet. The marked person is a magnet if that person takes a north trail alone. [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] is a magnet if she stands a thin watch.

## Now

The party is on the packed dirt hub of [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]]. Night has this fire. The stone ring sits at the center. Four woven mats lie 10 feet out from those coals. Unnamed wreck people sit in that light, fewer than the crushed prints that enter from the south. Fallen [[Aruhe - Stonepear]] sits by the coals. Living stonepears still hang. Two spears rest on the packed dirt south of the ring. The south trail runs 20 feet to gold-green grass still showing between the last trunks. The west trail runs 30 feet to the river through a root-cut. North trails run 20 feet into darker wood. [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]] is the walk beyond that, farther than a Dash. A 30-foot move stays on this hub. A Dash of 60 feet follows a trail off this ring. The fire is thinner or messier if beat 7 left it that way.

### Action cards

**Watch posting.** This is a Development. There is no fight on this card. Ask what they actually do, where everyone stands, how [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] and the [[Fate Spinner]] are protected, what is visible, and what deception or contingency they prepare. This beat does not collapse into one set-watch check.

**Watch states.** Fortified means they establish positions, lines of sight, and contingencies before [[Talon Skarn]] enters. Paired means opposed detection and a roughly even opening. Thin means [[Talon Skarn]] starts close, the first chain can already be set up, and the party reacts from disadvantage. Also record quiet or messy, and whether [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] is on the watch.

**[[Talon Skarn]] wants the carrier on a thin watch.** He does not appear on this card. The sun is gone.

**[[Aruhe - Deerstalker]] waits for another split.** It does not appear on this card. It does not appear on [[Session-11-09-Theft-on-the-Watch]].

**Garden mouths.** Unnamed. They listened. They want the garden. Sample: "She asked us to come admire her garden. This way. Leave the living plants."

**Ship mouths.** Unnamed. They call the listeners mad. They talk over a peel if someone holds this fire.

**[[Taking on Aruhe]].** Fallen [[Aruhe - Stonepear]] is receiving. Plucking a living plant is a claim. This camp notices, and Influence at this fire has Disadvantage. Surrounding life does not arrive on this card.

**Fallen [[Aruhe - Stonepear]].** **Intelligence (Nature) — identify.** `DC 10` → edible, not poison. `DC 15` or *Identify* → eating one is a Bonus Action and grants **Resistance to all damage** for 1 minute.

> [!narration] Initial Narration
> You see a round packed clearing under a tight leaf roof, wide enough for a fire. Wet river stones ring live coals at the center, and the smoke pools up into those leaves instead of rising. Heat comes off the stones. Four woven mats sit about ten feet out around that ring, some kicked crooked, and two spears lie on the dirt south of the coals.
>
> You hear the people in this light talking in broken pieces: admire the garden, this way, do not take the living plants. Fewer of them sit here than the crushed prints coming in from the south. No woman stands with them. Fallen stonepears rest by the coals, grey-green plates chipped pale, and living ones still hang from mossed branches, each a heavy pear of fitted stone scales. You smell woodsmoke trapped under the leaves. You feel the packed dirt hard underfoot.
>
> South, gold-green grass still shows between the last trunks. West, clear water talks through hanging roots, cool and close. North, packed trails go darker between buttress roots, and drip off leaf is the only small sound from that dark.
>
> What do you do?

## Procedure

**Development.** This hub is a watch-posting slice. There is no fight on this card. When the party posts a fortified, paired, or thin watch, sits awake in a knot, or starts walking, the scene ends. If they stall, keep the need for a watch visible, then ask.

Failures impose the Be ready for ruling only. There is no threat clock on this card.

If they force a march tonight, beat 9 is the column. Beat 9 does not start at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]].

If they all sit awake in a knot, [[Talon Skarn]] still comes, and a messy camp is his cover. Hand to beat 9 at this fire.

If they send the marked person to [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]] alone, the nearest watcher is already there, and isolation is worse. Hand to beat 9 as Thin.

If a party member takes open valley air, that party member leaves this hub for hawk sky. This is not the bloodhawk stoop.

**Combat mode:** if someone attacks this camp, stop looking and rebuild. Lose the prize. They still need a watch.

## Secondary objective

Keep the [[Fate Spinner]] hidden on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] while the watch is posted. Beats required: a pouch, wrap, or hand that is not in view before anyone sleeps. Ignore outcome: watch terms still lock. Later consequence: if the [[Fate Spinner]] is in view, [[Talon Skarn]] can read a target on her when beat 9 starts.

## How the Scene Resolves

If they fortify, pair, or thin the watch, they post that watch at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]], plus any RAW damage and conditions already applied. Record quiet or messy, and whether [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] is on it. Hand to [[Session-11-09-Theft-on-the-Watch]]. The sun is gone.

If they sit awake in a knot, they remain on this hub. [[Talon Skarn]] still comes. A messy camp is his cover. Hand to beat 9 at this fire.

If they force a march tonight, they are already walking, still the carrier, plus any RAW conditions already applied. Beat 9 is the column.

If they chased the garden tonight, they are still in this valley, committed past this hub. They take a north trail to [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]]. This is not [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]]. Hand to beat 9.

If the party left this camp, they are heading back toward the last grass, the river, the terrace line, the beach, or [[Uncertainty]], or a flyer is in hawk sky, plus any RAW conditions already applied. Rebuild from that.
