# Session 11 — Farthest camp

## Scene ends when

The split at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]] is on the table and the crew has a live choice (stay the night, try to extract, or play along toward a garden they will not reach tonight), then hand to beat 9's second-night watch at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]], or the crew breaks (forced march, attack, or leave). This is not [[Aruhe - Grasslands - Print Braid]], not [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], and not [[Talon Skarn]].

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Present this ring and the two stories, then ask.

**If ahead:** Still end on night coming and watch setup. Do not run [[Talon Skarn]] on this card. Do not reach [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]].

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** Some of these people want rescue. This camp wants a garden. They do not all want [[Uncertainty]].
- **Goal / exit:** Get the split on the table, then hand to beat 9, or break.
- **This ring:** A packed dirt hub sits under Quiet leaves, with a live stone fire, four sleeping mats, fallen [[Aruhe - Stonepear]], living stonepears still hanging, south grass-light, west river talk, and north Quiet spokes.
- **Claim:** Fallen stonepears are already down. Plucking a living plant is a [[Taking on Aruhe]] claim.
- **Silence:** Present this ring and the two stories, then wait.

The crew is on [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]], at the packed dirt hub. The smoke is this fire. The stone ring sits at the center. Four sleeping mats lie 10 feet out from those coals. Several people from the Calveno wreck sit in that light, fewer than the crushed prints entering from the south. Fallen stonepears are down by the fire. Living stonepears still hang from mossed branches over the ring. The south spoke runs 20 feet to gold-green grass still showing between the last trunks, then back toward [[Aruhe - Grasslands - Print Braid]]. The west spoke runs 30 feet to the river through a root-cut. North spokes run 20 feet into darker Quiet. A 30-foot move stays on this hub. A Dash of 60 feet follows a spoke off this ring. A Dash does not reach the garden, the beach, or [[Uncertainty]].

## DM truth

This camp wants to see the woman's garden. The woman is [[Aruhe - Hinewai]] / Hinewai. She is courteous to guests. She is drawing these people inland toward [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], which is still far beyond tonight. These people survived because they followed her rules: fallen fruit, leave the living plants. They are not charmed in a spell sense. They are caught on the invitation.

**Unrevealed:** Her name, the graves, and how far the garden is. Do not put those in the Open. Do not name [[Aruhe - Hinewai]], Hinewai, or [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]] at the table.

[[Talon Skarn]] does not care about the argument. Do not run him on this card.

### Action cards

**This ring.** Packed dirt around the fire is ordinary ground. Wood beyond the trail mouths is Difficult Terrain and heavily obscures beyond 10 feet. Leaf roof holds the smoke as a haze.

**Camp split.** Two stories are already in the talk. One story: a woman in the woods has been whispering, asking them to come admire her garden, telling them where to walk and what not to take. The other story: she is leading them deeper into danger, and anyone who listens is mad. This camp listened. They are alive. They want to see the garden. People who already left this fire, or who argue inside it, hold the second story. Do not invent named survivors.

**Invitation.** The people at this fire look into the spokes when the branches move. [[Aruhe - Hinewai]] is not standing at this fire as a named body. Do not stage [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]]. Do not treat this as a charm spell.

**[[Taking on Aruhe]] practice.** Already-picked [[Aruhe - Stonepear]] is down by this fire. Living stonepears around this ring still hang. Taking only fruit already down is not a claim. Plucking living plants at this fire is a claim. On that claim, do not roll the hazards table now. The later [[Aruhe-River-Grasslands-Tables]] roll is `2d8`, keeping the lower result.

**Fallen [[Aruhe - Stonepear]].** Eating one is a Bonus Action and grants **Resistance to all damage** for 1 minute.

**Attack is a break.** If someone attacks this camp, stop looking, lose the prize, and rebuild. Do not invent a statblock. Do not embed a roster. They still have a night watch.

The other says she is leading them into danger, and anyone who listens is mad.
>
> South, the packed prints come in from gold-green grass still showing between the last trunks. West, water talks through a root-cut, cool and close. North, the spokes darken into Quiet with no insect-saw, and the garden talk points that way. A branch stirs, and the people here look into the dark between the trunks while the talk thins.
>
> What do you do?

## Procedure

**Development.** This ring is a look-and-commit slice. There is no fight on this card. When the crew stays the night, plays along, extracts and waits until morning, or breaks, the scene ends. If they stall, keep the two stories visible, then ask. If they race, still end on that commit or that break. Do not add a deer-stalker. Do not roll the hazards table here. Do not run [[Talon Skarn]].

If a crew member takes open valley air, that crew member leaves this ring for hawk sky. Do not restage beat 1 on this card.

**Combat mode:** if someone attacks this camp, stop looking and rebuild. Lose the prize. Do not invent a statblock. They still have a night watch.

## Landing

If the crew stays the night, they leave this hub's leaf roof and post the second-night watch at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]]. Hand to beat 9. Do not run [[Talon Skarn]] here.

If the crew plays along toward the garden, they are still in this valley tonight, committed past Spoke Ring. They take the north spoke to [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]]. Do not reach [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]]. Hand to beat 9's second-night watch.

If Persuasion peeled some mouths toward [[Uncertainty]] and the crew waits until morning, they still post tonight's watch at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]]. Hand to beat 9.

If the crew forced a march tonight, some of the people at this fire balked or slipped into the north spokes, plus any RAW conditions already applied. Rebuild from that.

If the crew attacked this camp, the prize is gone. They still need a night watch. Rebuild from that. Do not invent a statblock.

If the crew left this camp, they are heading back toward [[Aruhe - Grasslands - Print Braid]], the river, the terrace line, the beach, or [[Uncertainty]], or a flyer is in hawk sky, plus any RAW conditions already applied. Rebuild from that. The mark can still sit on the locked quarry.
