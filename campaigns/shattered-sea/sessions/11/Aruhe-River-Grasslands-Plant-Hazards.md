# Aruhe River / Grasslands Plant Hazards

Paired with [[Aruhe-River-Grasslands-Tables]] for [[campaigns/shattered-sea/sessions/11/Session-11-Angry-Birds]]. Each Survival check rolls both tables. Run one row. No Grung. Do not restage beat 9 ([[Talon Skarn]]) or pull [[The Blight]].

## L0 · At a glance

- **Where:** The table is on a [[Grasslands - River Valleys]] bank, in the gold grass, at a razer crossing, or on the tree line that follows the river.
- **Party band:** Five PCs are level 5 and fight as 6. Tune to that band, not to a printed CR chart.
- **Hardest / easiest:** A roll of 1 is the hardest row. A roll of 8 is the easiest.
- **Spine:** The night still wants smoke, river, survivors, the woman in the woods, and [[Talon Skarn]]. Come back to that chart when the extra scene ends.

> [!mechanic] When to roll
> When the crew navigates a new stretch of grassland or river, they make a Wisdom (Survival) check against DC 13.
> They need 3 successes to reach the next landmark: landing to smoke, or smoke to camp.
> Failures do not count toward that 3. Use 2 successes if the night is short. Use 4 if they leave the water.
> Each check, pass or fail, roll 1d8 on this table and 1d8 on [[Aruhe-River-Grasslands-Tables]].
> Run exactly one of those two rows. Pick the row that matches the ground in front of them.
> A success means they see the chosen row coming and can go around, engage, or pay a cost and keep walking.
> A failure means the same row, already in reach or already in the stand.
> If they pluck living plants, fish for themselves, or kill to carry flesh, both rolls are 2d8 and you keep the lower number on each table, then still pick one row. Fallen fruit, passage, and free-flowing water are not a claim. See [[Aruhe Hunger Rule]].
> If a row would restage a live spine fight, use the other table's row. If both collide, reroll once among unused rows.

## L1 · At the table

### 1. Strangler Fig at a stone pinch — [[Strangler Fig]]

**When live:** This row is live on a roll of 1 when the DM picks the plant die, on a tree-line trail already pinched through stone.
**Now:** Aerial roots hang across a bottleneck. The trunk in that pinch is hollow.
**Say/show:** Read the narration. The tree does not walk. The trail does.
**Ask/offer:** Leave the pinch and take the long grass. Clear a root curtain. Walk under.
**Opposition/pressure:** The fig wants one body in the hollow trunk. It cannot chase.
**Resolve:** Going around costs time on the smoke. Staying in the pinch puts them in Root Lash range. Ending the scene by leaving the pinch counts as success.
**On success/partial/failure:** Success means they are past on the long way, or the curtain is cleared and no one is held. Partial means a grapple they break on the trail. Failure means someone is in the trunk, or the pinch is closed and the smoke trail is lost for this hour.
**If ignored:** The curtain hangs. The next traveler on this trail feeds it.
**Move on to:** The upriver prints and smoke on [[campaigns/shattered-sea/sessions/11/Session-11-Angry-Birds]], or the next Survival check if they keep walking.

> [!narration] Narration
> Curtains of hanging roots close a game trail where stone pinches the tree line. A broad trunk stands in that pinch, hollow at chest height, with pale bone showing in the bark. The roots hang still. Grass and river sit behind you. The trail ahead is the curtain.

### Run now

- **On the table:** One [[Strangler Fig]] fills a stone-pinched tree-line trail. Gold grass and the river are the long way around. Upriver smoke is still visible if anyone looks that way.
- **DM truth:**
> [!secret] Hidden
> The fig is rooted. Clearing the curtain or abandoning the trail ends the hunt. Escape DC 16 is on the owner Root Lash. An ally adjacent to the trunk can pull an engulfed creature free with the same DC 16 check. Fire is the strong answer and not the only one. [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] and [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] are the engulf risk. [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] can work from outside the pinch.
- **Procedure:** Show the curtain and the bones first. Root Lash starts when someone stays in the pinch. Do not start with a body already in the trunk.
- **If violence starts:** The fig uses two Root Lashes, then Hollow-Trunk Engulf if a grapple holds. Trail-Pinch (Recharge 5–6) closes a line if they try to slip past. It cannot retreat. If all grapples end and the curtain is cleared, they can pass.
- **If ignored:** The pinch stays closed. The long way is still open.

**Spotlight:** [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] on the pinch and the long way. [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] if he stays in the curtain. [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] from outside the stone.

### 2. Young Snakewood on bank canopy — [[Young Snakewood]]

**When live:** This row is live on a roll of 2 when the DM picks the plant die, along a bank or orchard edge with connected low branches.
**Now:** Ordinary-looking vines over the bank are drawing into a wedge.
**Say/show:** Read the narration. One jaw. One bundle. The river and open grass are off the wood.
**Ask/offer:** Step off the connected vegetation. Break the visible bundle. Walk under the low canopy.
**Opposition/pressure:** The young plant wants one body hauled into the low branches. It cannot chase across a clean gap.
**Resolve:** Leaving the connected wood ends the haul. Staying under it puts them in 30-foot Snatching Jaws range.
**On success/partial/failure:** Success means they are off the wood and the wedge goes still. Partial means a grab they break by killing the bundle. Failure means someone is hanging in the low canopy.
**If ignored:** The wedge waits on this bank. The next body on the connected wood is the meal.
**Move on to:** The edge-trail and smoke once they leave the wood, or the next Survival check.

> [!narration] Narration
> Low branches over the bank wear a mass of dull green-brown vines, thick as an arm where they twist. Five or six stems draw together into a wedge, and tiny pale flowers show along the inner edge. The low canopy shakes. The river and the open grass sit off that connected wood.

### Run now

- **On the table:** One [[Young Snakewood]] holds a bank-canopy lane. The river and open grass are clean gaps. Do not use adult reach, sap, or Reflexive Snare.
- **DM truth:**
> [!secret] Hidden
> One target. Bundle AC 14, 10 hit points. Escape DC 14 is on the owner page. [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] taking sky leaves this connected vegetation and enters hawk sky. [[Delmar Fisk]] in rapier range can cut the bundle. [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] can spot the tightening stems.
- **Procedure:** Show the wedge and the flowers before the clap. Snatching Jaws starts if they stay under the wood. Do not start with a body already aloft.
- **If violence starts:** Round one is one Snatching Jaws. Round two is Jaws plus Constrict or Reel if the bundle holds. The plant has no off-turn attack. Destroying the bundle ends that grapple.
- **If ignored:** It returns to looking like ordinary vine cover on this bank.

**Spotlight:** [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] on the stems. [[Delmar Fisk]] if he closes. [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] if she leaves the wood for sky.

### 3. Vine Lash over a bank trail — [[Vine Lash]]

**When live:** This row is live on a roll of 3 when the DM picks the plant die, on a tree-lined bank trail with stone or trunks for an anchor.
**Now:** Shoulder-thick leafless hangers drape the trail like rope.
**Say/show:** Read the narration. These squeeze on the ground. They do not haul into the canopy.
**Ask/offer:** Walk around the hanging bundle. Burn it. Step under.
**Opposition/pressure:** The vine wants a body held under the canopy long enough to feed through the roots. Fire makes it recoil.
**Resolve:** Leaving the trail lane skips the grab. Walking under puts them in 15-foot Tendril range.
**On success/partial/failure:** Success means they are off the lane or the bundles are broken. Partial means a grapple they cut free. Failure means someone is constricted on the trail, and the upriver prints are on the other side of the hangers.
**If ignored:** The hangers wait. The next walker on this lane is the meal.
**Move on to:** Upriver tracks and smoke, or the next Survival check.

> [!narration] Narration
> Shoulder-thick leafless hangers drape the bank trail like ordinary rope. Five or six of them hang together from the trees in one bundle. The rest of the vines do not move. The bundle hangs over the most natural-looking line along the water.

### Run now

- **On the table:** One [[Vine Lash]] hangs over a tree-lined bank trail. The river and open grass sit off that lane. This is not a [[Young Snakewood]].
- **DM truth:**
> [!secret] Hidden
> Two grapples. Bundle AC 12, 8 hit points per grasping bundle. Escape DC 12 is on the owner page. Fire vulnerability is the recoil. [[Delmar Fisk]] can cut a bundle in melee. [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] is a grab target on the trail. Do not place a second vine on the same target.
- **Procedure:** False Appearance until they enter the lane. First round is two Tendrils. Next round is one Tendril plus Constrict if a grapple holds.
- **If violence starts:** The vine wraps and squeezes. Attacking a grasping bundle (AC 12, 8 hit points) ends that grapple without needing the escape check. If both bundles break or the trail burns, it withdraws toward its root mass at 10 feet per round.
- **If ignored:** It stays rope. The lane stays a problem.

**Spotlight:** [[Delmar Fisk]] on the bundle. [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] if he walks the lane. [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] from off the trail.

### 4. Razer-Grass shatter crossing — [[Razer-Grass]]

**When live:** This row is live on a roll of 4 when the DM picks the plant die, at a torn crossing, wallow, or flood-scoured bend. Do not add a [[Thornback]].
**Now:** A winter-white island sits in the short ford. The glitter does not lean.
**Say/show:** Read the narration. The grass is the encounter. Fire, a Dash, a fall, or a smash is the fuse.
**Ask/offer:** Go around the island. Careful Passage. Soak it. Cross fast. Burn it.
**Opposition/pressure:** Intact blades cut. A shatter sprays glass. Burning it shatters the stand on purpose.
**Resolve:** Going around costs time on the smoke. Careful Passage is an action for 5 feet without movement damage. Crossing at speed pays 1d4 slashing per 5 feet, and a fall or Dash can Shatter.
**On success/partial/failure:** Success means they are past without a Shatter. Partial means cuts and they still hold the far bank. Failure means a Shatter, and someone is Glass-Choked or down in the white.
**If ignored:** The island stays. Damage here makes the next growth thicker.
**Move on to:** The long way around, then the spine, or the next Survival check.

> [!narration] Narration
> A waist-high island of winter-white blades sits in the torn crossing. Wind moves the gold grass around it. The white sparkles stay fixed. The short ford runs through that island. A thin glass sound comes off the stand when the wind hits.

### Run now

- **On the table:** Discrete [[Razer-Grass]] islands close a torn river crossing. No animal is in this row. Gold grass and a longer bend are the way around.
- **DM truth:**
> [!secret] Hidden
> Razor Field is 1d4 slashing per 5 feet through intact stands, no save. Careful Passage is an action: 5 feet with no movement damage. Shatter is DC 14 Dexterity, 7 (2d6) slashing, if someone falls prone in it, Dashes, is forced 10 feet, or smashes a section. Fire automatically Shatters that section and starts Glass Bloom. Glass-Choked is DC 14 Constitution, speed −10 feet, until a Short or Long Rest. [[Delmar Fisk]] pays if he Dashes the ford. [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] can work from outside the islands. [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] can take Careful Passage or the long way.
- **Procedure:** Show the fixed glitter and the glass sound before contact. Do not start with a Shatter already in the air.
- **If violence starts:** There is no creature to fight. Forced movement into the stand is the nasty combination if a later animal row is still live. They can weaponize the island against a pursuer that will not enter.
- **If ignored:** The crossing stays white. Later growth here is thicker.

**Spotlight:** [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] on the long way or Careful Passage. [[Delmar Fisk]] if he takes the ford at speed. [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] from outside the white.

### 5. Hot bloom on the wet margin — [[Grubnade]]

**When live:** This row is live on a roll of 5 when the DM picks the plant die, on a wet-margin or terrace-edge flower that ticks.
**Now:** A huge flower is open on the bank. Caterpillars chew in the throat. The bloom ticks.
**Say/show:** Read the narration. Predators already walk around it. Cute first.
**Ask/offer:** Walk wide of the flower. Leave it. Touch it. Shake it. Burn it. Pluck a grub.
**Opposition/pressure:** A disturbed grub swells and pops at the end of the current turn. A hot bloom holds several. One pop can disturb the next.
**Resolve:** Walking around costs a little time and no fight. Touch, damage, fire, thunder, or shaking the plant disturbs every mature grub on that flower.
**On success/partial/failure:** Success means they leave the bloom and remember the tick. Partial means one pop, they get clear, juveniles scatter into the leaves. Failure means a chain in the 5-foot flashes, and the flower is a scorch ring.
**If ignored:** The bloom keeps ticking. The next creature that shoves the plant pays.
**Move on to:** The bank prints around the flower, then the spine.

> [!narration] Narration
> A huge flower stands on the wet bank, open, with cream-green caterpillars longer than a thumb feeding in the throat. The bloom ticks. Five-foot scorch rings mark the mud around it. Animal tracks bend wide of the flower.

### Run now

- **On the table:** One hot bloom holds three or more mature [[Grubnade]]. Scorch rings and bent tracks are already on the mud. The river and the long grass are the walk-around.
- **DM truth:**
> [!secret] Hidden
> Harmless until touched. Disturbed means it detonates at the end of the current turn. Chemical Burst is DC 12 Dexterity, 7 (2d6) fire, 5-foot flash. Blast-Birth hurls 1d4 + 1 juveniles that cannot detonate. Normal footsteps nearby do not trigger it. DC 12 Perception notices a grub in dense foliage if the tick is not already obvious. [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] may pick the cute one. [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] can throw a plucked grub (improvised, 20/60) and that pluck disturbs it. Learned predators on the fauna table already refuse these flowers.
- **Procedure:** Make it cute first. Show the tick and the bent tracks. The delay to the end of the turn is the scene. After one pop, no check to remember.
- **If violence starts:** There is no attack routine. Fire, a shove, or a plucked grub is the disturbance. After the flash, juveniles are in the leaves and the bloom is spent.
- **If ignored:** The flower keeps ticking. Tracks still bend around it.

**Spotlight:** [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] if he reaches for a grub. [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] if someone treats it as a thrown weapon. [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] on the bent tracks.

### 6. Eight-foot gold-grass corridor

**When live:** This row is live on a roll of 6 when the DM picks the plant die, off the water in the gold field.
**Now:** Blades stand as high as a person. A crushed lane already cuts inland.
**Say/show:** Read the narration. Near the water the grass leans. A body moving through leaves a corridor that lasts for hours.
**Ask/offer:** Follow the existing crush. Cut a new lane. Keep to the leaning grass at the water. Climb or fly above it.
**Opposition/pressure:** The field hides a standing person and swallows landmarks. A new crush announces them down the valley. Fighting or Dashing here widens the cut.
**Resolve:** A Wisdom (Survival) check against DC 13 keeps the smoke and the river in relation while they move. Failure on that check is this row's blunder: they lose the landmark for this stretch and still leave a corridor.
**On success/partial/failure:** Success means they follow water or an old crush and the smoke stays in view. Partial means they keep the landmark and leave a loud new cut. Failure means the smoke is gone for this hour, and the corridor they made is the next thing anything follows.
**If ignored:** The old crush stays. Later fauna can use it as a charge line.
**Move on to:** The river or the old crush, then the spine.

> [!narration] Narration
> Gold-green blades stand as high as a person and close over the river behind you. Near the water the grass leans with the wind. A crushed corridor already cuts inland, the stems broken and slow to rise. The smoke is a smudge above the blades if anyone looks up.

### Run now

- **On the table:** Eight-foot gold grass with a lasting crushed corridor, leaning grass at the water, and smoke above the blades. This row is not an animal. Grass that does not lean is fauna, not this card.
- **DM truth:**
> [!secret] Hidden
> The crush lasts hours and is a later roll's highway. [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] is the likely reader of lean versus crush. [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] above the blades can see smoke and also enters hawk sky. Burning the grass announces them down the valley; treat that as lingering and a claim-style later roll if they stay. Difficult terrain in the intact stands.
- **Procedure:** Show the lean, the old crush, and the smoke smudge. Do not spawn a creature on this card. If they fight here anyway, they widen the corridor.
- **If violence starts:** There is no plant to kill. Forced movement and lost sightlines are the cost. A later fauna row can use the cut they just made.
- **If ignored:** The corridor remains. The smoke thins while they stand in it.

**Spotlight:** [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] on lean versus crush. [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] if she takes the air. [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] if they try to burn a lane.

### 7. Breathmelon in the current — [[Breathmelon]]

**When live:** This row is live on a roll of 7 when the DM picks the plant die, at a wet margin or slack shallows.
**Now:** Pale green melons bob in the water. Some still hang on living vines. One is already split and floating.
**Say/show:** Read the narration. Living fruit on the vine is a claim. The split one is already down.
**Ask/offer:** Take the cut melon. Leave them. Pluck a living one. Squeeze one underwater.
**Opposition/pressure:** A plucked living melon is a Hunger Rule claim and both later dice go 2d8 take lower. Underwater, a squeezed melon leaks trapped gas and is a loud tell in a claimed hole.
**Resolve:** Receiving the already-split fruit is passage. Identification is DC 13 Nature or Medicine on the owner page if they spend a minute. Eating is a Bonus Action: one hour of air and water breathing, swim speed equal to walking speed.
**On success/partial/failure:** Success means they take only what is already loose and move on. Partial means they learn the fruit works and they also made noise in the shallows. Failure means they plucked living fruit, and the next Survival check uses the claim rule.
**If ignored:** The melons keep bobbing. The split one drifts downstream.
**Move on to:** The water road or the bank, then the spine.

> [!narration] Narration
> Pale green melons bob in the shallows, rinds dimpled like lungs. One thumps hollow against a submerged log. Ribbons of underwater grass stream past it. A split melon already floats cut-side up, air-cells squeaking in the current. Whole fruit still hangs on the wet-margin vines.

### Run now

- **On the table:** Living [[Breathmelon]] on wet-margin vines, at least one already-split melon in the current, submerged grass ribbons, and a log. The bank is dry footing.
- **DM truth:**
> [!secret] Hidden
> The fruit is genuinely beneficial. The cost is possession, not poison. DC 15 or Identify gives the full hour of air and water breathing plus swim parity. Squeezing a melon underwater is a tell in otter water. [[Delmar Fisk]] and [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] are the likely waders. [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] can work from the bank. Do not run an otter hunt on this card; that is fauna row 4 or beat 5.
- **Procedure:** Show living vines and the already-cut melon as the choice. Do not hide which fruit is still attached.
- **If violence starts:** There is no melon attack. Noise in the shallows can justify picking the fauna die on the next check.
- **If ignored:** Loose fruit drifts. Living fruit stays on the vine.

**Spotlight:** [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] on take versus receive. [[Delmar Fisk]] if he enters the current. [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] from the bank.

### 8. Fallen fruit on the bank

**When live:** This row is live on a roll of 8 when the DM picks the plant die, on a used bank or fruiting margin.
**Now:** Overripe fruit is already on the ground. Living bushes stand next to it.
**Say/show:** Read the narration. What is down is offered. What is still on the stem is not.
**Ask/offer:** Take the fallen fruit. Walk on. Strip the living bushes.
**Opposition/pressure:** This is the easy Hunger Rule lesson. Fallen fruit feeds them. Living stems with blood or snapped wood are a claim.
**Resolve:** Taking only what is already down is passage. Stripping live bushes is a claim: both later dice become 2d8 take lower.
**On success/partial/failure:** Success means they eat what is down and the smoke stays the prize. Partial means they take fallen fruit and spend time sorting stems. Failure means they pick living bushes, and the next check is a claim roll.
**If ignored:** The fallen fruit keeps fermenting. The living bushes stay.
**Move on to:** The spine. Do not turn this into a combat unless they start a claim and you pick a later worse row.

> [!narration] Narration
> Overripe fruit lies on the bank: rinds, seeds, a citrus peel in one coil, no blood. Living bushes stand next to that pile with fruit still on the stem. The prints and the smoke continue upstream.

### Run now

- **On the table:** Already-down fruit (citrus, melon, or terrace leftovers), living bushes with fruit still attached, and upriver prints. No animal starts on this card.
- **DM truth:**
> [!secret] Hidden
> Match beat 4's lesson if that bank already played: fallen fruit has no blood; living stems are the claim. Ordinary restorative fruit may occur; do not invent a new mechanical item here. [[Lion Citrus]] or [[Breathmelon]] already down is enough. [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] is the likely reader of take versus receive. If they strip live bushes, the next Survival check uses the claim rule on both tables.
- **Procedure:** Put fallen and living fruit in the same look. Let them choose. Do not hide a curse in the flesh.
- **If violence starts:** Only if they make a claim and you convert this into a later row. This card has no fight of its own.
- **If ignored:** The pile stays. The smoke does not get closer while they argue over it.

**Spotlight:** [[Perrin Black-Jaw]] on the Hunger Rule. Anyone who reaches for a stem. [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] if he wants to keep moving on the prints.
