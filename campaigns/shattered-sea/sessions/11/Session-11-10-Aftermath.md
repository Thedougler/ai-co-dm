# Session 11 — Aftermath

![[aruhe-star-cut.jpg|Star Cut, night-watch aisle under a slit of stars]]

## Scene ends when

The party can name what is different and what they want next. This is the aftermath of the watch at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]], or on the column if they marched. It is not [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], and it is not a second night.

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** If [[Talon Skarn]] left with the [[Fate Spinner]], resolve one stoop on him, inland, then sleep. If they held it, keep the [[Fate Spinner]] on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] or on this dirt, give one survivor line, then sleep.

**If ahead:** Still end here. The garden invitation stays open. This valley does not reach [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]] tonight.

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** What they kept or lost after the watch is now visible, and isolation is still how a body becomes prey.
- **Goal / exit:** Name what is different and what they want next, then sleep or walk.
- **Danger:** If the [[Fate Spinner]] left this fire, the adult [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk]] hunts [[Talon Skarn]] in Open sky. Joining that air can make the party the meal. If they held it, this camp still splits ship versus garden.
- **Silence:** Present the changed watch, then wait. If the party freezes on Held, the night ends on sleep. If the party freezes on Stolen while anyone remains in Open sky, tick the clock once at the end of the round.
- **Situation magnets:** Anyone who climbs into Open sky. Anyone who walks a north trail alone. The [[Fate Spinner]] if it is in view.

## Now

The party is still on [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]] unless beat 8 broke. [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]] and the watch partner stand at the east fire's north edge. Sleeping rolls lie along both jungle walls. The north mouth is 40 feet of packed dirt from those coals. A 30-foot move from the fire stays on the aisle. A Dash of 60 feet does not reach [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring|Spoke Ring]], the beach, or [[Uncertainty]]. Walk speed on this dirt is ordinary. The wood beyond either jungle wall is Difficult Terrain and heavily obscures beyond 10 feet.

**Held.** The steal was disrupted. The [[Fate Spinner]] is on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], on the packed dirt at this watch, or recovered. [[Talon Skarn]] is already in Quiet north of the star-cut, empty-handed, farther than 60 feet. There is no hawk stoop, because nothing left this island. Speak Initial Narration.

**Stolen.** [[Talon Skarn]] has the [[Fate Spinner]] in a hooked blade. He has already left the aisle on his wings and hangs 40 feet up over the north mouth. The adult [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk]] and her young, if they lived, are already dropping on him, not on this camp. They try to haul him inland toward the darker wood, not toward the party. Speak the Open sky zone Narration as the first look.

**Hawk dead.** If the adult [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk]] died on beat 1, there is no stoop. [[Talon Skarn]] still leaves with the [[Fate Spinner]] on Stolen. Stay or chase him without hawks.

**Column.** If beat 9 was the trail they were on, keep them on that trail. Do not move them to [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Star Cut]]. Reuse the 40-foot lane and the 40-foot Open sky height.

**Knot.** If beat 9 sat at [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Spoke Ring]], start at that fire. Open sky is above that leaf roof. Living [[Aruhe - Stonepear]] still hangs there.

**Mark live.** After Initial Narration, the same garden words come once from beyond the firelight: come admire her garden, this way, leave the living plants. Nobody walks toward them. The [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]] stays off this card.

**Mark spent.** The people at this fire refuse to leave the firelight. Living fruit hangs untouched.

### Action cards

**Aftermath.** This is a Resolution. Show what changed, what it cost, and what they can still pursue. This is not a fight to the death. This valley does not reach [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]] tonight. The [[Aruhe - Deerstalker]] stays off this card.

**One adult [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk]] on Stolen only.** Loop: Canopy Dive → Talon Grab → Haul Aloft inland. Tell: the four wings fold and the crimson tips flash. The chosen meal is [[Talon Skarn]]. The party is not the chosen meal unless they hang in Open sky, hit the hawk, make loud magic, grab him, or fly at the young. Bloodied at **124 HP or fewer:** the adult Bloodhawk keeps [[Talon Skarn]] if it has him and still climbs inland. The adult Bloodhawk will not dive into closed canopy or eight-foot grass. Easy dial: Multiattack only, no Canopy Dive recharge. Canopy Dive or Beak is +13 to hit. Beak deals 28 piercing. Talon Grab is +13 to hit, 23 slashing, **Grappled** (escape `DC 19`) and **Restrained**. Fly 120 feet.

**Young [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk|Bloodhawks]].** Smaller four-winged charcoal hawks on the same dive. They hit whatever the adult flushes. They are pressure around the hunt, not a second boss. A young hawk's dive drives a flushed body 30 feet down. They will not follow into eight-foot grass or closed canopy.

**[[Talon Skarn]] on Stolen.** He is the isolated body in that air. AC 20. Hit points are whatever beat 9 left. He still has chained blades. He wants to keep the [[Fate Spinner]] and break contact inland. He does not turn this camp into a second theft. Compact numbers stay on [[Session-11-09-Theft-on-the-Watch]] if someone hits him.

**Garden mouths.** Unnamed. They listened. They still want the garden. Sample: "She asked us to come admire her garden. This way. Leave the living plants."

**Ship mouths.** Unnamed. They call the listeners mad. Sample: "She is leading you into danger. Anyone who listens is mad."

**[[Taking on Aruhe]].** Fallen fruit is receiving. Plucking a living plant is a claim. Surrounding life converges, hostile.

>

>
> What do you do?

## Procedure

**Aftermath.** Failures impose the Be ready for ruling only. Failures never advance the threat clock.

On Held, there is no threat clock. When the party names what is different and what they want next, or they sleep, or they walk, the scene ends.

On Stolen, the inverted hunt is live only in Open sky. After everyone has acted, if anyone remains in Open sky, tick once and resolve that tick. Freeze in Open sky still ticks once. Several failed checks in one round still produce one tick. If the whole party stays on this dirt, do not tick against them. Resolve the stoop on [[Talon Skarn]] in How the Scene Resolves.

If they climb into that air after him, beat 1's grammar applies: obvious bodies in the open can become the meal. If they stay on the ground, the hawks are his problem.

**Combat mode:** if the party commits to killing the adult [[Aruhe - Bloodhawk]], stop the clock and run the embedded Bloodhawk statblock. Do not turn this into a fight to the death unless they make themselves the meal.

Keep the [[Fate Spinner]] on [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], or recover it from a talon or a hooked blade.
