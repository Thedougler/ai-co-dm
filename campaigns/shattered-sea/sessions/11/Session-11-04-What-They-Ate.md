# Session 11 — What they ate

![[aruhe-river-line-bank.jpg|Line Bank, three-pile fruiting margin and crude fishing line]]

## Scene ends when

The party commits to following the north prints and smoke off this bank, handing to [[Session-11-05-Otter-Hole]].

This beat is thirty minutes.

**If behind:** Skip extra sorting of the three piles. Show the three piles, the pole, and the blood only at living stems. Ask, then end on the upriver commit.

**If ahead:** Still end on follow. Lingering to pick living fruit on this bank is a [[Taking on Aruhe]] claim, and the later hazards-table roll uses `2d8`, keeping the lower result. The trail and smoke remain.

## At a Glance

- **Stakes:** Easy food is on the ground. Living bushes have blood under them. Waiting lets the smoke thin.
- **Goal / exit:** Take fallen fruit and keep the north prints for [[Session-11-05-Otter-Hole]].
- **Danger:** Plucking living plants turns surrounding life. Open valley air is hawk sky.
- **Silence:** Present this bank, then wait. If the party freezes here, the smoke thins once at the end of the round.
- **Situation magnets:** Anyone lingering on this bank. Anyone plucking living stems.

## Now

The party is on [[Aruhe - River Line Bank]], facing north, just upvalley of [[Aruhe - Grasslands Torn Crossing]]. The river is immediately east. The prints are underfoot and run 60 feet north up this bank toward the smoke. The river-side pile, pole, and line sit 10 feet east of the prints, at the water. The downstream pile sits 20 feet south along this same bank. The inland pile sits 25 feet west toward the jungle wall. A 30-foot move stays on this bank. A Dash of 60 feet follows the prints north off this bank, or goes 40 feet south toward Torn Crossing. The smoke is a thin column on the northern horizon in this same valley. It is not reachable this slice. Apply any [[Taking on Aruhe]] claim already made.

### Action cards

**This bank.** Eight-foot gold-green grass is Difficult Terrain and heavily obscures beyond 10 feet. Movement leaves a crushed corridor.

The group ahead has been living off fruit already down on this bank, and off fish. Picking living bushes is what left the blood. This slice has no named predator. How far the smoke is stays unknown. How many people passed stays unknown. Do not start [[Aruhe - River Slack Basin]]. Do not invent a new mechanical fruit.

**[[Taking on Aruhe]] claim.** Fruit already on the ground of this bank is down. Fallen fruit has no blood. Taking only that fruit is not a claim. Plucking living plants, trapping, or killing to carry flesh does. On a claim, do not roll the hazards table now. The later [[Session-11-00-Random-Tables]] roll is `2d8`, keeping the lower result. The trail and smoke remain.

**Fallen fruit.** Living tawny orange fruit still hangs on the three bushes. Eating a still-firm fallen fruit is a Bonus Action and regains `8d4 + 8` hit points, as [[Aruhe - Redheart Berry]]. Fermenting rinds and split skins have no listed heal. Do not invent a new mechanical item. Stems snapped on the living plant with blood in the dirt are the claim already made by someone else.

**Identify.** **Intelligence (Nature) — identify**
- `DC 10` → The still-firm fallen fruit is edible, not poison. Fermenting rinds are already down.
- `DC 15` or *Identify* → Eating a still-firm fallen fruit is a Bonus Action and regains `8d4 + 8` hit points, as [[Aruhe - Redheart Berry]].

**Clue 2.** Optional **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 27`** only if they ask who that flyer is. [[Talon Skarn]] is that man-sized flyer up-sun, then gone. The check is not prompted.

> You see three fruiting bushes standing in reach along this wet margin, tawny orange globes still heavy on the living wood. Whole fruit and split skins heap at the roots: tawny orange ten feet toward the water, pale green twenty feet south along this bank, and a darker heap twenty-five feet west toward the jungle wall. The dirt under what has already fallen is clean. Dark red mud packs only around the living roots, where snapped stems hang over fruit still on the branch.
>
> Ten feet east, a wooden pole leans on a short frame, a thin line still hanging in the turquoise current, pale stone showing under the flow. Fish bones and silver scales lie on the wet sand beside a couple of woven baskets.
>
> You smell the split skins where they have gone sharp and sweet. You hear water pulling at the line, and you feel the wet sand sucking at the prints underfoot. Those prints keep north through this grass. North in this same valley, a thin column of smoke still stands on the horizon.
>
> What do you do?

## Procedure

**Clue.** This bank is a look-and-commit slice. There is no fight on this card. After everyone has acted, if anyone remains lingering on this bank, tick the threat clock once. Freeze still ticks once. Failures impose the Be ready for ruling only. Failures never advance the threat clock. When the party follows the north prints, the scene ends. Do not roll the hazards table here. Do not start [[Aruhe - River Slack Basin]].

If a party member takes open valley air, that person leaves this bank. Point at a [[Aruhe - Young Bloodhawk]].
