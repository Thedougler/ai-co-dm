# Session 08 Report

## Summary

The crew climbed out of the crater as Defenders. [[npc.iacopo-fieschi|Fieschi]] confessed a [[faction.tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] debt in public and paid: a thousand gold in trade credit, [[location.casa-falier|Casa Falier]], and a diamond ring. [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] had turned the [[location.mercatura|Mercatura]] crater into a missing-persons desk — past 314 fighting-age men. She vowed chase, dispatched two Passage ships, and gave [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] a favor. Shopping named [[npc.osset|Osset]] twice.

> [!narration] Player-facing — read or adapt at the table.
> The crew climbed out of the [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] crater as its Defenders. [[npc.iacopo-fieschi|Fieschi]] confessed his [[faction.tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] debt in public and paid for it: a thousand gold in trade credit, [[location.casa-falier|Casa Falier]] itself, and a diamond ring.
>
> The ink was barely dry when [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s Sending Stone warmed. [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]]: come home. Something dead had hit her too.
>
> [[location.mercatura|The Mercatura]]'s own crater sat cordoned off. [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] had turned it into a missing-persons desk, a borrowed table stacked with a list that would not stop growing, past 314 names by the time the queue thinned, every one a fighting-age man.
>
> [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] read the pattern as slaving weakening a fighting force by proxy, and pointed to [[creature.grung|Grung]] poison already turning up on ships that traded with the raiders.
>
> [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] dispatched two ships from [[faction.the-passage|The Passage]]'s own fleet, days behind, and stood before the grieving crowd: "[[faction.the-passage|The Passage]] stands with the citizens of [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]]. I vow to you, I will assemble a crew and make chase." She gave [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] a favor instead of a cash share, and word that [[npc.cobb|Cobb]]'s refit on their ship would be ready in twelve hours.
>
> Reward gold in pocket, the crew went shopping. On the way they fished a capsized gondolier, [[npc.paolo|Paolo]], out of the canal near the [[location.mercatura|Mercatura]].
>
> At [[business.cabinet-of-morsani|The Cabinet of Morsani]], [[npc.prospero-morsani|Prospero Morsani]] talked up trick-arrow quivers and a wrist-shield the crew nicknamed [[item.the-snap|The Snap]]. [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] closed the sale with a threat to flood his market with copies. She tried the shop's dragonhide belt first and put [[npc.prospero-morsani|Morsani]] on his back. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] lifted a torn page off his shelves. It named a monk who left [[faction.sentinels-of-the-eyrie|the Sentinels]] during their schism and was never heard from again: [[npc.osset|Osset]].
>
> When [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] read [[npc.prospero-morsani|Morsani]]'s real source for [[item.the-snap|The Snap]] — a corpse, probably — and said so to his face, the shop answered. A hidden gust-trap threw her out the door and twenty feet down the street.
>
> [[location.le-paludi|Le Paludi]] gave up two more threads. At [[business.la-cenere|La Cenere]], [[npc.lavinia-sordi|Lavinia Sordi]] sold [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] a cloak she called a simple Cloak of Protection. Identify made it a cursed Cloak of Displacement instead — [[item.nightmantle|Nightmantle]]. [[npc.lavinia-sordi|Lavinia]] said she had gotten it from a falcon-featured man who also called himself [[npc.osset|Osset]], a name [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] has never heard from anyone at the Eyrie.
>
> Down the canal, an armless goblin, [[npc.zort|Zort]], ran the animal pits at [[business.zorts-pits|Zort's Pits]]. He offered his [[region.midchain|Midchain]] reptile contact in trade for a prosthetic leg. [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] agreed to build him one.
>
> [[npc.osset|Osset]]'s name is loose in two mouths now, on two sides of a schism nobody in the crew had heard named before tonight. The raiders already have days of head start on the two ships giving chase.

## Key Events

1. **Public pay** — [[npc.iacopo-fieschi|Fieschi]]'s debt confessed. 1,000 gp trade credit, [[location.casa-falier|Casa Falier]], a ring.
2. **[[event.calveno-missing-persons|Desk]]** — 314+ fighting-age men. [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] reads slaving. Two Passage ships dispatched. [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s public vow. [[npc.cobb|Cobb]] refit in twelve hours.
3. **[[npc.prospero-morsani|Morsani]]** — [[item.the-snap|The Snap]] bought. [[npc.osset|Osset]] named on a stolen page. Shop throws [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]].
4. **[[npc.lavinia-sordi|Lavinia]]** — [[item.nightmantle|Nightmantle]] sold as Cloak of Protection. Second [[npc.osset|Osset]], falcon-featured, unknown at the Eyrie.
5. **[[npc.zort|Zort]]** — Prosthetic-for-contact. [[region.midchain|Midchain]] reptile name: [[npc.roka-sten|Roka Sten]] in [[settlement.sparhold|Sparhold]].

## Decisions Made

- **Decision:** [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] vows public chase and sends two Passage ships instead of holding the desk only.
  - **Alternative:** Quiet recovery, or hold the city.
  - **Consequence:** Passage is on the record. Two hulls already days behind.
- **Decision:** [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] takes a favor, not a cash share of the raid purse.
  - **Alternative:** Split gold.
  - **Consequence:** Favor outstanding; [[npc.cobb|Cobb]]'s twelve-hour refit is the practical share.
- **Decision:** [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] agrees to build [[npc.zort|Zort]] a prosthetic.
  - **Alternative:** Decline [[business.zorts-pits|Zort's Pits]] contact.
  - **Consequence:** A [[region.midchain|Midchain]] reptile name is owed once the leg exists.
- **Decision:** [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] keeps the cloak after Identify.
  - **Alternative:** Return it to [[npc.lavinia-sordi|Lavinia]].
  - **Consequence:** She is wearing [[item.nightmantle|Nightmantle]].
