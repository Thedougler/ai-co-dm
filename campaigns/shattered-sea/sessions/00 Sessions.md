# Sessions
Season 1 closed with [[Otar the Foul]] dead beneath [[Mercatura]]; the Calveno crisis, Defenders paper, 314 desk, and Osset traces are recorded in [[campaigns/shattered-sea/story/00 Story so far]].

## Season 2 — Midchain to Aruhe
[[Uncertainty]] left La Vasca with Sparhold as its first chosen stop. The crew rescued three storm survivors, was intercepted by [[HCS Ordinance]], and reached [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]] after disabling a Vantyrus follower ship without boarding it.

### Last session — Aruhe
Two Aruhe survivors came aboard: roughly 100–150 people came off the raid ships, most are dead, and the island is taking the rest. A landing party found dead people near taking-places and was driven back by a Great Crown Squid that stopped at the tree line. The crew stayed mobile; inland survivors remain inland. No fake per-session files are implied by this dump.
