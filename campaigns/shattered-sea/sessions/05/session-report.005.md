# Session 05 Report

## Summary

After [[npc.felix-aho|Felix Aho]]'s interrogation the party went back into the [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] sewer magazines. [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina Da'Virelli]] joined them. [[npc.ruma-delacroix|Ruma Delacroix]] gave up Room 6. [[npc.bazzoth-the-steeped|Bazzoth]] and Magazine Beta went into the water. They hit Room 8 mid-ritual: [[npc.solange-barret|Solange Barret]] chanting, four Elite Warriors in the way. The recording failed there. Recap is the DM's reconstruction, not a transcript.
