# Session 11 — Beat 01: Hook — Bloodhawk scare-and-drop

Disposable run card. Parent: [[Session 11 prep]]. Chart: palette, not railroad.

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: pending — player-facing prose for this beat._

## Role
**Hook** (action-heavy). ~25–35 min. Win = ground under closed canopy or grass, **not** killing the hawk.

## When
Immediately after recap. Party exposed in/over open thermal above the canopy.

## Pressure
A [[Bloodhawk]] arrives hunting the [[Great Crown Squid]] (party is collateral). Hawk owns open thermal; will not stoop closed canopy. Squid presses tree line; will not chase into open grass.

## Stakes
Safe landing · party cohesion · route into [[Old Gardens]] or beach-down toward [[Western Landing]].

## DM run
1. Present the sky threat as **suspense** (visible fuse), not surprise TPK.
2. Offer clear exits: closed canopy · open grass · rim cover / redirect predators at each other.
3. Land them at **A** [[Aruhe Drop Rim Court]].
4. Do not prolong into a full aerial fight unless they choose it and pay.

## Immediate
- Actors: [[Bloodhawk]], [[Great Crown Squid]], rim / canopy / grass exits
- Encounter card: [[Bloodhawk scare-and-drop]]
- Spotlight: [[Delmar Fisk]] (orders under drop)

## Hidden
> [!secret]- Ecology lock
> Hawk will not stoop closed canopy or deep grass. Squid stops at tree line. Scare-and-drop dial (Nick): lethal threat, win is getting down.

## If ignored
Second stoop / messy drop — gear loss, separation, or forced landing with worse footing. World still advances to A.
