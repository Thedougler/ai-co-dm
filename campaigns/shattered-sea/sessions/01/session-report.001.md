# Session 01 Report

## Summary

The party ambushed [[npc.barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]]'s boarding party in the hold of the [[vehicle.saltwright|The Saltwright]], killed both Enforcers, and turned [[npc.geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]]. The fight moved onto the gangplank with the [[vehicle.uncertainty|HCS Surety]]. [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] recognized his sister's toxin in the Alchemist's gas. The session ended with [[npc.barnaby-rook|Rook]] back aboard, firing at Beaumont.

> [!narration] Player-facing — read or adapt at the table.
> The hold of the [[vehicle.saltwright|Saltwright]] was dark and close, smelling of bilge water and shrimp. Above, Beaumont kept [[npc.barnaby-rook|Barnaby Rook]] at the wheel while three of [[npc.barnaby-rook|Rook]]'s crew came down the ladder. The Enforcer they called [[npc.capn-gorgeous|Cap'n Gorgeous]] walked in without hesitation. A poisoned arrow from [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]], a musket shot, and a shattered kneecap answered him. Black powder in that enclosed space deafened [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] and [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]. [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] had laid a Minor Illusion across the doorway; [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] dragged Gorgeous back through it. Two deckhands followed and did not return.
>
> [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] knelt at the foot of the ladder. [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] hit his hands at a run and he launched her through the hatch. Her wings snapped open and sent the two nearest [[faction.dravosi-crown|Dravosi]] sprawling. [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] and [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] came through a gap in the decking. Both Enforcers died in the press.
>
> The last [[creature.dravosi-deckhand|Dravosi Deckhand]], maybe nineteen, dropped his sword. [[npc.barnaby-rook|Rook]] called him a turncoat from the gangplank. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] asked the boy whether he would rather fight for men who threatened to shoot him, or men who would protect him. [[npc.geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]] said he would do whatever kept him alive today, picked the sword back up, and joined. He is, by the current count, the party's entire crew.
>
> [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] opened the gangplank fight with the Flask of Endless Water and threw [[npc.barnaby-rook|Rook]] into the water. The Enforcer fell. The Alchemist released a new canister. [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] and [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] resisted; [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] sent the cloud back with Gust. [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] placed the toxin as [[npc.simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s. He said nothing.
>
> The Alchemist fell. [[npc.barnaby-rook|Rook]] hauled himself back over the rail, raised his flintlock at Beaumont, and fired. The round struck Beaumont's salvaged shell at the shoulder and spun into the water. "Boy, I shit out harder things than you every mornin'." That's where we left it.

## Key Events

1. **Hold ambush** — Combined assault dropped [[npc.capn-gorgeous|Cap'n Gorgeous]] and two deckhands below. Black powder deafened [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] and [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]. [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s illusion hid the doorway.
2. **Weather-deck press** — [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] launched through the hatch; both [[creature.dravosi-enforcer|Dravosi Enforcers]] died.
3. **[[npc.geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey]] defects** — [[npc.geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey Draves]] dropped his sword, took [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s offer, and became the party's first crew.
4. **Gangplank** — Flask threw [[npc.barnaby-rook|Rook]] off the plank. Alchemist gas identified as [[npc.simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s [[creature.grung|Grung]] toxin. Session ended with [[npc.barnaby-rook|Rook]]'s shot bouncing off Beaumont.

## Decisions Made

- **Decision:** Take the boarding fight in the hold rather than surrender the [[vehicle.saltwright|Saltwright]].
  - **Alternative:** Hide or negotiate with [[npc.barnaby-rook|Rook]].
  - **Consequence:** Opens [[event.taking-of-the-surety|the taking of the Surety]]; Crown dead on a merchant brig.
- **Decision:** Spare and recruit [[npc.geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey]] instead of finishing the last deckhand.
  - **Alternative:** Kill him or leave him bound.
  - **Consequence:** Party has one crewman; [[npc.barnaby-rook|Rook]] names him turncoat on the plank.
- **Decision:** [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] does not speak the toxin match.
  - **Alternative:** Tell the crew [[npc.simone-tabarnack|Simone]] supplies [[faction.dravosi-crown|the Crown]].
  - **Consequence:** The fact stays private through the rest of the fight.
