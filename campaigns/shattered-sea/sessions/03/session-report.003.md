# Session 03 Report

## Summary

[[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] took a vision from below — a sixty-foot body, one word: *Grow* — then the party killed the whip-shark that had been striking the hull. Five days at sea. They renamed the prize *[[vehicle.uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* and put her in [[settlement.calveno|La Vasca]]'s cradle. [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] grieved the [[vehicle.vestra|Vestra]] as money, called off attacks, and took an unspecified favor. [[npc.master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] found [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] from the sun.

> [!narration] Player-facing — read or adapt at the table.
> [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] was on watch when the storm thinned and the water went somewhere else. Fathoms of cold pressure, worked stone, pale things drifting. Then his body was not his: sixty feet of muscle, a barbed [[region.the-tail|Tail]], a hull above, blood in the water. He dove, drove the barb in, and a whale sounded and took him with it. He watched a shape rise and strike the hull. One word burned: *Grow.*
>
> He fell from the rigging with [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]. [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] caught them both. The whip-shark that struck the hull was longer than the ship. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] emptied both swivels and the broadside. [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] flew out, seized it by [[region.the-tail|The Tail]], and hauled it clear. [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] put an arrow in one end and out the other. [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] healed [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] with a red light from his wrist [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s Arcana could not name. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] recovered the barb in manta form. They cut scales. [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] took three fertilized eggs.
>
> Five days at sea. [[npc.old-faas|Old Faas]] taught [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] the ropes. [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] and [[npc.sem-holst|Sem Holst]] patched the hull. [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] found [[npc.barnaby-rook|Rook]] had broken Imperial rules about [[region.drowned-maw|The Drowned Maw]] twice and kept no record. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] ran ship's business too long as [[pc.delmar-fisk|Admiral Fisk]] until [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]'s flask brought him back. On the second day she spotted a glowing outline on a parallel heading. [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] watched it a long time.
>
> [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] was too busy to fly Crown colours up the main approach. [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] took them the other way. On the approach the crew renamed the [[vehicle.uncertainty|HCS Surety]]: no prefix, just *[[vehicle.uncertainty|Uncertainty]]*. [[settlement.calveno|La Vasca]]'s covered dry dock had a cradle waiting. [[npc.cobb|Cobb]] met them at the water. [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] shook his hand and bolted. As [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] stepped ashore the water reached for him and said his name, then it was gone. [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] came down with an icy handshake, thanked the crew, and left for a week or two.
>
> [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] changed color, found a shortbow at [[business.casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]], and placed an egg at [[business.studio-orsini|Studio Orsini]]. [[deity.umberlee|Umberlee]]'s servant stopped [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] and [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] in [[location.mercatura|The Mercatura]] and said she would not sleep until [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] came to the [[location.kalowe-waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]]. He talked past her.
>
> At [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s kitchen, [[npc.enzo|Enzo]] and [[npc.ruk|Ruk]] stood watch. She heard about the *[[vehicle.vestra|Vestra]]* — lost to something in the water, lightning, then nothing — and grieved the money more than the ship. She called off the attacks already underway, ordered consolation for [[vehicle.vestra|The Vestra]]'s crew, and asked an unspecified favor. [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] agreed before hearing it. She gave him a sending stone.
>
> That afternoon something dove from the sun and knocked [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] flat: [[npc.master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]], who had searched [[settlement.kalowe|Kalowe]] and [[settlement.tidefall|Tidefall]] for her since the *[[vehicle.red-lady|Red Lady]]* went down. [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] came out of a barrel to shake one offered talon.

## Decisions Made

- **Decision:** Rename the prize *[[vehicle.uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* and take the side approach into [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]].
  - **Alternative:** Fly Crown colours on the main approach, or keep *[[vehicle.uncertainty|Surety]]*.
  - **Consequence:** The hull is a stolen cutter with a new name in [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s dock.
- **Decision:** [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] accepts [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s favor before hearing it.
  - **Alternative:** Ask the terms.
  - **Consequence:** He owes her. The sending stone is live.
- **Decision:** [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] talks past [[deity.umberlee|Umberlee]]'s servant instead of going to the shrine.
  - **Alternative:** Attend the shrine that day.
  - **Consequence:** The goddess's appointment is still open. See session 04.
