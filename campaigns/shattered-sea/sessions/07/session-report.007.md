# Session 07 Report

## Summary

Three nights earlier [[npc.thunk|Thunk]] counted cards behind [[location.tallow-row|Tallow Row]] and walked out with other people's silver. Then [[location.mercatura|The Mercatura]] crater: [[npc.otar-the-foul|Otar]] and five leech-spawn against a city that learned [[location.the-warren|The Warren]] beat. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] ended it with the chair and a shot through the eye. [[npc.iacopo-fieschi|Fieschi]] signed them as [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]]'s Defenders. [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s stone: come home; hundreds missing.

> [!narration] Player-facing — read or adapt at the table.
> Three nights before the crater, [[npc.thunk|Thunk]] stood at the *[[vehicle.uncertainty|Uncertainty]]*'s rail with a thousand gold of cannon and no memory of signing. [[npc.old-faas|Old Faas]] taught him cards, caught him mouthing a count, and walked him to the game behind the butcher's on [[location.tallow-row|Tallow Row]]. [[npc.grukan|Grukan]] on the door. [[npc.vittoria-sanzio|Vittoria Sanzio]] banking the chair with no cards. Lose the first hour, [[npc.old-faas|Faas]] said. Count the second.
>
> [[npc.thunk|Thunk]] counted. [[npc.thassos|Thassos]] stopped smiling. [[npc.old-faas|Faas]]'s boot found [[npc.thunk|Thunk]]'s ankle, one tap, and [[npc.old-faas|Faas]] folded a hand he should have won. They ran the alley with silver coming out of [[npc.thunk|Thunk]]'s arms and coats and one boot. [[npc.old-faas|Faas]] did not laugh, and has not said a word about halves.
>
> Then the ceiling over the summoning platform finished opening. What [[npc.solange-barret|Solange Barret]] became straightened to twelve feet, red hide cracked and weeping, and [[location.mercatura|The Mercatura]] had a thirty-foot crater under a bright [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] morning. [[npc.otar-the-foul|Otar]]'s wounds closed as fast as the crew opened them. Five slug-pale things clawed out of the rubble; one caught a trapped villager and started eating, and the more it ate the more [[creature.human|Human]] it looked.
>
> [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] went after a leech on a bystander. [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] found a line in her tattoo she had never used, ink flaring gold, then caught the tongue across her forearm. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] kicked the flying boots on, hauled two people upright, and took the same tongue across the ribs. [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]]'s owl and iron barrel worked the gap. [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] asked for inspiration mid-blast; [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] set his drum on the floor for him to touch.
>
> It went badly enough that [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] flew the crater lip and whistled the crow's-nest call. [[npc.master-kyzil|Kyzil]] heard it. Ninety feet of silent white feathers. His daggers took the brood apart.
>
> The town answered. Skillets and pots picked up [[location.the-warren|The Warren]]'s old signal: hide or get ready to fight. A woman heaved a jar of burning lamp oil into the crater. The flank they had been hitting stopped closing.
>
> [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] was in his mouth when [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] ended it. The chair he had rolled into existence a session earlier was still whole, so he flew down, grabbed it, spun, and put it into [[npc.otar-the-foul|Otar]]'s throat. Then the pistol through the eye. [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] came out into daylight. The pans gave two quick taps and stopped. Nobody cheered.
>
> The carcass rotted where it fell. A scrap of paper sat in the decay that nobody has read yet. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] found not one scratch on the chair and borrowed [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]]'s Bag of Holding to carry it home. The raid's take got names: [[item.solanges-authority-seal|Solange's Authority Seal]], and [[item.a-sliver-of-the-unstable-form|A Sliver of the Unstable Form]].
>
> [[npc.iacopo-fieschi|Iacopo Fieschi]] found them in the plaza, then an office with the smoking crater in the window. The [[faction.tessarine-concordat|Tessarine Concordat]] offered three hundred gold each and a retainer, because the [[faction.dravosi-crown|Dravosi]] were not defending anything. [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] turned her share down for repairs and street children. [[npc.iacopo-fieschi|Fieschi]] gave [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] a diamond ring worth five hundred; she kept it. [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] took a standing favor and sold [[npc.iacopo-fieschi|Fieschi]] on the Black-Jaws. [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] asked for tea, swirled it, sniffed it, and never drank. They all signed. [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] has Defenders now, on [[faction.tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] letterhead.
>
> Under it sat a question nobody could close. [[npc.iacopo-fieschi|Fieschi]] said no warning ever reached him. Someone in that room had been given strict orders by [[npc.rupert-knighton|Rupert Knighton]] to gather what she could and evacuate, days before the ceiling opened.
>
> They were arguing about where to eat when [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s stone warmed. [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]]: come home soon. Something bad has happened. Many, many people are missing. Hundreds.

## Decisions Made

- **Decision:** Kill [[npc.otar-the-foul|Otar]] in the open crater with the city watching, instead of fleeing the lip.
  - **Alternative:** Evacuate and leave the spawn to the crowd.
  - **Consequence:** They become the people who ended it. [[npc.iacopo-fieschi|Fieschi]] can hire that.
- **Decision:** Sign [[npc.iacopo-fieschi|Fieschi]]'s Defender paper.
  - **Alternative:** Take the gold and walk, or refuse the writ.
  - **Consequence:** [[faction.tessarine-concordat|Tessarine]] claim on the crew. Crown absence is on paper.
- **Decision:** [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] redirects her 300 gp; [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] takes a favor not coin.
  - **Alternative:** Even split.
  - **Consequence:** Repairs and children get her share. Black-Jaw standing with the city.
