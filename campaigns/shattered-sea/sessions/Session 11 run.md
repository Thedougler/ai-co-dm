# Session 11 — Run

This note is the primary DM control panel for tonight. Detailed inventory and densified prep live on [[Session 11 prep]] (L2). Individual beat cards are indexed on [[Session 11 beats]]. Soft Grasslands, the Rot, the Grove, and the Lava Tubes stay held as stubs and are not densified tonight.

## Glance (L0)

- **Stakes:** The party must survive Aruhe tonight by gaining Quiet intel **or** escaping by boat. Clearing the island is not a win condition. Fighting Grove mourners is not tonight’s climax.
- **Strong start (alone):** Begin with [[Session 11 Beat 00 - Last session recap]], then move into [[Session 11 Beat 01 - Hook Bloodhawk scare-and-drop]] while the party is exposed in open thermal air above the canopy. They win the Hook by getting under closed canopy or deep grass, not by killing the hawk.
- **Active pressure:** A [[Bloodhawk]] is hunting a [[Great Crown Squid]], and the party is collateral. [[Uncertainty]] remains mobile offshore. Inland Calveno wreck survivors are still inland.
- **Soft landing:** End the live pressure at [[Aruhe Quiet Green Verge]] (site I) **or** with a [[Western Landing]] pickup. The Grove is not the finish line.
- **Table:** Use a field Long Rest with watches. After the Long Rest, ask whether anyone ate; warn once; if someone went without food that day, they take 1 exhaustion next (this is **not** the [[Aruhe Hunger Rule]]).
- **Silence protocol:** Present one concrete situation and its public stakes, ask what the players do, then wait. If the table stays silent, advance only a visible independent pressure and show the cost of inaction.

## Secrets / clues (floating, location-agnostic)

1. Inland survivors exist and are moving toward the Quiet; the table needs at least three independent signs across the night.
2. The squid stops at the tree line, and the hawk will not stoop into closed canopy or deep grass.
3. Taking living things without receiving them converges hostility; receiving an offered benefit is different.
4. Free-flowing water, loose stone, and shed shell count as safe nonliving matter.
5. Fruit still on the vine becomes a Hunger claim if it is taken.
6. The ship is mobile, so pickup is a timing valve rather than a fixed anchor.
7. Choosing beach-down weakens the survivor trail; staying inland intensifies it.
8. Warren mouths foreshadow Blackrail as bait and are not a required entry tonight.
9. Quiet silence means eaten-out life, not safety by default.
10. Grove mourners do not hunt; memorial touch is a later-session danger.

## Revelation strip

- The Hunger Rule becomes legible at Taking-places through fiction, not through a lecture.
- A Quiet Verge soft landing yields intel and a camp choice, not a clear fight.
- Escaping by boat can abandon the inland survivor trail, and that cost should be named at the table.
