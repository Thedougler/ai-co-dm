# Session 09 Report

## Summary

The *[[vehicle.uncertainty|Uncertainty]]* left [[settlement.calveno|La Vasca]] refit and bound for [[settlement.sparhold|Sparhold]], the first stop on the [[creature.grung|Grung]] fleet's trail. A cold open played three doomed purple [[creature.grung|Grung]] guards on [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s beach — the [[region.aruhe|Hungry Isle]] — picked off by [[creature.wolfrabbit|wolfrabbit]] packs despite every escape they tried. [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s spellcaster had already pulled [[faction.fisks-fleet|the Fleet]]'s destination from a captured purple [[creature.grung|Grung]]: [[region.karath|Karath]], the [[creature.grung|Grung]] center of power.

En route the crew rescued three [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] captives clinging to wreckage — survivors of the eastern hurricane that cut the [[creature.grung|Grung]] fleet in half. Two days out, [[vehicle.hcs-ordinance|HCS Ordinance]] — the Knighton fleet's 96-gun man-of-war, carrying the Hound of [[deity.tyr|Tyr]] — intercepted them hunting [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]]. The crew played merchant, hid [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]], [[npc.ruma-delacroix|Ruma]], and [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]], and pointed the warship at [[region.faerun|Calveno]]. [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] handed [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] [[item.lamaraes-fang|Lamarae's Fang]] in trade for his help finding an axolotl.

> [!narration] Player-facing — read or adapt at the table.
> The cutter slid free of [[settlement.calveno|La Vasca]]'s cradle into a morning of wet timber and festival anxiety, the season's first true southerly already humming in the rigging.
>
> Before the heading was set, the table turned. You were a purple [[creature.grung|Grung]], saltwater slick, a sphere clutched against your chest since the hull broke on [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]]'s reef. Two guards from another garrison dragged themselves ashore beside you. The island every [[creature.grung|Grung]] knows from childhood: the [[region.aruhe|Hungry Isle]]. No rescue comes. A gray-haired captive squeezed a berry the size of a peach — and the brush exploded. Three wolf-sized rabbits, then more. You ran, you jumped, you swam, you threw a spear. It was not enough. The pack eats anyone who lands there.
>
> Back aboard, [[npc.cobb|Cobb]]'s refit was already done: four feet of draft, a different silhouette, a workshop, four new guns, and an icebreaker ram at the bow. [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] flew ahead over flotsam and found three sun-blasted men on a shattered hull — [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] captives, thrown from their captured ship when the hurricane cut [[faction.fisks-fleet|the Fleet]] in half.
>
> Then a three-deck wall of grey dropped out of the sun on an intercept course: [[vehicle.hcs-ordinance|HCS Ordinance]], ninety-six guns, flying [[faction.dravosi-crown|Dravosi]] colors. A young officer crossed first, dueling scars on his face, and asked for the captain. Behind him a tall grey-haired high elf scanned every face with cold manic joy — [[npc.aleksander-malone|the Hound of Tyr]]. He asked about the aberration that calls itself [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Gregory]]. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] sat in his [[item.fleet-commanders-chair|chair]], claimed merchant employ, and sent them to [[region.faerun|Calveno]]. When the inquisitor pushed, [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] laughed with [[deity.umberlee|Umberlee]]'s voice until the officer — [[npc.corbin-knighton|Corbin Knighton]] — reined his own hound in and the warship stood off for [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]].

## Key Events

1. **Cold open** — Borrowed-POV purple [[creature.grung|Grung]] guards on [[region.aruhe|Aruhe]], killed by [[creature.wolfrabbit|wolfrabbits]]. Re-presents [[event.aruhe-wreck|the Aruhe Wreck]] from the doomed guards' side.
2. **Interrogation intel** — [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s spellcaster pulled [[faction.fisks-fleet|the Fleet]]'s route from a purple [[creature.grung|Grung]]: captives bound for [[region.karath|Karath]], the [[creature.grung|Grung]] center of power.
3. **Refit tour** — Draft 4 ft, altered silhouette, workshop, cargo expanded, four new guns, icebreaker ram. HP 130, AC 11, 70 mi/day, 25 tons.
4. **Route** — [[settlement.sparhold|Sparhold]] chosen over Calo and [[settlement.fathomrush|Fathomrush]]: closest fortified lumber town to [[region.verdant-teeth|Grung territory]].
5. **Rescue** — Three [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] captives off a shattered hull. Eastern hurricane cut [[faction.fisks-fleet|the Fleet]] in half; the rear half lost or wrecked.
6. **Inspection** — [[vehicle.hcs-ordinance|Ordinance]] under [[npc.corbin-knighton|Corbin Knighton]] carrying [[npc.aleksander-malone|Malone]]. Merchant cover, hidden non-humans, redirect to [[region.faerun|Calveno]].
7. **[[item.lamaraes-fang|Lamarae's Fang]]** — [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] gives [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] the whip-shark-bone blade for his help finding an axolotl.

## Decisions Made

- **Decision:** Sail to [[settlement.sparhold|Sparhold]] rather than straight into [[region.verdant-teeth|the Teeth]].
  - **Alternative:** Calo or [[settlement.fathomrush|Fathomrush]].
  - **Consequence:** A fortified resupply stop dangerously close to [[region.karath|Grung]] water.
- **Decision:** Avoid combat with [[vehicle.hcs-ordinance|Ordinance]]; hold merchant cover, allow inspection, redirect to [[region.faerun|Calveno]].
  - **Alternative:** Run, or fight a ship with ~15× their guns.
  - **Consequence:** [[npc.aleksander-malone|The Hound]] and [[npc.corbin-knighton|Corbin]] sail for [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] with the crew's story.
- **Decision:** Hide [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]], [[npc.ruma-delacroix|Ruma]], and [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] during boarding.
  - **Alternative:** Present everyone and risk [[faction.dravosi-crown|Crown]] interest in non-humans.
  - **Consequence:** Only humans visible to the boarders.
- **Decision:** Do not sabotage [[vehicle.hcs-ordinance|Ordinance]] after it departs.
  - **Alternative:** Swim over, flood decks, or gunpowder accident.
  - **Consequence:** No traceability; no provoked war.
- **Decision:** Take the three rescued captives aboard as passengers.
  - **Alternative:** Leave them.
  - **Consequence:** Three mouths, three unverified stories.
- **Decision:** Warn [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] by [[item.fleet-commanders-chair|sending stone]] about the warship, the Hound, and the [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] search.
  - **Consequence:** [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] knows [[faction.dravosi-crown|the Crown]] is returning to [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]].
- **Decision:** [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] accepts [[item.lamaraes-fang|Lamarae's Fang]] and commits to helping [[pc.catarina-davirelli|Catarina]] find an axolotl.
