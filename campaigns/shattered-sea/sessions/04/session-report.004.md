# Session 04 Report

## Summary

[[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] sold the whip-shark eggs and bought a silent shortbow and boots of flying (for [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]]). [[npc.master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]] sparred the four of them on a Ponte Bassa rooftop. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] confessed [[faction.fisks-fleet|the Fleet]] and the [[item.pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]]; [[deity.umberlee|Umberlee]] answered through [[npc.branca|Branca]]. [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] sent them into [[location.the-warren|The Warren]] sewers. They burned a powder boat and took [[npc.felix-aho|Felix Aho]] alive. Two days until the festival bombs.

> [!narration] Player-facing — read or adapt at the table.
> [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] sold the whip-shark's three fertilized eggs at the [[location.le-paludi|Le Paludi]] market for 300 gp — a delicacy, the merchant said, not breeding stock. He spent it at [[business.casa-lupo|Casa Lupo]]: a silent shortbow for himself and boots of flying for [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]], once someone remembered [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] does not wear shoes.
>
> On the street a stranger looked [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] over and dismissed him. [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] vouched. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] hugged the man. The stranger was [[npc.master-kyzil|Master Kyzil]]. They made Ponte Bassa their base. [[npc.master-kyzil|Kyzil]] told [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] her pilgrimage was the hardest in Eyrie history, given because he believed she would survive it. He had felt [[region.drowned-maw|the Maw]] turn unruly since the wreck, and something foul moving up from the south.
>
> He challenged all four to a non-lethal rooftop spar, three rounds. [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] grappled him and rolled a natural one before his Downburst broke it. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] threw a chair. [[npc.master-kyzil|Kyzil]] pinned [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]'s shirt to the deck with one claw, then [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s hit finally landed on borrowed bardic inspiration. He was staying in [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] through the festival.
>
> Walking to the harbor, [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] told them the truth: he was a real admiral, a five-ship privateer fleet sponsored by the [[faction.chain-council|Chain Council]]. They had stolen the [[item.pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] from a shrine in [[location.umberlee-shrine-vel-orn|Vafnar]]. [[region.drowned-maw|the Maw]] destroyed [[faction.fisks-fleet|the Fleet]] within hours.
>
> At the harbor shrine [[deity.umberlee|Umberlee]] took [[npc.branca|Branca]]'s body, killed her, and brought her back to prove she could. Her price: the Pearl, in exchange for [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s life. He countered: the Pearl, and the people who commissioned the theft. She read the offer out of his head. *Bring me the pearl. We will talk then.*
>
> [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] swept [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]]'s legs the next morning for going to a goddess alone. Then [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s stone: *bring your friends, especially the blue one.* At the safe house she laid out six [[creature.grung|Grung]] sightings in [[location.the-warren|The Warren]] sewers — blue, green, one purple. No [[creature.grung|Grung]] had crossed the [[region.central-strait|Central Strait]] in living memory. She fed them a Hero's Feast and sent them down.
>
> They found directional markers and a crossbow trap that had failed to fire. The smell of black powder grew. At the coast, a boat: three [[creature.grung|Grung]] and eight barrels. [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] killed two from the ceiling. The boat went up on a fire arrow. [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] ran the fleeing purple down. It called [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] a traitor before he ended it.
>
> The captive, [[npc.felix-aho|Felix Aho]], talked. Attack points [[event.calveno-sewer-raid|Under the Festival]] crowds. Black powder against the sewer ceilings. Two days. Six ships. He told [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] that [[npc.simone-tabarnack|Simone]]'s people kill [[creature.grung|Grung]] who leave on sight, and he counted as one of them. He asked for a month of protection. They brought him to [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] in one piece. *I knew I chose the right people,* she said, and set [[npc.ruk|Ruk]] to guard him.

## Key Events

1. **Market and [[npc.master-kyzil|Kyzil]]** — Eggs sold. Silent bow and flying boots. Rooftop spar. Maw "unruly since the wreck."
2. **[[event.delmar-umberlee-bargain|Umberlee bargain]]** — [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] confesses [[faction.fisks-fleet|the Fleet]] and Pearl. [[deity.umberlee|Umberlee]] kills and returns [[npc.branca|Branca]]. Price is the Pearl; commissioners later.
3. **[[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]]'s briefing** — Six [[creature.grung|Grung]] in [[location.the-warren|The Warren]]. First crossing of the Strait in living memory. Hero's Feast.
4. **Powder boat** — Burned. Purple captive executed after naming [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] a traitor.
5. **[[npc.felix-aho|Felix]]** — Two-day bomb clock. Six ships. [[npc.simone-tabarnack|Simone]] kills leavers. Month of protection requested. Held by [[npc.ruk|Ruk]].

## Decisions Made

- **Decision:** Sell the eggs as food, not breeding stock.
  - **Alternative:** Keep or hatch them.
  - **Consequence:** 300 gp and a silent bow / flying boots. No live whip-shark young.
- **Decision:** [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] tells the crew, then goes to the shrine.
  - **Alternative:** Keep the Pearl secret.
  - **Consequence:** [[event.delmar-umberlee-bargain|The bargain]] is on the table; [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] is angry he went alone.
- **Decision:** Take [[npc.felix-aho|Felix]] alive to [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] instead of killing the last talker.
  - **Alternative:** Finish him in the sewer.
  - **Consequence:** Two-day clock and six-ship picture are known. [[npc.felix-aho|Felix]] is under Passage guard.
