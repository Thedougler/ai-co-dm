# Session 02 Report

## Summary

The party finished [[npc.barnaby-rook|Rook]] and silenced the [[vehicle.uncertainty|Surety]]'s guns. Beaumont named [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] a Friend of [[faction.the-passage|The Passage]] and warned that [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] was hunting him. [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] set galley terms. [[npc.ket|Ket]] fed and flew for [[region.murrat|Murrat]]. The prize cutter headed for [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] unmarked. A storm hit; the hull took three impacts.

> [!narration] Player-facing — read or adapt at the table.
> [[npc.barnaby-rook|Rook]] came back over the rail once. [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]]'s wind bent him; he fired at close range. Beaumont threw [[npc.bisou|Bisou]] through a gun port and she pissed in the powder. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] shot Ensign Wouters through the second port. The guns went quiet. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] threw a chair, called the wind, and put [[npc.barnaby-rook|Rook]] in the water again.
>
> Beaumont settled accounts. To [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]: a Friend of [[faction.the-passage|The Passage]]. Quietly: [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona Black-Jaw]] was looking for her lost grandson, and believed [[npc.anzolo|Anzolo]] had already been sent. He slid a Truth Stone to [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]]. [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] told the truth: former kinsmen hunted him; he stowed away to survive; nobody sent him.
>
> [[npc.geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey]] led them below. The galley smelled of bread and herbs. [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Shepherd Grigori]] asked what kind of pirates they were, told them about [[npc.capn-gorgeous|Cap'n Gorgeous]] and [[npc.rupert-knighton|Rupert Knighton]], and set one condition: anyone who wants off at the next port walks free. He mentioned they had forgotten to feed [[npc.ket|Ket]].
>
> [[npc.ket|Ket]] was a starving [[creature.moucheron|Moucheron]] [[npc.barnaby-rook|Rook]] had kept as a specimen. [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] filled a wine glass with his own blood. [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] moved on the cage; [[pc.crissdalynn-khinriss|Crissdalynn]] put herself between them and let [[npc.ket|Ket]] feed from her arm. [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] cast Hideous Laughter. They used Wouters' body as a lure and sprung the cage with Mage Hand. [[npc.ket|Ket]] fed, lifted off, and flew toward home.
>
> Crew assembled: [[npc.geoffrey-draves|Geoffrey]], [[npc.sem-holst|Sem Holst]], [[npc.alys-kuiper|Alys Kuiper]], [[npc.old-faas|Old Faas]], [[npc.thunk|Thunk]], and [[npc.noor|Noor]]. [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] would leave at [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]] for [[culture.il-gioco-delle-beffe|Il Gioco delle Beffe]]. [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] told the crew he was a Black-Jaw. [[npc.barnaby-rook|Rook]]'s cabin held 45 gp, [[item.letters-of-marque|Letters of Marque]], and a blunderbuss; under the floor, 110 gp and two garnets. Cargo held a pendant *For Mira, from the sea*, Mira's Blade (to [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]), and twenty vials [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] knew from the Alchemist's bandolier.
>
> Morning. Beaumont warned the weather toward [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]]. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] said he had a friend in [[pc.delmar-fisk|Admiral Fisk]]. [[npc.bisou|Bisou]] had lifted 15 gp of shinies; Beaumont called it fair. The [[vehicle.saltwright|Saltwright]] pulled away. The [[vehicle.uncertainty|HCS Surety]] set heading for [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]], still Crown-marked and unnamed.
>
> Two hours out a tropical storm hit from the east. Three hours in a Crown Isle opened to the northwest — probably [[region.aldenmere|Aldenmere]] — and closed. Then the hull took an impact. A second. A third. The [[vehicle.uncertainty|Surety]] lurched hard to port.

## Key Events

1. **[[vehicle.uncertainty|Surety]] guns silenced** — [[npc.bisou|Bisou]] fouled one port; [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar]] shot Wouters through the other. [[npc.barnaby-rook|Rook]] went into the water a second time.
2. **Beaumont's terms** — [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]] named Friend of [[faction.the-passage|The Passage]]. [[npc.nona-black-jaw|Nona]] is hunting him. [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] passed the Truth Stone.
3. **[[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]]'s table** — No trafficking, no cannibalism, Robin Hood near enough. Anyone who wants off at the next port walks free. [[npc.rupert-knighton|Rupert Knighton]] will send ships.
4. **[[npc.ket|Ket]] freed** — Fed, laughed down, then released. Flew toward [[faction.five-blades|Murrat / Five Blades]] country.
5. **Prize and heading** — Letters, coin, Mira's Blade, toxin vials. [[vehicle.saltwright|Saltwright]] parts. Storm, then three hull strikes.

## Decisions Made

- **Decision:** Free [[npc.ket|Ket]] instead of killing him.
  - **Alternative:** [[pc.perrin-black-jaw|Perrin]]'s first move (the longsword).
  - **Consequence:** A living [[creature.moucheron|Moucheron]] is loose and heading home; [[npc.shepherd-grigori|Grigori]] is glad.
- **Decision:** [[pc.jean-claude-tabarnack|Jean-Claude]] tells Beaumont the truth on the stone.
  - **Alternative:** Lie or refuse the stone.
  - **Consequence:** Beaumont treats him as a stowaway, not a planted agent.
- **Decision:** Keep the prize cutter Crown-marked and unnamed, heading [[settlement.calveno|Calveno]].
  - **Alternative:** Hide, scuttle, or rename at sea.
  - **Consequence:** They enter the storm still reading as Crown.
