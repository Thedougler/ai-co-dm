# Torn Crossing
![[attachments/shattered-sea/places/aruhe-the-grasslands.jpeg|Torn Crossing, grasslands hunting lane upvalley of Landing Bank]]
## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> Clear turquoise water wraps a rocky lip around a flood-torn crossing, with gold-green grass standing higher than a person on both sides of a muddy corridor of crushed stems and wet stone. Pale white stands rise in the scour, each blade broad and glass-edged, sparkles fixed on the leaves while the path beside them bends around rather than through. A dark jungle wall closes the inland edge. The river talks against the rocks, seed heads rasp where the current folds the lowest blades under, and the white stalks tick against each other when the wind touches them.

---
## At a glance
Torn Crossing is a flood-scoured hunting lane inside [[Aruhe - Grasslands]], just upvalley of [[Aruhe - River Landing Bank]]. A river lip bounds one side of the eight-foot grass. People already walked the crushed corridor toward inland smoke. Stay on a ridge or grass edge and the lane can be skipped; drop into the grass and it remains water, prints, pale glass stands, and cover that hides a standing body. Downstream returns to Landing Bank. A [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] owns this grass channel and hunts [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit|wolfrabbit]] packs that work the cut.

---
## If the party

- **Drink from the free-flowing river:** The water is drinkable.
- **Follow the prints upriver:** [[Aruhe - Line Bank|Line Bank]].
- **Walk back downstream:** [[Aruhe - River Landing Bank]].
- **Stay on a ridge or grass edge and skip the cut:** The water, prints, and hunting lane are lost.
- **Walk the eight-foot grass:** The grass is Difficult Terrain and heavily obscures beyond 10 feet. Movement leaves a crushed corridor. A clump that does not lean while the seed heads around it do is an animal.
- **Step onto a pale stand in the scour or grass:** [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]] blades hold fixed glitter and make a glassy tinkle. Contact deals `1d4` slashing per `5 feet` and counts as Difficult Terrain; spend an Action to move up to `5 feet` through it, and careful passage ignores movement damage. Dashing, falling, a shove, smashing, or burning the stand triggers a **Dexterity save — `DC 14`** for `2d6` slashing and a **Constitution save — `DC 14`** against **Glass-Choked** dust. Go around, use reach and cover, or keep weight on the surrounding grass.
- **Enter the deep water:** [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbits]] abort. The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] will not spend itself on long pursuit into the river.
- **Take fruit along the wet margin:** Fallen fruit is receiving. Plucking a living plant makes surrounding life converge, hostile. [[Taking on Aruhe]]
  - **Intelligence (Nature) — identify**
    - `DC 13` → Broad restorative function.
    - `DC 15` or *Identify* → Eating a [[Aruhe - Redheart Berry]] is a Bonus Action and regains `8d4 + 8` hit points.
- **Fish, trap, or kill to carry flesh:** Surrounding life converges, hostile. [[Taking on Aruhe]]

## Who

- [[Aruhe - Spiguar]]: This channel is its hunting range. It hunts [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit|wolfrabbit]] packs here from a living grass mantle, takes one body, and drags the kill into cover to feed. It will not willingly rush [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]], deep water, or wide bare ground.
- [[Aruhe - Wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbits]]: Packs work this bank when people and blood have already been here. When the [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] hunts them they jump [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]] rather than land in it, abort toward deep water and the bank-woods, and may bound through the print corridor. They still hit whoever is isolated, bleeding, or standing in their escape lane.

---
## What

- Eight-foot gold-green grass hides a standing body. Near the water the blades lean. Farther in, anything that does not lean is an animal.
- A muddy corridor of crushed stems and wet stone carries fresh upriver prints through the middle of the stretch.
- The river is gin-clear over pale stone and dark rock. Free-flowing water here is drinkable passage.
- Pale [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]] islands stand in the scour and in the grass, waist-high, with fixed sparkle and a thin glass tick. Trails bend around them. Old reddish smears mark a few lower stalks.
- [[Aruhe - Redheart Berry]] still hangs on short thick stems at the wet margin.
- Bank-woods and a jungle wall close the inland edge. The pack will not chase far under that shade. The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] drags meat that way before feeding.
- A thin column of smoke is visible upvalley in this same cut. It is not on this stretch.

## Where

- Grasslands site upvalley of [[Aruhe - River Landing Bank]]
- The cut continues upvalley through [[Aruhe - Grasslands]] to [[Aruhe - Line Bank|Line Bank]]
- Inland woods on the jungle side of the grass
- Downstream to [[Aruhe - River Landing Bank]]
- Smoke inland in this same valley, not a place on this stretch

---
## Why

- The prints and the water keep going inland.
- First hunting lane after the used stop on Landing Bank.
- The [[Aruhe - Spiguar]] hunts wolfrabbit packs in this cut. Pale stands, deep water, and the bank-woods are how the pack tries to leave that hunt.
