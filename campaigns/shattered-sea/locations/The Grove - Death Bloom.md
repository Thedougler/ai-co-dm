# The Grove / Death Bloom
> [!narration] Narration
> The Grove is where one tree, graves, and earth meet under a bloom unlike the surrounding excess. Roots and soil bind living wood to the dead. Leaf and earth move in deliberate quiet while distant island life continues beyond the boundary.



## At a glance
A restrained node where one tree, graves, and earth meet. The Grove / Death Bloom expresses restraint against the island's broader excess.

## Five-sentence kernel
- **Function:** A boundary and witness site for deciding whether Aruhe can be connected without being owned.
- **Fantastic:** A tree, graves, and earth form one living/dead place without collapsing into one master intelligence.
- **Conflict:** Surveyors seek a Tangle anchor while the Grove requires restraint and the island resists possession.
- **Promise:** Read graves, roots, soil, and restraint as information before choosing a route or connection.
- **Trajectory:** Claiming a living part or treating the dead as a resource breaks the node's restraint; careful passage makes a verdict possible.
