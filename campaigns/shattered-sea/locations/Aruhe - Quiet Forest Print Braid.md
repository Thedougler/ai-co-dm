# Print Braid
## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> Packed dirt splits into walking-width paths under Quiet trunks, one hard inland line holding crushed prints while other strands peel toward a gold-green grass tongue and a stone river bank. Stonepears hang like fitted grey scales beside mossed roots on that inland line, giant's guavas bow pink-gold over the grass edge, and oval fruit almost disappears under wet leaves in the wood forks. The river talks against cobbles on the lower bank, drip ticks off the roof onto packed dirt, and the small sounds of the grass stop as soon as the paths enter the wood.

---
## At a glance
Print Braid is the next Quiet seam after [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Cutoff Lip|Cutoff Lip]], still on the join of [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest]], [[Aruhe - Grasslands]], and [[Aruhe - River]]. Unlike the packed sleep-shelf and knee palisade behind it, this stretch is a dirt braid: one inland strand keeps the hard prints toward nearer smoke, and the other strands drop into wood, grass, and river stones. Stay on a ridge or turn back to Cutoff Lip and the braid can be skipped; walk the packed inland line and it remains prints, wood forks, a grass tongue, and a cobble ring at the water. The inland group kept the print strand. The wood forks have no matching feet.

## If the party

- **Drink from the free-flowing river:** The water is drinkable. That is not a claim.
- **Follow the packed inland prints:** The hard strand keeps north toward the smoke. That walk is beat 8.
- **Walk back south:** [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Cutoff Lip|Cutoff Lip]].
- **Stay on a ridge or turn back and skip the braid:** The wood forks, grass tongue, cobble ring, and print line are lost.
- **Walk a wood fork off the prints:** Wet leaf and hanging roots are Difficult Terrain. The wood heavily obscures beyond 10 feet. A body there is isolated from anyone who stayed on the inland strand.
- **Walk the grass tongue:** Eight-foot gold-green grass is Difficult Terrain and heavily obscures beyond 10 feet. Movement leaves a crushed corridor.
- **Shout into a wood fork:** The same word comes back stretched from that fork. No new information answers. The packed prints still do not enter the fork.
- **Search the cobble ring:** **Intelligence (Investigation)** or **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 12`** → Wet river stones sit in a ring on the southwest bank, with no coals and no kit. Searching them does not name who stacked them or why the inland group left the water strand. Failure → The searcher spends time at the stones and learns only that this bank is cold.
- **Take fruit along the braid:** Fallen fruit is receiving. Plucking a living plant makes surrounding life converge, hostile. [[Taking on Aruhe]]
  - **Intelligence (Nature) — identify**
    - `DC 13` → Broad function.
    - `DC 15` or *Identify* → Exact effect: [[Aruhe - Stonepear]] is Resistance to all damage for 1 minute; [[Aruhe - Giant's Guava]] sets a Primary Ability score to 25 for 1 hour; [[Aruhe - Ghost Plum]] grants the **Invisible** condition for 1 hour.
- **Fish, trap, or kill to carry flesh:** Surrounding life converges, hostile. [[Taking on Aruhe]] That water still belongs farther downslope. This braid is not [[Aruhe - River Slack Basin]].

## Who

- [[Aruhe - Deerstalker|Deer-Stalkers]] work this braid. They use a wood fork that has no matching prints, and they retreat from a grouped line on the inland strand. They will not walk [[Aruhe - Razer-Grass]].
- No one from the inland group is here now. Packed prints keep the inland strand toward the smoke. The wood forks, grass tongue, and cobble ring show no camp.

---
## What

- A braid of packed dirt paths sits under the first Quiet trunks. The inland strand is hard with crushed prints. Other strands peel into wood, grass, and river stones.
- Wood forks drop off the print line into wet leaf and hanging roots. Sight dies in a few paces. Those forks hold no matching feet.
- A gold-green grass tongue stands on the northeast edge, eight feet high where the Quiet roof breaks. [[Aruhe - Giant's Guava]] bows over that rim.
- [[Aruhe - Stonepear]] hangs on mossed roots along the inland strand. [[Aruhe - Ghost Plum]] hangs under wet leaves in the wood forks, hard to place until pollen catches its edge.
- A ring of wet cobbles sits on the southwest river bank. There is no fire and no kit. Free-flowing water there is drinkable.
- Past the first trunks the Quiet has no insect-saw and no bird chatter. Drip off leaf is the standing sound.
- A thin column of smoke stands nearer through the trees along the inland strand. It is not on this braid.

---
## Where

- **North:** Packed prints follow the inland strand through [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest]] toward inland smoke. This is the same valley, not a day's travel. The smoke is not on this braid. No established named camp on that trail yet.
- **East:** A grass tongue of [[Aruhe - Grasslands]] opens where the roof breaks. No established next named grassland site on that tongue.
- **South:** The packed line returns to [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest Cutoff Lip|Cutoff Lip]], then the bloody-bank detour toward [[Aruhe - River Slack Basin|Slack Basin]].
- **West:** Wood forks enter deeper [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest]]. The river bank and cobble ring sit southwest on [[Aruhe - River]].

---
## Why

- The packed prints keep going toward the nearer smoke.
- The braid offers wood, grass, and water as soon as the inland line splits.
- Fallen fruit hangs on the Quiet roof and the grass rim.
