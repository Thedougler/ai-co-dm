# Western Landing
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Aruhe ingest pending._

## At a glance
A jagged coral reef lies about half a mile offshore; low tide exposes teeth and one boat gap leads to a short shingle beach that takes minutes to cross end-to-end. Elsewhere the coast is cliffs, coves, or beaches trapped behind reef.

## Five-sentence kernel
- **Function:** The known sea approach and retreat edge for Aruhe's survey.
- **Fantastic:** A green country is screened by reef and stone, with only one usable boat gap in the western rim.
- **Conflict:** A crew needs a landing that can be reversed, while reef life and the Hunger Rule punish careless taking.
- **Promise:** Read tide, reef, shingle, and safe passage before committing inland.
- **Trajectory:** Bad timing traps a boat or loses a landing aid; careful observation preserves a route back to the sea.

## Aspects
`reef teeth, one gap, minutes of shingle`

## Senses
- **Sight:** Jagged coral, low-tide teeth, one boat gap, shingle, cliffs and coves beyond.
- **Sound:** Surf on coral, hull strain, and reef movement.
- **Touch / smell:** Salt spray, sharp coral, loose shingle, wet stone.

## Who is here
- **Reef fauna, fiction only:** giant sharks, giant crabs, blood hawks, Reef Skulls.
- Reef Skulls are dog-sized hermit crabs in hull pieces or skulls; they bury in shingle.

## Player verbs
`pilot, wait, observe, cross, mark, retreat, study reef movement, keep living things unclaimed`.

## Hooks
- Find the boat gap at a useful tide without turning reef fauna into possessions.
- Identify whether a landing aid is loose stone, shed shell, or a claimed living thing.
- Carry the survey inland while preserving a retreat route.

## Secrets (DM)
- The western landing is one edge of an island hundreds of miles deep; Karath's closeness does not make it a shortcut to Aruhe's heart.

## Mechanics and return
- Reef and shingle are hazardous terrain; telegraph low-tide teeth and trapped beaches. No reef-fauna mechanics are included.
- **Return state:** tide, gap visibility, boat condition, reef disturbance, retreat route.

## Reef hazards
- Aggressive coral, giant sharks, giant crabs, blood hawks, and Reef Skulls make the reef and shingle a landing problem rather than a neutral beach.
