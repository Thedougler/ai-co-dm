# Calven / Calveno
> [!narration] Narration
> Calven is the oldest-settled Crown Island; Calveno, its canal city, threads bridges and markets between the harbour and Le Paludi below. Dravosi flags mark the waterfront while Tessarine ledgers follow the traffic, and locks, bells, salt, and wet stone give the city its working texture.
