# Aruhe / Hungry Isle
> [!narration] Narration
> Aruhe, the Hungry Isle, is a Midchain wilderness of shore, scrub, tracks, and higher ground where the island eats what lands on it. Its open-water approach reaches survey ground and a proposed Tangle anchor, with the landing edge offering a route back to the sea.
## At a glance
The Hungry Isle eats what lands; the cold open pairs a purple Grung with wolfrabbits. It is a survey destination and proposed [[Tangle]] anchor, with [[Nona Black-Jaw]] asking Perrin to survey it.
## Five-sentence kernel
- **Function:** A wilderness landmark where landing, surveying, and anchoring Passage routes are all consequential.
- **Fantastic:** The island eats what lands on it.
- **Conflict:** Surveyors need a viable Tangle anchor, the island’s appetite rejects careless arrival, and strange local life occupies the same ground.
- **Promise:** Explore a hungry living landmark, learn its boundaries, and decide whether an anchor is worth the risk.
- **Trajectory:** A bad landing feeds the island or destroys the survey; a careful one makes a route possible while changing who can use it.
## 3Fs and contradiction
- **Fantastic:** ground and appetite are inseparable.
- **Familiar:** shore, scrub, high ground, tracks, and weather.
- **Functional:** landing sites, anchor points, and animal behaviour reveal safe/unsafe travel.
- **Contradiction:** it is a destination for connection that punishes contact.
- **Signatures:** things vanish where they land; purple Grung; wolfrabbit tracks and movement.
