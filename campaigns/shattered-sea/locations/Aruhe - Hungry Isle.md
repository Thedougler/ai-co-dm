# Aruhe / Hungry Isle
> [!narration] Narration
> A country-scale island in the eastern Midchain: roughly five hundred miles end to end and about a hundred fifty across at the waist. From offshore it reads as a green nation behind a jagged reef and stone rim — canopy, basalt, terraces, grass, and rivers — with no towns, fields, roads, or smoke. One boat gap opens the western edge; the heart sits hundreds of miles inland.



## At a glance
A country-scale island in the eastern Midchain: roughly 500 miles end-to-end and about 150 miles across at its waist. From offshore it looks like a green nation behind a rim of reef and stone, with no towns, fields, roads, or smoke; [[Karath]] is close to one edge, not to the heart hundreds of miles inland.

## Five-sentence kernel
- **Function:** A vast volcanic wilderness where survey, passage, shelter, and a possible [[Tangle]] anchor must coexist with a living island.
- **Fantastic:** Old basalt, vents, and lava tubes under the soil support too much life, while the island distinguishes passage from possession.
- **Conflict:** People need to cross, watch, shelter, and map Aruhe; the island reacts when anyone claims living things, and [[The Blight]] presses for its joined balance.
- **Promise:** Discover a country-sized ecology by choosing routes, testing boundaries, and deciding whether connection is possible without ownership.
- **Trajectory:** If nobody intervenes, the island remains abundant and internally balanced while every attempted possession draws aligned local hostility; a careless anchor fails or changes the pressure around it.

## 3Fs and contradiction
- **Fantastic:** A green nation with no civilization visible from offshore, whose living systems answer possession without being a hive mind.
- **Familiar:** Reef gap, beach, terraces, rainforest, grassland, rivers, lake, graves, caves, and volcanic stone.
- **Functional:** The island's edges, watercourses, ecological boundaries, and the distinction between passage and possession determine travel.
- **Contradiction:** Aruhe offers abundant food and drink yet punishes the act of taking living abundance for oneself.
- **Signatures:** a jagged reef rim with one boat gap; ancient terraces and extreme fruit; silence or converging life where a claim is made.

- **Loop:** Western Landing → Old Gardens → Grasslands / river valleys → Clear Lake → Grove / Death Bloom → Lava Tubes → Rot → Quiet → Old Gardens; the exact walk is long and country-scale.
- **Bypass:** Observe from offshore, use an existing free-flowing spring/stream/cave seepage, or survey from the edge; safer, but less certain about the heart.
- **Retreat:** Return along an unclaimed route to the landing and sea. A possession claim turns retreat into a pressure problem, not a simple withdrawal.
- **Tradeoff:** Shore routes are direct but reef-exposed; water routes offer drink and direction but invite the temptation to fish; inland routes reveal ecology at the cost of distance and exposure.

## Ecology and balance
- **Old volcanic body:** Basalt, vents, and lava tubes run beneath soil; no single master predator controls the island.
- **Niches:** The island is too much of everything, not a place of lack. Boundaries between niches are part of its balance.
- **The Quiet:** Rainforest silence means small life has been eaten out; [[aruhe/The Quiet]] is not merely a quiet road.
- **The Grove / Death Bloom:** A tree, graves, and earth meet in a restrained node rather than expressing the island's full excess.

## Information economy
- **Operational:** [[Aruhe Hunger Rule]] gives the precise distinction: passage is tolerated; possession of living things is answered.
- **Physical trace:** A plucked, uprooted, trapped, fished-for, or killed-and-carried thing marks a claim; the surrounding life converges hostilely.
- **Directional:** Reef rim and one gap, terrace lines, water flow, silence boundaries, decay, graves, and volcanic openings point to the child nodes.
- **Historical:** The terraces have unknown builders; no town, road, field, or smoke trail explains the island from offshore.
- **Failure cost:** Lost time, gear, position, shelter, or route safety; a failed observation should not erase the distinction between passage and possession.

## Pressure clock: survey / appetite / balance (4)
1. **Approach:** Reef and shingle reveal whether the party is observing, crossing, or trying to take.
2. **Test:** A claimed living thing draws converging hostility from surrounding life.
3. **Boundary:** A niche or water route is disturbed; the party must retreat, repair, or continue without possession.
4. **Verdict:** The survey supports, rejects, or makes costly a Tangle anchor; Aruhe remains a living balance rather than a captured asset.

Advance on plucking, uprooting, trapping, fishing for self, killing to carry flesh, loud disturbance, or repeated anchor marks. Slow with observation, passage, shelter, and deliberate retreat.

## Location moves
**Move:** The reef gap becomes the only visible route. **Trigger:** a landing attempt ignores the rim. **Result:** reef hazards and trapped beaches narrow the approach. **Opportunity:** study tides and keep the boat outside. **Consequence:** arrival is safer only if the party accepts a short beach and no easy alternate landing.

**Move:** Surrounding life converges. **Trigger:** anyone claims a living thing. **Result:** aligned hostility gathers without a hive mind. **Opportunity:** release, return, or change the action from possession to passage. **Consequence:** the party's route and shelter become contested.

**Move:** A niche boundary asserts itself. **Trigger:** loud conflict, repeated marking, or prolonged rest. **Result:** silence, decay, water, or large life changes what can be safely used. **Opportunity:** follow the ecological clue. **Consequence:** an easier route closes while another becomes legible.

**Move:** The anchor test refuses ownership. **Trigger:** an attempt to make Aruhe a Tangle asset. **Result:** the marked point holds only as passage, shifts, or vanishes. **Opportunity:** propose a non-possessive connection. **Consequence:** the Passage receives a route verdict, not a claim on the island.

## Geography ingest — continuity note
The dump confirms Aruhe's near-edge geography: from [[Karath]], its landing is one reef gap and a short shingle beach, close enough at the channel to seem swimmable, but Karath is only near Aruhe's rim. The island's heart remains hundreds of miles inland. Grung patrol the western reefs but will not set foot on the sand; a wrecked raiding hull sits above the tideline. Nothing here is a town: the island's bands (Old Gardens, Quiet, Grasslands, Rot, Clear Lake, and Grove) and lava tubes are an ecological country, not settlements.
