# Aruhe / Hungry Isle
![[attachments/shattered-sea/places/aruhe-hungry-isle.jpeg|Aruhe / Hungry Isle]]
## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> From offshore Aruhe is a green volcanic country in deep blue water. Forested ridges fold around a dark crater lake, gold-tan bands cut the lower slopes, pale beaches break the cliffs, and white water works a reef rim. No town, field, road, or smoke marks the island. The green and the gold crowd every slope, more life than a country this size should hold.

---
## At a glance
Aruhe is a country-scale island in the eastern [[Midchain]]: about 500 miles end to end and about 150 miles across at its waist. [[Karath]] sits close to one western edge, not to the heart hundreds of miles inland. [[Uncertainty]] is at or around the island and remains mobile. Child bands hold the inland walk: [[Aruhe - Beach|Western Landing]], [[Aruhe - Old Gardens]], [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest|the Quiet]], [[Aruhe - Grasslands]], [[Aruhe - River]], [[Aruhe - The Rot]], [[Aruhe - Clear Lake]], [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], and [[Aruhe - Lava Tubes]]. Observe from offshore for a safer, thinner survey; commit through the reef gap to reach the living country. [[Survey Aruhe as a Tangle anchor]] is open. No anchor decision has been made.

## If the party

- **Land at the known sea approach:** [[Aruhe - Beach|Western Landing]].
- **Observe from offshore:** Keep a safer position and lose inland information.
- **Follow the terrace route inland:** [[Aruhe - Old Gardens]] into [[Aruhe - Grasslands]].
- **Take the green route under the roof:** [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest|the Quiet]] toward [[Aruhe - The Rot|the Rot]].
- **Follow water uphill:** [[Aruhe - Grasslands]] into [[Aruhe - River]] and [[Aruhe - Clear Lake]].
- **Continue toward the last clearing:** [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]].
- **Descend into the volcanic body:** [[Aruhe - Lava Tubes]] from the Rot or the Grove.
- **Stay on a ridge, terrace edge, or open water:** Whole bands can be skipped; the heart stays hundreds of miles inland.
- **Drink free-flowing spring, stream, or cave seepage:** The water is drinkable.
- **Use loose stone or shed shell:** Nonliving take does not turn the island.
- **Take a living thing:** Pluck, uproot, trap, fish for self, or kill to carry flesh. Surrounding life converges, hostile. [[Taking on Aruhe]]
- **Survey a heading at country scale:**
  - **Wisdom (Survival) — `DC 15`** → Keep a useful heading toward a named band.
  - Failure → Lose time and position; the island remains the same size.
- **Read a niche boundary from silence, decay, water, grass, or graves:**
  - **Intelligence (Nature) — `DC 15`** → Name the adjoining band and the safer edge.
  - Failure → The next country is still there; the party spends time confirming it on foot.
- **Mark a [[Tangle]] survey without taking living things:** The mark holds only as passage. A possessive anchor fails or changes the pressure around it.

## Who

- [[The Blight]] is joined to the island as pressure, not a hive mind. The walking form and memorial live at [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]].
- Two Aruhe survivors are aboard [[Uncertainty]]. They report roughly 100–150 people came off the raid ships, most are dead, the island is taking the rest, fruit and island stuff kill people, open-water fishing seemed safer, and Grung are specifically marked.
- Inland survivors remain inland. The wreck field and taking-places are active survey pressure, not a resolved rescue.
- A landing party found terraces, wolfrabbits, black lotus, spore plants, dangerous caterpillars, blood and skulls at fruit sites, and at least six dead near taking-places. Jean-Claude killed a spore plant; an invisible [[Great Crown Squid]] rose from the canopy, cut Delmar, and tried to pull people in. Illusion and flight got the party back over the terraces. The squid stopped at the tree line.
- Purple Grung patrol the western reefs and will not set foot on the sand.
- A wrecked raiding hull sits above the tideline at [[Aruhe - Beach|Western Landing]].

## What

- Old basalt, vents, and lava tubes run under soil. No single master predator controls the island. Niches stay in balance by owning adjoining country; run from one and you are usually standing in the next.
- The island is too much of everything. Familiar plants and animals keep their own jobs. Growth, reproduction, healing, adaptation, and rot lost their brakes.
- Luminous roots run wrist-thick to thigh-thick from the shelf above [[Aruhe - Clear Lake]] through [[Aruhe - The Rot]] and stop at both unmarked graves in [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]]. Surface forest reaches down into [[Aruhe - Lava Tubes]] as pale root curtains.
- Fallen fruit and genuine offerings can be received. A living claim draws aligned local hostility. [[Taking on Aruhe]]

### Claims map - living stock
Aruhe is a set of claims, not one king. [[The Blight]] gardens pressure; it does not ride these bodies.

- **Closed canopy:** [[Great Crown Squid]] owns the crowns and selects isolated prey.
- **Hole in the canopy:** big [[Bloodhawk|Bloodhawks]] own the thermal and open sky; the small beach size-cap form is [[Small Blood Hawk|Small Blood Hawks]].
- **Eight-foot grass and valley floor:** [[Terror-Bird|Terror-Birds]] hold each open quarter-mile.
- **Occupied river and lake:** [[Aruhe River Otter|river otter]] families own their water.
- **Main tubes:** [[Blackrail]] owns miles of the cave road.
- **Beach shallows:** [[Reef Skull|Reef Skulls]] and [[Small Blood Hawk|Small Blood Hawks]] work the shingle and tide-meat.
- **First terraces:** [[Wolfrabbit|Wolfrabbits]], [[Grinning Ape|Grinning Apes]], and [[Vine Lash|Vine Lashes]] define the approach.
- **Mute floor:** [[Deer-Stalker|Deer-Stalkers]], [[Thornback|Thornbacks]], and waiting plants such as [[Strangler Fig|Strangler Figs]] hold the Quiet and Rot floor.

### Flora frame and zones
The plants are the country. Aruhe takes real plants and removes their brakes; it does not invent a new design.

- **Tideline:** Landing is shingle, not soil. [[Tideline moss]] stains the edge that survivors treat as the island's claim line; terrace risers begin beyond it.
- **Old terraces:** Feral yam vines and ordinary citrus fill broken steps and algae-green irrigation ditches. [[Redheart Berry]], [[Lion Citrus]], [[Giant's Guava]], hot blooms, [[Vine Lash]], and [[Young Snakewood]] belong here. See [[Aruhe - Old Gardens]].
- **Mute jungle:** Shield leaves, thick trunks, hanging roots, and sight that dies in a few paces. [[Strangler Fig]] and adult [[Snakewood]] own pinch points and trails; [[Stonepear]], [[Ghost Plum]], and [[Stillbloom]] mark the passage. See [[Aruhe - Quiet Forest]].
- **River cuts:** Lake-fed openings carry eight-foot gold grass. Near water the grass leans; farther in, anything that does not lean is an animal. [[Razer-Grass]], [[Quickeye Berry]], and [[Breathmelon]] belong to this open water country. See [[Aruhe - Grasslands]] and [[Aruhe - River]].
- **Sick interior:** Black leaf edges, failing bark, impossible fungi, and luminous roots all point upslope. [[Whisper Fig]], [[Skybladder]], [[Rotheart]], [[Corpsewood]], and sick larger Snakewood belong to the Rot. See [[Aruhe - The Rot]].
- **Lava tubes:** Pale curtains of living root hang through the ceilings and drip into the tubes. See [[Aruhe - Lava Tubes]].
- **Last clearing:** Luminous roots stop at two unmarked graves. Black flowers ring too-green grass; the Death Bloom tree, flowers, graves, and bound soil form one memorial. See [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]].

## Why

- Survey a possible [[Tangle]] anchor without treating a living island as property. [[Survey Aruhe as a Tangle anchor]]
- Cross, watch, shelter, and map a country-sized ecology while keeping a route back to the sea.
- Learn what fruit and water offer without plucking, uprooting, trapping, fishing for self, or killing to carry flesh.
- Aruhe's excess is the success of [[The Blight|Hinewai]]'s preservation. After [[Karath]] captured the elf archdruid and forced her to enlarge vermin, she fled with [[The Unnamed Companion]]; he drowned in the half-mile channel. She carried him inland, buried him beneath a fruit tree, took the offered fruit as a vow, and later fused herself to the land in a second grave. The memorial became the Death Bloom and the island's law. Everywhere beyond [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]], life was prevented from finishing. Take a living thing and animals attack.
