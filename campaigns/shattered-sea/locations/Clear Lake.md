## At a glance
A clear inland lake where water offers rest and direction, but fishing for oneself crosses the Hunger Rule. The lake is a meeting point for river valleys, large water life, and the Grove route.

## Five-sentence kernel
- **Function:** A freshwater landmark and route junction for surveyors following Aruhe's water.
- **Fantastic:** The water is accessible without being owned, while the lake's living abundance reacts to attempted possession.
- **Conflict:** People need to drink, shelter, and map; fishing, trapping, or killing to carry flesh makes the lake's life converge.
- **Promise:** Navigate clear water, observe enormous life, and prove a route can use a resource without claiming it.
- **Trajectory:** A non-possessive camp keeps the lake open; a fishing claim turns rest into a convergence point.

## Aspects
`clear water, enormous life, temptation to fish`

## Senses
- **Sight:** Clear water, shore, channels, and movement below or beyond the surface.
- **Sound:** Water, shore movement, and long submerged disturbances.
- **Touch / smell:** Cool drinkable water, wet stone, and lake air.

## Topology
- `[[Grasslands - River Valleys]] -- free-flowing water route --> Clear Lake`.
- `Clear Lake -- shore and watercourse route --> [[The Grove - Death Bloom]]`.
- **Bypass:** Stay on the river valley edge, avoiding the lake's large-life signs.
- **Retreat:** Return along the water without fishing, trapping, or carrying flesh.

## Who is here
- **Fiction only:** Great Crown Squid (60–80 feet, with tentacles) and Aruhe River Otter (12 feet).
- Water and fish are not an item or monster mechanics package here.

## Player verbs
`drink, observe, cross, shelter, map shore, study movement, refuse possession, retreat`.

## Hooks
- Watch a Great Crown Squid's scale without turning observation into a claim.
- Track the 12-foot Aruhe River Otter along the water route.
- Prove the lake can support passage and shelter without fishing for self.

## Secrets (DM)
- The lake's size and life are part of Aruhe's excess; there is no hidden poison trick in its drinkable water.

## Mechanics and return
- Treat water, shore, and crossing honestly; no squid or otter mechanics are included.
- **Return state:** water access, lake disturbance, shore route, and any possession trigger avoided or made.

## Hinewai continuity
The lakeward shelf is the direction of the original loss. Luminous roots run out from the Grove above Clear Lake, wrist-thick to thigh-thick in the Rot, and point home toward the memorial. They stop before the two graves. The lake remains a route and visible landmark, not the location of a portable phylactery.
