# Vel Orn
> [!narration] Narration
> Vel Orn's hidden Waveservant shrine keeps its ledger away from the public dock. Black stone, wet rope, and the absence left by a stolen pearl mark the place.

## At a glance
The shrine where [[Delmar Fisk]]'s commission stole the [[Pearl of Souls]]; feels hidden, devotional, and suddenly robbed.

## Canon
- The Pearl was one of seven instruments of [[Umberlee]], kept in shrine custody for years.
- [[Chain Council]] commissioners used Fisk's five-ship fleet to take it; the [[Tessarine Concordat]] used the Council as a cut-out to keep it from [[Aldric Drave]].
