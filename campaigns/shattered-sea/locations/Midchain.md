# Midchain
> [!narration] Narration
> Midchain is a crowded chain of small tropical islands, free ports, reefs, and local councils. Low islands and busy jetties break into reef channels marked by flags and pilots, while surf on coral, brine, pitch, wet sailcloth, and cooking smoke follow the routes between them.
## At a glance
Densely packed small tropical islands, free ports, reefs, and local councils; feels busy beyond enforcement.
## Five-sentence kernel
- **Function:** A crowded middle route where island councils and free ports move people and goods outside strong Crown control.
- **Fantastic:** Reefs, islands, and local knowledge make the same sea offer several different routes.
- **Conflict:** Councils want autonomy, the Crown wants records, Passage wants safe movement, and Grung clans expand through raids.
- **Promise:** Read local power quickly, choose a route through dangerous water, and turn partial information into leverage.
- **Trajectory:** The taken trail and Crown response make “thin enforcement” either a refuge or a pretext for occupation.
## 3Fs and contradiction
- **Fantastic:** A chain of small islands functions like a city with water for streets.
- **Familiar:** Docks, markets, reefs, ferries, and councils.
- **Functional:** Local pilots, harbour debts, and family ties matter more than distant law.
- **Contradiction:** Crowded enough to hide in; connected enough to spread news instantly.
- **Signatures:** reef marks; council tokens; boats choosing different channels around the same shoal.
## Place truth and current situation
- **Canon:** Midchain links [[Verdant Teeth]], [[Sparhold]], and [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]]; the Grung fleet’s trail points toward Sparhold.
- **DM seed (unconfirmed):** each free port recognizes a different token or debt, allowing social access without a single regional government.
- **At stake:** whether Midchain shelters the taken or becomes the Crown’s next controlled corridor.
## Aspects
`reef maze, council dock, news outruns authority`
## Senses
- **Sight:** low islands, crowded jetties, reef breaks, and signal flags.
- **Sound:** pilots calling depths, council bells, surf on coral, and competing dock offers.
- **Smell:** brine, fruit, pitch, wet sailcloth, and cooking smoke.
## Topology
- `[[Central Strait]] -- marked lane (fast, exposed, papers) --> Midchain`.
- `Midchain -- reef route (slow, hidden, pilot-dependent) --> [[Sparhold]]`.
- `Midchain -- open water (direct, weather-exposed, reveals approach) --> [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]]`.
- `Midchain -- jungle-water route (costly, information-rich, Grung attention) --> [[Verdant Teeth]]`.
- **Loop:** Sparhold → council dock → Aruhe → a different reef channel back.
- **Bypass:** unregistered pilot or Passage contact avoids a main dock for a favour.
- **Retreat:** council dock gives shelter while creating a record or debt.
- **Environmental connection:** reef surface versus channel depth changes speed, visibility, and escape.

## Information economy
- **Secret:** the trail from 314+ taken people is moving through Midchain toward Sparhold.
- **Witness:** dockworker or pilot saw unusual groups moved under guard (**DM seed, unconfirmed**).
- **Trace:** reef marks, changed wakes, or discarded tincture vessel (**DM seed, unconfirmed**).
- **Document:** council ledger records a vessel under a false cargo description (**DM seed, unconfirmed**).
- **Failure cost:** direction arrives with lost privacy, time, or a favour.
## Pressure
- **Quiet:** ordinary traffic and bargaining.
- **Alert:** raid report spreads; prices and questions change.
- **Contested:** Crown officers and councils dispute a dock.
- **Transformed:** council invites occupation or coordinates refusal.
## Location moves
**Move:** council closes a channel. **Trigger:** Grung evidence reaches the dock. **Result:** markers vanish and pilots demand proof. **Opportunity:** find the pilot who knows the old route. **Consequence:** bypass becomes a relationship.

**Move:** trail relocates. **Trigger:** party lingers or exposes a witness. **Result:** fresh wake or false lead. **Opportunity:** compare physical and social clues. **Consequence:** Sparhold clock advances on the wrong movement.

**Move:** Crown presence arrives. **Trigger:** three public incidents or a front step. **Result:** inspections expand. **Opportunity:** councils and Passage coordinate resistance. **Consequence:** every return shows less autonomy.
## Node keys
### M1 Council dock — information gate
**First impression:** boats queue beside competing local signs. **Invitation:** trade a token or question a pilot. **Affordances:** bargain, study wakes, hide cargo, compare ledgers. **Obstacle:** inconsistent authority; counterplay is proof or relationship; bypass is reef travel. **Payoff:** route, warning, or debt.
### M2 Reef cut — route decision
**First impression:** shallow water and broken sightlines. **Affordances:** pilot, anchor, observe, reroute. **Obstacle:** shallows telegraphed by birds, foam, and missing markers; open water bypasses at exposure cost. **Payoff:** time versus information.
### M3 Rumour crossing — social loop
**First impression:** news changes hands at any port, ferry, or safe kitchen. **Affordances:** Influence, trade, plant a story, verify. **Payoff:** advance the trail or alter who watches.
