# Clear Lake

## Art
![[attachments/shattered-sea/places/aruhe-the-clear-lake.jpeg]]
> [!narration] Narration
> Clear Lake fills a cup of steep green walls, wide enough that the far bank thins to a tree line and pale cliff scars. A shelf of black hexagonal stone steps into water so clear that schools of small fish show over round pale rocks and moss. Marsh grass and a slow brown channel gather along one side, then the color falls into deep blue where a long dark body lies under the surface. Cool wet air comes off the lake, and the stone underfoot is solid enough to drink from, walk, or keep along the shore toward the higher green.

## At a glance

An inland freshwater basin at the uphill end of Aruhe's rivers. Drinking, shelter, and mapping are passage; fishing for oneself is an [[Aruhe Hunger Rule]] claim. The water is occupied otter country, and enormous life uses the deep.

## Five-sentence kernel
- **Function:** Freshwater landmark and route junction for anyone following Aruhe's water inland.
- **Fantastic:** The lake can be drunk from and camped beside, while its living abundance answers attempted possession.
- **Conflict:** Surveyors need water, shelter, and a heading; fishing, trapping, or killing to carry flesh turns rest into a convergence.
- **Promise:** Use clear water, watch enormous life, and prove a route can take a resource without claiming it.
- **Trajectory:** A non-possessive camp keeps the lake open. A fishing claim makes the basin a pressure point.

## Aspects
`columnar shelf, glass shallows, occupied deep`

## Senses
- **Sight:** Black hexagonal shelf, glass-clear shallows over pale stones, schools of small fish, marsh grass and brown channels, deep blue water, a long dark body farther out, far bank thinning into trees and pale cliff scars.
- **Sound:** Quiet lapping at the shelf, marsh insects, and long submerged disturbances.
- **Touch / smell:** Cool drinkable water, wet stone, moss, marsh mud, and lake air.

## Local nodes
- **Columnar shelf:** Drink, camp, and survey. Solid basalt underfoot. Free-flowing lake water is safe to drink.
- **Glass shallows:** The bottom and the fish are visible. Wading is possible. Taking fish is a claim.
- **Marsh spit:** Slow brown channels and reeds along one bank. Follow without damming or trapping.
- **Deep water:** Crossing and observation. Entering the water is otter play. Enormous life at squid scale uses this country.
- **Outlet fans:** Downhill into [[Aruhe - River]]. Hours-scale water road; otter territory continues.
- **Lakeward shelf / higher green:** Shore and watercourse toward [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]]. Luminous roots begin above this shelf.

## Topology
- `[[Aruhe - Grasslands]] -- follow water uphill --> [[Aruhe - River]] -- source --> Clear Lake`.
- `Clear Lake -- outlet fans --> [[Aruhe - River]]`.
- `Clear Lake -- shore and watercourse route --> [[Aruhe - Memorial Grove]]`.

- **Bypass:** Stay on a river-valley edge and skip the basin. Safer, loses the lake's scale and large-life signs.
- **Retreat:** Return along the water without fishing, trapping, or carrying flesh.
- **Tradeoff:** The shelf is the easy camp and drink. The marsh is slower cover. Open water is the heading and the claim-temptation.

## Who is here
- **Occupied water:** [[Aruhe River Otter]] families. The lake family is larger than a river family; its matriarch is older than the wreck on the beach. Play, Hunt, and family memory live on the otter note. The lake matriarch is Huge and not statted there.
- **Deep water / adjacent canopy:** [[Great Crown Squid]] (60–80 feet, with tentacles). Combat math lives on the squid note.
- **Margins:** [[Unsaid Macaw]] along orchards and lake edges.
- Wet margins may hold [[Breathmelon]], as on the river. Fish are ecology, not a loot or monster package.

## Player verbs
`drink, observe, camp, map shore, follow marsh, study movement, refuse possession, cross, retreat`

## Hooks
- Watch squid-scale life without turning observation into a claim.
- Track the twelve-foot lake otters along the water route, and learn that entering the water is already a game.
- Prove the lake can support passage and shelter without fishing for self.
- Follow the shore toward higher green, or the outlet downhill, and record the basin as a durable heading.

## If they
- **Drink or camp on the shelf without taking living things:** the lake stays open.
- **Wade the shallows without taking fish:** otters notice; staying in the water is play.
- **Enter deep water:** otter play, and the long dark body is no longer only a distant shape.
- **Fish, trap, or kill to carry flesh:** [[Aruhe Hunger Rule]] claim. Surrounding life converges. Not a hive mind.
- **Follow marsh or shore toward higher green:** Grove route. Roots become visible in [[Aruhe - The Rot]].
- **Follow the outlet downhill:** [[Aruhe - River]].
- **Take a loose stone or drink free-flowing water:** allowed. Living fish, plants, and flesh are not.

## Threats (DM)
- Passage here is drink, watch, shelter, and map. Possession is plucking, uprooting, trapping, fishing for self, or killing to carry flesh. Procedure: [[Aruhe Hunger Rule]].
- Anything entering the water participates in the otters' play. Anything taken from the lake belongs to their family. If a family member is harmed, play stops: ten seconds of silence, then one person is struck from below. Details: [[Aruhe River Otter]].
- The deep holds enormous life at squid scale. Do not run squid math from this note.
- Marsh mud and reed banks can be Difficult Terrain. The shelf is honest stone. Treat crossing as real distance, not a skip.

## Secrets (DM)
- There is no hidden poison trick in the drinkable water. The danger is possession, not the drink.
- The lake's gin-clear look is tended, not natural. Otters cull grazers, keep shade off the water, and patrol the banks, the same garden-work as on the river.
- The lakeward shelf is the inland direction of Hinewai's original loss, not the drowning site. The companion drowned in the half-mile channel off [[Karath]]. She carried him inland. The memorial is the Grove, not this basin.
- Luminous roots run out from the Grove above Clear Lake, wrist-thick to thigh-thick in the Rot, and point home toward the memorial. They stop before the two graves. The lake is a route and a visible landmark, not a portable phylactery.

## Mechanics and return
- Use Study or Search for shore routes, current, otter sign, and the difference between free-flowing water and a living take. Use Utilize only for nonliving tools (loose stone, shed shell, existing camp craft).
- No otter, squid, fruit, or fish stat blocks belong on this page.
- **Return state:** water access, whether anyone entered the water, lake disturbance, shore route used, and any possession trigger avoided or made.
