# Crown Islands
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: place portrait pending._
## At a glance
Five large mountainous northern islands with fortified deep-water harbours and pale stone forts; feels occupied and watchful.
## Five-sentence kernel
- **Function:** The strongest Dravosi foothold and administrative counterweight to Midchain’s local councils.
- **Fantastic:** Mountain walls and deep-water forts turn a chain of islands into a system of gates.
- **Conflict:** Dravosi law and colonial order compete with Tessarine debt, Passage movement, and local survival.
- **Promise:** Read authority in architecture and paperwork, then comply, exploit, or evade it.
- **Trajectory:** Crisis in Midchain or the Maw lets the Crown convert inspection into occupation.
## 3Fs and contradiction
- **Fantastic:** Harbour forts control water far beyond their walls.
- **Familiar:** Ports, roads, officials, merchants, and mountain passes.
- **Functional:** Naval law, credit, and compulsory stops make movement legible.
- **Contradiction:** Most secure arc; least private one.
- **Signatures:** pale stone forts; deep-water harbour mouths; Crown papers crossing Tessarine accounts.
## Current situation
- **Canon:** connects to [[Central Strait]], [[Calder's Tooth and Port Tidefall]], and [[Calven and Calveno]]; strongest Dravosi foothold.
- **DM seed (unconfirmed):** fort-to-fort signals and mountain roads coordinate officials without a visible capital.

## Information economy
- **Secret:** authority is layered, not singular.
- **Witness:** Dravosi officer explains naval law; Tessarine creditor explains obligation.
- **Trace:** cargo bears both Crown clearance and Tessarine claim.
- **Document:** papers and debt records disagree over who may move it.
- **Failure cost:** delay, flagging, or obligation; contradiction remains visible.
## Pressure
