# Landing Bank
![[attachments/shattered-sea/places/aruhe-the-grasslands.jpeg|Landing Bank, first river-cut grass mouth after the terraces]]
## Overview
> [!narration] Narration
> Clear turquoise water runs along a gold-green bank where grass stands higher than a person, seed heads leaning where the current folds the lowest blades under. Pale stone shows through the shallows, and submerged grass bends with the flow. A muddy track of footprints cuts up the bank through crushed stems, past a ring of stones holding grey ash, a closed wooden crate, and orange fruit already off the plant. Glossy red berries still hang on short wet stems at the waterline, each round body pulled into deep ridges under curled leaves. Mossy terrace walls rise out of the trees on the inland side. The river keeps talking against the bank, and split skins smell sharp and sweet in the dirt.

---
## At a glance
Landing Bank is the first grassland mouth of [[Aruhe - River]] after [[Aruhe - Old Gardens]], where the water cuts [[Aruhe - Grasslands]] open. People already stopped here and walked upriver. Stay on the terrace edge and the cut can be skipped; drop into the grass and it remains water, prints, and the used stop. Downstream returns through the terraces toward [[Aruhe - Beach|Western Landing]].

## If the party

- **Drink from the free-flowing river:** The water is drinkable.
- **Follow the prints upriver:** [[Aruhe - Torn Crossing|Torn Crossing]].
- **Climb the terrace stone inland of the prints:** [[Aruhe - Old Gardens]].
- **Walk downstream through the terrace line:** [[Aruhe - Old Gardens]] toward [[Aruhe - Beach|Western Landing]] and [[Uncertainty]].
- **Stay on the terrace edge and skip the cut:** The water, prints, and used stop are lost.
- **Walk the eight-foot grass:** The grass is Difficult Terrain and heavily obscures beyond 10 feet. Movement leaves a crushed corridor.
- **Search the crate and fire scrape:** **Intelligence (Investigation)** or **Wisdom (Perception) — `DC 12`** → The fire scrape is cold ash in cleared dirt, and the crate can be taken. Opening it does not name how many people passed or whether they claimed fruit. Do not invent contents. Failure → The searcher spends time at the stop and learns only that it is fresh.
- **Take fruit along the wet margin:** Fallen fruit is receiving. Plucking a living plant makes surrounding life converge, hostile. [[Taking on Aruhe]]
  - **Intelligence (Nature) — identify**
    - `DC 13` → Broad restorative function.
    - `DC 15` or *Identify* → Eating a [[Aruhe - Redheart Berry]] is a Bonus Action and regains `8d4 + 8` hit points.
- **Eat a fallen [[Aruhe - Redheart Berry]]:** Bonus Action, regain `8d4 + 8` hit points.
- **Fish, trap, or kill to carry flesh:** Surrounding life converges, hostile. [[Taking on Aruhe]]

## Who

- No one is here now. The prints, ash, crate, and fruit skins show people already left upriver.
- Otter families hold [[Aruhe - River]] farther upvalley. This mouth is not their current hole.

---
## What

- Eight-foot gold-green grass hides a standing body. Near the water the blades lean; the used stop sits in a crushed corridor of prints.
- The river is gin-clear over pale stone, with submerged grass in the current. Free-flowing water here is drinkable passage.
- A ring of stones holds grey ash 15 feet from the water. A closed wooden crate, orange whole fruit, split skins, and a couple of bowls sit beside it. Crate contents are unknown. Do not invent them.
- [[Aruhe - Redheart Berry]] still hangs on short thick stems at the wet margin. Fruit already on the ground is off the plant. How many people passed, and whether they claimed living fruit, stay unknown.
- Mossy terrace walls and stacked garden stone stand 20 feet inland of the prints, with split orange fruit and pink-gold globes on the lower steps.
- A thin column of smoke is visible upvalley in this same cut. It is not on this bank.
