## At a glance
Sentinel monastery watching the Maw; feels disciplined, wind-cut, and full of withheld answers.
## Five-sentence kernel
- **Function:** A Sentinel watch and pilgrimage site overlooking the Drowned Maw.
- **Fantastic:** A monastery’s discipline is aimed at a trench and seal rather than ordinary borders.
- **Conflict:** Sentinels preserve control through silence while Countless probes the seal and Crissdalynn’s map tests the order.
- **Promise:** Earn access, study a withheld map, and decide whether secrecy protects or perpetuates danger.
- **Trajectory:** the crack becomes visible, Countless claims part of the map, or the seal answers Talon.

- **Fantastic:** watch is both spiritual discipline and containment infrastructure.

- **Functional:** watch, pilgrimage, and sealed knowledge govern access.
- **Contradiction:** watches the Maw by refusing to explain it.
- **Signatures:** wind-cut ledges; sealed records; map earned by pilgrimage.
