## At a glance

The rivers draining [[Aruhe - Clear Lake]] braid through Aruhe, cutting across the island's bands. They are the best road and an occupied one: fast, clear water belongs to [[Aruhe River Otter|River Otter]] families.

## Aspects

`clear water, lateral route, otter territory`

## Senses

- **Sight:** Green-blue channels, pale limestone, submerged grass, silver fish, and roots overhanging the banks.
- **Sound:** Current, cataracts, sudden rapids, and silences where sound vanishes in deep pools.
- **Touch / smell:** Cool water, mist at dawn and dusk, wet stone, and animal musk.

## Topology

- `[[Clear Lake]] -- outlet fans --> Aruhe River`.
- `Aruhe River -- lateral channels --> [[Aruhe - Grasslands]]`, [[The Rot]], and [[The Quiet]].
- `Aruhe River -- signature reach --> [[Long Reach]]`.
- **Fast route:** Water travel is hours-scale where navigable, but current, rapids, deep pools, and otter territory make it dangerous.
- **Slow route:** The grassland bank exposes travelers to predators; the jungle bank trades cover for roots, drops, and vertical threats.

## Who is here

- [[Breathmelon]] grows abundantly along wet margins.

## Player verbs

`follow, swim, ford, portage, read current, study banks, avoid claiming, learn a reach`

## Hooks

- Follow the current downhill or the river uphill toward [[Aruhe - Clear Lake]].
- Choose fast and dangerous water travel or slower bank travel.
- Record landings, portages, current, and seasonal signs to turn a first crossing into durable access.

## Threats (DM)

- Anything entering the water participates in the otters' play. Anything taken from the river belongs to their family.
- If a family member is harmed, play stops: ten seconds of silence, then one person is struck from below.
- The grassland bank carries eight-foot blades and rim predators. The jungle bank carries root curtains, drops, and things that hang above the water.
- Flood stage widens the grasslands and makes the current lethal. Dry stage exposes limestone shelves and concentrates the otters.

## Secrets (DM)

The river's clarity is tended, not natural. Otters cull grazers, drag shade trees clear, and patrol the margins. The beauty is their garden: concentrated fish, submerged growth, and unnaturally quiet insect life mark a tended reach.
