# Ashwalls
> [!narration] Narration
> Black volcanic spires in the Galewall’s lee: steam vents, warm cave mouths, and wrecks jammed in the stone. Sulfur, salt, and hot rock hang over surf and boarding calls. Shelter here is real — and not every useful handhold is a rescue.


## At a glance
Cold-water volcanic spires: last solid ground heading west and first safe lee returning east; feels hot, sulfurous, and opportunistic.

## Aspects
`volcanic lee, wreck salvage, false rescue`

## Senses
- Black spires, steam vents, warm cave mouths, and wrecks.
- Surf, vents, bats, and boarding calls.
- Sulfur, salt, hot stone, and wreck smoke.

## Who is here
- Crews sheltering in the lee.
- Giant bats, scorpions, and vultures.

## Connections
- → [[Galewall]] · [[Shattered Sea]] · [[Doldrums]]

## Hooks
- Reach the lee after the Galewall; decide whether apparent rescuers are rescuers.
- Salvage a wreck without treating every useful handhold as safe.

## Secrets (DM)
The white-fire crossings are disputed: rigging fused, spars split outward, and stormlight moved like a bird. Do not resolve the cause here.
