# The Rot

## Art
![[attachments/shattered-sea/places/aruhe-the-rot.jpeg]]
> [!narration] Narration
> The Rot is a low, wet reach of forest where dead growth does not clear away. It piles into black soil, split bark, and leaves eaten dark at the edges. Pale roots run above the ground in thick cords, bright as exposed veins, crossing old trunks and dipping into soft places where a boot would sink before finding stone underneath. Violet shelf fungi crowd the trees and fallen wood, and the whole region smells of wet earth, opened bark, and rot still warm enough to feed something. Paths here are made by what has broken down: a firm root-line, a darker channel through the muck, or a gap where the ground gives way toward stone below.

## At a glance
A decaying ecological region where fallen life, soil, and the island's excess meet. It is a clue-rich boundary between the Quiet, the Grove, and the volcanic underworld.

## Five-sentence kernel
- **Function:** A decomposition route that reveals how Aruhe returns living claims to the whole.
- **Fantastic:** Rot is not scarcity; it is a working niche within an island of too much life.
- **Conflict:** Surveyors need to cross and study decay while their own possessive actions can add pressure to the region.
- **Promise:** Read what has died, what feeds on it, and where boundaries lead without reducing the region to one predator.
- **Trajectory:** Disturbance makes the route hostile and obscures the ecology; careful passage opens clues to Grove and lava tubes.

## Aspects
`decay as abundance, clue-rich boundary, underworld breach`

## Senses
- **Sight:** Decomposing growth, dark soil, feeding traces, and breaks toward root or stone.
- **Sound:** Uneven feeding, insect movement, and damp ground.
- **Smell / touch:** Wet decay, rich earth, and soft ground over harder volcanic channels.

## Topology
- `[[The Quiet]] -- silence boundary --> The Rot`.
- `The Rot -- living/decay route --> [[The Grove - Death Bloom]]`.
- `The Rot -- subsurface breach --> [[Lava Tubes]]`.
- **Bypass:** Circle the boundary through grassland, losing decay clues.
- **Retreat:** Follow the known Quiet edge before the ground or life converges.

## Who is here
- **Fiction only:** Corpsewood, Blackrail, Root Roach, Strangler Fig, Stillbloom.
- No single master predator is established for the Rot.

## Player verbs
`study, cross, distinguish dead from living, follow decay, map a breach, retreat`.

## Hooks
- Find the difference between a shed part, dead material, and a living claim.
- Follow Blackrail signs toward the cave route; Blackrail is a 40-foot cave centipede in the fiction roster only.
- Determine whether decay points toward the Grove's restraint or the lava tubes' underworld.

## Secrets (DM)
- The Rot's pressure is aligned ecological hunger, not a hive mind. Its boundaries matter more than a boss creature.

## Mechanics and return
- Decay can be Difficult Terrain and a source of Study/Search clues; no creature or plant mechanics are included.
- **Return state:** decay signs, route boundary, breach status, and whether any living thing was claimed.

## Flora ecology
- Every leaf is black at the edge; bark splits without healing, new tissue fails, and fungi bloom in impossible colors.
- Exposed roots run above ground like bright, pulsing veins, all pointing upslope. Crossing one makes the light pulse in that direction.
- [[Whisper fig]] grows on the Quiet-to-Rot edge. [[Skybladder]] hangs in the sealed canopy, while [[Rotheart]] appears only on the sickest trees after spore-air has begun to wear a body down.
- [[Corpsewood]] patrols the deep tree line. [[Snakewood]] is larger and less healthy here; [[Strangler Fig|Strangler Figs]] still pinch old trails.
