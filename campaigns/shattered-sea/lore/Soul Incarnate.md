# Soul Incarnate

## Summary
Founding documents sealed beneath [[High Eyrie]] describe a transformation technique connected to Soul Incarnate. The current Sentinels know only that the documents are very old and do not know they concern a conscious being. [[Talon Vantyrus]] wants the transformation technique. The [[Fate Spinner]]’s reaction at [[Yssenmoor]] carries the signature Vantyrus recognizes.

## Known to
- [[Sentinels of the Eyrie]]: very old founding documents and a seal; not the conscious-being truth.
- [[Master Kyzil]]: no knowledge that the seal concerns a conscious being.
- [[Talon Vantyrus]]: recognizes the signature and seeks the transformation technique.
- [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]: carries the Fate Spinner; the full significance is unknown to her.

## Unknowns
The being’s identity, nature, motives, exact transformation technique, and what the seal contains are unknown. Do not invent mechanical effects here.
