# Tithe of the Bitch Queen
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Umberlee ingest pending._

## Summary
The Tithe is Umberlee's older hoard on the [[Sunken Crown]] seafloor, separate from the [[Pearl of Souls]]. Tidal caves expose it only at the decade's lowest tides. Jewels remain unclouded, gold untarnished, and statues look newly set down.

Taking any piece puts Umberlee's attention on that object. Squalls follow the ship carrying it until the piece touches saltwater again; handing it to someone else only hands them the weather. Her own [[Waveservants]] refuse to rule on whether the Pearl counts as Tithe.

## Known to
Sailors, salvage crews, and Waveservants know the warning. The exact caves and the decade-low tide window belong to the DM layer.

## Variants
Some sailors call every storm-taken treasure Tithe. The named Sunken Crown hoard is the older, specific use. The Pearl's status remains deliberately unresolved rather than a hidden ruling.

## Table use
A salt-stained salvage chart, an unclouded jewel, or a ship followed by squalls can identify the Tithe. Returning the piece to saltwater ends the direct weather-following pressure; it does not settle ownership or what the crew owes.
