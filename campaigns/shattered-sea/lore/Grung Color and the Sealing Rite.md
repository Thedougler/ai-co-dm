# Grung Color and the Sealing Rite
> [!narration] Narration
> Grung color is made, not born. Diet plus a daily one-hour soak seals gold, red, purple, blue, or green into the body as social evidence of what it currently carries. A red hat can mark Twiceborn censure — kin read the sign and walk past rather than claim the wearer.


## Summary
Grung color caste is made and maintained through diet plus a daily sealing rite, not inherited as a birthright. A Grung eats a specific diet, then spends one hour soaking; the soak is bodily maintenance and ritual discipline, not magic. Gold, red, purple, blue, and green are the known colors. Color is social evidence of what a body has taken in and sealed, not proof of ancestry or moral worth.

## Known to
The clans know the practical rule. Outsiders can learn it from bodies, food, bath sites, caste behavior, and a Grung whose color has changed or stalled. Blue scouts and go-betweens, purple officers and warriors, and green laborers are established role signals; the exact institutional duties of red remain UNKNOWN.

## Gold and the reagent farms
Gold-tier color requires a rare toxin grown on secret reagent farms in the interior of [[Karath]]. The farms are guarded and hidden even from lower castes. The gold presence is not explained by a public shrine or a simple inherited bloodline. Simone's bands and dots show a **stalled partial gold** over purple-black; she never received Karath toxin, so the visible change does not prove successful completion of the gold process.

## Censure and Twiceborn
Twiceborn censure is read through public signs, including a red hat or beret. Kin can recognize the sign and walk past the wearer rather than claim them. Jean-Claude's red beret is therefore socially legible inside the clan world even when outsiders treat it as disguise.

The complete Twiceborn procedure, its exact relation to diet, and whether every red hat means the same censure are UNKNOWN.

## Variants
- **Gold story:** gold is sacred authority, and serene gold issues the decrees that keep the clans whole.
- **Private reading:** diet, rite, congregation, and sacred ground produce the authority's visible force; the serene presence thins outside the Teeth.
- **UNKNOWN:** whether that private reading describes biology, magic, a constructed apex, or an effective social theology.
