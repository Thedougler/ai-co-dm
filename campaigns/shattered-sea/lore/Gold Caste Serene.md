# Gold Caste Serene
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Grung ingest pending._

## Fiction note
The Gold Caste Serene is a small amphibious body at the top of the Grung religious stack. It is unlookable: not merely difficult to see, but something no one can address by looking directly at it. Hundreds attend it on the most sacred ground in the [[Verdant Teeth]].

It does not fight and does not bargain. It issues decrees, and the jungle answers. Golds are the visible authority, but the Serene is the apex presence the congregation treats as certainty.

## Faith tap
The Serene's force runs through the faith of a close, convinced congregation rather than belonging to a gold in isolation. The tap thins within hours when the Serene is taken off the Teeth, the room's faith is broken, or the crowd is starved. Lower castes do not know this distinction; they call the result divine authority.

## Boundaries
This is a fiction and continuity note, not a statblock or math pass. Keep the exact nature of the Serene unresolved: god, construct, conduit, or effective theology are still open. Do not collapse it into the Antheri machine called [[Auralis]].
