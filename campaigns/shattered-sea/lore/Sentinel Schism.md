# Sentinel Schism

## Summary
The question was whether the Sentinels’ fate was to record without conclusion or whether moral duty required them to act. A snowy-owl senior master, later [[Talon Vantyrus]], called non-intervention cowardice and asked gifted barn-owl student [[Master Kyzil]] to break with him. Kyzil refused; Vantyrus left; the order closed ranks. Younger members, including [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]], were never told the schism happened.

## Known to
Kyzil remembers the break and the name Osset as a mistaken “third name,” but does not know Osset = Vantyrus. The Sentinels’ younger members do not know the schism occurred. [[Countless]] carries the break forward through its Rule of Two.

## Variants
The in-world third-actor interpretation is wrong: **Osset = Talon Vantyrus**. Whether the non-intervention doctrine is cowardice or duty remains a live question for play.
