# Ossketh

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer pending — rite is hidden workshop technique, not a public prayer._

## Summary
**Ossketh** is a learned transmutation rite Gold uses to finish caste color. It is technique, not prayer and not the daily soak. Diet and the one-hour soak stain and maintain a body; Ossketh is the stolen workshop step that **locks** a change. Fiction frame: [[Grung Color and the Sealing Rite]].

## Known to
Gold (and whoever stole the workshop). Lower caste are not supposed to know the rite exists. Ozzeth and Simone are the living/dead proof cases Midchain may brush against.

## Mechanics (DM) — 2024 house

### What Ossketh is not
- Not the daily soak (nonmagical maintenance).
- Not a Divine Domain feature.
- Not detectable as "Grung biology" — a creature using *Detect Magic* sees **Transmutation** on an active or incomplete seal, nothing on ordinary diet-stain or poison skin.

### Learning
A creature can learn Ossketh only by instruction from someone who already knows it (or equivalent stolen notes). Treat as a **ritual technique**, not a free spell known — Gold ration who gets it. Optional gate: spend downtime + a toxin sample of the target caste to practice.

### Casting profile
| | |
|---|---|
| **School** | Transmutation (ritual technique) |
| **Time** | 1 hour |
| **Range** | Touch |
| **Target** | One willing or restrained Grung (or Grung-bodied creature) |
| **Reagent** | Toxin matched to the **intended color**. True **gold** requires the rare Karath farm toxin. Substitutes can stain toward gold but cannot complete a sealed gold. |
| **Hands** | **Sealed result requires two practitioners:** a primary who runs Ossketh, and a second who performs **Complete the Seal** in the final minute. |

Spellcasters without the technique cannot substitute a random *Polymorph*; this is a specific learned procedure.

### Outcome A — Other-cast, sealed (intended use)
Primary runs Ossketh on **another** creature; second Completes the Seal.

- Target’s caste color becomes the intended color (**Sealed Color**).
- **No daily upkeep.** The change holds through rests, travel, and ordinary *Dispel Magic*.
- Reverse only with fiction-grade intervention (Gold undoing, or a later adventure procedure — do not hand out casual *Remove Curse* erasures).

### Outcome B — Self-cast (open / incomplete)
If the primary targets **themselves**, or no second Completes the Seal:

- The change **never finishes**. Target gains **Incomplete Seal** (below).
- Color drifts toward the intended caste but stays unstable / banded / mixed.
- **Upkeep required:** once per dawn, another creature who knows Ossketh must spend **10 minutes** patching the seal (touch, no reagent). If upkeep is missed, the seal **slips** one step (DM: more mottling, loss of intended caste readability, or slide toward Twiceborn patterning).
- A substitute gold toxin (Crown payment, non-Karath) on a self-cast produces **stalled partial gold** — purple/black broken with gold bands/dots (Simone). Still Incomplete Seal; never a Sealed gold.

### Incomplete Seal (condition)
- Visible unstable color (bands, mixed fields, stalled partial gold).
- Requires Ossketh upkeep each dawn or the seal slips.
- *Detect Magic:* Transmutation aura while Incomplete.
- Ending: Successful other-cast sealed Ossketh on the creature (with proper reagent and second hands), Gold undoing, or death.

### Twiceborn (censure state)
**Twiceborn** is Gold’s public name for an unreadable / mixed seal they refuse to admit as workshop failure.

- **Trigger (typical):** catastrophic slip, self-cast gone wrong, or skin showing two caste colors at once (e.g. blue and red).
- **Social:** kin can walk past rather than claim the wearer; a **red hat/beret** is a common public sign (Jean-Claude’s beret is legible inside clan country).
- **Mechanical (light):** While marked Twiceborn among caste-loyal Grung, the creature has disadvantage on Charisma checks to get aid, shelter, or recognition as kin **unless** the other party already rejects Gold’s story. No combat buff.
- Exact every-case procedure remains partially UNKNOWN — use this as the runnable core; expand only when play needs it.

### Worked examples
1. **Proper gold:** Diet prep → soak → Ossketh on subordinate + second Completes + Karath toxin → Sealed gold, no upkeep.
2. **Simone:** Self-cast Ossketh wanting gold + Crown substitute toxin → Incomplete Seal, stalled partial gold bands; needs ongoing patches; Gold never legitimizes.
3. **Ozzeth:** Unreadable blue+red → branded Twiceborn so Gold need not admit the rite; kept Simone’s incomplete seal patched until he died.

## Variants
- Lower-caste rumor: color is destiny. Workshop truth: diet + soak + (hidden) Ossketh.
- Whether Gold Serene “tap” uses sealed color as a wire is **not** Ossketh math — leave on congregation/faith lore.

## Design notes
- **Chassis:** ritual transmutation with a hard “second caster locks it” trade; self-cast pays forever with upkeep (like concentration debt across days).
- **Peers:** *Magic Jar*/curse-seal fantasy without stealing those spells; *Nystul’s* for “hidden workshop”; downtime crafting for reagents.
- **Knobs:** upkeep 1/Long Rest instead of dawn; sealed color vulnerable to *Dispel Magic* (DC 18) if you want softer locks; Twiceborn as pure fiction with zero dice.
