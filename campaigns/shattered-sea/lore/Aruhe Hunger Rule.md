# Aruhe Hunger Rule
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Aruhe ingest pending._

## Summary
Aruhe tolerates crossing, watching, sheltering, and passage. It reacts to **possession** of living things: pluck, uproot, trap, fish for oneself, or kill to carry flesh. Loose stone, a shed shell, and free-flowing spring, stream, or cave seepage are not possessions and are safe to use. Living things belong where they are; when someone claims a living thing, surrounding life converges in hostility—not as a hive mind, but as aligned hunger.

## Precise distinction
- **Passage tolerated:** Cross the island, watch life, shelter, map, and pass through without claiming a living thing.
- **Possession answered:** Plucking, uprooting, trapping, fishing for self, or killing to carry flesh is a claim.
- **Safe nonliving use:** Loose stone and shed shell are acceptable. Free-flowing spring, stream, and cave seepage are drinkable.
- **Living place:** Living things belong where they are; beneficial fruit is genuinely beneficial, with no hidden poison trick, but taking it from its living place is still possession.
- **Convergence:** On a claim, surrounding life converges hostilely. The response is ecological alignment, not a single mind or master predator.

## Known to
The rule is the operational truth of Aruhe. Surveyors can learn it from observation, traces, and consequences; the exact origin of the island and its joined ecology remains unknown.

## Variants
The name “Hungry Isle” means too much of everything, not lack. Outsiders may call any disappearance hunger, but the useful distinction is passage versus possession.

## Table use
Show the rule through a safe crossing, an observed living thing, a loose stone or shed shell, drinkable free flow, and the visible convergence after a claim. Do not make beneficial fruit secretly poisonous; the consequence is possession, not nutritional deceit.

## Do not
- Treat the response as a hive mind, single master predator, or arbitrary negation of player capabilities.
- Turn ordinary passage, watching, sheltering, loose stone, shed shell, or free-flowing water into possession.
- Write fruit or beast mechanics in this lore note.
