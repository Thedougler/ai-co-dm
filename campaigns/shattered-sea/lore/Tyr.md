# Tyr
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Crown ingest pending._

Tyr is the Dravosi Crown's court god: the god of oaths, commissions, courts martial, and the warrant that names a guilty person. Garrison chapels smell of sworn paper; scales and a warhammer mark lintels where another faith might hang a lantern. He takes the fort and the bench, not the wave.

A Crown officer can tithe [[Umberlee]] in the morning and sit a rating board in the afternoon without feeling hypocritical. The sea is a fact. The document is a claim. The Crown lives in that gap.

## Table use
Tyr appears in commissions, court language, garrison chapels, and the reservation policy around [[Aleksander Malone]].
