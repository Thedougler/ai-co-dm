# Grung and the Making of Aruhe

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Hinewai ingest pending._

## Summary (DM secret)
Karath captured Hinewai, an elf archdruid from a small self-contained island village, and forced her into its hatchery work. Captive spellcasters were dosed and kept slow, then given enough antidote to push ordinary vermin into giant toads, snakes, and spiders. Hinewai escaped with one companion; he drowned in the half-mile channel while they swam toward Aruhe, and she carried him inland to a fruit tree.

She buried him, took the island's offered fruit as a vow, and later fused soul and body to Aruhe in a second grave. The ritual killed her. The two graves, tree, black flowers, bound soil, and their relationship became Hinewai's memorial body and place-bound phylactery. Her successful refusal to let the dead companion be recycled became the law of an island where life cannot finish.

The Grung named the place Hungry Isle from one survivor's telling a generation ago and will not land. Their boats work the western reefs; the last landing party that took fruit, fish, forage, or trapped game was hunted down by animals that turned on Grung scent.

## Known to
The dump-authoritative autobiography belongs to the DM layer. Hinewai carries the truth directly, though memory rot left mostly hatred of Grung and takers, love of living things, love of Aruhe, and love for the man she carried uphill. Grung leadership and old sacred ground retain the crime in taboo and doctrine but do not volunteer it. Players encounter traces, censure, refusal, graves, and ecological consequences before a complete account.

## Variants and evidence
- **Clan public story:** protect the community, obey gold, keep the interior closed. The crime is not volunteered.
- **Aruhe's evidence:** the island answers possession with aligned hunger, not a single mind; fauna remain independent animals.
- **Hinewai's truth:** the joined ecology is the aftermath of a captive archdruid's grief and land-fusion, not a new Grung god or hive mind.
- **Older UNKNOWN framing:** prior notes did not know the druid's name, origin, companion, or whether a separate self remained. That uncertainty is superseded as canon by the 2026-09-05 dump, but may remain as in-world incomplete knowledge.
