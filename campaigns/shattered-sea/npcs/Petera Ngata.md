# Petera Ngata
> [!narration] Narration
> Petera feeds whoever can pay at the Last Berth and keeps one floorboard loose for a truth neither company can buy.

## Hook
She holds the only complete list of who went down and did not come back.

## Drive
Keep dead divers from becoming company property; sell food, not the list.

## Public face vs secret
Publicly a boardinghouse keeper. Secretly she knows Sunkline's false log is under the floorboard and refuses to sell the death list to either company.
