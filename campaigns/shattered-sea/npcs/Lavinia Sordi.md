## Exit from the Crown desk
Lavinia left a [[Dravosi Crown]] prison records desk over its substitute-toxin pipeline. She smuggled confiscated goods out on the way and then spent nine clean years with a canal commission before she would sell a curse in the open.

She will not name the facility. She does not have to: anyone who has watched a rating form decide a life already knows the tone.
