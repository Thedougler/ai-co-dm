# Otho

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: NPC ingest pending._

## Stub
An archfey on [[Otel Karn]] whose careless bargain stopped death from working on the island generations ago. Otho's old teaching became the founding philosophy of the [[Khlysty / the Flock]], and [[Shepherd Grigori]] was his star student.

> [!secret]- DM truth
> Otho is blind to the passage of time. He does not know centuries have elapsed or that Grigori's longevity is unusual. Grigori does not know Otho still lives or that Otho's technique predates him.
