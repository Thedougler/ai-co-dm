# Cosimo Verantio
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Tessarine ingest pending._

## Hook
A merchant who never needs to raise his voice can call in a city's future one note at a time.

## Public face
On paper Cosimo is a Tessarine merchant in Calveno, sometimes called **the Principal**. He can be pointed at in the city. He appears to serve the consortium's ordinary work: routes, houses, contracts, and credit.

## Look / voice
- Precise, patient, and comfortable in a counting house or Council gallery.
- Treats every conversation as a ledger entry.
- Never hurries a debt that can compound.

## Drive
**Want:** Accumulate obligation across councils, routes, houses, and inherited notes. **Fear:** Tessarine paper becoming untrusted or a council being freed from its obligations. **Method:** Let factors, successor firms, and heirs carry the work so no single debtor sees the circle.

## Public face vs secret
> [!secret]- The architect
> Cosimo is the Concordat's architect and secretly a Magus Dragon. He does not hoard coin; he hoards obligation. The factor network is his unwitting circle and does not know it. Burn Tessarine gold and a counting house is annoyed. Invalidate the debt or call the whole structure into question and Cosimo is cut.
>
> The source does not provide dragon math, a stat block, or a lifecycle. Keep the secret as a story pressure.

## The Pearl commission
Cosimo sat behind a [[Chain Council]] signatory as “the Principal” when the Council hired [[Delmar Fisk]]'s five-ship fleet to steal the [[Pearl of Souls]]. The real target was [[Aldric Drave]] and the weakening of Drave's Crown bloodline. The Council never learned that motive.

## Leverage and limits
- **Leverage:** Tessarine credit, Council obligations, inherited notes, route underwriting, and the factor network.
- **Need:** Trusted paper and people who continue to mistake obligation for ordinary commerce.
- **Limit:** Debt invalidated, a Council freed, or a broken mail trail exposed cuts directly at the hoard.

## Relationships
- [[Tessarine Concordat]] — architect and hidden principal.
- [[Fieschi]] — public-facing agent whose confession created containment pressure.
- [[Chain Council]] — commission mask; its signatory did not know the whole target.
- [[Aldric Drave]] — the real target of the Pearl commission.
- [[Detto Kalash]] — mail-run casualty whose correspondence the Concordat is burying.
