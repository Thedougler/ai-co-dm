# The Unnamed Companion

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Hinewai ingest pending._

## Hook
He is the absence beneath the fruit tree: the person Hinewai carried inland, and the reason the island still refuses to let one moment finish.

## Look / voice
No name, face, or surviving biography is established. He drowned in the half-mile channel between Karath and Aruhe while swimming toward freedom. Hinewai carried his body for days and buried him beneath the tree whose fruit he loved.

## Drive
He has no independent present-day drive; he is dead. In Hinewai's remaining memory, his grave is the reason for the Grove and the island's grief law.

## Relationships
- [[The Blight]] / Hinewai: companion in flight from Karath; she carried him inland and made the second grave her own.
- [[The Grove - Death Bloom]]: first, older, unmarked grave beneath the memorial tree.
- [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]]: his preservation is the origin pressure behind the island's excess and boundaries.

## Resources
The grave, the fruit he loved, and the preserved memory of his death are clues, not loot or a detachable magic object.
