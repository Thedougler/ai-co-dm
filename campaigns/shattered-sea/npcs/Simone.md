# Simone Tabarnack
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Grung ingest pending._

## Crown pipeline
Simone supplies the [[Dravosi Crown]] with substitute stock, not Karath farm product. The toxin has already turned up in privateer kit and in [[Calven and Calveno]]'s sewers. The sale is leverage and exposure: it gives Simone a buyer while proving the Crown can weaponize a Grung secret without owning the farms.

## Color and Ossketh
Simone cast [[Ossketh]] on herself because she wanted gold. Crown payment supplied a substitute toxin rather than the rare Karath farm toxin, so the change stalled at partial gold: purple-and-black skin broken into dots and bands of gold that she never hides. She still believes diet plus the rite is the whole machine and has never seen a true seal finish. Gold tolerated her but never legitimized her; they punished her with reputation rather than soldiers because a public trial would confirm the trick.

Her self-cast remains an incomplete seal. [[Ozzeth]] knew the rite and kept her version stable with ongoing help until his death covering her work in Calveno. Simone's visible body is evidence of the workshop's failure mode, not proof that she controls Karath's true reagent.

## Existing identity
Simone remains the compact purple-caste officer on [[Sorn Guarded Beach]], the gatekeeper who holds the beach and hunts [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] as a leak. She is Jean-Claude's sister; she still keeps the Sorn garrison while Botukuri works the farm island behind it.
