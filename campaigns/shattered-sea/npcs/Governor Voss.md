# Governor Voss
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Crown ingest pending._

## Office
Voss holds the civic seat at Port Tidefall. He receives petitions, civic reports, harbour complaints, and the visible business of a checkpoint. The Admiralty's sealed orders do not pass through him.

## Blind spot
Extra companies have arrived at [[Fort Crestwall]] with reef-diving gear and underwater charges marked for the [[Drowned Maw]]. The Governor never saw the orders and has not been told why. The split is deliberate: two authorities in one harbour, with the authority holding the guns withholding the operational record.

## Hook
A petition can reach Voss while a sealed order moves underneath it. He can act on the public harbour and still be unable to explain the Crown's Maw staging.
