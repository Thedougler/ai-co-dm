# Beaumont Sel

## Art
![[attachments/shattered-sea/portraits/beaumont-sel-portrait.webp]]

> [!narration] Narration
> Beaumont Sel is a smuggler and Passage contact who moves people through colonial pressure. He works through trusted introductions and routes that keep the network connected.
## Hook
Move people through colonial pressure without letting the Passage collapse; his immediate handle is the crew’s access aboard [[Saltwright]].
## Identity and public function
Passage smuggler, trusted introduction, and contact who made the crew visible to the network.
## Look / voice
- **Visual:** Reversible table cue: a practical travel coat marked by Saltwright work, with a Passage token kept out of casual sight; exact appearance is unknown.
- **Behavior:** Checks who can be trusted before naming a route; offers access through a person rather than a speech.
- **Voice principle:** Short trade-offs and names; every promise carries a condition.
- **Sample line:** “A route is only safe until the wrong person learns its name.”
## Drive
**Want:** Move people through colonial pressure while keeping the Passage’s trust intact. **Fear:** One exposed route collapses the network. **Method:** Saltwright access, introductions, and selective warnings.
## Public face vs secret
**Public:** Smuggler and useful ship contact. **Secret:** He can name who the Passage trusts; he warned [[Nona Black-Jaw]] he was hunting Perrin. The reason for that hunt is not established.
## Leverage / need / cost
- **Leverage:** Passage routes, Saltwright access, and trusted introductions.
- **Need:** Reliable people who will not burn a route or force the Passage into the open.
- **Cost of cooperation:** The crew becomes visible to the Passage and inherits the consequences of that trust.
## Limit and productive contradiction
He can open a route but cannot make it safe after exposure. He protects a clandestine network by making selected people visible to it.
## Attitude / request posture / influence
Friendly but **hesitant**. A demonstrated respect for Passage boundaries or a useful rescue moves him; demands for names spend trust.
## Relationships
- [[Nona Black-Jaw]]: warned her he was hunting Perrin.
- [[Perrin Black-Jaw]]: named him Friend of the Passage.
- [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]]: gave him [[Truth Stone]].
- The crew: introduced them aboard [[Saltwright]].
## Resources
Passage routes, Saltwright access, trusted introductions, and the ability to name trusted contacts.
## Next move / if ignored
Hold back the next introduction and let the Passage protect its routes without the crew. If ignored, access narrows and the crew remains visible without his guidance.
