# The Blight
> [!narration] Narration
> A druidic presence joined to Aruhe, felt as rooted attention and answered boundaries rather than a face behind a court. Pressure gathers where the Grove restrains excess and where a claim on living things draws aligned local hunger — not a hive-mind chorus.


## Hook
A druidic presence joined to Aruhe tests whether a survey can connect the island without turning it into a possession.

## Public face
The Blight appears as rooted attention, ecological pressure, and boundaries that answer a claim on living things. It is not a hive-mind chorus, a single master predator, or a court that speaks for every creature.

## Drive
- **Want:** Aruhe's living boundaries and balance preserved.
- **Fear:** A Tangle anchor, Grung claim, or visitor's harvest turning passage into ownership.
- **Method:** Pressure, ecological signs, and aligned local hunger rather than command over every creature.

## Public face vs secret
- **Public:** a druidic presence joined to Aruhe, legible through the Grove / Death Bloom and boundary pressure.
- **DM secret:** hundreds of years ago, Grung captured a druid from a far Midchain island, tortured her, drove her mad, and killed her companion when both escaped to Aruhe. That crime is the origin of Aruhe's present condition and of the Blight's joined presence.
- **UNKNOWN:** the druid's name, the far island, the companion's name, and whether any separate self remains within the Blight.

Do not place the confession in player-facing narration. Reveal it through traces, censure, ecological consequence, and incomplete testimony.

## Relationships
- [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]]: joined presence; its pressure is local and ecological.
- [[Aruhe Hunger Rule]]: operational expression of passage versus possession.
- [[Grung clans]]: origin perpetrators; the old crime remains hidden.
- [[Grung and the Making of Aruhe]]: DM origin account.
- [[Tangle]]: proposed connection that must not claim the island.
- [[Perrin Black-Jaw]]: surveyor whose choices test whether an anchor can be non-possessive.

## Resources
Can expose or close ecological boundaries, make the cost of possession legible, and offer a route decision through pressure. It does not provide a new stat block here.

## Do not
- Treat The Blight as a hive mind, single master predator, or fully statted monster.
- Add a resident settlement, named island court, or unearned biography.
