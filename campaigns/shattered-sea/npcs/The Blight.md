# The Blight
> [!narration] Narration
> A druidic presence joined to Aruhe, felt as rooted attention and answered boundaries rather than a face behind a court. Pressure gathers where the Grove restrains excess and where a claim on living things draws aligned local hunger — not a hive-mind chorus.


## Hook
A druidic presence joined to Aruhe tests whether the survey can connect the island without turning it into a possession.

## Look / voice
- Present as pressure, rooted attention, and a boundary that feels answered.
- The island's excess and restraint show around the presence; no separate town or court is implied.
- Voice, if any, is not a hive-mind chorus.

## Drive
- **Want:** Aruhe's living boundaries and balance preserved.
- **Fear:** A Tangle anchor or visitor's harvest turning passage into ownership.
- **Method:** Pressure, ecological signs, and aligned local hunger rather than command over every creature.

## Public face vs secret
- **Public face:** A druidic presence joined to the island, legible through the Grove / Death Bloom and boundary pressure.
- **Secret:** The Blight is not the island's single master and does not make its life a hive mind; its pressure is part of a larger balance between niches.

## Relationships
- [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]]: joined presence; its pressure is local and ecological.
- [[Aruhe Hunger Rule]]: enforces the distinction between passage and possession through consequence.
- [[Tangle]]: a proposed connection that must not claim the island.
- [[Perrin Black-Jaw]]: surveyor whose choices reveal whether the anchor can be non-possessive.

## Resources
Can expose or close ecological boundaries, make the cost of possession legible, and offer a route decision through pressure. It does not provide a monster stat block here.

## Appearances
- [[Survey Aruhe as a Tangle anchor]]

## Do not
- Treat The Blight as a hive mind, a single master predator, or a fully statted monster.
- Add a resident settlement, named island court, or unearned biography.
