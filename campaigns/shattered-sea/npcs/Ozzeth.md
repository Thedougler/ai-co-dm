# Ozzeth, the Twiceborn

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Grung ingest pending._

## What happened
Ozzeth's skin ran blue and red at once, unreadable to a caste order that required one answer. He found [[Ossketh]] first: the hidden Gold technique that could finish another body but could not complete when turned inward. Gold branded him **Twiceborn**, a censure rather than a title he chose, so they would not admit the rite was real.

He kept [[Simone]]'s self-cast version stable with ongoing help until he died covering her work in [[Calveno]] — the Calveno death is the end of his maintenance and protection, not a clean seal. He died buying time for the ritual and left Simone's partial gold visible.

## Use
Ozzeth is the dead witness that connects Gold's workshop, Simone's stalled color, and the public Twiceborn censure. His blue-and-red skin is a social contradiction before it is a combat detail. Gold could punish him by reputation without staging a trial that would confirm the trick.
