# Shepherd Grigori

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: NPC ingest pending._

## Current situation
Grigori and the crew parted as friends at [[La Vasca]] in [[Calveno]], but the Crown hunt is live. He is working through noble houses while Malone works backward through the same pattern. His warning that a [[Talon Vantyrus|Vantyrus]] follower ship was coming for [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]'s [[Fate Spinner]] remains a separate pressure, as does his request that the crew hurt [[Corbin Knighton]] without killing or breaking him; nobody accepted that bargain.

## Hook
A trusted healer asks for one more favor while the Crown calls him heresy. The crew must decide whether the debt of his care outweighs the danger of helping the man behind it.

## Look and voice
- Tall, dark-haired, wild-bearded, and lean from a ship-hardened life.
- Favors rich red-and-gold coats and nearly always has a glass of wine in hand.
- His hands run cold. He speaks with quiet precision, asking the question nobody was ready to answer.
- He heals freely and silently; a strange red light spills from his wrist when he works. Those he saves do not think to ask the price.

## Public face
A warm galley companion, practical healer, and friend of the crew. He feeds people, keeps the galley rule, and never directly asks the crew to do something wrong. The Crown's public accusation ties him to [[Surety]]'s destruction; his deeper nature is not public knowledge.

## Khlysty and the Flock
Grigori holds the office of **Korabl** within the Khlysty, an ecstatic spiritual tradition rooted on [[Otel Karn]]. **Khlysty** is the name used by the [[Dravosi Crown]]; its members call themselves the **Flock**. Their doctrine treats flesh as the vehicle of spirit rather than its enemy: excess and embodiment can be devotion.

Grigori believes he is the teaching's greatest practitioner and that he invented its central miracle. He did not. See [[Khlysty / the Flock]].

## DM truth: Hierarch and blood-anchors
> [!secret]- The Hierarch truth
> Grigori is a Hierarch: an undead being whose soul is bound to his own blood. When he transfuses his blood into another person—usually while saving them from an incurable illness—a dormant thread remains. Each living carrier is a blood-anchor, a phylactery-like tether to his continued existence. As long as any anchor survives, he cannot be permanently destroyed.
>
> He currently has two confirmed binds: a Tessarine ward in Calveno and a Dravosi merchant-lord's daughter. The threads can thin across generations and lose coherence, which is why he is scattering them rather than cultivating one line. Whether his healing of Jean-Claude left a live thread is unresolved; Perrin may also be bound from an earlier illness. Do not resolve either status here.

His visible mechanical color is fiction-facing only: silent healing, blood-aspected red light at the wrist, and the cold touch of an undead body. He has no fight sheet here; Grigori avoids being a front-line monster, preferring departure, healing, or a bargain.

## The army, not the heir
A traditional Hierarch cultivates one bloodline and eventually possesses a descendant through the Heir ritual. Grigori wants an evolution: an **army, not an heir**. He plans dormant threads through the noble houses of the [[Dravosi Crown]], [[Tessarine Concordat]], and [[Chain Council]], making blood-thinning and failing coherence irrelevant through breadth.

- Two confirmed binds are the Tessarine ward in Calveno and the Dravosi merchant-lord's daughter.
- [[House Knighton]] is his route into Dravosi naval aristocracy; he hopes to engineer a wound that makes [[Corbin Knighton]] need saving.
- He covets the [[Pearl of Souls]] as a shortcut to transcending his nature, but will not retrieve it himself.

## Otho's irony
> [!secret]- The older teacher
> The blood-anchor technique came from [[Otho]], an archfey on [[Otel Karn]] who made a careless bargain generations ago and stopped death from working on the island. Otho's old teaching became the Flock's founding philosophy, and Grigori was his star student. Otho is fey and blind to the passage of time: he does not know centuries have elapsed or that Grigori's longevity is unusual. Grigori does not know Otho still lives or that the technique predates him.

## Crew relationship
The crew of the [[Uncertainty]]—Jean-Claude, Perrin, Delmar, Crissdalynn, and Catarina—know the healer who saved [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] on the weather deck during a crisis at sea. Perrin sensed something wrong in the magic; Delmar remembers the cold handshake. They parted at [[La Vasca]] in Calveno thinking of Grigori as a friend.

- [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]]: the weather-deck healing may have left a live blood-thread; **unresolved**.
- [[Perrin Black-Jaw]]: Grigori cured an illness during the early shipboard days; a bind is possible, but **unresolved**. Perrin's connection to [[Nona Black-Jaw]] makes him exactly the sort of well-connected heir Grigori cultivates.
- The crew's friendship is real enough to be useful leverage, and Grigori never needs to state the bargain aloud.

## Arrogance and the Aldric whisper
Grigori cannot conceive that another Hierarch exists. Even Tessarine whispers about [[Aldric Drave]] describe someone with the same weakness—blood, anchors, and failing coherence—and Grigori hears only a warning about his own limits. Aldric is a rumor, not a confirmed rival in play.

## Drive and methods
- **Want:** An army of dispersed anchors, the Pearl as a shortcut, and continued freedom from Crown notice.
- **Fear:** Exposure before the network is broad enough to survive him.
- **Method:** Arrive as a healer, cure an incurable noble, and leave blood behind. Arrange circumstances so the right thing to do also serves him.
- **Blind spot:** His arrogance makes another Hierarch literally difficult for him to imagine.

## Relationships
- [[Khlysty / the Flock]] — Korabl; doctrine he thinks he authored.
- [[Otho]] — unknowingly his source and former teacher.
- [[Aleksander Malone]] — Crown instrument hunting him through the noble-house pattern.
- [[Dravosi Crown]] — accusation and target house network.
- [[Corbin Knighton]] / [[House Knighton]] — intended Dravosi naval route, not yet a confirmed bind.
- [[Talon Vantyrus]] — separate pressure around the Fate Spinner and Osset schism.

## If left alone
Grigori scouts noble houses, waits for a Knighton wound he can save, refreshes his threads through courtesy and contact, and works the crew indirectly for the Pearl. Malone's hunt runs whether the crew helps or not.
