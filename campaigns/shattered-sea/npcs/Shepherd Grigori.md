# Shepherd Grigori

*The Wine-Dark Shepherd*

![[shepherd-grigori-portrait.jpg|Shepherd Grigori in a red-and-gold coat with wine and crimson wrist-light]]

> [!narration] Shepherd Grigori
> Shepherd Grigori is tall, lean, and ship-hard, with dark hair, a wild beard, and a red-and-gold coat that looks too fine for the places he keeps turning up. A glass of wine is usually close to one hand, and when he offers the other, his skin is cold. He speaks softly and precisely, the sort of man who can make a galley table feel like a private chapel, and when he heals, red light gathers at his wrist without a prayer or a flourish.

> **DM thesis:** Grigori makes mercy dangerous because the cure is real, the kindness is real, and both leave a thread of him inside the person saved.

## Running Grigori

### First meeting

Grigori enters as a practical friend, never as a looming villain. Put him somewhere ordinary and intimate: a galley table, a sickroom, a private dock office, or a rented room with wine already poured. Let him be useful before he is frightening.

Use three signals every time:

- His hand is cold before anyone knows why.
- His voice stays soft when the room becomes dangerous.
- His healing uses red, viscous light from the wrist and no named prayer.

He asks for favors as if he is offering the crew a way to remain decent. His strongest line is not a threat; it is a question that makes refusal cost something.

### When the crew trusts him

Keep the friendship usable. Grigori saved [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]] on the weather deck, warned the crew about [[Talon Vantyrus]]'s follower ship, and parted from them at [[La Vasca]] as a friend. He can give true information and still be moving them toward his needs.

Default offers:

- A cure or stabilizing treatment that no public doctor can provide.
- A warning about the [[Dravosi Crown]], [[Aleksander Malone]], or noble-house movement.
- A route into a room where his own presence would create too much risk.
- A favor framed as restraint, such as harming [[Corbin Knighton]] without killing or breaking him.

### When pressed

Grigori does not deny the evidence first. He narrows the moral field. He asks who was hurt by being saved, whether a life freely preserved is a crime, and why the Crown should own the definition of heresy.

If accused in front of witnesses, he leaves before becoming a spectacle unless the witness is already useful. If cornered by friends, he offers one partial truth: his healing leaves "memory in the blood," his old teacher misunderstood the method, or the Crown is afraid of a pattern it cannot prove.

### When angered

Anger makes him colder, not louder. He stops asking questions and begins selecting people by usefulness: the sick, the indebted, the ambitious, and anyone connected to a house ledger. He prefers departure, a bargain, or a surgical display of power over a fair fight.

### Alternate context: the Hierarch revealed

> [!narration] Hierarch Revealed
> The red light at Grigori's wrist thickens until it looks less like magic than blood under glass. Fine crimson lines answer beneath nearby skin, each one tugging toward him for a heartbeat. His expression does not change. Only the warmth leaves the room.

When the Hierarch truth is visible, do not make him monstrous in body language. The horror is that the same kind healer is still present and still believes the thread he leaves behind is proof of devotion rather than theft.

# Blood-Anchor Doctrine

## The Hierarch truth

Grigori is a Hierarch: an undead being whose soul is bound to his own blood. When he transfuses his blood into another person, usually while saving them from an incurable illness, a dormant thread remains. Each living carrier is a blood-anchor, a phylactery-like tether to his continued existence. As long as any anchor survives, he cannot be permanently destroyed.

Confirmed binds:

- A Tessarine ward in [[Calven and Calveno|Calveno]].
- A Dravosi merchant-lord's daughter.

Unresolved possible binds:

- [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]], after the weather-deck healing.
- [[Perrin Black-Jaw]], from an earlier illness.

Do not resolve the Jean-Claude or Perrin status in this file. Their uncertainty is active table pressure.

## The army, not the heir

A traditional Hierarch cultivates one bloodline and eventually possesses a descendant through the Heir ritual. Grigori wants an evolution: an **army, not an heir**. He plans dormant threads through the noble houses of the [[Dravosi Crown]], [[Tessarine Concordat]], and [[Chain Council]], making blood-thinning and failing coherence irrelevant through breadth.

His current route is [[House Knighton]]. He hopes to engineer a wound that makes [[Corbin Knighton]] need saving, giving him a path into Dravosi naval aristocracy without making a public move against the Crown.

## Otho's irony

The blood-anchor technique came from [[Otho]], an archfey on [[Otel Karn]] who made a careless bargain generations ago and stopped death from working on the island. Otho's old teaching became the Flock's founding philosophy, and Grigori was his star student.

Otho is fey and blind to the passage of time. He does not know centuries have elapsed or that Grigori's longevity is unusual. Grigori does not know Otho still lives or that the technique predates him.

## Arrogance and the Aldric whisper

The [[Tessarine Concordat]] fears what [[Aldric Drave]] could do with the [[Pearl of Souls]]. Grigori mishears that fear as a warning about his own limit, not as evidence of a rival.

Grigori cannot conceive that another Hierarch exists. Even Tessarine whispers about Aldric describe someone with the same weakness - blood, anchors, and failing coherence - and Grigori hears only a warning about his own limits. Aldric is a rumor to him, not a confirmed rival in play.

# History

## Otel Karn

Grigori learned the embodied doctrine of the Flock on [[Otel Karn]], where flesh is treated as the vehicle of spirit rather than its enemy. He became Korabl by carrying that teaching furthest: not just excess as devotion, but blood as a vessel for continued will.

He believes he invented the central miracle. He did not.

## Calveno and the crew

The crew of the [[Uncertainty]] know the healer who saved Jean-Claude during a crisis at sea. Perrin sensed something wrong in the magic; Delmar remembers the cold handshake. They parted at La Vasca in Calveno thinking of Grigori as a friend.

During the night voyage in Session 10, Grigori appeared below deck with wine in hand. He thanked the crew for the Calveno warning, warned that a follower of [[Talon Vantyrus]] had sent a ship to rob them for the [[Fate Spinner]], and asked them to hurt Corbin Knighton without killing or breaking him. No one accepted that bargain.

## Crown hunt

[[Aleksander Malone]] is hunting Grigori through noble-house patterns before the Crown's formal heresy confirmation can finish. The crew now sits between a healer they owe, a zealot who considers that debt evidence, and a Crown accusation tied to the destroyed [[Surety]].

# If Left Alone

Grigori scouts noble houses, waits for a Knighton wound he can save, refreshes his threads through courtesy and contact, and works the crew indirectly toward the Pearl. Malone's hunt runs whether the crew helps or not.

Visible signs of advancement:

1. A noble illness receives a quiet cure no doctor understands.
2. A house ledger or ward record becomes suddenly difficult to access.
3. Malone arrives one step closer and treats the crew's friendship as evidence.
4. A possible anchor feels a cold pull when Grigori is threatened.
