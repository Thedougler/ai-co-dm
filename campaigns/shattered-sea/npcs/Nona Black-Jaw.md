# Nona Black-Jaw

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: standalone portrait pending._
>
## Hook
Secure Perrin’s survey of [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]] while turning the 314+ missing-persons case into a route the Passage can act on.
## Identity and public function
Calveno host and Passage patron; she assigns practical work through the Black-Jaw Run.
## Look / voice
- **Visual:** Kitchen heat, a guarded door, and a sending stone kept within reach.
- **Behavior:** Counts the cost of a favour before she names its reward; keeps [[Enzo]] and [[Ruk]] between the room and danger.
- **Voice principle:** Clipped, practical sentences; family language becomes business terms when pressure rises.
- **Sample line:** “Bring me a survey I can use, and I can put a route under it.”
## Drive
**Want:** Keep the Passage and Black-Jaw Run alive while recovering usable leads on the taken people. **Fear:** The network becomes one more resource colonial pressure can price out. **Method:** Safehouses, couriers, bodyguards, and favours tied to clear obligations.
## Public face vs secret
**Public:** A practical Calveno host offering protection and work. **Secret:** She is a major Passage power; she grieves [[Vestra]]’s money more than the ship. The vault does not establish what she knows about Vestra’s fate.
## Leverage / need / cost
- **Leverage:** Kitchen safehouse, bodyguards, sending stone, Passage contacts, and Aruhe access.
- **Need:** A trustworthy survey and people willing to carry risk for the missing-persons desk.
- **Cost of cooperation:** The party spends time and accepts Passage obligations; a favour can become a debt.
## Limit and productive contradiction
She cannot expose the whole Passage without endangering it. She protects people through a network whose survival requires transactional pressure.
## Attitude / request posture / influence
Friendly but **hesitant** about unbounded promises. Evidence of recovered people, a credible Aruhe survey, or a concrete route moves her; threats against her network close the door.
## Relationships / PC invitations
- [[Perrin Black-Jaw]]: surveyor and family connection; offer him a lead on Aruhe or Vestra.
- [[Delmar Fisk]]: useful captain-facing talent; ask him to choose which passage is safe.
- [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]: map-reading asset; invite her to compare Aruhe and Maw routes.
- [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]]: Grung perspective on the taken; ask what the Warren leads omit.
- [[Catarina Da'Virelli]]: practical salvage and transport; trade Passage access for a workable tool.
## Resources
Kitchen safehouse, [[Enzo]], [[Ruk]], sending stone, Passage routes, couriers, and Aruhe leverage.
## Next move / if ignored
Send a short request through the sending stone for the Aruhe survey, then redirect Passage attention to the missing-persons desk. If ignored, the crew loses a chance at priority access; the missing-persons case continues without their help.
