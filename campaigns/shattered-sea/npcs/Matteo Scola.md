# Matteo Scola

*Split-lip*

> [!narration] Matteo Scola
> The man in the grey salt-stiff shirt keeps one hand near his split lip and the other near the missing boot. Wet hair sticks flat to his forehead. He watches the living people first, the water second, and the dark north trails only when someone else looks that way.

## At a glance

| **Role** | Witness and rescued deserter |
| --- | --- |
| **Nature** | Frightened, ashamed, practical once someone gives him a task. |
| **Home** | Calveno wreck people on [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]]. |
| **Wants** | Stay with living people and get away from the claimed water. |
| **Fears** | Being left alone, being sent back toward the otter hole, and admitting he was wrong about the woman. |
| **Knows** | The others climbed out of [[Aruhe - River Slack Basin]] and kept following the riverbank prints toward the smoke. |
| **Limit** | He does not name [[Aruhe - Hinewai|the woman in the woods]], and he shuts down if pressed to explain her. |

> **DM thesis:** Matteo is the cost of choosing pride and separation on Aruhe: he can warn the party that the inland group is splitting, but only if they make him feel less like the next body in the water.

## Running Matteo Scola

### First meeting

Use Split-lip as the table handle until a player asks his name or he has been pulled onto the bank. He is clinging to roots in [[Aruhe - River Slack Basin]], one boot gone, split lip bleeding, hips in the slack while two [[Aruhe - River Otter|river otters]] play him loose from the bank.

Matteo asks for the bank before he explains anything. If rescued, he stays close to whoever pulled him out and answers in short, wet sentences.

**Sample line:** "They kept walking. I went back for the pack."

### If asked why he was alone

Matteo says the woman in the woods told them to admire her garden, walk carefully, and not take living plants. The garden mouths wanted to follow. Matteo called it madness, left the argument, and tried to prove the safer answer was back toward the river and the pack. The otters took him before he could return with anything.

He does not say Hinewai's name. He does not know the graves, the Death Bloom, or how far the garden lies.

### At the farthest camp

If Matteo reaches [[Session-11-06-Farthest-Camp]], he sits near the fire and away from the north trails. He is Friendly toward the people who rescued him and Hesitant toward any plan that sends him north alone. He can say the others kept walking toward the smoke, that some people want the garden, and that the group is fewer than the tracks.

Use **Charisma (Persuasion) — `DC 15`** only if the party tries to change where he will go tonight. Success means he talks about [[Uncertainty]] and stays with the party instead of drifting back toward the garden mouths. Failure means he answers the hole, watches the garden mouths, and goes quiet.

# History

## The inland split

Matteo survived the wrecks and moved inland with the Calveno group that reached the river and then the quiet forest. When the woman in the woods offered garden rules, he heard trap before hospitality. He refused the advice, separated from the group, and went back for a pack at the slack water.

## The otter hole

The river otters found him isolated. By the time the party reaches [[Session-11-05-Otter-Hole]], Matteo is not a brave scout or a secret guide. He is the visible proof that on Aruhe, a person alone becomes prey.
