## Hook
A senior factor can approve a route, salvage plan, or loan before the ship finishes turning in harbour.

## Look / voice
- Works from the stern cabin beside Amberreach's contract office and sealed mail.
- Signs in the field rather than summoning merchants to a counting house.
- Precise, reliable, and comfortable making a promise sound like a form.

## Drive
**Want:** Keep insured routes, salvage plans, and cargo oaths moving. **Fear:** A paper trail that makes reliable trade look unsafe. **Method:** Sign only what can be underwritten, then make the signed terms travel.

## Public face vs secret
**Public:** Senior factor empowered to sign loans up to two thousand gold, route deals, salvage plans, and cargo oaths. **Secret:** No additional secret is established; do not promote the ship's sealed mail into a hidden plot without evidence.
