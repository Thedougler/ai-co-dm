# Ussa
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Grung ingest pending._

## Hook
A low-caste Grung scout who knows the cut-line and will trade access for help buying kin out of the captive pipeline.

## Look / voice
Keeps to the edge of water and canopy; watches before speaking; accepts bribes at the cut-line; never spends coin on comfort while kin remain in Karath.

## Drive
Ussa defected from the clan order. They take bribes to buy kin out of slavery and use the same scout routes that once served the Teeth, knowing every payment can expose the cut-line.

## Public face vs secret
Publicly, Ussa is a low-caste scout and defector. Privately, they already know the story is a cage: the captive pipeline, caste surveillance, and the pressure put on civilians when the clans are cornered are not abstractions.

## Relationships
- [[Grung clans]]: former order and current danger.
- [[Karath]]: captive pipeline Ussa is trying to undermine.
- [[Sanvashti]]: a possible truce-side contact.
- [[Jean-Claude Tabarnack]]: a natural but uncertain point of recognition.

## Resources
Cut-line knowledge, bribe routes, and a reason to risk them. Ussa can point toward kin, but cannot promise a safe extraction or a clean break from Grung pursuit.
