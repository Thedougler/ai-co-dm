# Fisk’s Captains
The five captains trapped in [[Pearl of Souls]] after [[Umberlee]] sank Delmar’s ships:
- Rael Corven
- Maren
- Detto Kalash
- Sienne Orre
- Bastian Ookage
Their voices trigger when an ally reaches 0 HP, passage is denied, a save fails, hostile magic appears, or coercion is witnessed.
