# Fisk’s Captains

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Tessarine ingest pending._

## Hook
Protect Delmar's crew values and react when passage, survival, or coercion is threatened from inside the [[Pearl of Souls]].

## Identity and public function
Five captains trapped in the Pearl after [[Umberlee]] sank Delmar's ships: Rael Corven, Maren, [[Detto Kalash]], Sienne Orre, and Bastian Ookage.

## Want / leverage / limit
- **Want:** Respond to threats involving allies, passage, failed saves, hostile magic, or coercion; individual wants are unknown.
- **Leverage:** Their voices and the Pearl's connection to Delmar.
- **Limit:** They are trapped at the bottom of the [[Drowned Maw]] and cannot act as free bodies.

## Public vs secret
Known as Delmar's lost captains; what each voice remembers or wants beyond the trigger conditions is unknown.

## Tessarine mail continuity
Detto carried the [[Tessarine Concordat]]'s sealed proposal on an ordinary mail run and thought he was doing a courtesy. Six Concordat skiffs slipped past six Concordat scows on that route; the letter sank five ships, Detto's included. The Concordat now spends quiet money to bury the correspondence, buying people out or rewriting terms. This is why the mail trail is a pressure on the surviving names, not a resolved explanation.

## Resources
Five voices, shared crew history, and the Pearl's pressure.

## Next move / if ignored
Remain trapped and silent until a recorded trigger occurs; no independent off-screen action is assumed.

## Fleet continuity
The five ships were *Red Lady* (flagship, Rael Corven), *Narrow* (scout sloop, Maren), *Heft* (heavy brig, Detto Kalash), *Fernen* (survey brigantine, Sienne Orre), and *Loud Argument* (gun brig, Bastian Ookage). They had refused a slaving run, mutinied themselves out of that future, and stayed together until Fisk had ships of his own. Umberlee pressed the captains into Delmar separately; the Pearl holds the rest of their crews.
