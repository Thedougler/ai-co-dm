# Aleksander Malone

## Art

![[attachments/shattered-sea/character-references/aleksander-malone-reference-sheet.png|Aleksander Malone character reference sheet]]

> [!narration] Narration
> Aleksander Malone is a tall high elf with swept-up ash-blond hair, pointed ears, and bright white lenses that hide his eyes. A long charcoal coat hangs over black shoulder scales and pale metal-edged panels, drawn tight with a belt and finished with rune-marked gloves and heavy black boots; a sword rests at his side with fine marks running along its blade. He studies each face as if weighing a name, then turns with the small click of buckles and metal scales toward the next person in his hunt.

## Visual reference

The reference sheet establishes Malone's tall, narrow high-elf silhouette, swept-up ash-blond hair, pointed ears, bright white lenses, charcoal long coat, pale metal-edged panels, black shoulder scales, layered belt, rune-marked gloves, heavy black boots, and a sword with fine blade markings.

## Office and reservation
Malone is a high elf cleric of [[Tyr]] held in reserve for confirmed heresy, not ordinary crime. The Crown tried assigning him a handler once; the handler did not survive. The standing service proverb is that their dog needs a long leash and anyone holding the other end will be bitten. The reservation is policy, not preference.

The policy changed after Malone was loosed in public at [[Sarn's Landing]]. The results were ugly enough that formal heresy confirmation is required before he sails. Malone still believes the violence was judgment; the Crown calls it a paperwork problem it will not repeat in a visible harbour.

## Blackrule and hunt
Malone chose [[Blackrule]] as his chapterhouse. He leaves when heresy is confirmed and returns when the work is done. His current hunt for [[Grigori]] is separate from ordinary Crown inspection and from House Knighton's revenge: he is pursuing a heresy pattern through noble houses and does not know [[Aldric Drave]] exists.

[[Corbin Knighton]] can pull him back from needless killing during a boarding, but Corbin is not his handler. A new handler assignment is expected to end badly.
