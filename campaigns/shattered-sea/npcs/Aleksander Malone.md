# Aleksander Malone

> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: NPC ingest pending._

## Current situation
Malone is in or returning through [[Calveno]] after boarding the [[Uncertainty]] from the [[HCS Ordinance]] with [[Corbin Knighton]]. He is hunting Grigori through Midchain noble houses on his own authority. The crew knows the healer and his route; Malone does not yet know where Grigori is. That gap is closing.

## Hook
Force Malone to distinguish a Crown warrant, a heresy hunt, and the person he believes Grigori to be. Good-faith scripture or Tyr's law may slow him; mockery will not.

## Look and presence
Malone is a tall, spare High Elf in a Crown-service coat that has been through more than tailoring can disguise. The wool carries salt and something older. His hands rest open at his sides—not relaxed, but ready. Before he speaks, his fingers touch his lips in a quick, habitual blessing. His eyes finish with a person before the conversation starts.

## Office and policy
Malone is a cleric of [[Tyr]], the god of justice and law, and the [[Dravosi Crown]]'s reserved instrument for confirmed heresy. He is not a Crown officer in the ordinary chain of command and has no handler. The Crown tried assigning one once; the handler did not survive. The admiralty's standing wisdom is that their dog needs a long leash and anyone holding the other end will be bitten.

The policy changed after Malone was set loose in public at [[Sarn's Landing]], a Crown Islands fishing village. The results were ugly enough that he now sails only after formal heresy confirmation. Malone still believes the violence there was righteous judgment.

## Blackrule
Malone lives alone at [[Blackrule]], a chapterhouse he chose in the southern Midchain. It is carved from volcanic terrace-rock, with one narrow stair from a landing that fits a single small boat. There is no flag, banner, or staff. One hall serves as chapel and barracks; Tyr's scales are cut into the altar stone and an oiled weapon rack waits for the next case. The nearest steps are eroding past repair.

He trains alone on its highest terrace, leaves when heresy is confirmed, and returns when the work is done. The chapel sits empty between.

## Methods and faith
His faith is absolute and expressed as violence he considers sacramental. He finds guilt by watching who flinches at God—his method, not a spell. He speaks in scripture cadence with grim laughter and carries the energy of a sermon already in progress. Every killing is Tyr's judgment delivered through his hands.

Fiction-facing capabilities: charm and fear do not take hold; wounds close again unless necrotic force interrupts the regeneration; he carries a blessed longsword and a fan of blessed bayonets that explode in radiant light. In a fight he reads as cleric, fighter, and rogue layered together—divine channel, action surge, and precise mobile strikes. No full combat sheet is kept here.

## The Ordinance boarding
During the [[Uncertainty]]'s ninth voyage, the [[HCS Ordinance]] intercepted the crew. Malone boarded beside Corbin. He used confession magic: [[Delmar Fisk]] broke and confessed, while [[Catarina Da'Virelli]] resisted and laughed with [[Umberlee]]'s voice until Corbin pulled his hound back. The crew played merchant and hid Perrin, Ruma, and Jean-Claude below decks, redirecting the warship toward Calveno—where Grigori had gone.

## The hunt
The hunt predates the Ordinance inspection. Malone was already pursuing a pattern of infiltration through earlier noble houses, including a Tessarine ward and a Dravosi merchant-lord's daughter. He hunts heretics, not thieves, and does not care about the crew's theft or Knighton revenge plot except as they intersect Grigori.

He is working backward through noble households to find the healer who calls himself Shepherd Grigori. The Crown requires confirmation before he sails, but Malone may kill Grigori before official confirmation arrives. A ranked Crown officer declaring a target guilty might aim him cleanly; another handler assignment is simply another death.

## Corbin as leash
[[Corbin Knighton]] commands the Ordinance and functions as Malone's leash: calm and professional where Malone is not, able to pull him back when a boarding threatens to become needless killing. Corbin is not Malone's handler. He is the naval pressure point that can still keep the Hound pointed at the stated target.

## Public face vs secret
- **Public:** A Crown heresy-hunter and cleric of Tyr, reserved for cases ordinary naval law cannot contain.
- **Secret:** The Crown's Sarn's Landing policy exists because Malone's public-space judgment went ugly; he still considers it righteous. His live Grigori hunt began from earlier noble-house evidence, not from House Knighton's personal revenge.

## Dramatic stakes
Will Malone kill Grigori before the Crown's official heresy confirmation arrives? If so, what happens to a crew caught between a healer they owe, possible blood-threads, and a zealot who treats that debt as evidence of corruption?

## Relationships and levers
- [[Dravosi Crown]] — reserved asset for confirmed heresy, not a day-to-day officer.
- [[Tyr]] — absolute faith and the law he believes he delivers.
- [[Shepherd Grigori]] / [[Khlysty / the Flock]] — live hunt through the earlier noble-house pattern.
- [[Blackrule]] — chosen chapterhouse, not exile.
- [[Corbin Knighton]] — the leash that can still rein him in.

Scripture or Tyr's law argued in good faith may delay him. Mockery will not. Ordinary crime, theft, and a handler assignment do not move him.

## If left alone
He remains at Blackrule between cases, then leaves on his own judgment when the heresy trail points to a house or port. Once Crown confirmation arrives, the long leash becomes an aimed weapon.
