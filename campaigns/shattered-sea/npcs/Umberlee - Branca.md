## Identity and public function
Branca is a senior [[Waveservants|Waveservant]] in her mid-forties, stationed at the [[Calven and Calveno|Calveno]] harbour shrine on the [[Mercatura]] canal. Her salt-white hair is pulled back hard, she has a slight limp she stopped noticing years ago, and she consults a little leather tide-table booklet before looking at a person. Her warmth is practiced: the warmth of someone who has delivered bad news often enough to make it easy.

## Clarifying experience
When [[Delmar Fisk]] finally came to the shrine after once ducking her with a lie, Umberlee borrowed Branca's body. The goddess spoke through a priestess's mouth that was not using the priestess's voice, snapped Branca's neck in front of witnesses, and put the breath back before the candles had time to drip. Branca returned alive and called it a clarifying experience. Her faith is not despite the cruelty; the cruelty is the faith.

The price named that day was simple: **Bring me the pearl.** Only then would Umberlee discuss the commissioners. Branca remains the appointment-keeper and keeps the appointment alive.

## Current status
- **Alive:** restored immediately after the possession killing.
- **Location:** Calveno harbour shrine, pale arch on the Mercatura canal.
- **Work:** records tribute, reads tide tables, receives captains, and keeps Umberlee's appointment.
- **Disposition:** warm, procedural, and unfooled by Delmar's previous evasion.
