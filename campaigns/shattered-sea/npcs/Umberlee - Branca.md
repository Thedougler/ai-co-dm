# Umberlee / Branca

> [!narration] Narration
> Branca is a Waveservant priestess whose voice carried one unmistakable command: “Bring me the pearl.”
## Hook
Return [[Pearl of Souls]] to [[Umberlee]]; the possessed Waveservant vessel delivered one command: **Bring me the pearl.**
## Identity and public function
Branca was a Waveservant priestess used as a possessed vessel for a divine demand. The vessel is recorded dead; the extent and source of the pressure remain carefully bounded by known events.
## Look / voice
- **Visual:** Established visual details are unknown; the stable visible handle is the Waveservant priestess vessel and the Pearl demand.
- **Behavior:** Delivers an imperative, survives long enough to make the demand consequential, and is recorded as killed and returned to prove a point.
- **Voice principle:** Ritual imperative; the demand is short and leaves no explanation.
- **Sample line:** “Bring me the pearl.”
## Drive
**Want:** Return the Pearl to Umberlee. **Fear/need:** The divine need behind the demand is UNKNOWN. **Method:** Possession through Branca, Waveservant pressure, and the Pearl’s claim over drowned souls.
## Public face vs secret
**Public:** Waveservant priestess and vessel. **Secret:** A divine demand spoke through possession; Branca was killed and returned to prove a point. Whether the pressure can act through another vessel is UNKNOWN.
## Leverage / need / cost
- **Leverage:** Waveservant priesthood, the demand, and the Pearl’s connection to the five trapped captains.
- **Need:** The Pearl at the bottom of [[Drowned Maw]].
- **Cost of cooperation:** Delivering it may preserve the tribute system while surrendering the five souls; refusing leaves the demand and Maw pressure active.
## Limit and productive contradiction
The Pearl remains at the Maw bottom beyond normal divine retrieval; Umberlee cannot kill Delmar without losing the souls and cannot simply retrieve it beyond her claim. Divine pressure requires a mortal vessel even as the vessel’s status is dead.
## Attitude / request posture / influence
Hostile and **unwilling** to accept substitutes; the original demand is simple, while the larger bargain is UNKNOWN. Evidence about the Pearl, the five captains, or the Maw can change the available bargain without resolving divine intent.
