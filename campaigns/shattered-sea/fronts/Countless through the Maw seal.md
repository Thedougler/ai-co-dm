# Countless through the Maw seal
## Impending doom
[[Talon Vantyrus]] uses the Sentinel crack and [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]'s map to open a path through the Maw.

## Dangers
### The splinter order
- Type / impulse: arcane / break the old boundary before the old order can choose what replaces it
- Cast: [[Countless]]
### Sentinel secrecy
- Type / impulse: institutional / preserve control through silence
- Cast: [[Sentinels of the Eyrie]]

## Villain pressure
- Want: Turn the Sentinel crack and the map into a usable path through the Maw seal.
- Fear or need: The Sentinels may close the crack, or the crew may learn enough to make Countless' claim answerable.
- Leverage / means: [[Talon Vantyrus]], splinter teachings, Sentinel history, the seal, and [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]'s pilgrimage map.
- Limit: The crack is partial, the map is not solely Countless' property, and the seal's answer is not fully predictable.
- Contradiction: Countless needs Sentinel knowledge while claiming the Sentinels' secrecy is the problem.
- Tell: Map fragments, arguments about the old seal, and pressure around Crissdalynn arrive before Talon does.
- Escalation threshold: The crew publicly chooses between Sentinel secrecy and Countless access, or carries the map into the Maw while the crack is active.
- Stop conditions: The crew can close or redirect the crack, expose the history and negotiate a third path, bargain with Countless, or let the seal open at a cost they choose.

## Grim portents
1. [ ] **Crissdalynn's pilgrimage reveals the crack.**
   - Trigger: The pilgrimage enters the Maw and the map records a response the Sentinels have withheld.
   - Objective / target: Countless identifies a usable weakness in the old seal.
   - Resource spent / visible sign: The map, the storm, and the pilgrimage's route; a new mark or route cannot be dismissed as ordinary weather.
   - Consequence if unopposed: Talon can stop searching broadly and begin targeting the map and its bearer.
   - Player interruption: Compare the mark with Sentinel records, choose what to reveal, or change the route before Countless can read it.
   - Fallback / reaction: Countless takes an incomplete interpretation and applies pressure to obtain the missing piece; the crack remains a changed fact.
2. [ ] **Countless claims a piece of the map.**
   - Trigger: The crew, Crissdalynn, or the Sentinels carries the map near a Countless agent.
   - Objective / target: Talon secures enough of the map to locate the crack again.
   - Resource spent / visible sign: Splinter agents and a stolen or copied map segment.
   - Consequence if unopposed: The crew loses exclusive control of the route and Countless can test the seal without permission.
   - Player interruption: Protect the bearer, reveal a decoy, bargain for the fragment, or follow the thief to learn what Countless knows.
   - Fallback / reaction: Talon keeps a partial segment and changes the approach; the missing information becomes a live reason to pursue or negotiate.
3. [ ] **Sentinel secrecy becomes the public conflict.**
   - Trigger: The crew asks what the Sentinels sealed, or Countless makes the silence visible.
   - Objective / target: Both sides try to control who gets the old history and what action follows it.
   - Resource spent / visible sign: [[Master Kyzil]]'s authority, High Eyrie records, and Countless' splinter teaching.
   - Consequence if unopposed: The seal's history becomes a factional weapon rather than shared evidence.
   - Player interruption: Demand the evidence, protect Crissdalynn's account, or make a limited disclosure that preserves a third option.
   - Fallback / reaction: The Sentinels reveal one true cost but keep another record closed; Countless recruits around the gap.
4. [ ] **The seal answers Talon.**
   - Trigger: Talon brings the map's surviving piece to the crack while the Maw is already under pressure.
   - Objective / target: Countless opens a path through the Maw seal.
   - Resource spent / visible sign: Map, crack, and the seal's response; the boundary changes in a way the crew can witness.
   - Consequence if unopposed: Countless gains passage and the Maw becomes less containable.
   - Player interruption: Reach the site, sever the map's use, redirect the opening, or force a bargain before the path settles.
   - Fallback / reaction: Talon retreats with a partial opening rather than erase the failure; the next confrontation moves to the changed route.

## Stakes questions
- What did the Sentinels seal, and what would a third path cost?
- Is Countless trying to free a passage, claim it, or make the old order answer for it?
- Can Crissdalynn choose what her map means before either faction turns it into a key?
