# Maw fissure and the tribute system
## Impending doom
The [[Drowned Maw]] fissure opens beyond any system's ability to contain it, turning ordinary passage and harbour protection into a choice about who pays the cost.

## Dangers
### Tribute collapse
- Type / impulse: infrastructure / keep the system running even when its price changes
- Cast: [[Waveservants]]
### The Pearl signal
- Type / impulse: planar breach / draw an answer across the boundary
- Cast: [[Pearl of Souls]] / [[Antheri]]

## Villain pressure
- Want: Keep the tribute network operating long enough to hold the fissure closed, whatever that requires.
- Fear or need: A missed payment or a public loss of faith exposes that the network may be containment rather than mere custom.
- Leverage / means: Harbour infrastructure, clergy, the tribute forms, and the Pearl's connection to the Maw.
- Limit: Tribute is paid harbour by harbour; it cannot be enforced everywhere at once, and the Pearl is outside ordinary retrieval.
- Contradiction: A system that protects passage also makes every harbour responsible for a danger it cannot see.
- Tell: Tribute demands change form or rate just before currents change; compass needles and spoken promises become unusually important.
- Escalation threshold: The crew publicly refuses, redirects, or exposes a tribute demand while the fissure is already answering the Pearl.
- Stop conditions: The crew can stabilize or reroute the tribute system, remove the Pearl from the signal's reach, expose its cost and force a different bargain, or accept a costly opening rather than preserve it.

## Grim portents
1. [ ] **A harbour's tribute changes.**
   - Trigger: A harbour misses, changes, or substitutes its usual payment.
   - Objective / target: [[Waveservants]] revise the demand using coin, rope, fish bones, compass needles, or a spoken promise.
   - Resource spent / visible sign: Clergy, port records, and a new demand at the harbour interface.
   - Consequence if unopposed: The harbour is marked as owing more, and trust in safe passage becomes a political dispute.
   - Player interruption: Compare the old and new demands, find who benefits, or offer a different payment and make the cost public.
   - Fallback / reaction: Waveservants accept a substitute but mark the harbour for closer collection; they protect the system rather than pretend nothing changed.
2. [ ] **A current reverses in a new lane.**
   - Trigger: Tribute pressure and the Maw's strain coincide with a route choice.
   - Objective / target: The tribute network attempts to keep a passage lane usable while currents run against it.
   - Resource spent / visible sign: Port guidance, boats, and changed charts; a familiar lane no longer behaves familiarly.
   - Consequence if unopposed: Travel is cut off or forced through a different harbour, concentrating leverage there.
   - Player interruption: Survey the change, choose another route, carry an alternative tribute, or bring rival factions into the decision.
   - Fallback / reaction: The Waveservants close the lane and preserve the nearest working route, exposing which communities are prioritized.
3. [ ] **Something answers the Pearl across the boundary.**
   - Trigger: The [[Pearl of Souls]] signals while the Maw or an old [[Antheri]] breach is under strain.
   - Objective / target: The signal draws a response across the planar boundary.
   - Resource spent / visible sign: The Pearl, the wreck at the Maw bottom, and a response like the one that woke Auralis.
   - Consequence if unopposed: Things cross into the Shattered Sea and the fissure's pressure becomes immediate rather than bureaucratic.
   - Player interruption: Trace the signal to the Pearl, compare it with [[Shelfworks]] evidence, or sever the signal before choosing its destination.
   - Fallback / reaction: The signal shifts to another available connection; the Waveservants attempt containment and reveal more of their reach.
4. [ ] **The fissure outgrows the tribute system.**
   - Trigger: The network has changed demands and routes still cannot keep the Maw stable.
   - Objective / target: Every claimant must choose whether to preserve, replace, or abandon the old containment bargain.
   - Resource spent / visible sign: Harbour authority, the Pearl, Sentinel knowledge, and the remaining usable lanes.
   - Consequence if unopposed: The Drowned Maw opens beyond the tribute system's ability to contain it.
   - Player interruption: Force a shared choice, redirect the Pearl, expose the bargain, or evacuate a threatened route.
   - Fallback / reaction: The factions hold one opening at the cost of another, leaving a changed map and a new pressure rather than erasing the consequence.

## Stakes questions
- What does the tribute actually buy: protection, delay, control, or all three?
- Who gets to choose the cost when a harbour cannot pay it?
- If the Pearl can call across the boundary, is preserving the seal worth preserving the system around it?

## Drowned Maw ingest
The Pearl's signal and tribute system are one pressure seen from two heights. Umberlee's claim stops at elemental water; tribute may be the procedural containment holding the fissure. If ledgers go red long enough, currents reverse, unknown-quarry stone rises, and missing ships become a rate. Auralis is the Antheri machine holding the fissure and losing ground; entities from the far side include [[Leviathan]], [[Ridgeback]], and [[Krakling]], while something larger scrapes at the crack.
