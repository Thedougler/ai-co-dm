# The taken 314
## Impending doom
The Grung raiding fleet turns 314+ missing people into durable power: the taken move through a raid, captive, and hatchery pipeline centered on [[Verdant Teeth]] and [[Karath]], while the Crown uses the crisis to make occupation look like rescue. Aruhe is the current wreck field, not the end of the pipeline.

## Dangers
### The raiding fleet
- Type / impulse: military / take fighting-age people and turn absence into leverage, labor, and captive soldiers.
- Cast: [[Grung clans]], Vethka war proas, Karath hatchery crews.
### Colonial response
- Type / impulse: political / use the crisis to tighten control.
- Cast: [[Dravosi Crown]].

## Villain pressure
- **Want:** Keep the taken moving through Grung operations until the clans' reach and water position are secure.
- **Fear or need:** A recoverable trail, united families, or exposure of Karath could turn raids into liability.
- **Leverage / means:** Fleet, Vethka war proas, [[Verdant Teeth]], Karath tannin routes, captive casters, hatchery pens, [[Warren]] activity, toxins, Simone's report and pursuit, and Crown papers and inspections.
- **Limit:** Raiders depend on routes and secrecy; Karath depends on hidden labor and the secret gold farms; the Crown depends on the appearance of lawful protection.
- **Contradiction:** The same rescue can free captives or give an authority an excuse to occupy the people searching for them.
- **Tell:** Missing-persons records, Aruhe survivors, wreckage, Warren movements, tannin traces, and reports around [[Sparhold]] point toward the same system.
- **Escalation threshold:** The crew exposes the fleet's trail, reaches Karath's pipeline, or blocks a Crown inspection in public.
- **Stop conditions:** Recover people, break the route, bargain with a clan, expose the occupation, or force a rescue that leaves both powers changed.

## Grim portents
1. [ ] **The trail reaches Sparhold.** The first chosen stop makes movement and records align; the fleet moves the cohort onward.
2. [ ] **Aruhe becomes a lethal holding edge.** Roughly 100–150 came off raid ships; most are dead, and the island is taking the rest.
3. [ ] **Warren activity becomes actionable.** [[Felix Aho]], [[Ruma Delacroix]], and [[Bazzoth]] connect the trail to clan operations; the fleet burns a route if pressed.
4. [ ] **Karath's pipeline is identified.** A captive or trace names hatchery huts, captive casters, or tannin routes; the clans accelerate transfers and patrols.
5. [ ] **A Crown inspection becomes an occupation.** Papers, boarding parties, and soldiers remain after the immediate excuse passes.

## Stakes questions
- Can the crew recover people without strengthening another authority?
- Which clan, family, or route can choose independently rather than become Crown or fleet property?
- If the next raid is prevented, what happens to the people already taken?
