# The taken 314
## Impending doom
The Grung raiding fleet turns 314+ missing people into a permanent expansion of power, while the Crown uses the crisis to make occupation look like rescue.

## Dangers
### The raiding fleet
- Type / impulse: military / take fighting-age people and turn absence into leverage
- Cast: [[Grung clans]]
### Colonial response
- Type / impulse: political / use the crisis to tighten control
- Cast: [[Dravosi Crown]]

## Villain pressure
- Want: Keep the taken people moving through Grung operations until the clans' reach is secure.
- Fear or need: A recoverable trail and united families could turn the raids into a liability instead of power.
- Leverage / means: The fleet, [[Verdant Teeth]], the [[Warren]]'s activity, tinctures, and the Crown's papers, soldiers, and inspections.
- Limit: The raiders depend on routes and secrecy; the Crown depends on the appearance of lawful protection.
- Contradiction: The same emergency can free captives or give an authority an excuse to occupy the people searching for them.
- Tell: Missing-persons records, Warren movements, and reports around [[Sparhold]] begin pointing in the same direction.
- Escalation threshold: The crew exposes the fleet's trail or blocks a Crown inspection in public.
- Stop conditions: The crew can recover people, break the route, bargain with a clan, expose the occupation, or force a rescue that leaves both powers changed.

## Grim portents
1. [ ] **The trail reaches Sparhold.**
   - Trigger: Reports from [[Nona Black-Jaw]]'s missing-persons work align with movement toward [[Sparhold]].
   - Objective / target: The fleet uses Sparhold as the next leg of its operation.
   - Resource spent / visible sign: Records, witnesses, and the route itself; families can name the same missing cohort.
   - Consequence if unopposed: The search becomes a race after the captives have moved on, and the fleet's reach looks larger than it did.
   - Player interruption: Follow the trail, split the search, warn the next harbour, or use the [[Passage]] to move people out of the fleet's path.
   - Fallback / reaction: The fleet abandons the obvious route and leaves a thinner trail; the Crown offers an inspection as the fastest answer.
2. [ ] **The Warren activity becomes actionable.**
   - Trigger: [[Felix Aho]], [[Ruma Delacroix]], and [[Bazzoth]]'s activity connects the missing-persons trail to Grung operations.
   - Objective / target: The raiders protect their route and the information that links the fleet to the taken.
   - Resource spent / visible sign: Warren contacts, tinctures, and clan hierarchy; a lead is useful but not safe.
   - Consequence if unopposed: The fleet gains time to move the 314+ people and the clans can deny a single command structure.
   - Player interruption: Test the lead against another vector, confront an operative, protect a witness, or negotiate with a clan outside the raiding fleet.
   - Fallback / reaction: An operative burns a route or changes a story; the crew still keeps the cost of that test and a narrower lead.
3. [ ] **A recovered captive names the next raid.**
   - Trigger: The crew reaches a captive or survivor before the fleet can move them again.
   - Objective / target: The fleet's next target becomes actionable information rather than a rumour.
   - Resource spent / visible sign: The captive's account, Simone's pursuit, or the route's physical evidence.
   - Consequence if unopposed: Another community loses people and the fleet turns rescue into a repeating chase.
   - Player interruption: Guard the witness, act on the warning, or publish it so the Crown cannot claim exclusive authority.
   - Fallback / reaction: The fleet changes the next raid but cannot erase the recovered account; the Crown accelerates its inspection response.
4. [ ] **A Crown inspection becomes an occupation.**
   - Trigger: Public fear gives the [[Dravosi Crown]] permission to secure the route.
   - Objective / target: Papers, harbour stops, boarding parties, and soldiers take control in the name of rescue.
   - Resource spent / visible sign: Garrisons and naval law; an inspection remains after its immediate reason has passed.
   - Consequence if unopposed: Families gain a guard and lose movement, while the Crown controls the search and its blame.
   - Player interruption: Expose the inspection's cost, refuse its exclusive authority, move people through the [[Passage]], or make the Crown answer to the families.
   - Fallback / reaction: The Crown narrows its stated mandate but keeps a foothold; the fleet exploits the divided response.

## Stakes questions
- Can the crew recover people without strengthening another authority?
- Which clan, family, or route can make an independent choice rather than become Crown or fleet property?
- If the next raid is prevented, what happens to the people already taken?
