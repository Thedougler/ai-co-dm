# Tessarine Concordat
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Crown ingest pending._

## Public goal
Make trade and credit reliable across the islands through merchant houses, ledgers, property, and public contracts.

## Secret goal
Rule through obligation. In [[Calven and Calveno]], Tessarine houses hold six of seven Council seats and the money beneath the Dravosi flag.

## Crown rivalry
The [[Dravosi Crown]] is the other charter in the same sea. The Crown inspects, commissions, and courts-martials; the Concordat invoices, extends credit, and calls debts. They are rivals who share harbours and collect from the same fishermen. Both pay [[Umberlee]] because tribute is infrastructure, not a sect. Neither has solved the Grung interiors of the [[Verdant Teeth]], so both buy what they can at the beach.

## Methods
Loans, trade credit, property, public debts, administrators, and cut-outs. Relief creates a longer obligation rather than replacing force with kindness.

## Crown-linked complications
- The Concordat used the [[Chain Council]] as a cut-out for the Pearl theft because it feared [[Aldric Drave]] could use the instrument to grow beyond his bloodline phylacteries. That motive does not establish what the Crown's founder is.
- [[Simone]]'s Crown toxin pipeline is a substitute-stock problem, not Karath farm product; the flask has appeared in Calveno's sewers and privateer kit.
- [[Lavinia Sordi]] left a Crown prison records desk over the pipeline, smuggled confiscated goods out, and spent nine clean years with a canal commission before selling a curse in the open.
