# Sunkline
> [!narration] Narration
> Sunkline's sunburst flies over one Fathomrush pier. Pressure rigs, hot iron, and hired confidence face the drop.

## Public goal
Reach and commercialize the Mid-Works while keeping investors confident.

## Secret goal
Keep a forged mid-shelf trial certificate from discovery long enough to preserve Tessarine money and salvage priority.

## Methods
Salvage orders, company law, pressure-rig trials, and paperwork. When pressed, protect the false log and let crews absorb the risk.
