# Dravosi Crown
> [!narration] Narration

## Public goal
Secure the western approach and make the sea legible through naval law, inspections, and garrisons.

## Secret goal
Expand the foothold and control movement, salvage, and contracts while using the Grung crisis to make occupation look like protection.

## Methods
Papers, harbour stops, boarding parties, soldiers, and blame. Under pressure, the Crown narrows its stated mandate before keeping the foothold it already took.

## Faces
- Leader / authority: the Crown's officers and [[Barnaby Rook]]
- Operatives: inspection crews, soldiers, and the Hound of Tyr

## Reach / resources
[[Port Tidefall]], garrisons, the fleet, and the binding contract held by [[Catarina Da'Virelli]].
