## Public goal
No living public spokesman remains; the surviving public record presents Antheri as builders of a western-wall city and a route toward the Elemental Plane of Water.

## Secret goal
Unknown. Their ruins may explain the [[Drowned Maw]] fissure and the [[Pearl of Souls]] signal, but the surviving evidence does not identify whose plan the breach served.

## Methods
Antheri built into the western wall of the Maw toward the Elemental Plane of Water; a planar breach exceeded their city. What remains is encountered through ruins, records, and salvage rather than a speaking hierarchy.
