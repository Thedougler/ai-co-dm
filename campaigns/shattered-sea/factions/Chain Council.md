# Chain Council
> [!narration] Narration
> The Chain Council's seal can make a private theft look like public protection, especially when the water is already afraid.

## Public goal
Protect Midchain water and coordinate island law.

## Secret goal
Serve as the commission mask for interests that want the [[Pearl of Souls]] controlled without exposing who ordered the theft.

## Methods
Commissions, seized forts, sealed orders, and delegated authority. A five-ship commission sent [[Delmar Fisk]] to steal the Pearl from [[Vel Orn]].

## Faces
- Commission: [[Delmar Fisk]] / Fisk's five ships
- Hidden principal: not settled in the dump; the [[Tessarine Concordat]] used the Council as a cut-out to keep the Pearl from [[Aldric Drave]].

## Reach / resources
Council seals, legal cover, Crown-connected ports, and the ability to turn private action into public policy.

## Relations
- Complicated: [[Tessarine Concordat]], [[Dravosi Crown]], [[Waveservants]]
- Party: unknown
