# Cosimo Verantio / The Principal

## At the table
Run Cosimo as a merchant face wrapped around a revealed dragon truth: a tall, fine-scaled dragon in a cutaway counting-house coat, with seal-stamped paper orbiting him like filings around a magnet. Every sentence is a ledger entry. He wants the party to accept terms, not merely lose hit points.

**Default tactic:** open with **Call in the Note** when it catches two or more characters, then use **Paper Step** to move between visible documents while forcing the party to choose between chasing Cosimo and advancing the Paper Trust track. He does not command the factor network; the factors are an unwitting circle of people following ordinary work.

**Decisive win:** break Paper Trust to 0 by invalidating obligation and making Tessarine paper untrusted. Reducing Cosimo to 0 hit points is a local body victory, but if the track remains above 0 the Concordat's hoard survives as a campaign problem. Burning coin chests, gold, or counting-house furnishings is an annoyance, not a win.

## One-sentence identity
This is a merchant-faced Magus Dragon controller that turns contracts and trust into psychic pressure to accumulate obligation, fears untrusted Tessarine paper and freed councils, and gives players a visible paper-trust track to break instead of a coin hoard to burn.

## Fiction signature
- **Fantasy:** a dragon whose hoard is the obligation carried by councils, routes, houses, and inherited notes.
- **Signature:** every conversation becomes a ledger, and every stamped paper can become a threat or a lightning rod.
- **Goal:** make the Tessarine Concordat's obligations feel inevitable until Calveno and the Crown Islands ratify terms that place their futures under his paper.
- **Fear:** a debt being invalidated, a mail trail proving the structure is false, a council being freed, or a certificate becoming untrusted.
- **Counterplay:** invalidate a pivotal debt, expose the buried mail trail, free a council from an active obligation, break a certificate, protect useful paper anchors, and refuse to let coin destruction substitute for proof.
- **Proof:** the same seal appears on documents that should have different origins; when a note is challenged, Cosimo's scales lose their ink-black sheen and the orbiting papers fall out of formation.

## Role / rank / assumptions
**Primary role:** Controller. **Rank:** Solo/legendary boss. **Target:** CR 18, calibrated as a high-tier house monster for a party whose exact level and size are unknown. The numerical chassis is deliberately below a full ancient dragon's durability and breath-weapon profile; Cosimo spends his budget on contract pressure, movement, and an external objective rather than a dragon breath loop.

This is a full design, not a reskin: the decision loop is an objective fight about obligation and paper trust, so a normal dragon chassis cannot carry the encounter by itself. The statblock uses one revealed hybrid form rather than a second merchant-form block; the public disguise is fiction and a utility trait, not a second set of numbers.

## Paper Trust / Obligation Hoard state track

Start at **4 Paper Trust**. Keep this track beside Cosimo's hit points. Each mark is a proof point in the hoard, not a pile of gold. A proof point can be resolved once, and no single ability check should settle one by itself: ask for an actionable document, a witness or counter-signature, and a public consequence. Let different characters contribute with investigation, social pressure, forgery detection, magic, protection, or direct action.

| Paper Trust | State | What weakens Cosimo |
|---:|---|---|
| 4 | Ledger Closed | All printed options function. The four Tessarine-voting houses and the inherited notes still look like ordinary commerce. |
| 3 | Note Invalidated | A pivotal note is voided, rejected by its issuer, or proven unable to bind its named debtor. Cosimo loses one use of Legendary Resistance (2 remaining). |
| 2 | Mail Trail Exposed | The Detto correspondence or another decisive paper trail is made legible to a credible witness or public record. Cosimo loses one more use of Legendary Resistance (1 remaining); the DM should show his orbiting papers misalign. |
| 1 | Council Freed | A council or house is publicly freed from an active Concordat obligation, so refusal is now cheaper than compliance. Cosimo loses his remaining use of Legendary Resistance, and **Call in the Note** can target only one creature instead of its normal area. |
| 0 | Certificate Broken / Paper Trust Broken | A sealed certificate, writ, or other credential is publicly shown untrusted or invalid. The hoard is cut: Cosimo loses **Paper Step**, **Paper Lightning**, and **Call in the Note**, and cannot use Legendary Actions. The decisive objective is won. If he is still alive, he may bargain, flee, or fight as a dangerous dragon, but he no longer has the network's dragon-scale leverage. |

**What does not advance the track:** burning gold, smashing coin chests, robbing a counting house, killing an unwitting factor, or destroying a random office. Those can remove cover, reveal a clue, or create a complication, but they do not cut the hoard unless they produce one of the four proof points above.

**If Cosimo reaches 0 hit points first:** treat the immediate fight as won, but not the hoard. The party still needs to complete the Paper Trust cut in the aftermath before the Concordat can restore ordinary-looking obligations around the Principal's absence. Do not add a resurrection or dragon-lifecycle subsystem unless play establishes one.

### Table procedure for each proof point
1. **Show the paper:** place or name the note, mail packet, council record, or certificate so players know what can change.
2. **Offer at least two approaches:** for example, trace the mail chain or confront its recipient; compare a seal or secure a public refusal; use magic to authenticate or put a witness under protection.
3. **Make the consequence visible:** a house stalls, a seal flakes, a writ is refused, or a debtor walks away without the sky falling.
4. **Mark one state only when the fiction changes:** a failed check costs time, attention, reputation, or a resource; it does not erase the attempt or advance the track by itself.

## Signature decision loops

### Call in the Note
- **Tell:** Cosimo holds a sealed document toward the target and reads one quiet line aloud; the paper hums before the save.
- **Threat:** psychic damage plus a choice between lost reactions and lost movement.
- **Responses:** spread out, move beyond the sphere, break line of sight, use a paper objective to force his attention, or accept the lesser cost while another character advances the track.
- **Payoff:** Cosimo pressures several characters without deleting turns, and his paper threat is clear from round 1.

### Paper Step
- **Tell:** a nearby Tessarine seal lifts from the page and shines like a doorway.
- **Threat:** Cosimo relocates across the room and consumes an anchor.
- **Responses:** keep anchors out of his sight, put a character between Cosimo and useful evidence, ready an attack for his destination, or let him consume an unimportant paper while pursuing the real proof.
- **Payoff:** he remains mobile and the party chooses which paper matters; the movement costs the anchor and cannot happen without visible paper.

### Paper Lightning
- **Tell:** a seal blackens and the ink crawls toward the edge of its page.
- **Threat:** a small area around the anchor flashes with lightning.
- **Responses:** move away, destroy or relocate the anchor first, use cover, or accept the burst while working on a different proof point.
- **Payoff:** Cosimo punishes a clustered defense without a breath-weapon spam loop, and the anchor is consumed after the flare.

### Paper Trust state
- **Tell:** invalid notes shed ink, exposed mail threads itself across the floor, a freed council's seal cracks, and a broken certificate goes blank.
- **Threat:** every missed proof point leaves Cosimo's control intact; attacks against coin do not matter.
- **Responses:** pursue any of the four proof points, protect witnesses and documents, or fight the body while accepting that the campaign win remains open.
- **Payoff:** the party can win by changing the fiction, not by out-damaging an inflated dragon.

## Three-round offense / defense audit

### Likely first three rounds
1. **Round 1 — Position and terms:** reveal the dragon truth without a surprise round. Use **Call in the Note** if it catches at least two targets; otherwise use **Multiattack** or *Suggestion* on the character nearest a paper objective. Use **Ledger Step** to take a defensible line or **Demand Security** against the first character approaching a proof point.
2. **Round 2 — Pressure and choice:** if a proof point is active, use **Break the Seal** on its protector or **Multiattack** the character trying to secure it. Use **Paper Lightning** only when two or more characters cluster around an anchor. Use **Paper Step** to abandon a consumed anchor and make the party choose whether to pursue.
3. **Round 3 — Defend the real hoard:** if Paper Trust has fallen, protect the remaining proof point without undoing progress. If it remains at 4, use a recharged **Call in the Note** or *Dimension Door* to keep the fight moving. If bloodied, Cosimo bargains first, then retreats toward a visible document; he does not suddenly heal or gain a hidden phase.

### Math summary
At CR 18, Cosimo uses PB +6, AC 19, 255 HP, attack bonus +13, and save DC 21. A routine **Multiattack** deals 50 damage on two hits before accuracy, while a typical **Call in the Note** catches two targets for 56 total before saves. A realistic round uses one legendary attack or pressure option, not all three attacks: against 60% hit / failed-save assumptions, the first three rounds average roughly 47–60 damage per round before the party's responses, with control and objective pressure carrying the rest of the boss budget. His strongest axis is controller pressure and mobility; his weaknesses are ordinary melee access, no regeneration, no physical-damage resistance, and paper anchors that can be protected or made irrelevant.

### Defense honesty
- **Effective defense:** 255 HP, AC 19, elemental resistances, charm/fright immunity, and up to three Legendary Resistances. He has no regeneration, no broad weapon resistance, and no immunity to common damage types.
- **Fair bypass:** Paper Trust is a second target and removes the strongest defenses one state at a time. A party that cannot land high-level attacks can still pursue proof points and protect the evidence.
- **Condition pressure:** **Break the Seal** is one target, once per day, with a repeat save; **Call in the Note** offers a choice instead of a hard condition. Do not add repeated stun, paralyze, or dominated turns.
- **Solo economy:** three current-style Legendary Actions, a Bonus Action that requires visible paper, and no separate lair action by default. Cosimo stays active without doubling every statistic.

## Terrain, allies, and failure states

- **Best arena:** a counting room, council gallery, archive, or public hearing with visible documents, seals, ledgers, and multiple routes. Put the real proof point where the players can reach it; do not make the objective a hidden check.
- **Lightning rods:** paper anchors, a protected witness, a council seal, an open mail packet, or a certificate can all draw attention away from Cosimo. The factor circle is **not** a monster roster: factors, clerks, houses, and successor firms remain independent people making their own choices.
- **Allies:** use established Tessarine personnel only if the encounter calls for them, and run them as independent defenders, frightened clerks, or competing objectives—not puppets or a hive mind. This sheet does not add minion statblocks.
- **Optional Vault of the First Factor:** the [[Vault of the First Factor]] may be a physical analogue of the hoard and a possible retreat or lightning rod **only if play confirms it**. It is currently unconfirmed and unmapped. Do not hard-map it or grant it lair actions by default. If the table earns the location, a single vault seal can replace one ordinary paper anchor; keep the uncertainty until that reveal.
- **Escape:** Cosimo uses *Dimension Door* or *Teleport* when Paper Trust is 1 or higher and the party has not protected the final proof. If Paper Trust reaches 0, he can still flee as a dragon, but flight is damage control, not a hidden success.
- **Failure other than TPK:** the party kills or drives off Cosimo while leaving Paper Trust above 0; a council ratifies terms, a mail witness vanishes, or the Concordat's paper remains trusted. They win the room and lose the structure.

## Running notes

- **Opening line:** “You have mistaken the collateral for the wealth. The wealth is that you still believe the signature means something.”
- **Default target:** the character closest to a proof point, then the character carrying or protecting a visible document.
- **If the signature is answered:** stop using area pressure as the main plan. Use Seal Talons, Ledger Step, and ordinary spellcasting while the party works the track.
- **If Cosimo is cornered:** offer terms that create a player choice but do not make agreement mandatory. A bargain can buy time, reveal a clue, or cost a resource; it cannot restore a reduced Paper Trust mark without a new table-earned reversal.
- **Do not:** make factors telepathic, treat coin destruction as hoard damage, reveal the Vault as confirmed before play earns it, or bolt on a breath weapon that makes the paper game irrelevant.

## Design dossier

### Reference-gate dossier
- **Anchor:** [SRD 5.2.1](https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd) for current attack/save notation, Recharge wording, and Legendary Action timing. Certain rule anchor: each Legendary Action is one use at the end of another creature's turn, with uses returning at the start of the monster's turn. The fence uses explicit ranges, targets, DCs, timing, and partial damage.
- **Official numerical peer:** the public 2024 [Balor](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Monsters:Balor?expansion=34653) is CR 19 with 287 HP and AC 19 in current-facing summaries. Pattern: high-tier solo bosses can carry strong defenses and multiple distinct actions without relying on an inflated AC. Cosimo sits one CR lower with lower HP, no broad immunities, and a controller objective instead of a damage nova.
- **Role peer:** [Lazy GM's Monster Builder Resource Document](https://slyflourish.com/lazy_5e_monster_building_resource_document.html) treats controllers as creatures that trade direct damage for movement and position pressure, and warns that a solo needs more than one bag of hit points. Pattern: make the controller's placement and the player's answer explicit. Cosimo's Paper Trust track is the second target.
- **Mechanic peer:** Pointy Hat's public [Magus Dragon / Warden Dragons collection](https://pointyhat.myshopify.com/products/pdf-dragon-folio-collection) establishes the useful archetype pattern of a dragon whose hoard is a social or professional group and whose power is spellcaster-shaped. Pattern only: spellcasting, escape options, and a hoard that changes the fight. This sheet rewrites that pattern around Tessarine obligation and does not copy a statblock, lore, lifecycle, or ability text.
- **Second mechanic pattern:** the public [MCDM Flee, Mortals! preview](https://shop.mcdmproductions.com/blogs/news/flee-mortals-the-mcdm-monster-book-preview-packet) demonstrates concise off-turn villain choices and visible dramatic beats. Pattern: give a solo a short off-turn menu with movement, pressure, and one limited area option. Cosimo uses Ledger Step, Demand Security, Seal Talon, and Paper Lightning; none requires variable-cost 2014 Legendary Actions.
- **Shared pattern:** current high-tier solos need readable off-turn activity, clear action costs, and a weakness or alternate target. Controller pressure works when the tell appears before the consequence and the party can choose movement, cover, objective work, or resource expenditure.
- **Deliberate deviation:** Cosimo has no breath weapon, no coin hoard, no puppet circle, and no dragon lifecycle subsystem. His social hoard is represented by a four-mark Paper Trust track that changes the printed statblock in visible stages.
- **Counterplay:** move out of the note's area, refuse clustered anchors, protect evidence, pursue any of four proof points, and use body damage as time-buying rather than the only path to a campaign win.
- **Math note:** the CR 18 chassis uses the high-tier formula only as a check; the peer dossier wins where it conflicts. The expected first three rounds are approximately 47–60 damage per round under 60% hit / failed-save assumptions, plus short-lived movement and reaction pressure. Paper Trust removes Legendary Resistance, shrinks the signature action, and then removes the off-turn package rather than adding another damage phase.
- **2024/2014 note:** the design is written in 2024/2025 notation: one-use Legendary Actions, explicit save outcomes, `Recharge 5-6`, and `Speed`. No 2014 conversion or Monster Manual dragon text was pasted. The CR is a house target, not a claim about an official Magus Dragon.
- **Uncertainty / playtest:** party level, size, access to high-level magic, and the number of physical paper objectives are unknown. Playtest whether players can name at least one valid Paper Trust target by the end of round 1. If the track is invisible, add a witness or physical document; if the fight is too easy, protect evidence with terrain or independent defenders rather than raising Cosimo's AC or HP. If the party ignores the track, let body damage win the room but keep the hoard's incomplete state visible.
