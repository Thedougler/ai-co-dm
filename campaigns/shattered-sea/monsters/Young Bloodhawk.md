# Young Bloodhawk

> [!narration] Narration
> Charcoal hawks still short of the valley-holder's span share the inland thermal, wings dark with only a seam of crimson at the tips. The nape is thin red fluff, not a mane; the cream hook stays shut until something on the grass has already been flushed. They cut a pass and are gone.


## At the table
**Tactic:** hit what the adult flushes or what rises; pass, don't grab. **Tell:** crimson tips flash as they fold; one solid hit and that bird peels off.

## Role / dials
- **Rank:** minion / standard pressure — two birds, one pressure group. Not a second boss.
- **Scatter:** any damaging hit vs AC 13 on a targeted attack drives that bird off (Flush Scatter). Track HP 13 only for area effects.
- **Easy:** one bird. **Hard:** both dive the same flushed or rising target.
- **Do not** give them Talon Grab, Haul Aloft, or Canopy Dive — those belong to the adult [[Bloodhawk]].

## Terrain / friends
Grass / river / lake with the inland [[Bloodhawk]]. They flush for the adult's stoop. They will not stoop closed canopy or eight-foot grass. Not the beach size-cap [[Small Blood Hawk]].

## Canon ecology
Juveniles of the gargantuan inland sky-holder — same ordinary stooping hawk as the beach bird, still growing into the eighty-to-hundred-foot span. They learn the stoop by working as a pair, hitting whatever the adult flushes or whatever rises. They mark; they do not lift.

## Do not
- Treat as a second [[Bloodhawk]] or a Roc.
- Confuse with [[Small Blood Hawk]] (beach size-cap form).
- Paste Monster Manual / proprietary hawk text.
