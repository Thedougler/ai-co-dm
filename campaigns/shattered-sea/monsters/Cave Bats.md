## At the table
Three- to four-foot-winged cave bats leave at dusk through skylights and feed over the canopy. Their guano is what the cave eats. A colony taking flight all at once means something farther in already moved.

## Signature moves
Swarm out - Feed over the canopy - Warn by taking flight.
