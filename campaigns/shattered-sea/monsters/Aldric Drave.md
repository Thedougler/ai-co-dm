# Aldric Drave

## At the table
**Tactic:** begin in the archive-vault threshold, expose a living anchor only when it buys position, and make the party choose between Aldric, a reanimated relative, and the heir ritual. **Tell:** every bloodline effect travels through a visible red thread in the vault's family ledgers or a matching pulse beneath an anchor's skin. **Win condition:** body damage buys time; permanent victory comes from exhausting the living bloodline or interrupting the inhabit-heir transition.

## Fiction signature
- **Fantasy:** the founder-lich of the Dravosi Crown, wearing a dead patriarch's authority as a weapon.
- **Signature:** he turns family records and living heirs into a distributed soul, then spends relatives as immediate fuel.
- **Goal:** preserve the Crown's bloodline map until the strongest sorcerer heir can carry the next three-century cycle.
- **Fear:** an unrecorded heir, a severed genealogy, or a host who survives the transition without accepting his will.
- **Counterplay:** isolate anchors, break line of sight to deny Family Reunion and Blood Sacrifice, interrupt reanimation, and make the named heir the objective instead of grinding through Aldric's body.
- **Proof:** the ledgers twitch toward the nearest anchor, and the next spell leaves a red afterimage along the family's recorded line.

## Role / rank / assumptions
Controller, solo/legendary boss; full house design rather than a generic lich reskin. The intended encounter is a hard tier-4 or high tier-3 finale in the Crestwall vaults with a four-character party. Aldric has one strong axis—distributed control of position and resources—supported by spell pressure and three off-turn choices, while his AC remains moderate and his return mechanism is visible and bypassable. The statblock uses 2024/2025 notation while retaining the legacy bloodline chassis as a recalibrated house pattern.

## Bloodline / phylactery / inhabit-heir state track

Keep this beside Aldric's Hit Points. It is a DM-facing state track, not a census calculator. Count only living descendants the DM has established as carrying Aldric's blood; noble-house membership alone is not a reason to invent a new anchor during play.

| Bloodline state | Living anchors | Reform after body destruction | Table meaning |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Crowded line** | 10 or more | 1d10 days | The Crown's records are thick with viable branches. Aldric returns near the closest surviving anchor. |
| **Thinning line** | 5–9 | 1d10 weeks | A few houses still carry the burden; Aldric's next body is less stable and the nearest branch becomes a visible clue. |
| **Failing line** | 1–4 | 1d10 months | The network is brittle. The surviving anchor(s) become urgent story targets and Aldric cannot safely use a random distant branch. |
| **Severed line** | 0 | No reform | Body destruction is permanent unless Aldric has already completed the inhabit-heir transition. |

**When the body falls:** mark the current band, name the nearest living anchor, and remove any descendant who was actually killed or severed. Do not roll reform time at the table unless the campaign needs a precise deadline; announce the band and use the result to pace the next arc. A body-only victory is a delay, not a kill.

**Inhabit-heir overlay:** this overlay replaces the random-return question when Aldric's roughly three-century cycle reaches its story beat.

1. **Unchosen:** no heir has been named. Aldric reforms through the band above.
2. **Marked heir:** the strongest living sorcerer descendant is identified by a red line in the Crestwall ledgers. The mark is visible to anyone who examines the heir or the relevant family record; Aldric wants the heir alive.
3. **Inhabitation window:** when Aldric's body is destroyed, the marked heir becomes the visible objective. The transition takes until the end of the heir's next long rest, and Aldric cannot complete it while the heir is unconscious, warded from undead possession, or outside the vault network. Give the party more than one route: protect and move the heir, sever the mark with a suitable story-grade rite, or interrupt three blood seals in the vault with actions and DC 19 Arcana, Religion, Insight, or Medicine checks. A failed attempt makes the next seal lash out for 10 (3d6) necrotic damage but does not end the attempt.
4. **Occupied:** the transition completes; the heir becomes Aldric's body and uses this statblock, with the heir's face and memories surfacing between attacks. Killing this body uses the normal bloodline track until the party breaks the overlay.
5. **Permanent break:** interrupting the window or purging an occupied heir removes Aldric's claim on that heir. If no other living anchors remain, Aldric is permanently dead. If other anchors remain, he can return only under the normal band and must choose a new story-marked heir later.

Do not turn the three-century cycle into a timer during combat. The state to announce is simply **body destroyed**, **marked heir**, **inhabitation window**, or **occupied**. The party's reliable win condition is to expose and break the heir transition, then finish the remaining bloodline state; the NPC ecology note remains the canon source for who is secretly connected.

## Crestwall vault encounter

Start Aldric within 60 feet of a family archive, with 2–4 living bloodline anchors or records visible but not automatically hostile. The vault has narrow bridges, ledger stacks, blood-sealed doors, and cliff wind through broken murder-slits. Use the archive as a lightning rod: a character can spend an action to tear down a family chart, close a blood-seal, or carry an anchor out of sight. Do not assign the archive initiative; its state is the clue and objective.

The Governor and ordinary garrison do not know Aldric exists. If guards appear, they are independent obstacles or frightened witnesses, not automatic bloodline allies. Geoffrey Draves, Aldous Draves, and other named relatives remain ecology and story material unless the DM deliberately establishes one as an anchor in this scene.

## Tells and counterplay

- **Family Reunion:** red threads visibly converge on a nearby conscious anchor before Aldric's attack bonus rises. Separate the anchor, incapacitate it without killing it, or force Aldric away.
- **Blood Sacrifice:** a chosen relative's veins darken before the saving throw. Break line of sight, move the relative, or accept the damage while denying Aldric the recovery through a successful save.
- **Reanimate Family:** the corpse exhales a red mist before rising. Drag the corpse away, ready an attack for its brief turn, or use the moment to reach a seal.
- **Spell pressure:** Aldric's Metamagic is audible in the ledgers even when his spell is Subtle. Spread against area spells, preserve a Counterspell answer, and make him spend the limited options.
- **Return:** the nearest anchor's family mark glows when the body fails. The party can pursue the anchor, the marked heir, or the records; no single ability check is the whole solution.

## Three-round script / audit

1. **Round 1 — Claim the room:** Aldric uses Hypnotic Pattern or Fear only if it creates a visible split; otherwise he uses Multiattack on the most isolated intruder and spends a Legendary Action on movement or Family Influence. He keeps one anchor in sight and does not begin with Blood Sacrifice unless the tell can be seen.
2. **Round 2 — Spend the line:** use Blood Sacrifice on a relative who is already exposed, or use Sleet Storm / Dominate Person to force a rescue choice. Reanimate Family only when a corpse changes the route to a seal. Use Bloodline Bolt as the reliable action when the party has spread out.
3. **Round 3 — Close the vault:** if the party is working a family record or heir, use Bloodied Floor or Family Rally to contest the route. If bloodied, prefer defense through movement and the remaining anchor rather than a hidden heal; Blood Sacrifice is a visible resource trade, not an automatic reset.

**Math note:** at +11 to hit against a typical high-tier AC, two Bloodline Bolts average about 14–17 damage before the anchor rider and accuracy; a two-Bolt turn plus one Cantrip Legendary Action averages about 21–25 before saves, while a failed Blood Sacrifice adds 16 damage to one target and returns the same amount to Aldric. Three legendary-action uses add one cantrip and one Bolt, or one costly family effect, not a second full turn. The area spells and lair actions create position pressure but are not counted as automatic multi-target hits. HP 263 / AC 17 gives the solo time to show the state track without relying on immunities; the permanent-kill bypass is the intended answer to his effective longevity.

**Defense audit:** cold, necrotic, poison, nonmagical weapon resistance, and undead condition immunities are narrow enough to fit the fiction but do not stop magical weapons, radiant damage, force, or mental saves that are not listed. His strong Charisma and Constitution saves are offset by only moderate AC, no regeneration, no blanket magic resistance, and a visible anchor network. Legendary Resistance is intentionally absent: counterplay should target the line and the heir instead of turning every failed save into a veto.

## Design notes (dossier)

### Reference-gate dossier

- **Anchor / current notation:** [SRD 5.2.1](https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd) is the public rules anchor for explicit 2024/2025 ranges, save DCs, conditions, and Legendary Action timing. The fence writes ranges, targets, durations, and partial saves directly.
- **Official numerical peer:** the public [Lich](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195044-lich) benchmark is a high-CR spellcaster with strong defensive traits, a spell list, and off-turn pressure. Pattern used: a solo caster needs reliable basic pressure plus limited control. Deliberate deviation: Aldric is held around CR 19 with AC 17, 263 HP, no Legendary Resistance, no Magic Resistance, and no stock soul-container text.
- **Role peer:** the [Lazy GM Monster Builder Resource Document](https://slyflourish.com/lazy_5e_monster_building_resource_document.html) treats a controller's job as movement and position pressure rather than maximum direct damage, and warns that a solo needs more than a large HP pool. Pattern used: Crestwall routes, anchors, and the heir create alternate targets and visible movement choices.
- **Mechanic peers:** the user-provided legacy Hierarch reference at `/Users/nick/shattered-sea/wiki/shattered-sea/kill-the-shepherd/creature.hierarch.md` supplies the bloodline-anchor timing bands, Family Reunion, Metamagic, Blood Sacrifice, Reanimate Family, and Crestwall-style lair menu. The house [Death Bloom](./The%20Blight.md) sheet supplies the separate pattern of a body objective paired with a visible external state track. These are patterns only; Aldric's names, wording, heir overlay, vault tells, and action prose are original house text.
- **Shared pattern:** give the solo one readable resource network, one limited recovery trade, and off-turn choices that can be denied by moving or changing targets. Strong defenses need an observable bypass.
- **Deliberate deviation:** the bloodline is distributed through descendants rather than a detachable phylactery; reform timing is a DM state band, and the heir transition is a story pressure with a concrete encounter bypass rather than a new combat calculator.
- **Counterplay:** isolate or move anchors, deny line of sight, spread against spells, interrupt corpse use, and attack the marked heir or seals. Failure costs time, damage, or a harder return—not an unavoidable turn loss.
- **2024/2014 note:** the legacy CR 19 chassis was recalibrated into current attack/DC notation and the one-action Legendary Action economy used elsewhere in this vault. No Monster Manual prose was copied; the legacy timing pattern is retained as an explicit house assumption.
- **Uncertainty / playtest:** party level, radiant access, number of established anchors, and whether the heir is present are unknown. If Aldric dies before revealing the line, expose an anchor through the return tell rather than increasing HP. If Blood Sacrifice overperforms, make the target's tell earlier or reduce its range before cutting Aldric's core identity.

## Running notes

- **Preferred position:** beside the archive and within 10 feet of one conscious anchor only when he wants the bonus; never hide the visible route to the heir or seals.
- **Default choice:** Multiattack the isolated character, spend one Legendary Action on Cantrip or movement, and reserve Blood Sacrifice for a relative whose failure changes position or timing.
- **If pressured:** use Family Rally or Bloodied Floor to cross the vault, then let the party choose pursuit or heir work. Aldric does not heal except through Blood Sacrifice.
- **If the signature is answered:** use Bloodline Bolt, ordinary spellcasting, and short off-turn movement; the fight remains a dangerous controller encounter after anchors are separated.
- **Resource tracking:** mark daily Metamagic uses, Blood Sacrifice immunity until the next turn, 3 Legendary Actions returning at the start of Aldric's turn, and the lair effect used last round. Keep the separate bloodline and heir state beside the block.
- **Failure state:** body-only victory gives the party a reform deadline. If the marked heir is occupied, the next scene is a host crisis rather than a second identical body fight. If the bloodline is severed or the heir transition is purged at zero anchors, Aldric is permanently dead.
