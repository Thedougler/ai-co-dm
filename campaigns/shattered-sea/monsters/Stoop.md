# Stoop

> [!narration] Narration
> _A peregrine line — wings pin, body drops, talons snatch, then the sky takes them back. TotM pending._

## At the table
**NPC / monster technique** (not a PC subclass feature). Add to a flying aarakocra elite or Sentinel antagonist. Brief named it for **Talon Skarn**; vault villain is [[Talon Vantyrus]] — use the package on whoever plays the peregrine diver; don’t invent identity.

**Tell:** wings pin to the body; a whistling dive line.

## Role / dials
- **Minion flyer:** Stoop without the disarm.
- **Elite:** full package below.
- **Solo:** add legendary action “Climb 30 ft (fly)” after Stoop.

## Signature moves
Dive → strike → tear a held item free → climb.

## Terrain / friends
Open sky, mast height, cliff Eyrie approaches. Allies who catch or steal the dropped item.

## Loot / aftermath
Whatever was torn free; torn harness straps; a broken weapon on the deck.

## Technique package (house)
Add the following to a creature that has a flying speed:

### Trait
**Dive Line.** Opportunity attacks against this creature have disadvantage while it is flying and has moved at least 30 feet in a straight line during the same turn.

### Action option
**Stoop (Recharge 5–6).** If the creature is flying, it flies up to its speed toward one target it can see that is at least 20 feet below it. If it moves at least 40 feet straight toward that target, it makes one melee weapon attack (usually talons or a held weapon) against the target with advantage. On a hit:

- The attack deals an extra **9 (2d8)** damage (scale: 1d8 low CR, 3d8 high elite).
- The target must succeed on a **Strength saving throw** (DC = 8 + PB + Strength or Dexterity modifier, attacker’s choice) or drop **one** object it is holding of the attacker’s choice. The object lands in a space within 10 feet (attacker chooses empty space if possible).

After the attack (hit or miss), the creature can fly up to **half its flying speed** upward without provoking opportunity attacks from that target.

## Running notes
- Open with altitude; telegraph the pin-wing tell.
- Prefer held plot items, weapons, or map cases — not every belt pouch.
- Counterplay: Ready action, stay under cover, Disarming Attack first, Earthbind/Slow, grab the dropped object before the diver’s ally does.
- **Do not** give this to PCs as a class feature without a separate brew.

## Design notes (DM)
- Pattern peers: Peryton-style dive; Roc/Bloodhawk grab-lift (control without swallow); Battlemaster disarm as the item-tear fantasy.
- Trade: recharge + altitude requirement + half-speed climb; no permanent Flyby stack unless you cut Dive Line.
- Knob: remove extra dice and keep only disarm; or make dropped object fall straight down only.
