# HCS Sovereign
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Crown ingest pending._

## At a glance
The peak of the [[Dravosi Crown]]'s visible claim: a first-rate, copper-sheathed ship of the line built at [[Harwick]], more than a hundred guns and six years on the stocks. She arrives when the Crown needs the whole Scatter to see the flag at once.

## Use
The Sovereign does not patrol or escort. Word of her coming runs weeks ahead; she presents guns and waits. She cannot turn in the shallows and is not sent after a stolen cutter.
