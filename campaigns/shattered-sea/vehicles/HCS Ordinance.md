# HCS Ordinance
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Crown ingest pending._

## At a glance
A three-deck Crown warship with Tyr blind in gold scales on the bow: a court that floats and the big hull people actually meet in a lane.

## Use
The Ordinance ends pirate gangs by being seen. It carries inspection authority into the lane rather than waiting for the whole fleet to arrive. [[Corbin Knighton]] has her now; [[Aleksander Malone]] boarded her during the Grigori hunt.

## Vehicle surface
Three decks, bow figurehead, rails and boarding points, inspection space, and the command structure needed to turn a sighting into a summons. No deck plan or numeric vehicle math is established here.

## Who is here
[[Corbin Knighton]], Crown officers and ratings, and the reserved heresy-hunter [[Aleksander Malone]] when a confirmed case warrants him.

## Secrets (DM)
The Ordinance is the Crown's practical court: its presence can make a lane legible before a shot is fired.
