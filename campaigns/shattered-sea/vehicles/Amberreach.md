# Amberreach
> [!narration] Narration
> _Visualizer: Tessarine ingest pending._

## At a glance
A wide grey cargo galleon out of Calveno, low in the water and deliberately boring in the useful way.

## Aspects
`reliable paperwork, numbered cargo, open gun ports`

## Vehicle surface / topology
Cargo ports are numbered. Gun ports remain open so pirates think twice. A stern cabin sits beside the contract office and sealed mail. One hold is bonded under a trade treaty that both the Crown Admiralty and the Concordat dislike and honour; Crown inspectors do not open it.

## Who is here
- [[Oriana Becht]], senior factor, signing loans, route deals, salvage plans, and cargo oaths in the stern cabin.
- Tessarine factors, couriers, and merchants who prefer predictable loss to heroic risk.

## Connections
- → [[Calven and Calveno]] · [[Calder's Tooth and Port Tidefall]] · [[Silkvane]]

## Hooks
- Board for a route deal, salvage plan, cargo oath, or sealed-mail question.
- Test whether a bonded hold is truly beyond Crown inspection.
- Follow a cargo record whose reliability is more dangerous than a pirate's threat.

## Secrets (DM)
Amberreach files trips three ports ahead, insures cargo, and pays most losses inside forty days. No numeric vehicle statistics are established here.
