# The Red Wake
## Current status
Open. The Red Lady is rising in the crew's trail, and more boats share the danger.

## Patron
None; the wake follows the crew's route.

## Objective
Understand or stop the [[Red Lady - Dead Lady]] as she rises in the crew's trail, then determine what must change so boats and crews stop vanishing on shared lanes.

## Stakes
More boats and crews vanish on shared lanes. The Red Wake also touches the Pearl, the Drowned Maw, and the only survivors of the original sinking.

## Nodes
- [[Red Lady - Dead Lady]]: raised wreck and immediate threat
- [[Drowned Maw]]: source of the wrongness
- [[Delmar Fisk]]: sole survivor of the original sinking and Pearl bearer
- [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]: sole survivor of the original sinking and map bearer
- [[Pearl of Souls]]: unresolved connection

## Conclusions → clues
### Conclusion: what is driving the wreck
1. It follows crew lanes rather than appearing at random.
2. It is tied to Umberlee's Red Wake.
3. The original survivors' connection to the Pearl remains unresolved and points back toward the Maw.

### Conclusion: the Red Wake is part of the route problem
1. More boats and crews vanish on lanes the crew still uses.
2. The Drowned Maw is named as the source of the wrongness.
3. The Maw's tribute and Pearl pressures can change which lanes remain safe or legible.

## Rewards
- A safer route or a meaningful explanation of the Red Lady.
- Leverage over the Pearl, Umberlee, or the Maw's changing lanes.

## Collection hull
The Dead Lady is the raised Red Lady, a collection hull for Umberlee: holds opened to the sea, cargo dumped as tribute, drowned crew spectral and recognizable to Fisk's sailors. It is still fisherman talk out of Kalowe; no public dock has tied it to Delmar. If Delmar points toward the Maw, the red hull stands off and its clock pauses.
