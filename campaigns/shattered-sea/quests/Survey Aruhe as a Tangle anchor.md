# Survey Aruhe as a Tangle anchor
## Current status
Open and active now. The crew is at/around [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]]; two survivors are aboard, inland survivors remain in the terraces, and a Great Crown Squid stopped at the tree line after the landing party disturbed a spore plant. No anchor decision has been made.

## Patron
[[Nona Black-Jaw]]

## Objective
Survey [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]] for a viable [[Tangle]] anchor, then report whether the island can connect safely to existing Runs without turning living Aruhe into a possession.

## Stakes
The [[Passage]] loses a needed route or anchor if Aruhe fails. Perrin's family obligation remains unresolved, and a bad connection could put an existing Run at risk. A route that treats the island as property may provoke the Hunger Rule; the taking-places show that people are already paying for possession.

## Nodes
- [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]]: current survey site, inland survivors, taking-places, and crown squid boundary
- [[Western Landing]]: one boat gap, reef hazards, and retreat edge
- [[Old Gardens]]: ancient terraces and genuine abundance
- [[The Quiet]] · [[Grasslands - River Valleys]] · [[The Rot]]: ecological route boundaries
- [[Clear Lake]] · [[The Grove - Death Bloom]] · [[Lava Tubes]]: water, restraint, and volcanic underworld nodes
- [[Karath]]: near-edge neighbor, not a shortcut to Aruhe's heart
- [[Perrin Black-Jaw]]: assigned surveyor and family obligation
- [[Warren]]: deepest anchor and elder teaching
- [[Tangle]]: route network the decision must serve

## Conclusions → clues
### Conclusion: Aruhe answers possession
1. Survivors report that fruit and island stuff get people killed; open-water fishing seemed safer.
2. Taking-places contain blood, skulls, and at least six dead near the terraces.
3. The spore plant and crown squid show that a claimed living thing can draw a larger boundary response.
4. The squid stopped at the tree line; the crew's retreat and mobile ship remain the safest known boundary.

### Conclusion: the survey has a cost beyond cartography
1. Nona's assignment makes the survey a Passage obligation.
2. Perrin's family history makes the decision personal as well as logistical.
3. Warren's elder teaching supplies a standard the island cannot be judged by maps alone.
4. The reef, terraces, graves, survivors, and canopy threat provide independent physical clues.

## Rewards
- A route decision the Passage can act on.
- Better leverage for Perrin's family and the Tangle's future.
- A precise answer about passage versus possession, even if the answer is “do not anchor.”
