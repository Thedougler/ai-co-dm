# Survey Aruhe as a Tangle anchor
## Current status
Open. Aruhe is the next survey site; no anchor decision has been made.

## Patron
[[Nona Black-Jaw]]

## Objective
Survey [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]] for a viable [[Tangle]] anchor, then report whether the island can connect safely to existing Runs.

## Stakes
The [[Passage]] loses a needed route or anchor if Aruhe fails. Perrin's family obligation remains unresolved, and a bad connection could put an existing Run at risk.

## Nodes
- [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]]: survey site and contested ground
- [[Perrin Black-Jaw]]: assigned surveyor and family obligation
- [[Warren]]: deepest anchor and elder teaching
- [[Tangle]]: route network the decision must serve

## Conclusions → clues
### Conclusion: Aruhe can or cannot anchor the Tangle
1. Aruhe's terrain and routes show what a physical connection can support.
2. Local relationships show whether the anchor can be used without making the island an unwilling asset.
3. A viable or impossible hidden connection to existing Runs settles the route question.

### Conclusion: the survey has a cost beyond cartography
1. Nona's assignment makes the survey a Passage obligation.
2. Perrin's family history makes the decision personal as well as logistical.
3. Warren's elder teaching supplies a standard the island cannot be judged by maps alone.

## Rewards
- A route decision the Passage can act on.
- Better leverage for Perrin's family and the Tangle's future.
