# Survey Aruhe as a Tangle anchor
## Current status
Open. Aruhe is the next survey site; no anchor decision has been made. Its country-scale geography is now known, but the route remains uncharted in play.

## Patron
[[Nona Black-Jaw]]

## Objective
Survey [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]] for a viable [[Tangle]] anchor, then report whether the island can connect safely to existing Runs without turning living Aruhe into a possession.

## Stakes
The [[Passage]] loses a needed route or anchor if Aruhe fails. Perrin's family obligation remains unresolved, and a bad connection could put an existing Run at risk. A route that treats the island as property may provoke the Hunger Rule.

## Nodes
- [[Aruhe - Hungry Isle]]: country-scale survey site and contested ground
- [[aruhe/Western Landing]]: one boat gap, reef hazards, and retreat edge
- [[aruhe/Old Gardens]]: ancient terraces and genuine abundance
- [[aruhe/The Quiet]] · [[aruhe/Grasslands - River Valleys]] · [[aruhe/The Rot]]: ecological route boundaries
- [[aruhe/Clear Lake]] · [[aruhe/The Grove - Death Bloom]] · [[aruhe/Lava Tubes]]: water, restraint, and volcanic underworld nodes
- [[Karath]]: near-edge neighbor, not a shortcut to Aruhe's heart
- [[Perrin Black-Jaw]]: assigned surveyor and family obligation
- [[Warren]]: deepest anchor and elder teaching
- [[Tangle]]: route network the decision must serve

## Conclusions → clues
### Conclusion: Aruhe can or cannot anchor the Tangle
1. Aruhe's terrain, country-scale distances, and routes show what a physical connection can support.
2. The [[Aruhe Hunger Rule]] distinguishes a tolerated passage from possession of living things.
3. Local relationships, including [[The Blight]], show whether the anchor can be used without making the island an unwilling asset.
4. A viable or impossible hidden connection to existing Runs settles the route question.

### Conclusion: the survey has a cost beyond cartography
1. Nona's assignment makes the survey a Passage obligation.
2. Perrin's family history makes the decision personal as well as logistical.
3. Warren's elder teaching supplies a standard the island cannot be judged by maps alone.
4. The reef, terraces, silence, water, graves, and lava tubes provide independent physical clues.

## Rewards
- A route decision the Passage can act on.
- Better leverage for Perrin's family and the Tangle's future.
- A precise answer about passage versus possession, even if the answer is “do not anchor.”
