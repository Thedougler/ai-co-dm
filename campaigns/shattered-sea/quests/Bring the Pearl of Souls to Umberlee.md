# Bring the Pearl of Souls to Umberlee
## Patron
[[Umberlee / Branca]]
## Objective
Decide whether and how to bring the Pearl from the bottom of the [[Drowned Maw]] to Umberlee.
## Stakes
The five souls in it may be lost into the Maw; Umberlee cannot kill Delmar without losing them and cannot retrieve it beyond her claim.
## Nodes
- [[Delmar Fisk]]: bearer
- [[Drowned Maw]]: wreck and Pearl
- [[Waveservants]]: tribute network
## Conclusions → clues
### Conclusion: the Pearl is still at the Maw bottom
1. Fisk’s five captains remain trapped. 2. Umberlee’s demand is unchanged. 3. The wreck is beyond normal divine retrieval.
