# Grigori and the Crown hunt
## Current status
Open. [[Shepherd Grigori]] remains aboard the pursued route, and the Crown's accusation is unresolved.

## Patron
[[Shepherd Grigori]]

## Objective
Learn Grigori's nature, test why the [[Dravosi Crown]] blames him for Surety, and choose whether to protect, expose, or hand him over before the [[Hound of Tyr]] reaches him aboard [[HCS Ordinance]].

## Stakes
The Hound of Tyr reaches Grigori aboard HCS Ordinance; the crew inherits the hunt. The choice can place Grigori, the crew, and the Crown's story in the same line of fire.

## Nodes
- [[Shepherd Grigori]]: passenger and healer
- [[HCS Ordinance]]: pursuit and Crown authority
- [[Dravosi Crown]]: accusation and naval law
- [[Surety]]: the charge the Crown uses

## Conclusions → clues
### Conclusion: what Grigori is
1. His bread-and-herbs routine shows the ordinary face he presents.
2. Red light from his wrist exposes a repeatable fact his routine does not explain.
3. The Crown's blame for Surety gives an independent reason to question both Grigori's story and the Crown's.

### Conclusion: the Crown's hunt is also a political move
1. HCS Ordinance turns the accusation into a naval pursuit.
2. The Crown's papers, harbour stops, and boarding parties can enforce its version of events.
3. The crew inherits the hunt once Grigori is aboard their route.

## Rewards
- A tested account of Grigori and Surety.
- A choice about the Crown's authority and Grigori's future.
