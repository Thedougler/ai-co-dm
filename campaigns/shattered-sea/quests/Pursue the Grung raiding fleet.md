# Pursue the Grung raiding fleet
## Current status
Open. The trail is active and points toward [[Sparhold]].

## Patron
[[Nona Black-Jaw]]

## Objective
Follow the trail from the 314+ taken fighting-age people toward [[Sparhold]], identify the fleet's operating route, and recover people or make a meaningful break in the raids.

## Stakes
Families disappear, the raiders' reach grows, and a Crown rescue can become an occupation. A successful pursuit must help the taken without handing every route to another authority.

## Nodes
- [[Sparhold]]: next stop and route lead
- [[Verdant Teeth]]: clan strongholds and fleet reach
- [[Warren]]: operative leads and a separate network of obligations
- [[Nona Black-Jaw]]: missing-persons records
- [[Simone]]: pursuit and tinctures

## Conclusions → clues
### Conclusion: the trail points into Grung operations
1. Nona's missing-persons desk tracks the 314+ taken.
2. [[Felix Aho]], [[Ruma Delacroix]], and [[Bazzoth]]'s Warren activity connects to the trail.
3. Simone's pursuit and tinctures point toward the same Grung operations.

### Conclusion: the search cannot rely on the Crown alone
1. The trail points toward Sparhold before a Crown inspection is established.
2. The [[Passage]] can move people through routes official systems do not control.
3. The Crown's naval law, boarding parties, and soldiers are also tools of expanded control.

## Rewards
- People recovered, leverage with Nona, and a clearer Grung map.
- A route or relationship that remains outside exclusive Crown control.
