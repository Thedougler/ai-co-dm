# Complete the Maw map pilgrimage

## Current status
Open. Crissdalynn’s exile condition remains incomplete, and the map’s meaning is contested. Kyzil has told her the pilgrimage is not over without explaining how.

## Patron
[[Master Kyzil]]

## Objective
Map the [[Drowned Maw]] from inside a storm and return with an account. Crissdalynn was exiled at 14 for treating craft too carelessly, not from malice.

## Stakes
The map may carry a Fate Spinner imprint from [[Yssenmoor]] and become the route to the Soul Incarnate transformation technique. [[Countless]] hunts its carrier; completion triggers [[Soul Incarnate's Watch]].

## Nodes
- [[High Eyrie]]: order, ledgers, and guarded seal
- [[Drowned Maw]]: map destination and storm
- [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]: pilgrim and carrier
- [[Calveno]] and [[Kalowe]]: current and search history
- [[Yssenmoor]]: hidden ley-line reaction

## Conclusions → clues
### Conclusion: what the map reveals
1. Reversed currents and storm observations are part of the Maw record.
2. The Fate Spinner can carry a signature from Yssenmoor.
3. Completing the pilgrimage activates [[Soul Incarnate's Watch]].

### Conclusion: the map is not only a record
1. [[Talon Vantyrus]] wants the transformation technique in the founding documents.
2. [[Countless]] hunts Fate Spinner carriers.
3. The seal may answer when imprint, crack, and pressure meet.

## Rewards
- Crissdalynn’s standing and a usable Maw map.
- Evidence for a choice between Sentinel record, Countless access, and a third path.
