# Complete the Maw map pilgrimage
## Current status
Open. Crissdalynn's exile condition remains incomplete, and the map's meaning is contested.

## Patron
[[Master Kyzil]]

## Objective
Map the [[Drowned Maw]] from inside a storm, complete [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]'s exile condition, and return with an account the Sentinels can act on.

## Stakes
The Sentinel exile continues, and the map may be claimed by [[Countless]]. A map that is accurate but hidden can still leave the seal vulnerable.

## Nodes
- [[High Eyrie]]: order, judgement, and guarded knowledge
- [[Drowned Maw]]: map destination and storm
- [[Crissdalynn Khinriss]]: pilgrim and map bearer
- [[Countless]]: rival claimant to the map's meaning

## Conclusions → clues
### Conclusion: what the map reveals
1. Reversed currents mark a route that ordinary charts do not explain.
2. Stone of unknown quarry links the Maw to a material source the crew cannot place yet.
3. The Maw's behaviour after the wreck shows what changed around the [[Pearl of Souls]].

### Conclusion: Countless can use the map as more than a record
1. The map records the Sentinel crack during Crissdalynn's pilgrimage.
2. Countless claims a piece or reading of the map.
3. The seal answers [[Talon Vantyrus]] when map, crack, and pressure meet.

## Rewards
- Crissdalynn's standing and a usable Maw map.
- Evidence for a choice between Sentinel secrecy, Countless access, and a third path.
