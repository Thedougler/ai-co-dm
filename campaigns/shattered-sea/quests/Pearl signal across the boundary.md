# Pearl signal across the boundary
## Current status
Open. The signal's destination and consequence remain unknown.

## Patron
None; the signal itself is the hook.

## Objective
Trace what the [[Pearl of Souls]]' signal draws across the planar boundary, identify the connection between the old breach and the present Maw, and choose whether to interrupt or follow it.

## Stakes
Things cross into the Shattered Sea; the Maw fissure and tribute system strain. Following the signal may reveal the breach while allowing the boundary to answer it.

## Nodes
- [[Pearl of Souls]]: signal source
- [[Antheri]] / [[Shelfworks]]: old breach evidence and salvage
- [[Drowned Maw]]: boundary and trench
- [[Auralis]]: woke when the signal answered
- [[Waveservants]]: tribute system under strain

## Conclusions → clues
### Conclusion: the signal is not local
1. Auralis woke when it answered.
2. The Pearl signaled across the boundary.
3. Antheri's planar breach predates the current powers.

### Conclusion: the signal is part of the Maw's current pressure
1. The Drowned Maw is where the Pearl and wreck remain.
2. The tribute system strains as the fissure responds.
3. Shelfworks preserves old breach evidence that can be compared with the present signal.

## Rewards
- A clearer history of the Maw and Antheri breach.
- A choice about what may cross, what the tribute system protects, and what the Pearl should become.
