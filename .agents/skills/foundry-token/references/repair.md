# Repair lines

Send one line. Keep the rest of the prompt.

- Face or 3/4 shot — `Same token. Steeper camera. More top of the skull. Less face. Keep south-facing. Keep magenta field.`
- Standing portrait — `Reject portrait. Overhead stamp only. Crown of head in the upper third, feet in the lower third.`
- Wrong facing — `Rotate the body so toes and weapon point to the bottom edge.`
- Weapon or limb cropped — `Pull the camera up. Entire weapon and every limb inside the square.`
- Floor or shadow field — `Delete the ground. Magenta field only. No contact shadow.`
- Ring or border appeared — `Delete the ring and border. Isolated cutout on magenta only.`
- Identity drift with a reference — `Restore the reference colors and silhouette. Keep the overhead camera.`
- Tiny subject swimming in magenta — `Scale the subject up to fill 85 percent of the square. Keep padding even.`
- Subject jammed against the edge — `Add even padding on all four sides. Keep the full silhouette.`
