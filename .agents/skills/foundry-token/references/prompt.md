# Locked Imagine prompt

Fill braces. Keep camera, facing, frame, background, and forbidden blocks verbatim.

```
Foundry VTT battlemap token. True overhead token art, steep top-down view from directly above.

Camera: locked straight down over the crown of the head. 90-degree overhead. No tilt. No isometric. No 3/4 hero angle. No portrait.

Facing: south. Chest, belt buckle, knees, and toes point toward the bottom of the frame. The spine is a vertical line down the canvas. Top of the head and hair or helmet dome are visible. Face is not toward the camera. Eyes do not look up at the viewer.

Subject: {SIZE} {CREATURE}, {IDENTITY}.
Kit: {GEAR}.
Pose: {POSE}.
Silhouette must stay readable at 100 px on a battlemap. Arms, tails, wings, and weapons stay slightly off the torso.

Style: {STYLE}.
Light: even overhead key, painted form shadow on the body only, no ground shadow.

Frame: perfect square 1:1. Full body centered like a stamp. {FRAMING}. Even padding on all four sides. Do not crop extremities.

Background: isolated cutout on flat solid chroma magenta #FF00FF. No floor, no ground plane, no environment, no circular token ring, no border, no text, no UI.

Forbidden: looking at camera, visible skyward face, isometric view, cinematic three-quarter view, worm's-eye, falling-backward pose, broken-neck pose, perspective warp, scenery, drop shadow on the background, token frame.
```
