## 8. Agency checkpoints

### Before each beat
- What makes this situation arise now?
- What can players perceive?
- What are at least two meaningful responses?
- What refusal, retreat, bargain, or alternate route is plausible?

### After each beat
- What is now true?
- What did players choose, create, ignore, or lose?
- Which candidates are obsolete? Which emergent beat is promoted?
- Does the next beat preserve polarity without forcing a scene?

### End-of-session audit
- Did the Hook fire once and only once?
- Did middle beats alternate Development and Cliffhanger?
- Did Climax precede Resolution, with the correct preceding polarity?
- Did failed, ignored, or player-created goals change the world?
- Did Resolution show consequences rather than promise a GM-mandated rematch?
