# Capture — Story so far (Nick dump 2026-09-05)

Source for wiki-ingest. Distill into story/session notes; do not leave as permanent inbox summary.

---

The story so far is a crew that did not start as a crew, on a stolen Crown cutter that did not start as theirs, chasing a raid that already got away.

They work the Shattered Sea: Crown Islands under Dravosi guns, the busy Strait with Calveno sitting in the middle of it, then the Midchain islands that answer to themselves, and south of that the Verdant Teeth, where the Grung keep their own country. The Drowned Maw is the wound in the west. Umberlee has a claim on that water. The Sentinels of the Eyrie watch it from the air and do not like her priests. The Passage is the old warren folk who slipped colonial pressure and now run holds and favors out of kitchens and dry docks.

The five people on the *Uncertainty* came in sideways.

Delmar Fisk used to be a real admiral. The Chain Council backed a five-ship privateer fleet under him. They lifted the Pearl of Souls from Umberlee's shrine at Vel Orn, and within hours the goddess wrecked the fleet over the Maw. The *Red Lady* went down with the rest. Crissdalynn Khinriss was already on that ship. She is an aarakocra monk the High Eyrie banished as a girl and sent on a pilgrimage her master thought she might actually survive. Perrin Black-Jaw is Nona Black-Jaw's grandson. His family brig, the *Vestra*, died to something in the water after the Pearl woke what lives under the Maw. He washed up and stowed on a merchant hull. Jean-Claude Tabarnack is Grung. He left his people after a reprisal killed Pell; Simone Tabarnack, who rose to warlord among the clans, has been hunting him ever since. Catarina Da'Virelli is a Calveno workshop woman who joined later, with an iron barrel and an owl.

They first met as cargo and stowaways on Beaumont Sel's *Saltwright*.

## How they stole a ship

Barnaby Rook brought the Crown cutter *HCS Surety* alongside the *Saltwright* for an inspection that was always going to be a boarding. The four of them hit his people in the hold. Cap'n Gorgeous died there. A boy named Geoffrey Draves dropped his sword, took Delmar's offer, and became the first crew they had. On the gangplank Jean-Claude threw Rook into the water and recognized the alchemist's gas as Simone's toxin. He did not say so. Rook climbed back aboard and put a round into Beaumont's salvaged shell. It bounced.

They finished it the next night. Beaumont's otter fouled a gun port. Delmar shot the other. Rook went in the water again. Beaumont settled accounts: Perrin is a Friend of the Passage, and Nona is already hunting her lost grandson. Jean-Claude told the truth on a stone — nobody sent him; he stowed to stay alive. Below decks they found Shepherd Grigori in the galley, who asked what kind of pirates they were and set one rule: anyone who wants off at the next port walks free. They also found Ket, a starving Moucheron Rook had kept as a specimen. They fed him and let him fly home toward Murrat.

They took Rook's letters, coin, a blade marked for Mira, and twenty toxin vials. The *Saltwright* peeled off for Kalowe. The prize cutter kept Crown paint and no new name and headed for Calveno. A storm came in from the east. Something hit the hull three times from below.

## Calveno

Perrin took a vision from that water: a sixty-foot body, a barb in a whale, one word burned in — *Grow.* Then they killed the whip-shark that had been striking them. Crissdalynn flew out, grabbed it by the tail, and hauled it clear. Grigori healed Jean-Claude with a red light from his wrist that Perrin could not name. They cut scales, kept the barb, and Jean-Claude took three fertilized eggs.

Five days later they slipped Calveno the quiet way, renamed the prize *Uncertainty*, and put her in La Vasca under Cobb. The water reached for Delmar as he stepped ashore and said his name. Grigori shook hands like ice and left for a week or two of festival business. A Waveservant stopped Delmar in the Mercatura and said she would not sleep until he came to the shrine. He talked past her.

Nona already knew the *Vestra* was gone. She grieved the money first. She called off the attacks already in motion, asked an unspecified favor, and Perrin said yes before he heard the terms. She gave him a sending stone. That afternoon Master Kyzil dropped out of the sun and flattened Crissdalynn. He had been searching Kalowe and Tidefall since the *Red Lady* sank.

Jean-Claude sold the whip-shark eggs as food, not breeding stock, and bought a silent bow and flying boots. Kyzil sparred all four of them on a Ponte Bassa roof and said the Maw had been unruly since the wreck, with something foul coming up from the south. Walking to the harbor, Delmar finally told the others what he was: admiral, five ships, Pearl thief. At the shrine Umberlee took Branca's body, killed her, and brought her back to prove the point. Her price is the Pearl, in exchange for Delmar's life. He tried to add the people who commissioned the theft. She read it out of his head. *Bring me the pearl. We will talk then.*

## The festival under the streets

Nona's stone went warm: bring your friends, especially the blue one. Six Grung sightings in the Warren sewers — blue, green, one purple. No Grung had crossed the Central Strait in living memory. She fed them and sent them down.

They burned a powder boat at the coast and took Felix Aho alive. He talked. Attack points under festival crowds. Black powder against sewer ceilings. Two days. Six ships. Simone's people kill leavers on sight, and he counted Jean-Claude as one of them. He asked for a month of protection. They delivered him to Nona in one piece.

Catarina came down with them on the next push. Ruma Delacroix gave up her magazine without a fight and has been running Passage check-ins ever since. Bazzoth went into the drink with his barrels. They hit the primary chamber mid-ritual: Solange Barret chanting, elite warriors in the way.

Festival morning happened on the roofs while that fight was still live. Kyzil refused the city's prank war. Thunk, blackout drunk, signed a thousand gold of new cannon onto ship credit. Nona caught a tomato in the fish market and threw it back.

In the chamber Vashu and Ozzeth died covering Solange. Perrin called his patron and heard *Not yet,* then coughed seawater. Solange said she would not have her princesses stopped. Ozzeth spent his last spells getting her clear. Grappled and poisoned, she still reached Catarina, spoke "Agni," and dropped the ceiling. Where she had knelt, something twelve feet tall stood up.

That was Otar. The Mercatura had a thirty-foot crater under a bright morning. Five pale leech-things came out of the rubble; the more they ate, the more human they looked. Delmar whistled the crow's-nest call. Kyzil came in ninety feet of white feathers and cut the brood apart. The town answered with the Warren's old skillet-and-pot signal. A woman heaved burning oil into the hole. Delmar flew the lip, put the chair he had rolled into existence earlier through Otar's throat, then a pistol through the eye. Catarina came out of its mouth into daylight. Nobody cheered. The carcass rotted where it fell. A scrap of paper sat in the decay that nobody has read.

Iacopo Fieschi signed them as Calveno's Defenders on Tessarine letterhead, because the Crown was not defending anything. Catarina turned her share toward repairs and street children. Crissdalynn kept a diamond ring. Perrin took a standing favor and sold Fieschi on the Black-Jaws. Under the signatures sat a problem: Fieschi said no warning ever reached him, and someone in that room had been given strict orders by Rupert Knighton to gather what she could and evacuate days before the ceiling opened.

They were still arguing about lunch when Nona's stone warmed. Come home. Hundreds missing.

## The desk and the chase

Nona turned the crater into a missing-persons table. Past three hundred and fourteen names by the time the queue thinned, every one a fighting-age man. Jean-Claude read it as slaving meant to weaken a fighting force by proxy, with Grung poison already turning up on ships that traded with the raiders. Nona put two Passage hulls to sea days behind, vowed chase in public, gave Perrin a favor instead of coin, and told them Cobb would have the *Uncertainty* ready in twelve hours.

Shopping turned up a name twice. At Morsani's cabinet they bought a wrist-shield they call the Snap. Delmar lifted a torn page that named Osset, a monk who left the Sentinels during a schism and was never heard from again. When Catarina said Morsani's source for the Snap was probably a corpse, the shop threw her twenty feet down the street. At La Cenere, Lavinia Sordi sold Crissdalynn a "cloak of protection" that identified as Nightmantle, a cursed displacement cloak. Lavinia said she got it from a falcon-featured man who also called himself Osset — a name Crissdalynn has never heard from anyone at the Eyrie. Down the canal, armless Zort offered a Midchain reptile contact in trade for a prosthetic. Catarina agreed. The name behind that contact is Roka Sten in Sparhold.

Season one closed there. Calveno held. The festival did not eat the city. The levy is still gone.

## Into the Midchain

The *Uncertainty* left La Vasca rebuilt: shallower draft, different silhouette, a workshop, four new guns, an icebreaker ram. First stop chosen was Sparhold, the closest fortified lumber town to Grung water.

Nona's spellcaster had already pulled a destination from a captured purple: Karath, the Grung center of power. The captives are bound there. That is not this season's live job. This season's live job is the wreck field the hurricane made when it cut the raiding fleet in half.

A cold open put them in the skins of three purple guards who washed onto Aruhe — the Hungry Isle every Grung child is taught not to land on. They ran, jumped, swam, threw a spear. Wolfrabbit packs ate them anyway.

On the real heading Crissdalynn found three sun-blasted Calveno men on shattered hull. The eastern storm had thrown them when the fleet split. The crew took them aboard.

Two days out, the *HCS Ordinance* dropped out of the sun: ninety-six guns, Knighton colors, Corbin Knighton on the first boat, and behind him Aleksander Malone, the Hound of Tyr, scanning faces with cold joy and asking after the aberration that calls itself Shepherd Gregory. They hid Perrin, Ruma, and Jean-Claude. Delmar sat in the fleet chair and claimed merchant employ. Catarina laughed with Umberlee's voice until Corbin reined his own hound in and the warship stood off for Calveno. They warned Nona by stone. Catarina handed Delmar Lamarae's Fang, a whip-shark-bone blade, in trade for help finding an axolotl.

## What happened last session

On the night run toward Aruhe, Grigori came below decks. He thanked them for the Calveno warning. He said Talon Vantyrus had sent a ship one or two hours behind to rob them for the thing Crissdalynn carries — the dreidel people also call the Fate Spinner — and he tied that hunt to the same Sentinel schism Osset's name already pointed at. He also said Delmar is under Umberlee's curse, and he asked them to hurt Corbin Knighton without killing him or breaking his mind. No one signed that bargain.

They did not wait to be boarded. Gust slowed the following hull. A broadside and fire left it listing and unable to chase. They did not go over the side to finish the crew.

Aruhe came up next. Two living wreck-people came off the shore and said the same thing in different words: maybe a hundred, a hundred and fifty came off the raid ships; most are dead; the island answers taking. Fruit and island stuff get people killed. Fishing the open water seemed safer. Grung look specifically marked.

A landing party went into the terraces anyway. Wolfrabbits. Black lotus. Spore plants. Caterpillars that should not be touched. Fruit sites with blood and skulls. At least six dead near the taking-places. Jean-Claude killed a spore plant and something that had been waiting under the trees came up invisible: a crown squid with tentacles that reach farther than a ship is long. It cut Delmar and tried to pull people in. They used illusion and flight and got back over the terraces. It stopped at the tree line. The ship stayed mobile instead of anchoring in. The inland survivors are still inland.

## Where that leaves them

They are Calveno's Defenders on Tessarine paper, Passage-adjacent by blood and favor, and still a stolen Crown cutter with a green crew that cannot man all the new guns at once. Three rescued men from the storm are aboard. Two more from Aruhe just told them the island itself is the other half of the prison. Karath still has the bulk of the taken — hundreds of fighting-age Calveno men — and that debt is sitting south of this chapter.

Umberlee still wants the Pearl. Malone and Corbin are in Calveno with a story to test and a hunt for Grigori. Simone still wants Jean-Claude. Osset's name is in two shops and nowhere in Crissdalynn's Eyrie. Vantyrus wants the dreidel. The chair Delmar used on Otar has no scratch on it. The scrap in Otar's rot is still unread. Nona's unspecified favor is still unspecified. And Aruhe is right there, plenty and mute, with people still in the terraces and a thing in the canopy that does not follow past the trees.
