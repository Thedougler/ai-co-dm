35: # Umberlee's Hidden Shrine
36: 
37: [[deity.umberlee|Umberlee]]'s shrine on Vel-Orn, cut into black stone in the Sunken Crown where five small islands ring the Blue Hole. Not a bright harbour shrine collecting routine tribute, this shrine grows older and quieter, reaching toward the drowned instead of the living.

51: A shrine carved into black stone on Vel-Orn's cliff face, accessible only by difficult skiff landing at timed surge. Salt and tide have worn the entrance smooth. Kelp wraps the pillars. No freshwater or shelter exists outside.

58: ## The Shrine
59: 
60: The shrine entrance is a mouth in the cliff face, worn smooth by salt spray and years of tide. Kelp wraps the pillars. Candles burn inside where wind should have killed them, and the shrine smells of brine, tallow, and wet stone, with something much older underneath. Sound behaves strangely here: footsteps soften to nothing. The shrine marks debts, keeps drowned names, and preserves an older [[deity.umberlee|Umberlee]] worship of the Blue Hole. Inside, a ledger chamber holds names, tokens, coins, fish bones, carved shells, and bits of ship or [[character-option.sailor|Sailor]] preserved as witness. Every object documents a death.
61: 
62: Not on colonial charts. Reaching it requires prior knowledge, a guide, or difficult navigation. Vel-Orn's approach is sheer, reef-bound, and unfriendly to casual landings.
63: 
64: The deepest chamber formerly held the [[item.pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] over a tidal pool built to its requirements. [[faction.fisks-fleet|Fisk's fleet]] stole the Pearl before the campaign, triggering their own destruction. (See [[faction.fisks-fleet|Fisk's Fleet]] and [[item.pearl-of-souls|Pearl of Souls]] for details on the theft and aftermath.) The shrine's protective function over the Sunken Crown blessing is now failing. [[deity.umberlee|Umberlee]] has stopped waiting on the debt and collects it off the water herself; see [[situation.the-red-wake|The Red Wake]]. [[pc.delmar-fisk|Delmar Fisk]] experiences dread near this shrine.
65: 
66: Sessions sometimes call this shrine "Vafnar," an in-fiction mishearing of the name.

69:

71: - **Ledger Chamber**: holds [[faction.fisks-fleet|Fisk's fleet]] scored as one breath.
72: - **Pearl Chamber**: the Pearl's absence registers as pressure and silence. *Detect magic* finds nothing.
73: - **War Room**: maps and records of the theft. Fisk's name in the margin.

75: ## Notable NPCs

[[deity.umberlee|Umberlee]] is patient. You have a debt, and the debt grows with every tide."* To anyone from [[location.keth-naar|Keth-Naar]]: *"You came to ask why the seas turned. This is where the anger began."*

79: [[location.keth-naar|Keth-Naar]]'s elders carry this shrine's crisis on their own island's page, where the ration timeline and the hunters' split over open water are recorded. [[npc.elder-anton|Elder Anton]] names the shrine directly as "the disruption origin" in his own Relationships.

56: ## Overview
57: 
58: [[region.midchain|Midchain]] free port. Softest of the little kingdoms: it bends toward shared rule and still bristles at [[faction.chain-council|the Council]]'s presence. Founding myth, generations worn: a tyrant (the Grask, in the oldest telling) personally held the reef-gap toll — the same chokepoint that is now the Waveservant tribute point. His dock bosses mutinied together; no one of them could have done it alone. Power stayed split so there would never be one throat to cut again.
59: 
60: Roughly 500 ft. across three reef-linked islets joined by stone bridges, the whole site ringed by a reef with a single navigable gap. The largest islet sits at dock level, crowded with cranes, warehouses, and the main dry dock, where repairs run around the clock. The other two rise only slightly higher, threaded by repair sheds and [[vehicle.the-narrow|The Narrow]] alleys where [[character-option.rogue|Rogue]] crews hide. Open water gives clear sight lines between islets; bridges bottleneck the rest, and the dock bosses watch every crossing. The reef gap is the only way in or out by sea, marked by the [[location.kalowe-waveservant-shrine|Waveservant Shrine]] where every hull pays toll. Population roughly 1,800 who call the docks home, more than double that whenever a fleet works the dry docks.
