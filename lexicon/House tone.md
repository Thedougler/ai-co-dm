# House tone
The Shattered Sea is **deadly, political, and weird** in that order of table pressure: consequences land on bodies, routes, families, and communities; no authority is neutral; and ordinary infrastructure keeps opening onto the uncanny.

- **Deadliness:** Missing people, vanished boats, the Maw, and Otar made danger concrete. Telegraph risk through visible consequences, give more than one way to respond, and let rescue, retreat, bargains, and sacrifice change the world instead of treating combat as the only climax.
- **Politics:** Papers, tribute, credit, contracts, inspections, and family obligations are tools of power. Every offer has a cost and a claimant. Show who benefits, who is made responsible, and which people an institution leaves underneath it; do not flatten factions into good or evil.
- **Weirdness:** Keep the strange attached to a noun and a consequence: the Pearl signals across a boundary, the Maw reverses currents, the Red Wake follows lanes, and the old seal answers a map. Start with the ordinary harbour or ship, then let the impossible interrupt it without explaining away the players' uncertainty.
- **Table promise:** Season 2 is a sandbox of pressures, not a route script. Put clues in multiple places, keep fronts interruptible, and let the crew choose which obligation becomes the next beat. Failure should create a changed route, relationship, or cost rather than erase the clue.
